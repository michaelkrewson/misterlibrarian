A dated vision: with the elders of Judah sitting before him, Ezekiel is carried in visions of God to Jerusalem and shown the image of jealousy at the gate, seventy elders with censers before carved figures, women weeping for Tammuz, and about twenty-five men bowing to the sun with their backs to the temple.
The sixth year, sixth month, fifth day: evidently the sixth year of King Jehoiachin’s exile, since chapter 1 identifies its own date as the fifth.

1
וַיְהִי בַּשָּׁנָה הַשִּׁשִּׁית, בַּשִּׁשִּׁי בַּחֲמִשָּׁה לַחֹדֶשׁ, אֲנִי יוֹשֵׁב בְּבֵיתִי, וְזִקְנֵי יְהוּדָה יוֹשְׁבִים לְפָנָי; וַתִּפֹּל עָלַי שָׁם, יַד אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה.
And it happened in the sixth year, in the sixth month, on the fifth of the month, as I was sitting in my house and the elders of Judah were sitting before me, that the hand of the Lord Jehovah fell upon me there.note
Y sucedió en el año sexto, en el mes sexto, a los cinco del mes, estando yo sentado en mi casa y los ancianos de Judá sentados delante de mí, que cayó allí sobre mí la mano del Señor Jehová.
2
וָאֶרְאֶה, וְהִנֵּה דְמוּת כְּמַרְאֵה-אֵשׁ--מִמַּרְאֵה מָתְנָיו וּלְמַטָּה, אֵשׁ; וּמִמָּתְנָיו וּלְמַעְלָה, כְּמַרְאֵה-זֹהַר כְּעֵין הַחַשְׁמַלָה.
And I looked, and here—a likeness like the appearance of fire: from the appearance of his waist and downward, fire; and from his waist and upward, like the appearance of a shining, like the gleam of chashmal.note
Y miré, y he aquí: una semejanza como la apariencia de fuego; desde la apariencia de sus lomos para abajo, fuego; y desde sus lomos para arriba, como la apariencia de un fulgor, como el destello del jashmal.
3
וַיִּשְׁלַח תַּבְנִית יָד, וַיִּקָּחֵנִי בְּצִיצִת רֹאשִׁי; וַתִּשָּׂא אֹתִי רוּחַ בֵּין-הָאָרֶץ וּבֵין הַשָּׁמַיִם וַתָּבֵא אֹתִי יְרוּשָׁלְַמָה בְּמַרְאוֹת אֱלֹהִים, אֶל-פֶּתַח שַׁעַר הַפְּנִימִית הַפּוֹנֶה צָפוֹנָה, אֲשֶׁר-שָׁם מוֹשַׁב, סֵמֶל הַקִּנְאָה הַמַּקְנֶה.
And he put out the form of a hand and took me by a lock of my head; and spirit lifted me up between the earth and the heavens, and brought me in visions of God to Jerusalem, to the entrance of the inner gate that faces north, where the seat of the image of jealousy was, which provokes to jealousy.note
Y extendió la forma de una mano y me tomó por un mechón de mi cabeza; y espíritu me alzó entre la tierra y los cielos, y me trajo en visiones de Dios a Jerusalén, a la entrada de la puerta interior que mira al norte, donde estaba el asiento de la imagen de los celos, la que provoca a celos.
4
וְהִנֵּה-שָׁם--כְּבוֹד, אֱלֹהֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל:  כַּמַּרְאֶה, אֲשֶׁר רָאִיתִי בַּבִּקְעָה.
And look, the glory of the God of Israel was there, like the appearance that I had seen in the valley.note
Y he aquí, allí estaba la gloria del Dios de Israel, como la apariencia que yo había visto en el valle.
5

And he said to me, “Son of man, lift up your eyes toward the north.” And I lifted up my eyes toward the north, and look, north of the gate of the altar was this image of jealousy, in the entryway.note
Y me dijo: «Hijo de hombre, alza tus ojos hacia el norte». Y alcé mis ojos hacia el norte, y he aquí, al norte de la puerta del altar estaba esta imagen de los celos, en el ingreso.
6
וַיֹּאמֶר אֵלַי--בֶּן-אָדָם, הֲרֹאֶה אַתָּה מהם מָה הֵם עֹשִׂים; תּוֹעֵבוֹת גְּדֹלוֹת אֲשֶׁר בֵּית-יִשְׂרָאֵל עֹשִׂים פֹּה, לְרָחֳקָה מֵעַל מִקְדָּשִׁי, וְעוֹד תָּשׁוּב תִּרְאֶה, תּוֹעֵבוֹת גְּדֹלוֹת.  {פ}
And he said to me, “Son of man, do you see what they are doing? Great abominations that the house of Israel are doing here, to go far from my sanctuary. But you will yet see again great abominations.”note
Y me dijo: «Hijo de hombre, ¿ves lo que ellos hacen? Grandes abominaciones que la casa de Israel hace aquí, para que haya alejamiento de mi santuario. Pero todavía volverás a ver grandes abominaciones».

וַיָּבֵא אֹתִי, אֶל-פֶּתַח הֶחָצֵר; וָאֶרְאֶה, וְהִנֵּה חֹר-אֶחָד בַּקִּיר.

Y me llevó a la entrada del atrio, y miré, y he aquí: un agujero en la pared.
8

Y me dijo: «Hijo de hombre, cava a través de la pared». Y cavé a través de la pared, y he aquí: una entrada.
9

Y me dijo: «Entra y ve las malas abominaciones que ellos hacen aquí».

Y entré y miré, y he aquí: toda forma de reptil y de bestia, cosa detestable, y todos los ídolos de la casa de Israel, grabados en la pared por todo alrededor.
11

And seventy men of the elders of the house of Israel, with Jaazaniah son of Shaphan standing in their midst, were standing before them, each with his censer in his hand; and the thick cloud of incense was going up.note
Y setenta hombres de los ancianos de la casa de Israel, con Jaazanías hijo de Safán de pie en medio de ellos, estaban de pie delante de ellos, cada uno con su incensario en la mano; y subía la espesa nube del incienso.
12
וַיֹּאמֶר אֵלַי, הֲרָאִיתָ בֶן-אָדָם, אֲשֶׁר זִקְנֵי בֵית-יִשְׂרָאֵל עֹשִׂים בַּחֹשֶׁךְ, אִישׁ בְּחַדְרֵי מַשְׂכִּיתוֹ:  כִּי אֹמְרִים, אֵין יְהוָה רֹאֶה אֹתָנוּ--עָזַב יְהוָה, אֶת-הָאָרֶץ.
And he said to me, “Have you seen, son of man, what the elders of the house of Israel are doing in the dark, each in the chambers of his imagery? For they say, ‘Jehovah does not see us; Jehovah has forsaken the land.’”note
Y me dijo: «¿Has visto, hijo de hombre, lo que los ancianos de la casa de Israel hacen en las tinieblas, cada uno en las cámaras de su imaginería? Porque dicen: “Jehová no nos ve; Jehová ha abandonado la tierra”».
13
וַיֹּאמֶר, אֵלָי:  עוֹד תָּשׁוּב תִּרְאֶה תּוֹעֵבוֹת גְּדֹלוֹת, אֲשֶׁר-הֵמָּה עֹשִׂים.
And he said to me, “You will yet see again great abominations that they are doing.”note
Y me dijo: «Todavía volverás a ver grandes abominaciones que ellos hacen».
14
וַיָּבֵא אֹתִי, אֶל-פֶּתַח שַׁעַר בֵּית-יְהוָה, אֲשֶׁר, אֶל-הַצָּפוֹנָה; וְהִנֵּה-שָׁם הַנָּשִׁים יֹשְׁבוֹת, מְבַכּוֹת אֶת-הַתַּמּוּז.  {ס}
And he brought me to the entrance of the gate of the house of Jehovah that is toward the north, and here—the women were sitting, weeping for the Tammuz.note
Y me llevó a la entrada de la puerta de la casa de Jehová que está hacia el norte, y he aquí: las mujeres estaban sentadas, llorando al Tamuz.
15
וַיֹּאמֶר אֵלַי, הֲרָאִיתָ בֶן-אָדָם; עוֹד תָּשׁוּב תִּרְאֶה תּוֹעֵבוֹת, גְּדֹלוֹת מֵאֵלֶּה.
And he said to me, “Have you seen, son of man? You will yet see again abominations greater than these.”note
Y me dijo: «¿Has visto, hijo de hombre? Todavía volverás a ver abominaciones mayores que estas».
16
וַיָּבֵא אֹתִי, אֶל-חֲצַר בֵּית-יְהוָה הַפְּנִימִית, וְהִנֵּה-פֶתַח הֵיכַל יְהוָה בֵּין הָאוּלָם וּבֵין הַמִּזְבֵּחַ, כְּעֶשְׂרִים וַחֲמִשָּׁה אִישׁ; אֲחֹרֵיהֶם אֶל-הֵיכַל יְהוָה, וּפְנֵיהֶם קֵדְמָה, וְהֵמָּה מִשְׁתַּחֲוִיתֶם קֵדְמָה, לַשָּׁמֶשׁ.
And he brought me into the inner court of the house of Jehovah, and here—at the entrance of the temple of Jehovah, between the porch and the altar, about twenty-five men, with their backs to the temple of Jehovah and their faces to the east; and they were bowing down to the east, to the sun.note
Y me metió en el atrio interior de la casa de Jehová, y he aquí: a la entrada del templo de Jehová, entre el pórtico y el altar, unos veinticinco hombres, de espaldas al templo de Jehová y de cara al oriente; y se postraban hacia el oriente, ante el sol.
17
וַיֹּאמֶר אֵלַי, הֲרָאִיתָ בֶן-אָדָם--הֲנָקֵל לְבֵית יְהוּדָה, מֵעֲשׂוֹת אֶת-הַתּוֹעֵבוֹת אֲשֶׁר עָשׂוּ-פֹה:  כִּי-מָלְאוּ אֶת-הָאָרֶץ חָמָס, וַיָּשֻׁבוּ לְהַכְעִיסֵנִי, וְהִנָּם שֹׁלְחִים אֶת-הַזְּמוֹרָה, אֶל-אַפָּם.
And he said to me, “Have you seen, son of man? Is it a light thing to the house of Judah to do the abominations that they have done here? For they have filled the land with violence, and have turned again to provoke me to anger; and here they are putting the branch to their nose.note
Y me dijo: «¿Has visto, hijo de hombre? ¿Es cosa ligera para la casa de Judá hacer las abominaciones que han hecho aquí? Porque han llenado la tierra de violencia, y han vuelto a provocarme a ira; y he aquí que se ponen la rama en la nariz.
18
וְגַם-אֲנִי אֶעֱשֶׂה בְחֵמָה, לֹא-תָחוֹס עֵינִי וְלֹא אֶחְמֹל; וְקָרְאוּ בְאָזְנַי קוֹל גָּדוֹל, וְלֹא אֶשְׁמַע אוֹתָם.
And I too will act in fury; my eye will not spare, and I will not have pity; and though they cry in my ears with a loud voice, I will not hear them.”note
Y yo también actuaré con furia; mi ojo no perdonará, y no tendré compasión; y aunque griten en mis oídos a gran voz, no los oiré».

Translator’s Notes

Verses 1–4 · Ezekiel 8:1–4 a dated vision — the figure of fire — lifted to Jerusalem — the glory there

⭐ A date and witnesses. The sixth year, sixth month, fifth day. Chapter 1 gives the thirtieth year, fourth month, fifth day, and identifies it as the fifth year of King Jehoiachin’s exile (1:2); this is evidently the sixth year of the same count. The elders of Judah are sitting with the prophet when it happens, and the hand of the Lord Jehovah ‘falls’ on him, the verb of falling where 3:22 had the hand simply ‘upon’ him. The ASV has “the hand of the Lord Jehovah fell there upon me”.

⚠ Fire, or a man. The Hebrew we follow reads ‘the appearance of fire’ (esh). The word is one letter short of ish, ‘man’, and several versions have a man here: the NIV “I saw a figure like that of a man”, while the ASV keeps “a likeness as the appearance of fire”. “His waist and upward” stands in two verses of the Hebrew Bible, here and 1:27. There the gleam of chashmal (chashmal) is above and fire below; here too. The ‘shining’ is zohar, the noun of Daniel 12:3, ‘shine like the shining of the vault’ (Daniel 12:3, now on these pages; zohar).

⭐ By a lock of hair. A hand is put out, only its ‘form’ (tavnit), and takes him by a tsitsit of his head, the word for a tassel (tsitsit). The verse does not name whose hand beyond the figure of verse 2. ‘And spirit lifted me up’ is a Hebrew form that returns at 11:1 (now on these pages), and 3:12 says the same in English; “between the earth and the heavens” stands in three verses of the Hebrew Bible, and he travels ‘in visions of God’, as at 1:1.

⚠ The image of jealousy. “The image of jealousy” (semel haqqin’ah) stands in two verses, here and verse 5. Semel is the word of Deuteronomy 4:16 (now on these pages), ‘the form of any figure’ (that page prints semel as ‘figure’, this chapter as ‘image’), and of the carved image Manasseh set in the house of God and later removed (2 Chronicles 33:7 and 15). The last word, hammaqneh, ‘which provokes to jealousy’, is built on the same root (qin’ah). The ASV has “the image of jealousy, which provoketh to jealousy” and the NIV “the idol that provokes to jealousy”.

⭐ The glory is there too. “The glory of the God of Israel” stands in five verses of the Hebrew Bible, all in Ezekiel, beginning here (kavod). It appears as it did in the valley (3:23), in the same sanctuary that holds the image.

Verses 5–6 · Ezekiel 8:5–6 look north — great abominations — who goes far

⭐ North. Told to lift his eyes northward, he sees the image north of the altar gate, in the entryway (bi’ah, a different word from the ‘entrance’ of the other verses). The prophet is told to look at what the elders suppose Jehovah does not see (verse 12).

⚠ To go far. “Great abominations” (to’evot gedolot), the two words, stands in three verses of the Hebrew Bible, verses 6, 13 and 15 here, a refrain that climbs the vision; in verse 15 they are the comparative, ‘greater than these’. The clause ‘to go far from my sanctuary’ leaves open who goes: God, driven off, is what most versions understand. The ASV has “that I should go far off from my sanctuary” and the NIV “things that will drive me far from my sanctuary”. The word for ‘what they’ in this verse is written as one and read as two; the page prints both.

Verses 7–13 · Ezekiel 8:7–13 the hole in the wall — the carved figures — seventy elders in the dark

⭐ Through the wall. He digs through the wall and finds an entrance (petach), the word of verses 3, 7, 14 and 16; a ‘gate’ is sha’ar.

⭐ What is on the wall. Creeping things, beasts, a detestable thing (sheqets, sheqets) and the idols (gillulim), ‘carved’, mechuqqeh. Deuteronomy 4:16–18 (now on these pages) had forbidden making the form (tavnit) of beast, bird, creeping thing and fish; that page prints tavnit as ‘likeness’, and this chapter prints ‘form’ to keep it apart from demut, the ‘likeness’ of verse 2. The ASV has “portrayed upon the wall round about”, the Geneva “painted upon the wall round about” and the NWT 2013 “carved on the wall all around”. This translation prints ‘carved’.

⭐ Seventy. “Seventy men of the elders” stands in three verses, Numbers 11:16 and 24 and here. Seventy elders of Israel had also gone up and seen the God of Israel (Exodus 24:9–10, now on these pages). Here seventy stand before carved idols. Among them is Jaazaniah son of Shaphan. A Shaphan was the scribe of King Josiah (2 Kings 22:3), and Ahikam son of Shaphan protected Jeremiah (Jeremiah 26:24, now on these pages); the verse does not say whether this is the same family. “The cloud of incense” stands in two verses of the Hebrew Bible: here and Leviticus 16:13, where on the Day of Atonement it ‘covers the cover that is on the testimony’. The ASV has “the odor of the cloud of incense went up” and the KJV “a thick cloud of incense went up”. This translation prints ‘thick’, with the KJV.

⚠ In the dark. They stand ‘before them’, the images; some versions add ‘worshiping’. The ‘chambers of his imagery’, chadrei maskito, use the noun of the ‘figured stone’ (maskit). Their saying, “Jehovah does not see us”, stands in two verses, here and Ezekiel 9:9 (now on these pages), where the two sayings come in the other order and without ‘us’. The KJV has “the Lord hath forsaken the earth” and the ASV “Jehovah hath forsaken the land”.

Verses 14–15 · Ezekiel 8:14–15 the women weeping — “greater than these”

⚠ The Tammuz. “Tammuz” stands in one verse of the Hebrew Bible, this one. The Hebrew says ha-tammuz, with the article. The verse says only that the women were weeping for him; the note on Daniel 11 speaks of Tammuz-Adonis. The ASV has “weeping for Tammuz” and leaves the name alone, the NIV adds a title, “mourning the god Tammuz”, and the Douay changes the name, “mourning for Adonis”.

⭐ Greater. Verses 6 and 13 say ‘great abominations’; only here is it ‘greater than these’.

Verse 16 · Ezekiel 8:16 between porch and altar — backs to the temple — bowing to the sun

⭐ Between porch and altar. “Between the porch” (bein ha-ulam) stands in two verses: here and Joel 2:17 (now on these pages), where the priests are to weep between the porch and the altar and ask Jehovah to spare. Here about twenty-five men stand in that very place with their backs to the temple and their faces east.

⚠ The sun. Bowing to the sun was named among forbidden things (Deuteronomy 17:3, now on these pages; 4:19). The verb is spelled irregularly in the Hebrew, a form that stands in this one verse. The text does not call the men priests. The ASV has “they were worshipping the sun toward the east”.

Verses 17–18 · Ezekiel 8:17–18 “a light thing” — violence — the branch — “I will not hear”

⭐ A light thing. The question is the one asked of Ahab (1 Kings 16:31, now on these pages; naqel). The charge adds violence: ‘they have filled the land with violence’, as the earth was filled with it before the flood (Genesis 6:11), and as 7:23 had said of the city.

⚠ The branch. What the gesture means is not stated, and this translation does not supply a meaning. The Hebrew we follow reads ‘their nose’. The ASV has “they put the branch to their nose”; the NWT 2013 has “they are thrusting out the branch to my nose”, and the Geneva reads a stink: “they have cast out stink before their noses”.

⭐ No hearing. The words of 7:9 return: my eye will not spare, and I will not have pity. Then: though they cry with a loud voice, I will not hear. The next chapter opens with a loud voice in the prophet’s ears.

Patterns worth carrying forward

Where this translation keeps the exact / distinct words: “abominations” at verses 6, 9, 13, 15 and 17; “entrance” for petach at verses 3, 7, 8, 14 and 16, “entryway” for bi’ah at verse 5, and “gate” apart from both; “form” for tavnit at verses 3 and 10 and “likeness” for demut at verse 2; “house of Israel” at verses 6, 10, 11 and 12 and “house of Judah” at verse 17.

Echoes paid. The figure of fire and chashmal of chapter 1; the glory seen in the valley of chapter 3; and the forbidden forms of Deuteronomy 4.

Echoes planted. ‘Jehovah does not see’ at 9:9 and my eye will not spare at 9:10 (both now on these pages).

Ezekiel 9, now on these pages: a loud voice, six men with weapons from the upper gate, and a man in linen with a writer’s inkhorn.
