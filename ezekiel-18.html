Ezekiel is told to answer a proverb used in Israel, that children pay for their fathers: each one is judged by his own ways, and whoever turns back will live.
The chapter gives no date.

1
וַיְהִי דְבַר-יְהוָה, אֵלַי לֵאמֹר.
And the word of Jehovah came to me, saying:note
Y vino a mí la palabra de Jehová, diciendo:
2
מַה-לָּכֶם, אַתֶּם מֹשְׁלִים אֶת-הַמָּשָׁל הַזֶּה, עַל-אַדְמַת יִשְׂרָאֵל, לֵאמֹר:  אָבוֹת יֹאכְלוּ בֹסֶר, וְשִׁנֵּי הַבָּנִים תִּקְהֶינָה.
“What do you mean, you who use this proverb on the ground of Israel, saying, ‘The fathers eat sour grapes, and the children’s teeth are set on edge’?note
«¿Qué quieren decir ustedes, que usan este proverbio en el suelo de Israel, diciendo: “Los padres comen las uvas agrias, y los dientes de los hijos tienen la dentera”?
3
חַי-אָנִי, נְאֻם אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה:  אִם-יִהְיֶה לָכֶם עוֹד, מְשֹׁל הַמָּשָׁל הַזֶּה--בְּיִשְׂרָאֵל.
As I live, declares the Lord Jehovah, you will no longer have occasion to use this proverb in Israel.note
Vivo yo, declara el Señor Jehová, que ya no tendrán ocasión de usar este proverbio en Israel.
4
הֵן כָּל-הַנְּפָשׁוֹת לִי הֵנָּה, כְּנֶפֶשׁ הָאָב וּכְנֶפֶשׁ הַבֵּן לִי-הֵנָּה:  הַנֶּפֶשׁ הַחֹטֵאת, הִיא תָמוּת.
Look, all the souls are mine; as the soul of the father, so also the soul of the son, they are mine. The soul that sins, it shall die.note
Miren, todas las almas son mías; como el alma del padre, así también el alma del hijo, mías son. El alma que peque, esa morirá.
5
וְאִישׁ, כִּי-יִהְיֶה צַדִּיק; וְעָשָׂה מִשְׁפָּט, וּצְדָקָה.
And a man, if he is righteous and does justice and righteousness—note
Y un hombre, si es justo y hace derecho y justicia:
6
אֶל-הֶהָרִים, לֹא אָכָל, וְעֵינָיו לֹא נָשָׂא, אֶל-גִּלּוּלֵי בֵּית יִשְׂרָאֵל; וְאֶת-אֵשֶׁת רֵעֵהוּ לֹא טִמֵּא, וְאֶל-אִשָּׁה נִדָּה לֹא יִקְרָב.
on the mountains he has not eaten, and his eyes he has not lifted up to the idols of the house of Israel, and his neighbor’s wife he has not defiled, and a woman in her impurity he does not approach;note
sobre los montes no ha comido, y sus ojos no ha alzado a los ídolos de la casa de Israel, y a la mujer de su prójimo no ha contaminado, y a una mujer en su impureza no se acerca;
7
וְאִישׁ, לֹא יוֹנֶה--חֲבֹלָתוֹ חוֹב יָשִׁיב, גְּזֵלָה לֹא יִגְזֹל; לַחְמוֹ לְרָעֵב יִתֵּן, וְעֵירֹם יְכַסֶּה-בָּגֶד.
and he wrongs no one: he returns his pledge for a debt, he commits no robbery, he gives his bread to the hungry and covers the naked with a garment;note
y no oprime a nadie: devuelve su prenda por una deuda, no comete robo, da su pan al hambriento y cubre al desnudo con ropa;
8
בַּנֶּשֶׁךְ לֹא-יִתֵּן, וְתַרְבִּית לֹא יִקָּח--מֵעָוֶל, יָשִׁיב יָדוֹ; מִשְׁפַּט אֱמֶת יַעֲשֶׂה, בֵּין אִישׁ לְאִישׁ.
he does not lend at interest, and takes no increase; he draws back his hand from injustice; he does true justice between man and man;note
no presta a interés, y no toma aumento; retira su mano de la injusticia; hace verdadera justicia entre hombre y hombre;
9
בְּחֻקּוֹתַי יְהַלֵּךְ וּמִשְׁפָּטַי שָׁמַר, לַעֲשׂוֹת אֱמֶת:  צַדִּיק הוּא חָיֹה יִחְיֶה, נְאֻם אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה.
in my statutes he walks, and my judgments he has kept, to act truly—he is righteous; he shall surely live, declares the Lord Jehovah.note
en mis estatutos anda, y mis juicios ha guardado, para obrar con verdad: él es justo; de cierto vivirá, declara el Señor Jehová.
10
וְהוֹלִיד בֵּן-פָּרִיץ, שֹׁפֵךְ דָּם; וְעָשָׂה אָח, מֵאַחַד מֵאֵלֶּה.
And if he fathers a violent son, a shedder of blood, who does to a brother any one of these thingsnote
Y si engendra un hijo violento, derramador de sangre, que hace a un hermano cualquiera de estas cosas
11
וְהוּא, אֶת-כָּל-אֵלֶּה לֹא עָשָׂה--כִּי גַם אֶל-הֶהָרִים אָכַל, וְאֶת-אֵשֶׁת רֵעֵהוּ טִמֵּא.
—and he himself has done none of all these—but even on the mountains he has eaten, and his neighbor’s wife he has defiled,note
—y él mismo no ha hecho ninguna de todas estas—, sino que hasta sobre los montes ha comido, y a la mujer de su prójimo ha contaminado,
12
עָנִי וְאֶבְיוֹן, הוֹנָה--גְּזֵלוֹת גָּזָל, חֲבֹל לֹא יָשִׁיב; וְאֶל-הַגִּלּוּלִים נָשָׂא עֵינָיו, תּוֹעֵבָה עָשָׂה.
the poor and needy he has wronged, he has committed robberies, a pledge he does not return, and to the idols he has lifted up his eyes, he has done abomination,note
al pobre y al necesitado ha oprimido, ha cometido robos, la prenda no la devuelve, y a los ídolos ha alzado sus ojos, ha hecho abominación,
13
בַּנֶּשֶׁךְ נָתַן וְתַרְבִּית לָקַח, וָחָי:  לֹא יִחְיֶה, אֵת כָּל-הַתּוֹעֵבוֹת הָאֵלֶּה עָשָׂה--מוֹת יוּמָת, דָּמָיו בּוֹ יִהְיֶה.
at interest he has lent, and increase he has taken—and shall he live? He shall not live. All these abominations he has done; he shall surely be put to death; his blood shall be upon him.note
a interés ha prestado, y aumento ha tomado: ¿y vivirá? No vivirá. Todas estas abominaciones ha hecho; de cierto se le dará muerte; su sangre será sobre él.
14
וְהִנֵּה, הוֹלִיד בֵּן, וַיַּרְא, אֶת-כָּל-חַטֹּאת אָבִיו אֲשֶׁר עָשָׂה; וַיִּרְאֶה, וְלֹא יַעֲשֶׂה כָּהֵן.
And look, he fathers a son, who sees all his father’s sins that he has done; he sees, and does not do the like:note
Y mira, este engendra un hijo, que ve todos los pecados que su padre ha cometido; los ve, y no hace cosa semejante:
15
עַל-הֶהָרִים, לֹא אָכָל, וְעֵינָיו לֹא נָשָׂא, אֶל-גִּלּוּלֵי בֵּית יִשְׂרָאֵל; אֶת-אֵשֶׁת רֵעֵהוּ, לֹא טִמֵּא.
on the mountains he has not eaten, and his eyes he has not lifted up to the idols of the house of Israel, his neighbor’s wife he has not defiled,note
sobre los montes no ha comido, y sus ojos no ha alzado a los ídolos de la casa de Israel, a la mujer de su prójimo no ha contaminado,
16
וְאִישׁ, לֹא הוֹנָה--חֲבֹל לֹא חָבָל, וּגְזֵלָה לֹא גָזָל; לַחְמוֹ לְרָעֵב נָתָן, וְעֵרוֹם כִּסָּה-בָגֶד.
and he has wronged no one, he has taken no pledge, and has committed no robbery; he has given his bread to the hungry and covered the naked with a garment;note
y no ha oprimido a nadie, no ha tomado ninguna prenda, y no ha cometido robo; ha dado su pan al hambriento y ha cubierto al desnudo con ropa;
17
מֵעָנִי הֵשִׁיב יָדוֹ, נֶשֶׁךְ וְתַרְבִּית לֹא לָקָח--מִשְׁפָּטַי עָשָׂה, בְּחֻקּוֹתַי הָלָךְ:  הוּא, לֹא יָמוּת בַּעֲוֺן אָבִיו--חָיֹה יִחְיֶה.
from the poor he has drawn back his hand, interest and increase he has not taken, my judgments he has done, in my statutes he has walked—he shall not die for his father’s iniquity; he shall surely live.note
del pobre ha retirado su mano, interés y aumento no ha tomado, mis juicios ha cumplido, en mis estatutos ha andado: este no morirá por la iniquidad de su padre; de cierto vivirá.
18
אָבִיו כִּי-עָשַׁק עֹשֶׁק, גָּזַל גֵּזֶל אָח, וַאֲשֶׁר לֹא-טוֹב עָשָׂה, בְּתוֹךְ עַמָּיו; וְהִנֵּה-מֵת, בַּעֲוֺנוֹ.
His father, because he practiced oppression, committed robbery on a brother, and did what is not good among his people—look, he dies for his iniquity.note
Su padre, porque practicó la extorsión, cometió robo contra un hermano e hizo lo que no es bueno en medio de su pueblo, mira, muere por su iniquidad.
19
וַאֲמַרְתֶּם, מַדֻּעַ לֹא-נָשָׂא הַבֵּן בַּעֲוֺן הָאָב; וְהַבֵּן מִשְׁפָּט וּצְדָקָה עָשָׂה, אֵת כָּל-חֻקּוֹתַי שָׁמַר וַיַּעֲשֶׂה אֹתָם--חָיֹה יִחְיֶה.
And you say, ‘Why does the son not bear the father’s iniquity?’ But the son has done justice and righteousness, has kept all my statutes and done them; he shall surely live.note
Y ustedes dicen: “¿Por qué no carga el hijo con la iniquidad del padre?”. Pero el hijo ha hecho derecho y justicia, ha guardado todos mis estatutos y los ha cumplido; de cierto vivirá.
20
הַנֶּפֶשׁ הַחֹטֵאת, הִיא תָמוּת:  בֵּן לֹא-יִשָּׂא בַּעֲוֺן הָאָב, וְאָב לֹא יִשָּׂא בַּעֲוֺן הַבֵּן--צִדְקַת הַצַּדִּיק עָלָיו תִּהְיֶה, וְרִשְׁעַת רשע הָרָשָׁע עָלָיו תִּהְיֶה.  {ס}
The soul that sins, it shall die. A son shall not bear the father’s iniquity, and a father shall not bear the son’s iniquity; the righteousness of the righteous shall be upon him, and the wickedness of the wicked shall be upon him.note
El alma que peque, esa morirá. El hijo no cargará con la iniquidad del padre, ni el padre cargará con la iniquidad del hijo; la justicia del justo será sobre él, y la maldad del malvado será sobre él.
21
וְהָרָשָׁע, כִּי יָשׁוּב מִכָּל-חַטֹּאתָו אֲשֶׁר עָשָׂה, וְשָׁמַר אֶת-כָּל-חֻקוֹתַי, וְעָשָׂה מִשְׁפָּט וּצְדָקָה--חָיֹה יִחְיֶה, לֹא יָמוּת.
And the wicked, if he turns back from all his sins that he has done, and keeps all my statutes and does justice and righteousness, he shall surely live; he shall not die.note
Y el malvado, si se vuelve de todos sus pecados que ha cometido, y guarda todos mis estatutos y hace derecho y justicia, de cierto vivirá; no morirá.
22
כָּל-פְּשָׁעָיו אֲשֶׁר עָשָׂה, לֹא יִזָּכְרוּ לוֹ:  בְּצִדְקָתוֹ אֲשֶׁר-עָשָׂה, יִחְיֶה.
None of his transgressions that he has done shall be remembered against him; for his righteousness that he has done he shall live.note
Ninguna de sus rebeliones que ha cometido será recordada contra él; por su justicia que ha hecho vivirá.
23
הֶחָפֹץ אֶחְפֹּץ מוֹת רָשָׁע, נְאֻם אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה:  הֲלוֹא בְּשׁוּבוֹ מִדְּרָכָיו, וְחָיָה.  {ס}
Do I take any delight at all in the death of the wicked? declares the Lord Jehovah. Is it not that he turn back from his ways and live?note
¿Acaso me deleito yo en la muerte del malvado?, declara el Señor Jehová. ¿No es más bien en que se vuelva de sus caminos y viva?
24
וּבְשׁוּב צַדִּיק מִצִּדְקָתוֹ, וְעָשָׂה עָוֶל, כְּכֹל הַתּוֹעֵבוֹת אֲשֶׁר-עָשָׂה הָרָשָׁע יַעֲשֶׂה, וָחָי--כָּל-צִדְקֹתָו אֲשֶׁר-עָשָׂה לֹא תִזָּכַרְנָה, בְּמַעֲלוֹ אֲשֶׁר-מָעַל וּבְחַטָּאתוֹ אֲשֶׁר-חָטָא בָּם יָמוּת.
And when a righteous man turns back from his righteousness and does injustice, doing like all the abominations that the wicked man does—shall he live? None of his righteous deeds that he has done shall be remembered; for his trespass that he has committed and for his sin that he has sinned, for them he shall die.note
Y cuando un justo se vuelve de su justicia y hace injusticia, haciendo como todas las abominaciones que hace el malvado, ¿vivirá? Ninguna de las obras justas que ha hecho será recordada; por la transgresión que ha cometido y por el pecado que ha pecado, por ellos morirá.
25
וַאֲמַרְתֶּם, לֹא יִתָּכֵן דֶּרֶךְ אֲדֹנָי:  שִׁמְעוּ-נָא, בֵּית יִשְׂרָאֵל, הֲדַרְכִּי לֹא יִתָּכֵן, הֲלֹא דַרְכֵיכֶם לֹא יִתָּכֵנוּ.
And you say, ‘The way of the Lord is not fair.’ Hear now, house of Israel: Is it my way that is not fair? Is it not your ways that are not fair?note
Y ustedes dicen: “El camino del Señor no es recto”. Oigan ahora, casa de Israel: ¿Es mi camino el que no es recto? ¿No son los caminos de ustedes los que no son rectos?
26
בְּשׁוּב-צַדִּיק מִצִּדְקָתוֹ וְעָשָׂה עָוֶל, וּמֵת עֲלֵיהֶם:  בְּעַוְלוֹ אֲשֶׁר-עָשָׂה, יָמוּת.  {ס}
When a righteous man turns back from his righteousness and does injustice, and dies for them, for his injustice that he has done he shall die.note
Cuando un justo se vuelve de su justicia y hace injusticia, y muere por ellas, por su injusticia que ha hecho morirá.
27
וּבְשׁוּב רָשָׁע, מֵרִשְׁעָתוֹ אֲשֶׁר עָשָׂה, וַיַּעַשׂ מִשְׁפָּט, וּצְדָקָה--הוּא, אֶת-נַפְשׁוֹ יְחַיֶּה.
And when a wicked man turns back from his wickedness that he has done and does justice and righteousness, he will keep his soul alive.note
Y cuando un malvado se vuelve de la maldad que ha cometido y hace derecho y justicia, él conservará viva su alma.
28
וַיִּרְאֶה וישוב וַיָּשָׁב, מִכָּל-פְּשָׁעָיו אֲשֶׁר עָשָׂה--חָיוֹ יִחְיֶה, לֹא יָמוּת.
Because he sees and turns back from all his transgressions that he has done, he shall surely live; he shall not die.note
Porque ve y se vuelve de todas sus rebeliones que ha cometido, de cierto vivirá; no morirá.
29
וְאָמְרוּ בֵּית יִשְׂרָאֵל, לֹא יִתָּכֵן דֶּרֶךְ אֲדֹנָי:  הַדְּרָכַי לֹא יִתָּכְנוּ, בֵּית יִשְׂרָאֵל--הֲלֹא דַרְכֵיכֶם, לֹא יִתָּכֵן.
And the house of Israel says, ‘The way of the Lord is not fair.’ Is it my ways that are not fair, house of Israel? Is it not your ways that are not fair?note
Y la casa de Israel dice: “El camino del Señor no es recto”. ¿Son mis caminos los que no son rectos, casa de Israel? ¿No son los caminos de ustedes los que no son rectos?
30
לָכֵן אִישׁ כִּדְרָכָיו אֶשְׁפֹּט אֶתְכֶם, בֵּית יִשְׂרָאֵל--נְאֻם, אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה:  שׁוּבוּ וְהָשִׁיבוּ מִכָּל-פִּשְׁעֵיכֶם, וְלֹא-יִהְיֶה לָכֶם לְמִכְשׁוֹל עָוֺן.
Therefore I will judge you, house of Israel, each one according to his ways, declares the Lord Jehovah. Turn back, and turn away from all your transgressions, and it will not be to you a stumbling block of iniquity.note
Por tanto, los juzgaré, casa de Israel, a cada uno según sus caminos, declara el Señor Jehová. Vuélvanse, y apártense de todas sus rebeliones, y no les será tropiezo de iniquidad.
31
הַשְׁלִיכוּ מֵעֲלֵיכֶם, אֶת-כָּל-פִּשְׁעֵיכֶם אֲשֶׁר פְּשַׁעְתֶּם בָּם, וַעֲשׂוּ לָכֶם לֵב חָדָשׁ, וְרוּחַ חֲדָשָׁה; וְלָמָּה תָמֻתוּ, בֵּית יִשְׂרָאֵל.
Throw off from yourselves all your transgressions by which you have transgressed, and make for yourselves a new heart and a new spirit; for why will you die, house of Israel?note
Arrojen de ustedes todas sus rebeliones con que se han rebelado, y háganse un corazón nuevo y un espíritu nuevo; pues ¿por qué han de morir, casa de Israel?
32
כִּי לֹא אֶחְפֹּץ בְּמוֹת הַמֵּת, נְאֻם אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה:  וְהָשִׁיבוּ, וִחְיוּ.  {פ}
For I take no delight in the death of the one who dies, declares the Lord Jehovah. So turn back, and live!”note

Translator’s Notes

Verses 1–4 · Ezekiel 18:1–4 the sour grapes — “all the souls are mine”

⭐ This proverb. Mashal again, ‘on the ground of Israel’ as at 12:22. The ‘you’ of verses 2–3 is plural.

⭐ Sour grapes. Boser is unripe fruit. The saying is also quoted at Jeremiah 31:28 (31:29 in English Bibles). There the fathers ‘ate’, a completed action; here the Hebrew has ‘eat’. ‘Are set on edge’ (tiqhenah) stands in three verses of the Hebrew Bible: this one and two in Jeremiah 31. The word printed ‘children’ is ‘sons’, the same that is ‘son’ from verse 4 on.

⭐ The complaint it carries. The proverb says that one generation pays for another. Lamentations 5:7 says it without the grapes. Exodus 20:4 (20:5 in English Bibles) speaks of Jehovah ‘attending to the guilt of fathers upon sons’; this chapter does not quote or mention that line, and how the two fit together has long been discussed.

⚠ Souls. Nefesh is the living person. ‘The soul that sins’ stands in two verses of the Hebrew Bible, here and verse 20. The NIV has “The one who sins”.

Verses 5–9 · Ezekiel 18:5–9 the righteous man

⭐ Justice and righteousness. The pair David is said to do at 2 Samuel 8:15, here asked of any man. It frames the chapter’s cases (verses 5, 19, 21 and 27).

⭐ A list. The acts run on from verse 6 to verse 9, mostly as ‘not’ and a verb. The Hebrew moves between completed and continuing forms, and the English keeps the difference where it can.

⭐ On the mountains. Eating there is set beside lifting the eyes to the idols. The NIV has “eat at the mountain shrines”.

⭐ In her impurity. The word is niddah, and the verb ‘approach’ is that of Leviticus 18:19.

⭐ The pledge. A thing held against a loan. Deuteronomy 24:13 says to return it at sunset. The Hebrew here is compressed, ‘his pledge a debt he returns’. Bread for the hungry and cover for the naked also stand together at Isaiah 58:7.

⭐ Interest and increase. Neshek and tarbit, the pair of Leviticus 25:36. The spelling tarbit stands in six verses of the Hebrew Bible. The KJV has “usury” for the first, the NWT 1984 “interest” and “usury”.

⭐ He shall surely live. The verb is doubled for force. Spelled chayoh yichyeh it stands in five verses, and spelled chayo yichyeh in four; all nine are in Ezekiel.

Verses 10–13 · Ezekiel 18:10–13 a violent son

⭐ Violent. Parits, from ‘break through’; a plural of it is the ‘robbers’ of 7:22. The KJV has “a robber”.

⚠ Does to a brother. The Hebrew of verse 10 is difficult: ‘and does a brother from one of these’. The ASV has “doeth any one of these things”, leaving the word out; the KJV “the like to any one of these things”.

⚠ Who did none of these? Verse 11 says ‘and he himself has done none of all these’. ‘He’ may be the father, who avoided them. The NIV has “though the father has done none of them”; the ASV reads of “duties”, which makes it the son who left them undone.

⚠ Shall he live? Verse 13 asks and answers. The NWT 1984 reads it as one statement, “he positively will not keep living”.

⭐ His blood upon him. The words damav bo stand in two verses of the Hebrew Bible: here and Leviticus 20:9. The responsibility for the death is his own.

Verses 14–18 · Ezekiel 18:14–18 a grandson who sees

⚠ He sees. ‘Sees’ comes twice in verse 14. Some read the second as ‘fears’, a change of vowels: the ASV has “feareth”, the KJV “considereth”.

⭐ The list again. It is shorter and not identical: the woman in her impurity is not named, ‘on the mountains’ has another preposition from verses 6 and 11, and where the first man returns a pledge (verse 7) this one takes none.

⚠ From the poor. Verse 17 has ‘from the poor he has drawn back his hand’, where verse 8 has ‘from injustice’. The NIV has “from mistreating the poor”.

⭐ His father. Verse 18 returns to the violent man. ‘His people’ is plural in Hebrew, ‘his peoples’, his kin.

Verses 19–20 · Ezekiel 18:19–20 “why does the son not bear it?”

⭐ An objection. The hearers ask why the son does not bear his father’s iniquity. The answer is what the son has done.

⭐ The rule. Deuteronomy 24:16 forbids a court to put sons to death for fathers or fathers for sons. It is quoted at 2 Kings 14:6. Verse 20 states what each one bears, in both directions.

⭐ Upon him. The second ‘wicked’ is written without ‘the’ and read with it. ‘The righteousness of the righteous’ stands in two verses of the Hebrew Bible: here and 33:12.

⭐ A later question. The disciples ask whether a man or his parents sinned, that he was born blind (John 9:2).

Verses 21–24 · Ezekiel 18:21–24 turning, either way

⭐ Turns back. Shuv drives the rest of the chapter. A wicked man who turns from sins lives; a righteous man who turns from righteousness dies, as at 3:20.

⭐ Not remembered. The same words are said of transgressions (verse 22) and of righteous deeds (verse 24).

⭐ Any delight? The verb is doubled, and this form of the question stands in one verse of the Hebrew Bible. The answer is in verse 32. The Douay has “Is it my will”.

⭐ Trespass. Ma’al, breach of faith, the word of 14:13 and 17:20. The NIV has “unfaithfulness”.

Verses 25–29 · Ezekiel 18:25–29 “the way of the Lord is not fair”

⚠ Not fair. The verb takan is about measuring or weighing; at 1 Samuel 2:3 deeds are ‘weighed’. The KJV has “not equal”, the NIV “not just”, the NWT 1984 “not adjusted right”.

⚠ The Lord. The people’s words have Adonai, ‘the Lord’, not the divine name, in verses 25 and 29. The NWT 1984 prints “Jehovah” in both. The complaint returns at 33:17 and 33:20.

⭐ Way and ways. Verse 25 asks about ‘my way’ and verse 29 about ‘my ways’. In verse 29 a plural ‘your ways’ takes a singular verb.

⭐ His soul alive. The turning man ‘will keep his soul alive’ (verse 27). The KJV has “save his soul alive”. In verse 28 ‘turns back’ is written one way and read another.

⚠ Dies for them. In verse 26 ‘them’ is plural though ‘injustice’ before it is singular.

Verses 30–32 · Ezekiel 18:30–32 a new heart — “turn back, and live”

⭐ Turn back, and turn away. The pair shuvu vehashivu stands in two verses of the Hebrew Bible: 14:6 and here. The NWT 1984 has “Turn back, yes, cause a turning back”.

⭐ Stumbling block. ‘A stumbling block of iniquity’ in this form stands in two verses of the Hebrew Bible: here and 44:12. Compare 14:3. The KJV has “so iniquity shall not be your ruin”.

⭐ Make yourselves. ‘A new heart’ stands in two verses of the Hebrew Bible. Here the hearers are told to make one. At 36:26 Jehovah says ‘I will give you’ one, and 11:19 promises a new spirit. The text sets command and gift down without comparing them. See heart and spirit.

⭐ Why will you die? Asked again at 33:11, with the same house of Israel.

⭐ And live. The verb for ‘turn back’ here is the second one of verse 30, printed ‘turn away’ there. The last words are two commands, ‘turn back, and live’. The Douay has “return ye and live”.

Patterns worth carrying forward

Where this translation keeps the exact / distinct words: “turn back” from verse 21 to the end; “transgressions” at 22, 28, 30 and 31, apart from the “trespass” of 24; “iniquity” apart from “injustice” and “wickedness”; and “soul” at 4, 20 and 27.

Echoes paid. The proverb on the ground of Israel from 12:22; the righteous man who turns from 3:20; and ‘turn back, and turn away’ from 14:6.

Echoes planted. Much of this chapter returns at Ezekiel 33:10–20. A list with eating on the mountains and taking interest returns at Ezekiel 22:9–12 (now on these pages).

Ezekiel 19, now on these pages: a dirge for the princes of Israel: a lioness and her cubs, and a vine.
