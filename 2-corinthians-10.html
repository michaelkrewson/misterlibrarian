The same letter, still written from Macedonia. Paul appeals to the Corinthians by the meekness and gentleness of Christ, answers those who say his letters are weighty but his presence weak, and describes the weapons of his warfare that tear down arguments. He refuses to boast beyond the measure God gave him, and ends on the one whom the Lord commends.
Undated by the text: the chapter names no year. It continues the letter of chapters 2 to 9 without a break, and it turns to the sharper tone of the last chapters.

2 Corinthians 10 — The Mister Translation

💬 Discuss this chapter
1
Αὐτὸς δὲ ἐγὼ Παῦλος παρακαλῶ ὑμᾶς διὰ τῆς πραΰτητος καὶ ἐπιεικείας τοῦ Χριστοῦ, ὃς κατὰ πρόσωπον μὲν ταπεινὸς ἐν ὑμῖν, ἀπὼν δὲ θαρρῶ εἰς ὑμᾶς·
I myself, Paul, appeal to you by the meekness and gentleness of Christ, I who am lowly when face to face with you, but bold toward you when away.note
Yo, Pablo, les hago una súplica por la apacibilidad y la bondad del Cristo, yo que parezco poca cosa cuando estoy con ustedes, pero soy valiente cuando estoy ausente.
2
δέομαι δὲ τὸ μὴ παρὼν θαρρῆσαι τῇ πεποιθήσει ᾗ λογίζομαι τολμῆσαι ἐπί τινας τοὺς λογιζομένους ἡμᾶς ὡς κατὰ σάρκα περιπατοῦντας.
I ask that when I am present I may not have to be bold with the confidence I intend to use against some who think we walk according to the flesh.note
Ruego que, cuando esté presente, no tenga que ser valiente y tomar duras medidas contra algunos que piensan que nos guiamos por un punto de vista humano.
3
ἐν σαρκὶ γὰρ περιπατοῦντες οὐ κατὰ σάρκα στρατευόμεθα—
For though we walk in the flesh, we do not wage war according to the flesh.note
Porque, aunque vivimos como humanos, no guerreamos como lo hacen los humanos.
4
τὰ γὰρ ὅπλα τῆς στρατείας ἡμῶν οὐ σαρκικὰ ἀλλὰ δυνατὰ τῷ θεῷ πρὸς καθαίρεσιν ὀχυρωμάτων— λογισμοὺς καθαιροῦντες
For the weapons of our warfare are not fleshly, but powerful through God for tearing down strongholds.note
Porque las armas de nuestra guerra no son humanas, sino que Dios las hace poderosas para derrumbar fortalezas.
5
καὶ πᾶν ὕψωμα ἐπαιρόμενον κατὰ τῆς γνώσεως τοῦ θεοῦ, καὶ αἰχμαλωτίζοντες πᾶν νόημα εἰς τὴν ὑπακοὴν τοῦ Χριστοῦ,
We tear down arguments and every proud barrier raised against the knowledge of God, and we take every thought captive to obey Christ.note
Estamos derrumbando razonamientos y toda barrera que se alza contra el conocimiento de Dios. Estamos haciendo prisionero todo pensamiento para que obedezca al Cristo.
6
καὶ ἐν ἑτοίμῳ ἔχοντες ἐκδικῆσαι πᾶσαν παρακοήν, ὅταν πληρωθῇ ὑμῶν ἡ ὑπακοή.
And we are ready to punish every disobedience, once your own obedience is complete.note
Y estamos listos para castigar toda desobediencia tan pronto como la obediencia de ustedes sea completa.
7
Τὰ κατὰ πρόσωπον βλέπετε. εἴ τις πέποιθεν ἑαυτῷ Χριστοῦ εἶναι, τοῦτο λογιζέσθω πάλιν ἐφʼ ἑαυτοῦ ὅτι καθὼς αὐτὸς Χριστοῦ οὕτως καὶ ἡμεῖς
You look at what is before your eyes. If anyone is confident that he belongs to Christ, let him reckon this again: just as he belongs to Christ, so do we.note
Ustedes juzgan las cosas por su apariencia. Si alguien está convencido de que le pertenece a Cristo, que medite una vez más en esto: igual que él le pertenece a Cristo, nosotros también.
8
ἐάν τε γὰρ περισσότερόν τι καυχήσωμαι περὶ τῆς ἐξουσίας ἡμῶν, ἧς ἔδωκεν ὁ κύριος εἰς οἰκοδομὴν καὶ οὐκ εἰς καθαίρεσιν ὑμῶν, οὐκ αἰσχυνθήσομαι,
For even if I boast a bit more about our authority, which the Lord gave for building you up and not for tearing you down, I will not be ashamed.note
Porque, aunque presumiera un poco más de la autoridad que el Señor nos dio para edificarlos, y no para derribarlos, no tendría de qué avergonzarme.
9
ἵνα μὴ δόξω ὡς ἂν ἐκφοβεῖν ὑμᾶς διὰ τῶν ἐπιστολῶν·
I do not want to seem to be trying to frighten you with my letters.note
No quiero dar la impresión de que intento atemorizarlos con mis cartas.
10
ὅτι Αἱ ἐπιστολαὶ μέν , φησίν, βαρεῖαι καὶ ἰσχυραί, ἡ δὲ παρουσία τοῦ σώματος ἀσθενὴς καὶ ὁ λόγος ἐξουθενημένος.
For some say, “His letters are weighty and forceful, but his bodily presence is weak, and his speech is contemptible.”note
Algunos dicen: «Sus cartas son de peso y enérgicas, pero su presencia es débil y su forma de hablar es despreciable».
11
τοῦτο λογιζέσθω ὁ τοιοῦτος, ὅτι οἷοί ἐσμεν τῷ λόγῳ διʼ ἐπιστολῶν ἀπόντες, τοιοῦτοι καὶ παρόντες τῷ ἔργῳ.
Let such a person understand that what we are in word by letters when absent, we will be in deed when present.note
Quien diga eso, que tenga en cuenta que lo que decimos en las cartas cuando no estamos presentes es lo que también haremos cuando estemos presentes.
12
Οὐ γὰρ τολμῶμεν ἐγκρῖναι ἢ συγκρῖναι ἑαυτούς τισιν τῶν ἑαυτοὺς συνιστανόντων· ἀλλὰ αὐτοὶ ἐν ἑαυτοῖς ἑαυτοὺς μετροῦντες καὶ συγκρίνοντες ἑαυτοὺς ἑαυτοῖς οὐ συνιᾶσιν.
We do not dare to class ourselves or compare ourselves with some who commend themselves. But when they measure themselves by themselves and compare themselves with themselves, they show that they lack understanding.note
No nos atrevemos a ponernos al mismo nivel que algunos que se recomiendan a sí mismos ni a compararnos con ellos. Pero, cuando ellos se miden según su propio criterio y se comparan con ellos mismos, demuestran que no tienen entendimiento.
13
ἡμεῖς δὲ οὐκ εἰς τὰ ἄμετρα καυχησόμεθα, ἀλλὰ κατὰ τὸ μέτρον τοῦ κανόνος οὗ ἐμέρισεν ἡμῖν ὁ θεὸς μέτρου, ἐφικέσθαι ἄχρι καὶ ὑμῶν—
We will not boast beyond our measure, but according to the measure of the sphere God assigned to us, a sphere that reaches even to you.note
Sin embargo, nosotros no presumiremos de cosas que están fuera de los límites de nuestra asignación, sino de cosas dentro del límite del territorio que Dios fijó para nosotros y que llega incluso a ustedes.
14
οὐ γὰρ ὡς μὴ ἐφικνούμενοι εἰς ὑμᾶς ὑπερεκτείνομεν ἑαυτούς, ἄχρι γὰρ καὶ ὑμῶν ἐφθάσαμεν ἐν τῷ εὐαγγελίῳ τοῦ Χριστοῦ—
We are not overextending ourselves, as if we had not reached you; for we were the first to reach you with the good news of Christ.note
No estamos yendo más allá de los límites de nuestro territorio, como si este no los incluyera a ustedes. De hecho, fuimos los primeros en llegar a ustedes con las buenas noticias acerca del Cristo.
15
οὐκ εἰς τὰ ἄμετρα καυχώμενοι ἐν ἀλλοτρίοις κόποις, ἐλπίδα δὲ ἔχοντες αὐξανομένης τῆς πίστεως ὑμῶν ἐν ὑμῖν μεγαλυνθῆναι κατὰ τὸν κανόνα ἡμῶν εἰς περισσείαν,
We do not boast beyond our measure, in other people’s labors. But we hope that, as your faith grows, we will be greatly enlarged among you within our sphere,note
No estamos presumiendo de cosas que están fuera de los límites de nuestra asignación, es decir, del trabajo de otros. Más bien, tenemos la esperanza de que, según vaya creciendo su fe, lo que hemos hecho también crezca dentro de nuestro territorio.
16
εἰς τὰ ὑπερέκεινα ὑμῶν εὐαγγελίσασθαι, οὐκ ἐν ἀλλοτρίῳ κανόνι εἰς τὰ ἕτοιμα καυχήσασθαι.
so that we may preach the good news beyond you, and not boast about what has already been done in another’s sphere.note
Podremos anunciar las buenas noticias en las regiones que están más allá de la suya, para no presumir de lo que ya se ha hecho en el territorio de otro.
17
Ὁ δὲ καυχώμενος ἐν κυρίῳ καυχάσθω·
“Let the one who boasts boast in Jehovah.”note
«Pero el que presume, que presuma de Jehová».
18
οὐ γὰρ ὁ ἑαυτὸν συνιστάνων ἐκεῖνός ἐστιν δόκιμος, ἀλλὰ ὃν ὁ κύριος συνίστησιν.
For it is not the one who commends himself who is approved, but the one whom Jehovah commends.note
Porque el que es aprobado no es aquel que se recomienda a sí mismo, sino aquel al que Jehová recomienda.
Verses 1–6 · the meekness of Christ, the weapons of warfare, and every thought taken captive
“The meekness and gentleness of Christ.” dia tēs prautētos kai epieikeias tou Christou, through the meekness and gentleness of Christ. Paul appeals in two words that describe a manner, not a rank, and he sets his lowliness in person against his boldness in writing. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “by the mildness and kindness of the Christ.” The ASV “by the meekness and gentleness of Christ.” The KJV “by the meekness and gentleness of Christ.”

“The weapons of our warfare.” ta hopla tēs strateias hēmōn, the weapons of our warfare, and dynata tō theō, powerful by God, for the tearing down of strongholds. The war is spoken of as a campaign whose arms are not fleshly. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “the weapons of our warfare are not fleshly, but powerful by God.” The ASV “the weapons of our warfare are not of the flesh, but mighty before God.” The KJV “the weapons of our warfare are not carnal, but mighty through God.”

“Every thought into captivity.” aichmalōtizontes pan noēma eis tēn hypakoēn tou Christou, taking every thought captive to the obedience of Christ. The verb is the one for taking prisoners of war. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “bringing every thought into captivity to make it obedient to the Christ.” The ASV “bringing every thought into captivity to the obedience of Christ.”

Verses 7–11 · the appearance and what belongs to Christ, the letters and the presence
“Just as he belongs to Christ, so do we.” kathōs autos Christou houtō kai hēmeis, just as he is Christ’s, so also are we. The sentence turns the claim back on the rival who boasts of belonging to Christ: he is not the only one. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “Just as he belongs to Christ, so do we also.” The ASV “even as he is Christ’s, so also are we.” The KJV “as he is Christ’s, even so are we Christ’s.”

“His letters are weighty.” hai epistolai men, phēsin, barieai kai ischyrai, the letters, they say, are weighty and strong. The verse quotes what the critics say, and the quotation sets the letters against the presence. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “His letters are weighty and forceful, but his presence in person is weak and his speech contemptible.” The ASV “His letters, they say, are weighty and strong; but his bodily presence is weak, and his speech of no account.” The KJV “his bodily presence is weak, and his speech contemptible.”

Verses 12–16 · the measure that reaches to you, and the territory that God measured out
“They measure themselves by themselves.” autoi en heautois heautous metrountes, they measure themselves among themselves. The word metron, measure, returns through this paragraph, and the sentence says the rivals have no measure but themselves. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “when they measure themselves by themselves and compare themselves with themselves, they are without understanding.” The ASV “measuring themselves by themselves, and comparing themselves with themselves, are without understanding.” The KJV “but they measuring themselves by themselves, and comparing themselves among themselves, are not wise.”

“Within the boundary that God measured out.” kata to metron tou kanonos hou emeristen hēmin ho theos metrou, according to the measure of the rule God apportioned to us as a measure. The word kanōn is a rule or a line, the measured area of the work. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “within the boundary of the territory that God measured out to us.” The ASV “according to the measure of the province which God apportioned to us as a measure.” The KJV “according to the measure of the rule which God hath distributed to us.”

“Other men’s labors.” en allotriois kopois, in another’s labors. The sentence says Paul has not claimed what others have done, and he hopes to preach beyond them. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “we are not boasting outside our assigned boundaries about the labors of someone else.” The KJV “Not boasting of things without our measure, that is, of other men’s labours.”

Verses 17–18 · let the one who boasts boast in Jehovah, and the one whom he commends
“Let the one who boasts boast in Jehovah.” ho de kauchōmenos en kyriō kauchasthō, let him who boasts boast in the Lord. The sentence echoes Jeremiah 9:23–24, where boasting is set in the knowledge of the Lord. The NWT renders the Lord as “Jehovah” in a quotation. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “But the one who boasts, let him boast in Jehovah.” The ASV “But he that glorieth, let him glory in the Lord.”

“The one whom the Lord commends.” ou gar ho heauton synistanōn ekeinos estin dokimos, alla hon ho kyrios synistēsin, for it is not the one who commends himself who is approved, but the one whom the Lord commends. The verb synistēmi is the verb of recommendation Paul used of himself at 3:1. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “the one whom Jehovah recommends.” The KJV “but whom the Lord commendeth.”

Patterns worth carrying forward

Where this translation keeps the exact words: “weapons” for hopla at 10:4; “measure” for metron at 10:13 and 10:15, and “sphere” for kanōn in the same run of verses; “commends” for synistēmi at 10:12 and 10:18.

Echoes paid. The weapons of 6:7 return at 10:4; the commending of 3:1 returns in the verse about the one whom the Lord commends (10:18); the boasting of 9:2 returns as the boasting that must stay within the measure (10:13–16).

Echoes planted. The letters of 10:10 are the letters of the earlier chapters; the chapter’s appeal to meekness and the rival teachers are carried into the last three chapters.
