1
וַיְהִי דְבַר-יְהוָה, אֵלַי לֵאמֹר.
And the word of Jehovah came to me, saying:note
Y vino a mí la palabra de Jehová, diciendo:
2
וְאַתָּה בֶן-אָדָם, שָׂא עַל-צֹר קִינָה.
“And you, son of man, take up a dirge for Tyre,note
«Y tú, hijo de hombre, levanta una endecha por Tiro,
3
וְאָמַרְתָּ לְצוֹר, הישבתי הַיֹּשֶׁבֶת עַל-מְבוֹאֹת יָם, רֹכֶלֶת הָעַמִּים, אֶל-אִיִּים רַבִּים;  {ס}  כֹּה אָמַר, אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, צוֹר, אַתְּ אָמַרְתְּ אֲנִי כְּלִילַת יֹפִי.
and say to Tyre, you who sit at the entrances of the sea, trader of the peoples to many islands: Thus says the Lord Jehovah: O Tyre, you have said, ‘I am the perfection of beauty.’note
y di a Tiro, tú que te sientas a las entradas del mar, comerciante de los pueblos para muchas islas: Así dice el Señor Jehová: Oh Tiro, tú has dicho: “Yo soy de perfecta hermosura”.
4
בְּלֵב יַמִּים, גְּבוּלָיִךְ; בֹּנַיִךְ, כָּלְלוּ יָפְיֵךְ.
In the heart of the seas are your borders; your builders have perfected your beauty.note
En el corazón de los mares están tus límites; tus edificadores perfeccionaron tu hermosura.
5
בְּרוֹשִׁים מִשְּׂנִיר בָּנוּ לָךְ, אֵת כָּל-לֻחֹתָיִם; אֶרֶז מִלְּבָנוֹן לָקָחוּ, לַעֲשׂוֹת תֹּרֶן עָלָיִךְ.
Of cypresses from Senir they built all your planks for you; they took a cedar from Lebanon to make a mast upon you.note
De cipreses de Senir te construyeron todos tus tablones; tomaron un cedro del Líbano para hacer sobre ti un mástil.
6
אַלּוֹנִים, מִבָּשָׁן, עָשׂוּ, מִשּׁוֹטָיִךְ; קַרְשֵׁךְ עָשׂוּ-שֵׁן בַּת-אֲשֻׁרִים, מֵאִיֵּי כִּתִּיִּם.
Of oaks from Bashan they made your oars; your boards they made of ivory with boxwood, from the islands of Kittim.note
De encinas de Basán hicieron tus remos; tus tablas las hicieron de marfil con boj, de las islas de Quitim.
7
שֵׁשׁ-בְּרִקְמָה מִמִּצְרַיִם הָיָה מִפְרָשֵׂךְ, לִהְיוֹת לָךְ לְנֵס; תְּכֵלֶת וְאַרְגָּמָן מֵאִיֵּי אֱלִישָׁה, הָיָה מְכַסֵּךְ.
Fine linen with embroidered work from Egypt was your sail, to be your banner; blue and purple from the islands of Elishah was your covering.note
Lino fino con bordado de Egipto fue tu vela, para servirte de estandarte; azul y púrpura de las islas de Elisa fue tu cubierta.
8
יֹשְׁבֵי צִידוֹן וְאַרְוַד, הָיוּ שָׁטִים לָךְ; חֲכָמַיִךְ צוֹר הָיוּ בָךְ, הֵמָּה חֹבְלָיִךְ.
The inhabitants of Sidon and Arvad were your rowers; your wise men, O Tyre, were in you; they were your pilots.note
Los habitantes de Sidón y de Arvad fueron tus remeros; tus sabios, oh Tiro, estaban en ti; ellos eran tus pilotos.
9
זִקְנֵי גְבַל וַחֲכָמֶיהָ הָיוּ בָךְ, מַחֲזִיקֵי בִּדְקֵךְ; כָּל-אֳנִיּוֹת הַיָּם וּמַלָּחֵיהֶם הָיוּ בָךְ, לַעֲרֹב מַעֲרָבֵךְ.
The elders of Gebal and her wise men were in you, repairing your damage; all the ships of the sea and their sailors were in you to trade your barter.note
Los ancianos de Gebal y sus sabios estaban en ti, reparando tus averías; todas las naves del mar y sus marineros estaban en ti para negociar tu trueque.
10
פָּרַס וְלוּד וּפוּט הָיוּ בְחֵילֵךְ, אַנְשֵׁי מִלְחַמְתֵּךְ; מָגֵן וְכוֹבַע תִּלּוּ-בָךְ, הֵמָּה נָתְנוּ הֲדָרֵךְ.
Persia and Lud and Put were in your army, your men of war; small shield and helmet they hung up in you; they gave you your splendor.note
Persia y Lud y Fut estaban en tu ejército, tus hombres de guerra; escudo pequeño y yelmo colgaron en ti; ellos te dieron tu esplendor.
11
בְּנֵי אַרְוַד וְחֵילֵךְ, עַל-חוֹמוֹתַיִךְ סָבִיב, וְגַמָּדִים, בְּמִגְדְּלוֹתַיִךְ הָיוּ; שִׁלְטֵיהֶם תִּלּוּ עַל-חוֹמוֹתַיִךְ, סָבִיב--הֵמָּה, כָּלְלוּ יָפְיֵךְ.
The sons of Arvad and your army were on your walls all around, and Gammadim were in your towers; their shields they hung on your walls all around; they perfected your beauty.note
Los hijos de Arvad y tu ejército estaban sobre tus muros en derredor, y los gammadim estaban en tus torres; sus escudos colgaron sobre tus muros en derredor; ellos perfeccionaron tu hermosura.
12
תַּרְשִׁישׁ סֹחַרְתֵּךְ, מֵרֹב כָּל-הוֹן; בְּכֶסֶף בַּרְזֶל בְּדִיל וְעוֹפֶרֶת, נָתְנוּ עִזְבוֹנָיִךְ.
Tarshish was your merchant because of the abundance of all riches; with silver, iron, tin and lead they gave for your wares.note
Tarsis fue tu mercader por la abundancia de toda clase de riquezas; con plata, hierro, estaño y plomo dieron por tus artículos.

13
יָוָן תֻּבַל וָמֶשֶׁךְ, הֵמָּה רֹכְלָיִךְ; בְּנֶפֶשׁ אָדָם וּכְלֵי נְחֹשֶׁת, נָתְנוּ מַעֲרָבֵךְ.
Javan, Tubal and Meshech were your traders; with souls of men and vessels of bronze they gave your barter.note
Javán, Tubal y Mesec fueron tus comerciantes; con almas de hombres y vasijas de bronce dieron tu trueque.

14
מִבֵּית, תּוֹגַרְמָה, סוּסִים וּפָרָשִׁים וּפְרָדִים, נָתְנוּ עִזְבוֹנָיִךְ.
From the house of Togarmah they gave horses and horsemen and mules for your wares.note
De la casa de Togarma dieron caballos y jinetes y mulos por tus artículos.
15
בְּנֵי דְדָן רֹכְלַיִךְ, אִיִּים רַבִּים סְחֹרַת יָדֵךְ; קַרְנוֹת שֵׁן והובנים וְהָבְנִים, הֵשִׁיבוּ אֶשְׁכָּרֵךְ.
The sons of Dedan were your traders; many islands were the market of your hand; horns of ivory and ebony they brought back to you as payment.note
Los hijos de Dedán fueron tus comerciantes; muchas islas fueron el mercado de tu mano; cuernos de marfil y ébano te trajeron en pago.
16
אֲרָם סֹחַרְתֵּךְ, מֵרֹב מַעֲשָׂיִךְ; בְּנֹפֶךְ אַרְגָּמָן וְרִקְמָה וּבוּץ, וְרָאמֹת וְכַדְכֹּד, נָתְנוּ, בְּעִזְבוֹנָיִךְ.
Aram was your merchant because of the abundance of your works; with turquoise, purple and embroidered work and byssus and coral and rubies they gave for your wares.note
Aram fue tu mercader por la abundancia de tus obras; con turquesa, púrpura y bordado y biso y coral y rubíes dieron por tus artículos.
17
יְהוּדָה וְאֶרֶץ יִשְׂרָאֵל, הֵמָּה רֹכְלָיִךְ; בְּחִטֵּי מִנִּית וּפַנַּג וּדְבַשׁ וָשֶׁמֶן, וָצֹרִי, נָתְנוּ, מַעֲרָבֵךְ.
Judah and the land of Israel were your traders; with wheat of Minnith and pannag and honey and oil and balm they gave your barter.note
Judá y la tierra de Israel fueron tus comerciantes; con trigo de Minit y panag y miel y aceite y bálsamo dieron tu trueque.
18
דַּמֶּשֶׂק סֹחַרְתֵּךְ בְּרֹב מַעֲשַׂיִךְ, מֵרֹב כָּל-הוֹן; בְּיֵין חֶלְבּוֹן, וְצֶמֶר צָחַר.
Damascus was your merchant in the abundance of your works, because of the abundance of all riches, with wine of Helbon and white wool.note
Damasco fue tu mercader en la abundancia de tus obras, por la abundancia de toda clase de riquezas, con vino de Helbón y lana blanca.
19
וְדָן וְיָוָן מְאוּזָּל, בְּעִזְבוֹנַיִךְ נָתָנּוּ; בַּרְזֶל עָשׁוֹת קִדָּה וְקָנֶה, בְּמַעֲרָבֵךְ הָיָה.
Vedan and Javan from Uzal gave for your wares; wrought iron, cassia and cane were among your barter.note
Vedán y Javán de Uzal dieron por tus artículos; hierro labrado, casia y caña estaban en tu trueque.
20
דְּדָן, רֹכַלְתֵּךְ, בְּבִגְדֵי-חֹפֶשׁ, לְרִכְבָּה.
Dedan was your trader in saddlecloths for riding.note
Dedán fue tu comerciante en paños para montar.
21
עֲרַב וְכָל-נְשִׂיאֵי קֵדָר, הֵמָּה סֹחֲרֵי יָדֵךְ; בְּכָרִים וְאֵילִם וְעַתּוּדִים, בָּם סֹחֲרָיִךְ.
Arabia and all the princes of Kedar were the merchants of your hand; in lambs and rams and he-goats, in these they were your merchants.note
Arabia y todos los príncipes de Cedar fueron los mercaderes de tu mano; en corderos y carneros y machos cabríos, en esto fueron tus mercaderes.
22
רֹכְלֵי שְׁבָא וְרַעְמָה, הֵמָּה רֹכְלָיִךְ; בְּרֹאשׁ כָּל-בֹּשֶׂם וּבְכָל-אֶבֶן יְקָרָה, וְזָהָב, נָתְנוּ, עִזְבוֹנָיִךְ.
The traders of Sheba and Raamah were your traders; with the chief of every spice and with every precious stone and gold they gave for your wares.note
Los comerciantes de Sabá y de Raama fueron tus comerciantes; con lo principal de toda especia y con toda piedra preciosa y oro dieron por tus artículos.
23
חָרָן וְכַנֵּה וָעֶדֶן, רֹכְלֵי שְׁבָא; אַשּׁוּר, כִּלְמַד רֹכַלְתֵּךְ.
Haran and Canneh and Eden, the traders of Sheba, Asshur and Chilmad were your trader.note
Harán y Cane y Edén, los comerciantes de Sabá, Asur y Quilmad fueron tu comerciante.
24
הֵמָּה רֹכְלַיִךְ, בְּמַכְלֻלִים, בִּגְלוֹמֵי תְּכֵלֶת וְרִקְמָה, וּבְגִנְזֵי בְּרֹמִים; בַּחֲבָלִים חֲבֻשִׁים וַאֲרֻזִים, בְּמַרְכֻלְתֵּךְ.
They were your traders in splendid attire, in wraps of blue and embroidered work, and in chests of many-colored cloth, bound with cords and made of cedar, among your merchandise.note
Ellos eran tus comerciantes en galas espléndidas, en mantos de azul y bordado, y en cofres de telas de colores, atados con cuerdas y hechos de cedro, en tu mercancía.
25
אֳנִיּוֹת תַּרְשִׁישׁ, שָׁרוֹתַיִךְ מַעֲרָבֵךְ; וַתִּמָּלְאִי וַתִּכְבְּדִי מְאֹד, בְּלֵב יַמִּים.
The ships of Tarshish were your caravans for your barter; and you were filled and grew very heavy in the heart of the seas.note
Las naves de Tarsis eran tus caravanas para tu trueque; y te llenaste y te hiciste muy pesada en el corazón de los mares.
26
בְּמַיִם רַבִּים הֱבִאוּךְ, הַשָּׁטִים אֹתָךְ; רוּחַ, הַקָּדִים, שְׁבָרֵךְ, בְּלֵב יַמִּים.
Into many waters your rowers have brought you; the east wind has broken you in the heart of the seas.note
A muchas aguas te han llevado tus remeros; el viento del este te ha quebrado en el corazón de los mares.
27
הוֹנֵךְ, וְעִזְבוֹנַיִךְ, מַעֲרָבֵךְ, מַלָּחַיִךְ וְחֹבְלָיִךְ; מַחֲזִיקֵי בִדְקֵךְ וְעֹרְבֵי מַעֲרָבֵךְ וְכָל-אַנְשֵׁי מִלְחַמְתֵּךְ אֲשֶׁר-בָּךְ, וּבְכָל-קְהָלֵךְ אֲשֶׁר בְּתוֹכֵךְ, יִפְּלוּ בְּלֵב יַמִּים, בְּיוֹם מַפַּלְתֵּךְ.
Your riches and your wares, your barter, your sailors and your pilots, those who repair your damage and those who trade your barter, and all your men of war who are in you, with all your assembly that is in your midst, will fall into the heart of the seas on the day of your fall.note
Tus riquezas y tus artículos, tu trueque, tus marineros y tus pilotos, los que reparan tus averías y los que negocian tu trueque, y todos tus hombres de guerra que hay en ti, con toda tu asamblea que está en medio de ti, caerán en el corazón de los mares en el día de tu caída.
28
לְקוֹל, זַעֲקַת חֹבְלָיִךְ, יִרְעֲשׁוּ, מִגְרֹשׁוֹת.
At the sound of the cry of your pilots the pasturelands will quake.note
Al sonido del clamor de tus pilotos temblarán los pastizales.
29
וְיָרְדוּ מֵאֳנִיּוֹתֵיהֶם, כֹּל תֹּפְשֵׂי מָשׁוֹט--מַלָּחִים, כֹּל חֹבְלֵי הַיָּם:  אֶל-הָאָרֶץ, יַעֲמֹדוּ.
And all who handle the oar will come down from their ships; the sailors, all the pilots of the sea, will stand on the land,note
Y descenderán de sus naves todos los que manejan el remo; los marineros, todos los pilotos del mar, se quedarán en tierra,
30
וְהִשְׁמִיעוּ עָלַיִךְ בְּקוֹלָם, וְיִזְעֲקוּ מָרָה; וְיַעֲלוּ עָפָר עַל-רָאשֵׁיהֶם, בָּאֵפֶר יִתְפַּלָּשׁוּ.
and they will make their voice heard over you, and cry out bitterly; and they will bring up dust on their heads; in ashes they will roll.note
y harán oír su voz por ti, y clamarán amargamente; y echarán polvo sobre sus cabezas; en cenizas se revolcarán.

31
וְהִקְרִיחוּ אֵלַיִךְ קָרְחָה, וְחָגְרוּ שַׂקִּים; וּבָכוּ אֵלַיִךְ בְּמַר-נֶפֶשׁ, מִסְפֵּד מָר.
And they will make themselves bald for you, and gird on sackcloth, and weep for you in bitterness of soul, with bitter lamentation.note
Y se harán calvos por ti, y se ceñirán de saco, y llorarán por ti con amargura de alma, con amargo lamento.
32
וְנָשְׂאוּ אֵלַיִךְ בְּנִיהֶם קִינָה, וְקוֹנְנוּ עָלָיִךְ:  מִי כְצוֹר, כְּדֻמָה בְּתוֹךְ הַיָּם.
And in their wailing they will take up a dirge for you, and chant over you: ‘Who is like Tyre, like one silenced in the midst of the sea?note
Y en su gemido levantarán por ti una endecha, y entonarán sobre ti: “¿Quién como Tiro, como la silenciada en medio del mar?

33
בְּצֵאת עִזְבוֹנַיִךְ מִיַּמִּים, הִשְׂבַּעַתְּ עַמִּים רַבִּים; בְּרֹב הוֹנַיִךְ וּמַעֲרָבַיִךְ, הֶעֱשַׁרְתְּ מַלְכֵי-אָרֶץ.
When your wares went out from the seas, you satisfied many peoples; with the abundance of your riches and your barter you enriched the kings of the earth.note
Cuando tus artículos salían de los mares, saciabas a muchos pueblos; con la abundancia de tus riquezas y de tu trueque enriquecías a los reyes de la tierra.
34
עֵת נִשְׁבֶּרֶת מִיַּמִּים, בְּמַעֲמַקֵּי-מָיִם; מַעֲרָבֵךְ וְכָל-קְהָלֵךְ, בְּתוֹכֵךְ נָפָלוּ.
Now you are broken by the seas, in the depths of the waters; your barter and all your assembly have fallen in your midst.note
Ahora estás quebrada por los mares, en las profundidades de las aguas; tu trueque y toda tu asamblea han caído en medio de ti.

Translator’s Notes

Verses 1–3 · Ezekiel 27:1–3 a dirge for Tyre

⭐ A dirge. Qinah, as at 19:1 and 26:17. The chapter pictures Tyre as a ship: built, manned, loaded, wrecked and mourned. It gives no date.

⭐ The perfection of beauty. Tyre says it of herself. The words kelilat yofi stand in two verses of the Hebrew Bible: here and Lamentations 2:15, where passers-by ask it about Jerusalem. The root of ‘perfection’ returns in verses 4 and 11.

⚠ One form written, another read. In verse 3 ‘you who sit’ is written one way and read another (ketiv/qere); the same happens in verse 15 with ‘ebony’.

Verses 4–11 · Ezekiel 27:4–11 the ship and her crew

⭐ Timber. Cypress from Senir, which Deuteronomy 3:9 gives as the Amorite name for Hermon; cedar from Lebanon, as for Solomon (1 Kings 5:20); oaks from Bashan.

⚠ Verse 6. The letters bat ashurim, ‘daughter of Ashurites’, are usually read as ‘with boxwood’, divided differently, and the translation follows that. The KJV has “the company of the Ashurites have made thy benches of ivory”, the ASV “inlaid in boxwood”. Kittim is the NIV’s “Cyprus”.

⭐ Sail and colours. Fine linen and embroidered work, then blue and purple, the stuffs of the tabernacle (Exodus 26:31) and of the clothes given to Jerusalem at 16:10. A banner is what is lifted to be seen.

⭐ The crew. Sidon and Arvad row; Tyre’s own wise men pilot; the elders of Gebal, whose men cut stone for Solomon (1 Kings 5:32), see to the repairs. The ‘sailors’ are the word of Jonah 1:5. The KJV has “calkers” for those who repair the damage.

⚠ The army. Soldiers from far off hang their shields on the city: here the picture is of walls and towers, not a ship. The ‘small shield’ of verse 10 is the one named at 23:24; the shields of verse 11 are another word, as at 2 Samuel 8:7.

⚠ Verse 11. ‘Your army’ is read by the NIV as a place, “Helek”. Gammadim is left as it stands; the ASV has “valorous men”, the Douay “the Pygmeans”.

Verses 12–25 · Ezekiel 27:12–25 the traders of Tyre

⭐ A list of the world. Many of the names are those of the table of nations: Javan, Tubal, Meshech, Togarmah, Elishah, Tarshish (Genesis 10:2–4), Sheba, Raamah and Dedan (10:7). The road runs from Tarshish in the far west to Arabia, Damascus and Asshur.

⭐ The words of trade. Two names for the dealers, ‘merchant’ and ‘trader’, take turns. ‘Wares’ and ‘barter’ are two words for what changes hands; ‘merchandise’ (verse 24) is the word of 26:12, and ‘riches’ is not the ‘wealth’ of that verse. Each keeps one English word here.

⭐ Metals. Silver, iron, tin and lead, the metals of the furnace at 22:18. Here they are cargo.

⭐ Souls of men. Verse 13 puts people in the list, beside bronze vessels. Revelation 18:13 ends its list of Babylon’s cargo with ‘human souls’.

⚠ Names that differ. In verse 15 the Hebrew has Dedan, which returns in verse 20; the NIV reads “Rhodes”. In verse 16 it has Aram; the NWT 1984 has “Edom”.

⚠ Stuffs and stones. Buts, ‘byssus’, is another word for fine cloth than the ‘fine linen’ of verse 7. The ‘rubies’ are kadkod, which stands in two verses of the Hebrew Bible, this one and Isaiah 54:12.

⭐ Judah and Israel. They send food and tsori, balm, which the Ishmaelites carried from Gilead (Genesis 37:25). Wheat went from Solomon to Hiram (1 Kings 5:25). Pannag stands in this one verse, and its meaning is not known for certain; the NIV has “confections”.

⚠ Hard places. Verses 19, 23 and 24 are difficult. ‘From Uzal’ (verse 19) takes me’uzal as the name met at Genesis 10:27; the KJV has “going to and fro”, the ASV “with yarn”. The ‘lambs’ of verse 21, karim, are spelled like the ‘battering rams’ of 4:2.

⭐ Ships of Tarshish. The great ships of 1 Kings 10:22. ‘Grew very heavy’ can also mean ‘grew very glorious’, as the KJV has it; a loaded ship is about to meet a storm.

Verses 26–28 · Ezekiel 27:26–28 the east wind

⭐ The east wind. It dried up the fruit of the vine at 19:12 and was to wither the planting of 17:10. Here it breaks the ship, in the many waters.

⭐ The heart of the seas. Tyre’s borders were there (verse 4); she grew heavy there (verse 25); there she is broken (verse 26) and there everything falls (verse 27).

⭐ Everything goes down. Verse 27 gathers up the words of the whole chapter: riches, wares, barter, sailors, pilots, menders, those who trade, soldiers.

⚠ Pasturelands. Migreshot, the open land round a town (Numbers 35:2). The KJV has “suburbs”, the NIV “shorelands”.

Verses 29–36 · Ezekiel 27:29–36 the sailors mourn

⭐ Mourning. Ashes, dust on the head, baldness and sackcloth, as at 7:18. ‘Lamentation’ has the root of ‘lament’ (24:16). Revelation 18:19 has the sailors over Babylon throw dust on their heads.

⭐ Who is like Tyre? Revelation 18:18 has the same kind of cry over the great city.

⚠ Silenced. The word in verse 32 stands for ‘one made silent’, as the ASV takes it; the KJV has “destroyed”. The dirge that begins there runs, in this translation, to the end of the chapter.

⭐ Hiss. As over Jerusalem (Lamentations 2:15; Jeremiah 19:8). ‘Appalled’ and ‘terrors’ are the words of 26:16 and 26:21. A like close ends 28:19.

Patterns worth carrying forward

Where this translation keeps the exact / distinct words: “merchant” and “trader” are kept apart; “wares” and “barter” likewise; “heart of the seas” at 4, 25, 26 and 27; “beauty” at 3, 4 and 11; and “dirge” at 2 and 32.

Echoes paid. The dirge and the islands of chapter 26; the table of nations (Genesis 10); the metals of 22:18; the beauty of Lamentations 2:15.

Echoes planted. ‘In the heart of the seas’ again at 28:2 and 28:8.
