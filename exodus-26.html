Exodus 26 — The Mister Translation

1
וְאֶת-הַמִּשְׁכָּן תַּעֲשֶׂה, עֶשֶׂר יְרִיעֹת: שֵׁשׁ מָשְׁזָר, וּתְכֵלֶת וְאַרְגָּמָן וְתֹלַעַת שָׁנִי--כְּרֻבִים מַעֲשֵׂה חֹשֵׁב, תַּעֲשֶׂה אֹתָם.
And you shall make the tabernacle with ten curtains: fine twined linen, and blue, and purple, and scarlet yarn — with cherubim worked in by a skilled designer you shall make them.note
Y harás el tabernáculo con diez cortinas: lino fino torcido, y azul, y púrpura, y grana —con querubines tejidos por un diseñador hábil las harás.
2
אֹרֶךְ הַיְרִיעָה הָאַחַת, שְׁמֹנֶה וְעֶשְׂרִים בָּאַמָּה, וְרֹחַב אַרְבַּע בָּאַמָּה, הַיְרִיעָה הָאֶחָת; מִדָּה אַחַת, לְכָל-הַיְרִיעֹת.
The length of each curtain: twenty-eight cubits, and the width of each curtain: four cubits; one measure for all the curtains.note
El largo de cada cortina: veintiocho codos, y el ancho de cada cortina: cuatro codos; una misma medida para todas las cortinas.
3
חֲמֵשׁ הַיְרִיעֹת, תִּהְיֶיןָ חֹבְרֹת, אִשָּׁה, אֶל-אֲחֹתָהּ; וְחָמֵשׁ יְרִיעֹת חֹבְרֹת, אִשָּׁה אֶל-אֲחֹתָהּ.
Five curtains shall be joined to one another, and the other five curtains shall be joined to one another.note
Cinco cortinas estarán unidas la una a la otra, y las otras cinco cortinas estarán unidas la una a la otra.
4
וְעָשִׂיתָ לֻלְאֹת תְּכֵלֶת, עַל שְׂפַת הַיְרִיעָה הָאֶחָת, מִקָּצָה, בַּחֹבָרֶת; וְכֵן תַּעֲשֶׂה, בִּשְׂפַת הַיְרִיעָה, הַקִּיצוֹנָה, בַּמַּחְבֶּרֶת הַשֵּׁנִית.
And you shall make loops of blue on the edge of the outermost curtain in the one set; and so you shall make on the edge of the outermost curtain in the second set.note
Y harás presillas de azul en el borde de la cortina más externa del primer juego; y así harás en el borde de la cortina más externa del segundo juego.
5
חֲמִשִּׁים לֻלָאֹת, תַּעֲשֶׂה בַּיְרִיעָה הָאֶחָת, וַחֲמִשִּׁים לֻלָאֹת תַּעֲשֶׂה בִּקְצֵה הַיְרִיעָה, אֲשֶׁר בַּמַּחְבֶּרֶת הַשֵּׁנִית: מַקְבִּילֹת, הַלֻּלָאֹת, אִשָּׁה, אֶל-אֲחֹתָהּ.
Fifty loops you shall make on the one curtain, and fifty loops you shall make on the edge of the curtain that is in the second set; the loops opposite one another.note
Cincuenta presillas harás en la una cortina, y cincuenta presillas harás en el borde de la cortina que está en el segundo juego; las presillas una frente a la otra.
6
וְעָשִׂיתָ, חֲמִשִּׁים קַרְסֵי זָהָב; וְחִבַּרְתָּ אֶת-הַיְרִיעֹת אִשָּׁה אֶל-אֲחֹתָהּ, בַּקְּרָסִים, וְהָיָה הַמִּשְׁכָּן, אֶחָד.
And you shall make fifty clasps of gold, and join the curtains to one another with the clasps, so that the tabernacle becomes one whole.note
Y harás cincuenta broches de oro, y unirás las cortinas la una a la otra con los broches, de modo que el tabernáculo forme un solo conjunto.
7
וְעָשִׂיתָ יְרִיעֹת עִזִּים, לְאֹהֶל עַל-הַמִּשְׁכָּן; עַשְׁתֵּי-עֶשְׂרֵה יְרִיעֹת, תַּעֲשֶׂה אֹתָם.
And you shall make curtains of goat hair for a tent over the tabernacle; eleven curtains you shall make them.note
Y harás cortinas de pelo de cabra para una tienda sobre el tabernáculo; once cortinas las harás.
8
אֹרֶךְ הַיְרִיעָה הָאַחַת, שְׁלֹשִׁים בָּאַמָּה, וְרֹחַב אַרְבַּע בָּאַמָּה, הַיְרִיעָה הָאֶחָת; מִדָּה אַחַת, לְעַשְׁתֵּי עֶשְׂרֵה יְרִיעֹת.
The length of each curtain: thirty cubits, and the width of each curtain: four cubits; one measure for the eleven curtains.note
El largo de cada cortina: treinta codos, y el ancho de cada cortina: cuatro codos; una misma medida para las once cortinas.
9
וְחִבַּרְתָּ אֶת-חֲמֵשׁ הַיְרִיעֹת, לְבָד, וְאֶת-שֵׁשׁ הַיְרִיעֹת, לְבָד; וְכָפַלְתָּ אֶת-הַיְרִיעָה הַשִּׁשִּׁית, אֶל-מוּל פְּנֵי הָאֹהֶל.
And you shall join five of the curtains by themselves, and six of the curtains by themselves, and fold over the sixth curtain at the front of the tent.note
Y unirás cinco de las cortinas por sí solas, y seis de las cortinas por sí solas, y doblarás la sexta cortina al frente de la tienda.
10
וְעָשִׂיתָ חֲמִשִּׁים לֻלָאֹת, עַל שְׂפַת הַיְרִיעָה הָאֶחָת, הַקִּיצֹנָה, בַּחֹבָרֶת; וַחֲמִשִּׁים לֻלָאֹת, עַל שְׂפַת הַיְרִיעָה, הַחֹבֶרֶת, הַשֵּׁנִית.
And you shall make fifty loops on the edge of the outermost curtain in the one set, and fifty loops on the edge of the curtain in the second set.note
Y harás cincuenta presillas en el borde de la cortina más externa del primer juego, y cincuenta presillas en el borde de la cortina del segundo juego.
11
וְעָשִׂיתָ קַרְסֵי נְחֹשֶׁת, חֲמִשִּׁים; וְהֵבֵאתָ אֶת-הַקְּרָסִים בַּלֻּלָאֹת, וְחִבַּרְתָּ אֶת-הָאֹהֶל וְהָיָה אֶחָד.
And you shall make fifty clasps of bronze, and put the clasps into the loops, and join the tent together so that it becomes one whole.note
Y harás cincuenta broches de bronce, y meterás los broches en las presillas, y unirás la tienda de modo que forme un solo conjunto.
12
וְסֶרַח, הָעֹדֵף, בִּירִיעֹת, הָאֹהֶל--חֲצִי הַיְרִיעָה, הָעֹדֶפֶת, תִּסְרַח, עַל אֲחֹרֵי הַמִּשְׁכָּן.
And the part that hangs over, the excess in the curtains of the tent — the half-curtain that remains over shall hang over the back of the tabernacle.note
Y la parte que sobresale, lo que sobra en las cortinas de la tienda —la media cortina que sobra colgará sobre la parte trasera del tabernáculo.
13
וְהָאַמָּה מִזֶּה וְהָאַמָּה מִזֶּה, בָּעֹדֵף, בְּאֹרֶךְ, יְרִיעֹת הָאֹהֶל: יִהְיֶה סָרוּחַ עַל-צִדֵּי הַמִּשְׁכָּן, מִזֶּה וּמִזֶּה--לְכַסֹּתוֹ.
And the cubit on the one side, and the cubit on the other side, of what remains over in the length of the curtains of the tent, shall hang over the sides of the tabernacle, on the one side and on the other, to cover it.note
Y el codo de un lado, y el codo del otro lado, de lo que sobra en el largo de las cortinas de la tienda, colgará sobre los lados del tabernáculo, de un lado y del otro, para cubrirlo.
14
וְעָשִׂיתָ מִכְסֶה לָאֹהֶל, עֹרֹת אֵילִם מְאָדָּמִים, וּמִכְסֵה עֹרֹת תְּחָשִׁים, מִלְמָעְלָה. {פ}
And you shall make a covering for the tent of rams’ skins dyed red, and a covering of tachash skins above it.note
Y harás una cubierta para la tienda de pieles de carnero teñidas de rojo, y una cubierta de pieles de tachash encima.
15
וְעָשִׂיתָ אֶת-הַקְּרָשִׁים, לַמִּשְׁכָּן, עֲצֵי שִׁטִּים, עֹמְדִים.
And you shall make the boards for the tabernacle of acacia wood, standing upright.note
Y harás las tablas para el tabernáculo de madera de acacia, puestas de pie.
16
עֶשֶׂר אַמּוֹת, אֹרֶךְ הַקָּרֶשׁ; וְאַמָּה וַחֲצִי הָאַמָּה, רֹחַב הַקֶּרֶשׁ הָאֶחָד.
Ten cubits the length of a board, and a cubit and a half the width of each board.note
Diez codos el largo de una tabla, y un codo y medio el ancho de cada tabla.
17
שְׁתֵּי יָדוֹת, לַקֶּרֶשׁ הָאֶחָד--מְשֻׁלָּבֹת, אִשָּׁה אֶל-אֲחֹתָהּ; כֵּן תַּעֲשֶׂה, לְכֹל קַרְשֵׁי הַמִּשְׁכָּן.
Two tenons for each board, joined one to the other; so you shall make for all the boards of the tabernacle.note
Dos espigas para cada tabla, unidas la una a la otra; así harás para todas las tablas del tabernáculo.
18
וְעָשִׂיתָ אֶת-הַקְּרָשִׁים, לַמִּשְׁכָּן, עֶשְׂרִים קֶרֶשׁ, לִפְאַת נֶגְבָּה תֵימָנָה.
And you shall make the boards for the tabernacle: twenty boards for the south side, southward.note
Y harás las tablas para el tabernáculo: veinte tablas para el lado sur, hacia el sur.
19
וְאַרְבָּעִים, אַדְנֵי-כֶסֶף, תַּעֲשֶׂה, תַּחַת עֶשְׂרִים הַקָּרֶשׁ: שְׁנֵי אֲדָנִים תַּחַת-הַקֶּרֶשׁ הָאֶחָד, לִשְׁתֵּי יְדֹתָיו, וּשְׁנֵי אֲדָנִים תַּחַת-הַקֶּרֶשׁ הָאֶחָד, לִשְׁתֵּי יְדֹתָיו.
And you shall make forty sockets of silver under the twenty boards: two sockets under the one board for its two tenons, and two sockets under the next board for its two tenons.note
Y harás cuarenta basas de plata debajo de las veinte tablas: dos basas debajo de una tabla para sus dos espigas, y dos basas debajo de la siguiente tabla para sus dos espigas.
20
וּלְצֶלַע הַמִּשְׁכָּן הַשֵּׁנִית, לִפְאַת צָפוֹן, עֶשְׂרִים, קָרֶשׁ.
And for the second side of the tabernacle, on the north side: twenty boards.note
Y para el segundo lado del tabernáculo, el lado norte: veinte tablas.
21
וְאַרְבָּעִים אַדְנֵיהֶם, כָּסֶף: שְׁנֵי אֲדָנִים, תַּחַת הַקֶּרֶשׁ הָאֶחָד, וּשְׁנֵי אֲדָנִים, תַּחַת הַקֶּרֶשׁ הָאֶחָד.
And their forty sockets of silver: two sockets under the one board, and two sockets under the next board.note
Y sus cuarenta basas de plata: dos basas debajo de una tabla, y dos basas debajo de la siguiente tabla.
22
וּלְיַרְכְּתֵי הַמִּשְׁכָּן, יָמָּה, תַּעֲשֶׂה, שִׁשָּׁה קְרָשִׁים.
And for the rear of the tabernacle, westward, you shall make six boards.note
Y para la parte trasera del tabernáculo, hacia el occidente, harás seis tablas.
23
וּשְׁנֵי קְרָשִׁים תַּעֲשֶׂה, לִמְקֻצְעֹת הַמִּשְׁכָּן--בַּיַּרְכָתָיִם.
And two boards you shall make for the corners of the tabernacle at the rear.note
Y dos tablas harás para las esquinas del tabernáculo en la parte trasera.
24
וְיִהְיוּ תֹאֲמִם, מִלְּמַטָּה, וְיַחְדָּו יִהְיוּ תַמִּים עַל-רֹאשׁוֹ, אֶל-הַטַּבַּעַת הָאֶחָת; כֵּן יִהְיֶה לִשְׁנֵיהֶם, לִשְׁנֵי הַמִּקְצֹעֹת יִהְיוּ.
And they shall be doubled from beneath, and together they shall be complete at the top, at the one ring; so it shall be for both of them, for the two corners they shall be.note
Y serán dobles desde abajo, y juntas serán completas en su parte superior, hasta el primer anillo; así será para ambas, para las dos esquinas serán.
25
וְהָיוּ, שְׁמֹנָה קְרָשִׁים, וְאַדְנֵיהֶם כֶּסֶף, שִׁשָּׁה עָשָׂר אֲדָנִים: שְׁנֵי אֲדָנִים, תַּחַת הַקֶּרֶשׁ הָאֶחָד, וּשְׁנֵי אֲדָנִים, תַּחַת הַקֶּרֶשׁ הָאֶחָד.
So there shall be eight boards, and their sockets of silver: sixteen sockets; two sockets under the one board, and two sockets under the next board.note
Así habrá ocho tablas, y sus basas de plata: dieciséis basas; dos basas debajo de una tabla, y dos basas debajo de la siguiente tabla.
26
וְעָשִׂיתָ בְרִיחִם, עֲצֵי שִׁטִּים, חֲמִשָּׁה, לְקַרְשֵׁי צֶלַע-הַמִּשְׁכָּן הָאֶחָד.
And you shall make bars of acacia wood: five for the boards of the one side of the tabernacle,note
Y harás barras de madera de acacia: cinco para las tablas de un lado del tabernáculo,
27
וַחֲמִשָּׁה בְרִיחִם, לְקַרְשֵׁי צֶלַע-הַמִּשְׁכָּן הַשֵּׁנִית; וַחֲמִשָּׁה בְרִיחִם, לְקַרְשֵׁי צֶלַע הַמִּשְׁכָּן, לַיַּרְכָתַיִם, יָמָּה.
and five bars for the boards of the other side of the tabernacle, and five bars for the boards of the side of the tabernacle at the rear, westward;note
y cinco barras para las tablas del otro lado del tabernáculo, y cinco barras para las tablas del lado del tabernáculo en la parte trasera, hacia el occidente;
28
וְהַבְּרִיחַ הַתִּיכֹן, בְּתוֹךְ הַקְּרָשִׁים, מַבְרִחַ, מִן-הַקָּצֶה אֶל-הַקָּצֶה.
and the middle bar, in the midst of the boards, shall pass through from end to end.note
y la barra del medio, en medio de las tablas, pasará de un extremo al otro.
29
וְאֶת-הַקְּרָשִׁים תְּצַפֶּה זָהָב, וְאֶת-טַבְּעֹתֵיהֶם תַּעֲשֶׂה זָהָב--בָּתִּים, לַבְּרִיחִם; וְצִפִּיתָ אֶת-הַבְּרִיחִם, זָהָב.
And you shall overlay the boards with gold, and make their rings of gold as holders for the bars, and you shall overlay the bars with gold.note
Y recubrirás las tablas de oro, y harás sus anillos de oro como alojamientos para las barras, y recubrirás las barras de oro.
30
וַהֲקֵמֹתָ, אֶת-הַמִּשְׁכָּן: כְּמִשְׁפָּטוֹ--אֲשֶׁר הָרְאֵיתָ, בָּהָר. {ס}
And you shall raise up the tabernacle according to its plan, which you were shown on the mountain.note
Y levantarás el tabernáculo conforme a su plan, que te fue mostrado en el monte.
31
וְעָשִׂיתָ פָרֹכֶת, תְּכֵלֶת וְאַרְגָּמָן וְתוֹלַעַת שָׁנִי--וְשֵׁשׁ מָשְׁזָר; מַעֲשֵׂה חֹשֵׁב יַעֲשֶׂה אֹתָהּ, כְּרֻבִים.
And you shall make a veil of blue, and purple, and scarlet yarn, and fine twined linen; the work of a skilled designer it shall be made, with cherubim.note
Y harás un velo de azul, y púrpura, y grana, y lino fino torcido; obra de un diseñador hábil se hará, con querubines.
32
וְנָתַתָּה אֹתָהּ, עַל-אַרְבָּעָה עַמּוּדֵי שִׁטִּים, מְצֻפִּים זָהָב, וָוֵיהֶם זָהָב--עַל-אַרְבָּעָה, אַדְנֵי-כָסֶף.
And you shall hang it on four pillars of acacia overlaid with gold, their hooks of gold, on four sockets of silver.note
Y lo colgarás de cuatro columnas de acacia recubiertas de oro, sus ganchos de oro, sobre cuatro basas de plata.
33
וְנָתַתָּה אֶת-הַפָּרֹכֶת, תַּחַת הַקְּרָסִים, וְהֵבֵאתָ שָׁמָּה מִבֵּית לַפָּרֹכֶת, אֵת אֲרוֹן הָעֵדוּת; וְהִבְדִּילָה הַפָּרֹכֶת, לָכֶם, בֵּין הַקֹּדֶשׁ, וּבֵין קֹדֶשׁ הַקֳּדָשִׁים.

Notes on this translation

A tent within a tent, and one word for ‘curtain’ that quietly stops meaning the same thing

The tabernacle proper is not the outer shape a visitor would have seen — it is the INNER lining, ten linen curtains woven with cherubim (vv1–6), draped and clasped together first. Only after that inner set is finished does the text move outward to an eleven-curtain goat-hair covering ‘for a tent OVER the tabernacle’ (v7) — a second, larger, coarser layer whose whole job is to protect the fine one inside it, followed in turn by two more weatherproofing layers of dyed rams’ skins and tachash skins (v14, the same uncertain word already flagged at 25:5). Structurally: sanctuary, then tent, then weather — three concentric shells, most sacred nearest the center.

‘Worked in by a skilled designer’ (v1) is a specific craft-term, not a generic compliment, and this chapter is where its full significance becomes visible. The Talmud (Yoma 72b) and later commentators distinguish this term (ma’aseh choshev) from a second one this chapter also uses (ma’aseh rokem, v36): choshev-work is woven so the design reads identically on BOTH faces of the fabric, while rokem-work is stitched onto one face only, leaving the reverse plain. The distinction is not decorative trivia — it tracks exactly which curtains in this chapter get cherubim (the inner lining here, and the veil at v31, both choshev) and which do not (the outer screen at v36, plain rokem). The deeper into the structure, the more expensive the weave.

⚠ A wall no one can agree was solid, and a board with two ‘hands’

Whether the boards of vv15–29 were solid planks or open frames is a genuine, unresolved debate, and the two English-language camps split almost exactly down denominational and chronological lines. KJV, ASV, and NASB all read ‘boards’ — solid slabs of acacia, standing edge to edge like a plank wall. NIV, ESV, and NLT all read ‘frames’ — an open, ladder-like construction, vertical members joined by crosspieces, with the curtain fabric itself forming the visible wall. The practical case for frames is straightforward: a solid acacia plank ten cubits (roughly fifteen feet) tall and a cubit and a half wide would be extraordinarily heavy for forty-eight of them to be carried through a wilderness by hand, and a solid wall would hide the cherubim-woven inner curtains from the priests serving inside — the very curtains this chapter spent its first six verses describing in loving detail. No consensus exists; this translation keeps the traditional ‘boards’ while flagging the case for the alternative.

⚠ Each board’s two tenons (v17) are, in the Hebrew, literally its two ‘HANDS’ (yadot, the plural of yad, ‘hand’) — the pegs at its base reaching down into the paired silver sockets below, ninety-six sockets in all funded, later in the book, by the half-shekel census offering of every counted Israelite man (Exodus 38:25–27, not yet on these pages). The chapter closes this section (v30) with ‘according to its mishpato’ — the same root, mishpat, that named the whole law-code four chapters back (the Mishpatim, Exodus 21–23, already on these pages); here it means simply ‘plan’ or ‘design’, but the echo is real — the tabernacle has its own mishpat, its own binding ordinance, restated from Moses’s own pattern-vision at 25:9 and 25:40.

⚠ The curtain a much later writer will not name, and the third pair of cherubim on these pages

The veil that hangs before the ark (vv31–33) is the THIRD set of cherubim to appear on these pages, and each pair has done something structurally different. The first pair stood at Eden’s gate, barring the way back to the tree of life (Genesis 3:24, already on these pages). The second pair, hammered in gold, spread their wings over the ark’s cover and framed the place where God promised to meet Moses (Exodus 25:18–22, already on these pages). This third pair, woven into the veil itself, now marks the boundary a human being crosses to reach that meeting-place — ‘the veil shall DIVIDE for you between the holy place and the most holy’ (v33). Guardian, then witness, then threshold.

⚠ A much later scene turns on this exact curtain, or possibly its plainer neighbor, and the Greek text that reports it will not say which. When Jesus dies, ‘the curtain of the sanctuary was torn in two, from top to bottom’ (Matthew 27:51, already on these pages) — and this chapter is the reason that ambiguity is real, not a translator’s vagueness. Hebrew names two entirely distinct hangings here: this VEIL (parochet), guarding the ark itself, woven with cherubim by the same craft as the tabernacle’s inner lining; and the plainer SCREEN (masakh) at the tent’s outer door, v36 below, embroidered but bearing no cherubim at all. The Septuagint’s Greek translates BOTH Hebrew words with the same term, katapetasma — so when Matthew’s Gospel later uses that one Greek word for the curtain torn at the crucifixion, the text genuinely cannot say, and does not try to say, which of these two curtains — the inner or the outer — is meant.

The plainer curtain, and a craft-word that completes the contrast

The screen at the tent’s public entrance (vv36–37) uses the same four dyed threads as every curtain in this chapter, but not the same craftsmanship, and the difference is the whole point of the chapter’s design. Its ‘work of an embroiderer’ (ma’aseh rokem) is stitched onto one face of the fabric only — visible to anyone approaching from outside, plain from within — and it carries no cherubim, unlike the inner curtains (v1) and the veil before the ark (v31), both made by the two-faced choshev weave. A worshiper standing at the tabernacle’s door sees color without guardians; only past the veil, where almost no one ever stands, do the cherubim appear at all.

Notes on this translation

Decisions. ‘Boards’ kept for keresh (vv15–29) rather than the increasingly common ‘frames’, per the note above — the traditional reading, with the case for the alternative flagged rather than adopted. ‘Veil’ kept for parochet (v31) and ‘screen’ for masakh (v36) as two distinct English words for two distinct Hebrew ones, since the whole point of this chapter’s note is that they are not interchangeable. Verse numbering follows the Hebrew exactly; Hebrew and English Exodus 26 both run 1–37.

A note on order: Exodus stands on these pages at chapters 1–28 — the tabernacle’s furniture (25) and its own frame, coverings, and curtains (26) both specified, Exodus 27’s altar and enclosing courtyard, and now Exodus 28’s priestly garments too. Exodus 29, the ordination ceremony itself, remains open, along with the rest of the book.
