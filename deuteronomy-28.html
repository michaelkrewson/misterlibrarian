The blessings and the curses of the covenant, set out at length after chapter 27 staged the ceremony that will pronounce them: fourteen verses of blessing (v1–14) closed by an open Masoretic break, then fifty-four verses of curse (v15–68) with no break in them at all — disease, drought, defeat, the boil of Egypt, madness, a house built and not lived in, a yoke of iron, a nation from far away as the eagle swoops, a siege in which parents eat their children, exile among all the peoples, and a return to Egypt in ships to a slave market with no buyer — before the closing verse (v69) names all of it the words of the covenant made in Moab besides the covenant at Horeb.
Still the plains of Moab in the fortieth year (1:3), with no narrated event, and the third and last chapter of the weekly portion Ki Tavo. Nothing happens; everything is conditional and future — if you truly listen (v1), if you do not listen (v15) — and the chapter’s own horizon runs from the settled land (barns, vineyards, olive trees, walled towns) through a siege and an exile to the far end of the earth, the span the later books will narrate as history. ⭐ 69 verses in the Hebrew and 68 in every English Bible: the Hebrew’s v69, these are the words of the covenant, is the English 29:1, so from here to the end of the book the two numberings differ by one, and the four-chapter run of matching counts that began at chapter 24 ends here.

1
וְהָיָה, אִם-שָׁמוֹעַ תִּשְׁמַע בְּקוֹל יְהוָה אֱלֹהֶיךָ, לִשְׁמֹר לַעֲשׂוֹת אֶת-כָּל-מִצְוֺתָיו, אֲשֶׁר אָנֹכִי מְצַוְּךָ הַיּוֹם--וּנְתָנְךָ יְהוָה אֱלֹהֶיךָ, עֶלְיוֹן, עַל, כָּל-גּוֹיֵי הָאָרֶץ.
And it shall be, if you truly listen to the voice of Jehovah your God, to keep and to do all his commandments that I am commanding you today, that Jehovah your God will set you high above all the nations of the earth.note
Y sucederá que, si de veras escuchas la voz de Jehová tu Dios, guardando y cumpliendo todos sus mandamientos que yo te mando hoy, Jehová tu Dios te pondrá en alto sobre todas las naciones de la tierra.
2
וּבָאוּ עָלֶיךָ כָּל-הַבְּרָכוֹת הָאֵלֶּה, וְהִשִּׂיגֻךָ: כִּי תִשְׁמַע, בְּקוֹל יְהוָה אֱלֹהֶיךָ.
And all these blessings shall come upon you and overtake you, when you listen to the voice of Jehovah your God.note
Y vendrán sobre ti todas estas bendiciones y te alcanzarán, cuando escuches la voz de Jehová tu Dios.
3
בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה, בָּעִיר; וּבָרוּךְ אַתָּה, בַּשָּׂדֶה.
Blessed are you in the city, and blessed are you in the field.note
Bendito eres tú en la ciudad, y bendito eres tú en el campo.
4
בָּרוּךְ פְּרִי-בִטְנְךָ וּפְרִי אַדְמָתְךָ, וּפְרִי בְהֶמְתֶּךָ--שְׁגַר אֲלָפֶיךָ, וְעַשְׁתְּרוֹת צֹאנֶךָ.
Blessed is the fruit of your belly and the fruit of your ground and the fruit of your livestock — the calving of your cattle and the lambing of your flock.note
Bendito es el fruto de tu vientre y el fruto de tu suelo y el fruto de tus animales: las crías de tus vacas y los corderos de tu rebaño.
5
בָּרוּךְ טַנְאֲךָ, וּמִשְׁאַרְתֶּךָ.
Blessed is your basket and your kneading-bowl.note
Bendita es tu canasta y tu artesa.
6
בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה, בְּבֹאֶךָ; וּבָרוּךְ אַתָּה, בְּצֵאתֶךָ.
Blessed are you when you come in, and blessed are you when you go out.note
Bendito eres tú cuando entras, y bendito eres tú cuando sales.
7
יִתֵּן יְהוָה אֶת-אֹיְבֶיךָ הַקָּמִים עָלֶיךָ, נִגָּפִים לְפָנֶיךָ: בְּדֶרֶךְ אֶחָד יֵצְאוּ אֵלֶיךָ, וּבְשִׁבְעָה דְרָכִים יָנוּסוּ לְפָנֶיךָ.
Jehovah will make your enemies who rise up against you be struck down before you: by one way they will come out against you, and by seven ways they will flee before you.note
Jehová hará que tus enemigos que se levanten contra ti sean derrotados delante de ti: por un camino saldrán contra ti, y por siete caminos huirán delante de ti.
8
יְצַו יְהוָה אִתְּךָ, אֶת-הַבְּרָכָה, בַּאֲסָמֶיךָ, וּבְכֹל מִשְׁלַח יָדֶךָ; וּבֵרַכְךָ--בָּאָרֶץ, אֲשֶׁר-יְהוָה אֱלֹהֶיךָ נֹתֵן לָךְ.
Jehovah will command the blessing to be with you in your barns and in everything you put your hand to, and he will bless you in the land that Jehovah your God is giving you.note
Jehová mandará que la bendición esté contigo en tus graneros y en todo aquello a lo que pongas la mano, y te bendecirá en la tierra que Jehová tu Dios te da.
9
יְקִימְךָ יְהוָה לוֹ לְעַם קָדוֹשׁ, כַּאֲשֶׁר נִשְׁבַּע-לָךְ: כִּי תִשְׁמֹר, אֶת-מִצְוֺת יְהוָה אֱלֹהֶיךָ, וְהָלַכְתָּ, בִּדְרָכָיו.
Jehovah will establish you for himself as a holy people, as he swore to you, if you keep the commandments of Jehovah your God and walk in his ways.note
Jehová te establecerá para sí como pueblo santo, como te juró, si guardas los mandamientos de Jehová tu Dios y andas en sus caminos.
10
וְרָאוּ כָּל-עַמֵּי הָאָרֶץ, כִּי שֵׁם יְהוָה נִקְרָא עָלֶיךָ; וְיָרְאוּ, מִמֶּךָּ.
And all the peoples of the earth will see that the name of Jehovah is called over you, and they will be afraid of you.note
Y verán todos los pueblos de la tierra que el nombre de Jehová es llamado sobre ti, y te temerán.
11
וְהוֹתִרְךָ יְהוָה לְטוֹבָה, בִּפְרִי בִטְנְךָ וּבִפְרִי בְהֶמְתְּךָ וּבִפְרִי אַדְמָתֶךָ--עַל, הָאֲדָמָה, אֲשֶׁר נִשְׁבַּע יְהוָה לַאֲבֹתֶיךָ, לָתֶת לָךְ.
And Jehovah will make you overflow with good — in the fruit of your belly and in the fruit of your livestock and in the fruit of your ground — on the ground that Jehovah swore to your fathers to give you.note
Y Jehová te hará rebosar de bien: en el fruto de tu vientre y en el fruto de tus animales y en el fruto de tu suelo, sobre el suelo que Jehová juró a tus padres darte.
12
יִפְתַּח יְהוָה לְךָ אֶת-אוֹצָרוֹ הַטּוֹב אֶת-הַשָּׁמַיִם, לָתֵת מְטַר-אַרְצְךָ בְּעִתּוֹ, וּלְבָרֵךְ, אֵת כָּל-מַעֲשֵׂה יָדֶךָ; וְהִלְוִיתָ גּוֹיִם רַבִּים, וְאַתָּה לֹא תִלְוֶה.
Jehovah will open to you his good storehouse, the heavens, to give the rain of your land in its season and to bless all the work of your hand; and you will lend to many nations, and you yourself will not borrow.note
Jehová te abrirá su buen almacén, los cielos, para dar la lluvia de tu tierra a su tiempo y para bendecir toda la obra de tu mano; y prestarás a muchas naciones, y tú no tomarás prestado.
13
וּנְתָנְךָ יְהוָה לְרֹאשׁ, וְלֹא לְזָנָב, וְהָיִיתָ רַק לְמַעְלָה, וְלֹא תִהְיֶה לְמָטָּה: כִּי-תִשְׁמַע אֶל-מִצְוֺת יְהוָה אֱלֹהֶיךָ, אֲשֶׁר אָנֹכִי מְצַוְּךָ הַיּוֹם--לִשְׁמֹר וְלַעֲשׂוֹת.
And Jehovah will make you the head and not the tail, and you will be only above and you will not be beneath — if you listen to the commandments of Jehovah your God that I am commanding you today, to keep and to do them,note
Y Jehová te pondrá por cabeza y no por cola, y estarás solamente arriba y no estarás abajo, si escuchas los mandamientos de Jehová tu Dios que yo te mando hoy, para guardarlos y cumplirlos,
14
וְלֹא תָסוּר, מִכָּל-הַדְּבָרִים אֲשֶׁר אָנֹכִי מְצַוֶּה אֶתְכֶם הַיּוֹם--יָמִין וּשְׂמֹאול: לָלֶכֶת, אַחֲרֵי אֱלֹהִים אֲחֵרִים--לְעָבְדָם. {פ}
and do not turn aside from all the words that I am commanding you today, to the right or to the left, to go after other gods to serve them. {P}note
y no te apartas de todas las palabras que yo les mando hoy, ni a la derecha ni a la izquierda, para ir tras otros dioses para servirles. {P}
15
וְהָיָה, אִם-לֹא תִשְׁמַע בְּקוֹל יְהוָה אֱלֹהֶיךָ, לִשְׁמֹר לַעֲשׂוֹת אֶת-כָּל-מִצְוֺתָיו וְחֻקֹּתָיו, אֲשֶׁר אָנֹכִי מְצַוְּךָ הַיּוֹם--וּבָאוּ עָלֶיךָ כָּל-הַקְּלָלוֹת הָאֵלֶּה, וְהִשִּׂיגוּךָ.
And it shall be, if you do not listen to the voice of Jehovah your God, to keep and to do all his commandments and his statutes that I am commanding you today, that all these curses shall come upon you and overtake you.note
Y sucederá que, si no escuchas la voz de Jehová tu Dios, guardando y cumpliendo todos sus mandamientos y sus estatutos que yo te mando hoy, vendrán sobre ti todas estas maldiciones y te alcanzarán.
16
אָרוּר אַתָּה, בָּעִיר; וְאָרוּר אַתָּה, בַּשָּׂדֶה.
Cursed are you in the city, and cursed are you in the field.note
Maldito eres tú en la ciudad, y maldito eres tú en el campo.
17
אָרוּר טַנְאֲךָ, וּמִשְׁאַרְתֶּךָ.
Cursed is your basket and your kneading-bowl.note
Maldita es tu canasta y tu artesa.
18
אָרוּר פְּרִי-בִטְנְךָ, וּפְרִי אַדְמָתֶךָ--שְׁגַר אֲלָפֶיךָ, וְעַשְׁתְּרֹת צֹאנֶךָ.
Cursed is the fruit of your belly and the fruit of your ground — the calving of your cattle and the lambing of your flock.note
Maldito es el fruto de tu vientre y el fruto de tu suelo: las crías de tus vacas y los corderos de tu rebaño.
19
אָרוּר אַתָּה, בְּבֹאֶךָ; וְאָרוּר אַתָּה, בְּצֵאתֶךָ.
Cursed are you when you come in, and cursed are you when you go out.note
Maldito eres tú cuando entras, y maldito eres tú cuando sales.
20
יְשַׁלַּח יְהוָה בְּךָ אֶת-הַמְּאֵרָה אֶת-הַמְּהוּמָה, וְאֶת-הַמִּגְעֶרֶת, בְּכָל-מִשְׁלַח יָדְךָ, אֲשֶׁר תַּעֲשֶׂה--עַד הִשָּׁמֶדְךָ וְעַד-אֲבָדְךָ מַהֵר, מִפְּנֵי רֹעַ מַעֲלָלֶיךָ אֲשֶׁר עֲזַבְתָּנִי.
Jehovah will send against you the curse, the panic and the rebuke in everything you put your hand to, that you do, until you are destroyed and until you perish quickly, because of the evil of your deeds, in that you have forsaken me.note
Jehová enviará contra ti la maldición, el pánico y la reprensión en todo aquello a lo que pongas la mano, que hagas, hasta que seas destruido y hasta que perezcas pronto, a causa de la maldad de tus obras, por la que me has abandonado.
21
יַדְבֵּק יְהוָה בְּךָ, אֶת-הַדָּבֶר--עַד, כַּלֹּתוֹ אֹתְךָ, מֵעַל הָאֲדָמָה, אֲשֶׁר-אַתָּה בָא-שָׁמָּה לְרִשְׁתָּהּ.
Jehovah will make the pestilence cling to you until it has consumed you from off the ground that you are entering to possess.note
Jehová hará que la peste se te pegue, hasta que te haya consumido de sobre el suelo al que entras para poseerlo.
22
יַכְּכָה יְהוָה בַּשַּׁחֶפֶת וּבַקַּדַּחַת וּבַדַּלֶּקֶת, וּבַחַרְחֻר וּבַחֶרֶב, וּבַשִּׁדָּפוֹן, וּבַיֵּרָקוֹן; וּרְדָפוּךָ, עַד אָבְדֶךָ.
Jehovah will strike you with wasting disease and with fever and with inflammation and with burning heat and with the sword and with blight and with mildew, and they will pursue you until you perish.note
Jehová te herirá con consunción y con fiebre y con inflamación y con ardor y con la espada y con tizón y con añublo, y te perseguirán hasta que perezcas.
23
וְהָיוּ שָׁמֶיךָ אֲשֶׁר עַל-רֹאשְׁךָ, נְחֹשֶׁת; וְהָאָרֶץ אֲשֶׁר-תַּחְתֶּיךָ, בַּרְזֶל.
And your skies that are over your head will be bronze, and the earth that is under you iron.note
Y tus cielos que están sobre tu cabeza serán de bronce, y la tierra que está debajo de ti, de hierro.
24
יִתֵּן יְהוָה אֶת-מְטַר אַרְצְךָ, אָבָק וְעָפָר: מִן-הַשָּׁמַיִם יֵרֵד עָלֶיךָ, עַד הִשָּׁמְדָךְ.
Jehovah will make the rain of your land powder and dust; from the heavens it will come down on you until you are destroyed.note
Jehová dará por lluvia de tu tierra polvo fino y polvo; desde los cielos descenderá sobre ti, hasta que seas destruido.
25
יִתֶּנְךָ יְהוָה נִגָּף, לִפְנֵי אֹיְבֶיךָ--בְּדֶרֶךְ אֶחָד תֵּצֵא אֵלָיו, וּבְשִׁבְעָה דְרָכִים תָּנוּס לְפָנָיו; וְהָיִיתָ לְזַעֲוָה, לְכֹל מַמְלְכוֹת הָאָרֶץ.
Jehovah will make you be struck down before your enemies: by one way you will go out against them, and by seven ways you will flee before them; and you will become a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth.note
Jehová hará que seas derrotado delante de tus enemigos: por un camino saldrás contra ellos, y por siete caminos huirás delante de ellos; y serás motivo de espanto para todos los reinos de la tierra.
26
וְהָיְתָה נִבְלָתְךָ לְמַאֲכָל, לְכָל-עוֹף הַשָּׁמַיִם וּלְבֶהֱמַת הָאָרֶץ; וְאֵין, מַחֲרִיד.
And your carcass will be food for every bird of the heavens and for the beasts of the earth, and there will be no one to frighten them away.note
Y tu cadáver servirá de comida a toda ave de los cielos y a las bestias de la tierra, y no habrá quien las espante.
27
יַכְּכָה יְהוָה בִּשְׁחִין מִצְרַיִם, ובעפלים וּבַטְּחֹרִים, וּבַגָּרָב, וּבֶחָרֶס--אֲשֶׁר לֹא-תוּכַל, לְהֵרָפֵא.
Jehovah will strike you with the boil of Egypt and with the tumours and with the scab and with the itch, from which you cannot be healed.note
Jehová te herirá con la úlcera de Egipto y con los tumores y con la sarna y con la comezón, de los que no podrás sanar.
28
יַכְּכָה יְהוָה, בְּשִׁגָּעוֹן וּבְעִוָּרוֹן; וּבְתִמְהוֹן, לֵבָב.
Jehovah will strike you with madness and with blindness and with bewilderment of heart.note
Jehová te herirá con locura y con ceguera y con turbación de corazón.
29
וְהָיִיתָ מְמַשֵּׁשׁ בַּצָּהֳרַיִם, כַּאֲשֶׁר יְמַשֵּׁשׁ הַעִוֵּר בָּאֲפֵלָה, וְלֹא תַצְלִיחַ, אֶת-דְּרָכֶיךָ; וְהָיִיתָ אַךְ עָשׁוּק וְגָזוּל, כָּל-הַיָּמִים--וְאֵין מוֹשִׁיעַ.
And you will be groping at noon as the blind man gropes in the dark, and you will not make your ways succeed; and you will be only oppressed and robbed, always, with no one to save you.note
Y andarás a tientas al mediodía, como anda a tientas el ciego en la oscuridad, y no harás prosperar tus caminos; y no serás sino oprimido y robado, siempre, sin que haya quien te salve.
30
אִשָּׁה תְאָרֵשׂ, וְאִישׁ אַחֵר ישגלנה יִשְׁכָּבֶנָּה--בַּיִת תִּבְנֶה, וְלֹא-תֵשֵׁב בּוֹ; כֶּרֶם תִּטַּע, וְלֹא תְחַלְּלֶנּוּ.
You will betroth a wife, and another man will lie with her; you will build a house, and you will not live in it; you will plant a vineyard, and you will not put it to use.note
Te desposarás con una mujer, y otro hombre se acostará con ella; edificarás una casa, y no vivirás en ella; plantarás una viña, y no llegarás a usarla.
31
שׁוֹרְךָ טָבוּחַ לְעֵינֶיךָ, וְלֹא תֹאכַל מִמֶּנּוּ--חֲמֹרְךָ גָּזוּל מִלְּפָנֶיךָ, וְלֹא יָשׁוּב לָךְ; צֹאנְךָ נְתֻנוֹת לְאֹיְבֶיךָ, וְאֵין לְךָ מוֹשִׁיעַ.

Tu buey, degollado ante tus ojos, y no comerás de él; tu asno, robado de delante de ti, y no volverá a ti; tus ovejas, entregadas a tus enemigos, y no tendrás quien te salve.
32
בָּנֶיךָ וּבְנֹתֶיךָ נְתֻנִים לְעַם אַחֵר, וְעֵינֶיךָ רֹאוֹת, וְכָלוֹת אֲלֵיהֶם, כָּל-הַיּוֹם; וְאֵין לְאֵל, יָדֶךָ.

Tus hijos y tus hijas, entregados a otro pueblo, y tus ojos mirándolo y desfalleciendo por ellos todo el día; y no está en el poder de tu mano.
33
פְּרִי אַדְמָתְךָ וְכָל-יְגִיעֲךָ, יֹאכַל עַם אֲשֶׁר לֹא-יָדָעְתָּ; וְהָיִיתָ, רַק עָשׁוּק וְרָצוּץ--כָּל-הַיָּמִים.
The fruit of your ground and all your labour a people you have not known will eat; and you will be only oppressed and crushed, always.note
El fruto de tu suelo y todo tu trabajo lo comerá un pueblo que no has conocido; y no serás sino oprimido y quebrantado, siempre.
34
וְהָיִיתָ, מְשֻׁגָּע, מִמַּרְאֵה עֵינֶיךָ, אֲשֶׁר תִּרְאֶה.
And you will be driven mad by the sight of your eyes that you will see.note
Y enloquecerás por lo que verán tus ojos.
35
יַכְּכָה יְהוָה בִּשְׁחִין רָע, עַל-הַבִּרְכַּיִם וְעַל-הַשֹּׁקַיִם, אֲשֶׁר לֹא-תוּכַל, לְהֵרָפֵא--מִכַּף רַגְלְךָ, וְעַד קָדְקֳדֶךָ.
Jehovah will strike you with a malignant boil on the knees and on the legs, from which you cannot be healed — from the sole of your foot to the crown of your head.note
Jehová te herirá con una úlcera maligna en las rodillas y en las piernas, de la que no podrás sanar, desde la planta de tu pie hasta tu coronilla.
36
יוֹלֵךְ יְהוָה אֹתְךָ, וְאֶת-מַלְכְּךָ אֲשֶׁר תָּקִים עָלֶיךָ, אֶל-גּוֹי, אֲשֶׁר לֹא-יָדַעְתָּ אַתָּה וַאֲבֹתֶיךָ; וְעָבַדְתָּ שָּׁם אֱלֹהִים אֲחֵרִים, עֵץ וָאָבֶן.
Jehovah will lead you, and your king whom you set up over you, to a nation that you have not known, you nor your fathers; and there you will serve other gods, wood and stone.note
Jehová te llevará a ti, y a tu rey que pongas sobre ti, a una nación que no has conocido, ni tú ni tus padres; y allí servirás a otros dioses, de madera y de piedra.
37
וְהָיִיתָ לְשַׁמָּה, לְמָשָׁל וְלִשְׁנִינָה--בְּכֹל, הָעַמִּים, אֲשֶׁר-יְנַהֶגְךָ יְהוָה, שָׁמָּה.
And you will become an object of dismay, a byword and a taunt among all the peoples where Jehovah will drive you.note
Y serás motivo de asombro, refrán y burla entre todos los pueblos adonde Jehová te conduzca.
38
זֶרַע רַב, תּוֹצִיא הַשָּׂדֶה; וּמְעַט תֶּאֱסֹף, כִּי יַחְסְלֶנּוּ הָאַרְבֶּה.
Much seed you will carry out to the field, and little you will gather in, for the locust will consume it.note
Mucha semilla sacarás al campo, y poco recogerás, porque la langosta la devorará.
39
כְּרָמִים תִּטַּע, וְעָבָדְתָּ; וְיַיִן לֹא-תִשְׁתֶּה וְלֹא תֶאֱגֹר, כִּי תֹאכְלֶנּוּ הַתֹּלָעַת.
Vineyards you will plant and work, but wine you will not drink and you will not store up, for the worm will eat it.note
Viñas plantarás y las trabajarás, pero vino no beberás ni almacenarás, porque el gusano se lo comerá.
40
זֵיתִים יִהְיוּ לְךָ, בְּכָל-גְּבוּלֶךָ; וְשֶׁמֶן לֹא תָסוּךְ, כִּי יִשַּׁל זֵיתֶךָ.
Olive trees you will have throughout all your territory, but with oil you will not anoint yourself, for your olives will drop off.note
Olivos tendrás en todo tu territorio, pero con aceite no te ungirás, porque tu aceituna se caerá.
41
בָּנִים וּבָנוֹת, תּוֹלִיד; וְלֹא-יִהְיוּ לָךְ, כִּי יֵלְכוּ בַּשֶּׁבִי.
Sons and daughters you will beget, but they will not be yours, for they will go into captivity.note
Hijos e hijas engendrarás, pero no serán tuyos, porque irán al cautiverio.
42
כָּל-עֵצְךָ, וּפְרִי אַדְמָתֶךָ, יְיָרֵשׁ, הַצְּלָצַל.
All your trees and the fruit of your ground the whirring swarm will take possession of.note
De todos tus árboles y del fruto de tu suelo tomará posesión el enjambre zumbador.
43
הַגֵּר אֲשֶׁר בְּקִרְבְּךָ, יַעֲלֶה עָלֶיךָ מַעְלָה מָּעְלָה; וְאַתָּה תֵרֵד, מַטָּה מָּטָּה.
The stranger who is in your midst will rise above you higher and higher, and you will go down lower and lower.note
El forastero que está en medio de ti subirá sobre ti más y más arriba, y tú bajarás más y más abajo.
44
הוּא יַלְוְךָ, וְאַתָּה לֹא תַלְוֶנּוּ; הוּא יִהְיֶה לְרֹאשׁ, וְאַתָּה תִּהְיֶה לְזָנָב.
He will lend to you, and you will not lend to him; he will be the head, and you will be the tail.note
Él te prestará a ti, y tú no le prestarás a él; él será la cabeza, y tú serás la cola.
45
וּבָאוּ עָלֶיךָ כָּל-הַקְּלָלוֹת הָאֵלֶּה, וּרְדָפוּךָ וְהִשִּׂיגוּךָ, עַד, הִשָּׁמְדָךְ: כִּי-לֹא שָׁמַעְתָּ, בְּקוֹל יְהוָה אֱלֹהֶיךָ--לִשְׁמֹר מִצְוֺתָיו וְחֻקֹּתָיו, אֲשֶׁר צִוָּךְ.
And all these curses will come upon you and pursue you and overtake you until you are destroyed, because you did not listen to the voice of Jehovah your God, to keep his commandments and his statutes that he commanded you.note
Y vendrán sobre ti todas estas maldiciones, y te perseguirán y te alcanzarán hasta que seas destruido, porque no escuchaste la voz de Jehová tu Dios, para guardar sus mandamientos y sus estatutos que él te mandó.
46
וְהָיוּ בְךָ, לְאוֹת וּלְמוֹפֵת; וּבְזַרְעֲךָ, עַד-עוֹלָם.
And they will be on you for a sign and for a wonder, and on your seed for ever —note
Y estarán sobre ti por señal y por prodigio, y sobre tu descendencia para siempre,
47
תַּחַת, אֲשֶׁר לֹא-עָבַדְתָּ אֶת-יְהוָה אֱלֹהֶיךָ, בְּשִׂמְחָה, וּבְטוּב לֵבָב--מֵרֹב, כֹּל.
because you did not serve Jehovah your God with joy and with gladness of heart, out of the abundance of everything;note
por cuanto no serviste a Jehová tu Dios con alegría y con bondad de corazón, por la abundancia de todo;
48
וְעָבַדְתָּ אֶת-אֹיְבֶיךָ, אֲשֶׁר יְשַׁלְּחֶנּוּ יְהוָה בָּךְ, בְּרָעָב וּבְצָמָא וּבְעֵירֹם, וּבְחֹסֶר כֹּל; וְנָתַן עֹל בַּרְזֶל, עַל-צַוָּארֶךָ, עַד הִשְׁמִידוֹ, אֹתָךְ.
and you will serve your enemies whom Jehovah will send against you, in hunger and in thirst and in nakedness and in want of everything; and he will put a yoke of iron on your neck until he has destroyed you.note
y servirás a tus enemigos que Jehová enviará contra ti, con hambre y con sed y con desnudez y con falta de todo; y él pondrá un yugo de hierro sobre tu cuello, hasta destruirte.
49
יִשָּׂא יְהוָה עָלֶיךָ גּוֹי מֵרָחֹק מִקְצֵה הָאָרֶץ, כַּאֲשֶׁר יִדְאֶה הַנָּשֶׁר: גּוֹי, אֲשֶׁר לֹא-תִשְׁמַע לְשֹׁנוֹ.
Jehovah will raise up against you a nation from far away, from the end of the earth, as the eagle swoops — a nation whose tongue you will not understand,note
Jehová levantará contra ti una nación de lejos, del extremo de la tierra, como se abalanza el águila: una nación cuya lengua no entenderás,
50

a nation fierce of face, that will not lift the face of an old man and will not show favour to a boy.note
una nación de rostro fiero, que no levantará el rostro al anciano ni se apiadará del muchacho.
51

And it will eat the fruit of your livestock and the fruit of your ground until you are destroyed — it will leave you no grain, new wine or fresh oil, no calving of your cattle or lambing of your flock — until it has made you perish.note
Y comerá el fruto de tus animales y el fruto de tu suelo, hasta que seas destruido; no te dejará grano, ni vino nuevo, ni aceite fresco, ni crías de tus vacas ni corderos de tu rebaño, hasta hacerte perecer.
52

Y te sitiará en todas tus puertas, hasta que caigan tus murallas altas y fortificadas en las que confías, en toda tu tierra; y te sitiará en todas tus puertas, en toda tu tierra que Jehová tu Dios te ha dado.
53

And you will eat the fruit of your belly, the flesh of your sons and your daughters whom Jehovah your God has given you, in the siege and in the distress with which your enemy will distress you.note
Y comerás el fruto de tu vientre, la carne de tus hijos y de tus hijas que Jehová tu Dios te ha dado, en el asedio y en la angustia con que te angustiará tu enemigo.
54

The man who is tender among you, and very delicate — his eye will be evil toward his brother and toward the wife of his bosom and toward the rest of his children that he has left,note
El hombre tierno entre ti, y muy delicado: su ojo será malo con su hermano y con la mujer de su seno y con el resto de sus hijos que le queden,
55

para no dar a ninguno de ellos de la carne de sus hijos que coma, porque no le habrá quedado nada, en el asedio y en la angustia con que te angustiará tu enemigo en todas tus puertas.

The tender and delicate woman among you, who never ventured to set the sole of her foot on the ground out of delicacy and tenderness — her eye will be evil toward the husband of her bosom and toward her son and toward her daughter,note
La tierna entre ti y la delicada, que nunca probó a asentar la planta de su pie sobre la tierra, por delicadeza y por ternura: su ojo será malo con el marido de su seno y con su hijo y con su hija,
57
וּבְשִׁלְיָתָהּ הַיּוֹצֵת מִבֵּין רַגְלֶיהָ, וּבְבָנֶיהָ אֲשֶׁר תֵּלֵד, כִּי-תֹאכְלֵם בְּחֹסֶר-כֹּל, בַּסָּתֶר--בְּמָצוֹר, וּבְמָצוֹק, אֲשֶׁר יָצִיק לְךָ אֹיִבְךָ, בִּשְׁעָרֶיךָ.

y con la placenta que le sale de entre los pies, y con los hijos que dé a luz; porque se los comerá, por falta de todo, en secreto, en el asedio y en la angustia con que te angustiará tu enemigo en tus puertas.

If you do not keep and do all the words of this law that are written in this book, to fear this glorious and fearsome name, Jehovah your God,note
Si no guardas y cumples todas las palabras de esta ley, que están escritas en este libro, para temer este nombre glorioso y temible, a Jehová tu Dios,

Jehová hará extraordinarias tus plagas y las plagas de tu descendencia: plagas grandes y duraderas, y enfermedades malignas y duraderas.

Y hará volver sobre ti todas las dolencias de Egipto, ante las cuales temblabas, y se te pegarán.

También toda enfermedad y toda plaga que no está escrita en el libro de esta ley, Jehová las hará subir sobre ti, hasta que seas destruido.

Verses 1–6 · Blessed are you in the city, and blessed are you in the field (Deuteronomy 28:1–6) — the doubled verb of Marah, blessings that overtake like an army, six blessed in four verses, the fruit of the belly that Elizabeth will bless, the basket of chapter 26 beside the kneading-bowl of the exodus night, and Psalm 121’s going out and coming in

★★ If you truly listen is a doubled verb, and the first place the Bible doubles it is the first law of the exodus. Shamoa tishma, listening you shall listen: spelled out in full, the doubled form stands in six verses of the Hebrew Bible, and the first is Exodus 15:26 at Marah (already on these pages) — if you will diligently listen to the voice of Jehovah your God … I will not put on you any of the diseases that I put on the Egyptians, which is the sentence v60 of this chapter will turn inside out. Then it is Sinai’s offer at Exodus 19:5, the covenant code’s at 23:22, the release year’s at Deuteronomy 15:5 (all already on these pages), this verse, and Zechariah 6:15 (not yet on these pages); 11:13 says it in the plural and spells it short. The KJV and ASV read ‘hearken diligently’, the Geneva ‘obey diligently’, the NIV and TLB ‘fully obey’, the NWT 1984 ‘without fail listen’, and the Douay ‘hear the voice’, with no doubling at all; the RV 1909 reads «oyeres diligente», the RV60 «oyeres atentamente», the NVI «realmente escuchas», the TNM 1987 «escuchas sin falta» and the TNM 2019 «de veras escuchas». What listening earns is the adjective elyon, high — the word in God’s own title, El Elyon — and high above all stands in four verses of the Hebrew Bible: 26:19 (already on these pages) and this one, where it is Israel, and Psalms 83:19 and 97:9 (neither yet on these pages), where it is Jehovah, most high over all the earth. A people is given God’s adjective twice; God keeps it in the other two.

⭐ The blessings overtake you, and overtaking is what an army does to people who run. Hissig is the pursuer’s verb: the enemy at the sea boasts I will pursue, I will overtake (Exodus 15:9), the redeemer of blood overtakes the manslayer on a long road (19:6, both already on these pages), and here what runs a man down is a blessing. V15 gives the same verb to the curses and v45 adds pursue in front of it — the Song of the Sea’s pair exactly, said of Israel — and Lamentations 1:3 (already on these pages) reports it done: all her pursuers overtook her. The NIV reads ‘accompany you’ and the NVI «te acompañarán siempre», making the pursuit a companion; the KJV, ASV, Douay and NWT 1984 all read ‘overtake’, the RV60 «te alcanzarán», and the TLB drops the verb and prints the six blessings as a list. Then the six. Blessed are you stands in six verses of the Hebrew Bible, and the first two are v3 and v6, said to a whole nation; after them Saul says it to Samuel and to David (1 Samuel 15:13, 26:25), and then David says it to God (1 Chronicles 29:10) and the psalmist after him (Psalm 119:12; none yet on these pages) — barukh attah Jehovah, the three words that open every blessing in Jewish prayer to this day. Four verses hold six blessed; v16–19 will answer them with six cursed.

★ The fruit of your belly is the phrase Elizabeth will say to Mary, and the kneading-bowl beside the basket is the one Israel carried out of Egypt on its shoulders. Peri bitnekha: this project printed the fruit of your belly at 7:13 (already on these pages) and keeps it; the phrase, with this suffix, stands in seven verses of the Hebrew Bible — 7:13, four times in this chapter (v4, v11, v18, and v53, where it is eaten), 30:9 and Psalm 132:11 (neither yet on these pages). Jacob’s who has withheld from you the fruit of the womb (Genesis 30:2, already on these pages) is the same noun without the suffix. And Luke 1:42 (already on these pages), blessed is the fruit of your womb, eulogēmenos ho karpos tēs koilias sou, is v4’s first clause said in Greek to one woman. The KJV, ASV and Geneva read ‘fruit of thy body’, the Douay and NIV ‘womb’, the NWT 1984 ‘fruit of your belly’ and the NWT 2013 ‘your children’; the RV 1909, RV60 and NVI read «fruto de tu vientre», the TNM 2019 «tus hijos». ⚠ The RV 1909 drops the fruit of your ground from v4 altogether — it reads «el fruto de tu vientre, y el fruto de tu bestia» — and the RV60 puts it back. The calving and lambing are 7:13’s pair, and one of the two words is a goddess’s name (see the dictionary). Then v5: the basket is 26:2’s, the firstfruits basket of two chapters ago, and the kneading-bowl is the exodus’s — the noun stands in four verses of the Hebrew Bible: the frogs climb into your kneading bowls at Exodus 7:28, the people carry theirs bound up in their cloaks on their shoulders at 12:34 (both already on these pages), and it is blessed here and cursed at v17. The shelf does not agree what it is: the ASV, NIV and NWT 1984 read ‘kneading-trough’, the Geneva ‘thy dough’, the KJV ‘thy store’ and the Douay ‘thy stores’ (the Vulgate’s reliquiae, leftovers) — and the RV 1909 follows the Latin into «tus sobras», your leftovers, which the RV60 corrects to «artesa de amasar». The last blessing, when you come in and when you go out, is Psalm 121:8’s pair the other way round (already on these pages): Jehovah will keep your going out and your coming in.

⭐ A name called over something is a claim of ownership, and only here is the thing owned a whole people. Shem Jehovah niqra alekha: a name is called over the ark (2 Samuel 6:2), over Solomon’s house (1 Kings 8:43, and Jeremiah 7:10–14, where it is the temple’s undoing), and, in the plainest case, by a woman asking a man to marry her — let your name be called over us (Isaiah 4:1; none yet on these pages); Amos 9:12 extends it to all the nations over whom my name is called, the verse Acts 15:17 quotes. With you as its object the clause stands in one verse of the Hebrew Bible. The KJV turns it round, ‘thou art called by the name’, as do the ASV, ‘thou art called by the name of Jehovah’, and the NIV, ‘you are called by the name’; the Geneva keeps the Hebrew’s shape, the Name as the subject, ‘is called upon over thee’, the Douay ‘is invocated upon thee’, the NWT 1984 ‘Jehovah’s name has been called upon you’; the TLB drops the idiom and says that ‘you belong to’ him, which is not the words but is what the words mean. The RV 1909 reads «el nombre de Jehová es llamado sobre ti», the RV60 «invocado sobre ti», the NVI «al reconocerte como el pueblo» of God, the name paraphrased away, and the TNM 2019 «tú llevas el nombre de Jehová». A holy people (v9) is 7:6, 14:2 and 26:19 (all already on these pages), but here for the first time it is conditional — if you keep — and sworn.

★ The heavens are a storehouse, and the lending clause of chapter 15 is said again with a different verb. His good storehouse stands in one verse of the Hebrew Bible; the noun is a treasury or a granary, the place a king keeps what he has not yet spent, and Job 38:22 asks whether a man has entered the storehouses of the snow (not yet on these pages). The KJV and ASV read ‘his good treasure, the heaven’, the Douay ‘his excellent treasure’, the NIV ‘the heavens, the storehouse of his bounty’, the NWT 1984 ‘his good storehouse’, the TLB ‘his wonderful treasury of rain’; the RV 1909 reads «su buen depósito», the RV60 «su buen tesoro», the TNM 1987 «su buen almacén». Deuteronomy 15:6 (already on these pages) promised the same thing in nearly the same English — you shall lend to many nations and you yourself shall not borrow — but its Hebrew verb was avat, to take or give a pledge, and this verse’s is lavah, the ordinary word for a loan; this clause, with this verb, stands in one verse of the Hebrew Bible, and v44 will hand the same verb to the stranger. The head and not the tail is likewise undone at v44; Isaiah 9:13 and 19:15 (neither yet on these pages) take head and tail as a phrase for everybody. And to the right or to the left (v14) is the clause the book gave its king three chapters ago — 17:20, so that he does not turn aside from the commandment, right or left (already on these pages) — now said to the nation, as Moses said it of his own road through Edom at 2:27, and as Joshua 1:7 and Josiah’s epitaph at 2 Kings 22:2 (neither yet on these pages) will repeat it. The Hebrew’s open paragraph mark falls after this verse, and the next break of any kind does not come until after v68: fourteen verses of blessing are one paragraph, and fifty-four verses of curse are one paragraph.

★★ The six curses are the six blessings word for word — except that two are swapped and one clause is missing, and the first two words have been said before, to a snake and to a murderer. The evil of your deeds, roa ma’alalekha, is a phrase the prophets will wear out — Isaiah 1:16, Jeremiah 21:12, 26:3 (all already on these pages) — and this is where it starts. Patterns worth carrying forward. This chapter is built as a mirror, and it reads best held up to its own first fourteen verses: every blessing has its curse, usually in the blessing’s own words, and the reader who keeps a finger in v3–14 while reading v15–68 will find the seams — the two curses swapped, the livestock clause missing, the lender turned borrower. Nearly every sentence of the curse is a sentence some earlier page said as a promise, a law or a story: Leviticus 26 above all, but also the exodus (the boil, the kneading-bowls, the way you shall never see again), the release year, the war law, the king law, and the dove. Watch the verbs the book has made its own turn against the book — cling, shake loose, rejoice, overtake — and watch the five and there is no one tighten toward the last one, the buyer. Two words are written one way and read another (v27, v30), and the shelf divides along both seams; two verses let God’s own I through (v20, v68). And the chapter ends by calling all of it the words of the covenant: the next chapter will assemble the whole people, down to the woodcutter and the water-carrier, to stand inside those words.
