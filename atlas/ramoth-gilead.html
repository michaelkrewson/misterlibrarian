Ramoth in Gilead approximate

The second of the first three cities of refuge, assigned to the tribe of Gad (Deuteronomy 4:43). ⭐ This same town becomes, centuries later, one of the most fought-over cities in the divided kingdom — the site of Ahab's death (1 Kings 22) and Jehu's anointing (2 Kings 9). ⚠ Its identification with Tell er-Rumeith is widely proposed but not certain. ⭐ Joshua 20:8 (now on these pages) names it second of the three in the east, “out of the tribe of Gad.”
