Valley of Siddim (the Salt Sea) approximate

Where the kings gave battle, glossed by the text itself as 'the Salt Sea' — the Dead Sea basin, 'pit after pit of tar' (14:3, 8, 10). The bitumen the basin still seeps trapped the fleeing kings. The exact cities of the plain are debated (see Sodom and Gomorrah).
