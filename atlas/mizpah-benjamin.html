🗺️ Atlas

Mizpah (of Benjamin) approximate

“The watchtower” — a town of Benjamin on the road north from Jerusalem, distinct from the Mizpah in Gilead where Israel camped against Ammon (Judges 10:17, now on these pages). In Judges 20 (now on these pages) all Israel gathers here “as one man, from Dan to Beersheba”, hears the Levite’s account of Gibeah and resolves on war against Benjamin; the oath not to give their daughters to Benjamin was sworn here too (Judges 21:1, now on these pages). Samuel later gathers Israel at Mizpah and judges there (1 Samuel 7:5–6, now on these pages), where the people pour out water before Jehovah and the Philistines are routed by thunder, and it is one of the three towns of his yearly circuit (7:16). The identification with Tell en-Nasbeh is the usual one; Nebi Samwil has also been proposed. The map point follows Tell en-Nasbeh.
