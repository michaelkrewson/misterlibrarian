🗺️ Atlas

The Negev territory approximate

The arid south of Canaan, toward which Abram travels by stages (12:9) — the land's dry margin, naturally on the way down to Egypt.

in the text Gen 12:9
Approximate. The Negev is defined by rainfall, not by a treaty — it is the arid belt south of the Judean hills, roughly from the Beersheba basin down to the wilderness of Zin. The Hebrew word simply means 'the dry country' (and, by extension, 'the south'), so its boundary is a gradient, drawn here at the conventional limits.
