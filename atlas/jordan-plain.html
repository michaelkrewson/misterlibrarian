The Plain of the Jordan (Kikkar) approximate

The round, well-watered lower Jordan valley (13:10) — 'like the garden of Jehovah, like the land of Egypt' — that Lot chooses (13:11) over staying with Abram. Its beauty and its coming ruin are named in the very same verse.
