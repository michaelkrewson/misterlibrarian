Ezekiel prophesies against Pharaoh and all Egypt; a later word in the same chapter gives Egypt to Nebuchadrezzar as wages for his army.
The tenth year, tenth month, twelfth day; verse 17 is dated in the twenty-seventh year, on the first of the first month.

Ezekiel 29 — The Mister Translation

💬 Discuss this chapter
1
בַּשָּׁנָה, הָעֲשִׂרִית, בָּעֲשִׂרִי, בִּשְׁנֵים עָשָׂר לַחֹדֶשׁ--הָיָה דְבַר-יְהוָה, אֵלַי לֵאמֹר.
In the tenth year, in the tenth month, on the twelfth of the month, the word of Jehovah came to me, saying:note
En el año décimo, en el mes décimo, a los doce del mes, vino a mí la palabra de Jehová, diciendo:
2
בֶּן-אָדָם--שִׂים פָּנֶיךָ, עַל-פַּרְעֹה מֶלֶךְ מִצְרָיִם; וְהִנָּבֵא עָלָיו, וְעַל-מִצְרַיִם כֻּלָּהּ.
“Son of man, set your face against Pharaoh king of Egypt, and prophesy against him and against all Egypt.note
«Hijo de hombre, pon tu rostro contra el faraón, rey de Egipto, y profetiza contra él y contra todo Egipto.
3
דַּבֵּר וְאָמַרְתָּ כֹּה-אָמַר אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, הִנְנִי עָלֶיךָ פַּרְעֹה מֶלֶךְ-מִצְרַיִם, הַתַּנִּים הַגָּדוֹל, הָרֹבֵץ בְּתוֹךְ יְאֹרָיו:  אֲשֶׁר אָמַר לִי יְאֹרִי, וַאֲנִי עֲשִׂיתִנִי.
Speak and say, Thus says the Lord Jehovah: Look, I am against you, O Pharaoh king of Egypt, the great sea-beast that lies down in the midst of his canals, who has said, ‘My Nile is my own, and I have made myself.’note
Habla y di: Así dice el Señor Jehová: Mira, yo estoy contra ti, oh faraón, rey de Egipto, la gran bestia marina que se echa en medio de sus canales, que ha dicho: “Mi Nilo es mío, y yo me he hecho”.
4
וְנָתַתִּי חחיים חַחִים בִּלְחָיֶיךָ, וְהִדְבַּקְתִּי דְגַת-יְאֹרֶיךָ בְּקַשְׂקְשֹׂתֶיךָ; וְהַעֲלִיתִיךָ, מִתּוֹךְ יְאֹרֶיךָ, וְאֵת כָּל-דְּגַת יְאֹרֶיךָ, בְּקַשְׂקְשֹׂתֶיךָ תִּדְבָּק.
And I will put hooks in your jaws, and make the fish of your canals cling to your scales; and I will bring you up from the midst of your canals, and all the fish of your canals will cling to your scales.note
Y pondré garfios en tus mandíbulas, y haré que los peces de tus canales se peguen a tus escamas; y te haré subir de en medio de tus canales, y todos los peces de tus canales se pegarán a tus escamas.
5
וּנְטַשְׁתִּיךָ הַמִּדְבָּרָה, אוֹתְךָ וְאֵת כָּל-דְּגַת יְאֹרֶיךָ, עַל-פְּנֵי הַשָּׂדֶה תִּפּוֹל, לֹא תֵאָסֵף וְלֹא תִקָּבֵץ--לְחַיַּת הָאָרֶץ וּלְעוֹף הַשָּׁמַיִם, נְתַתִּיךָ לְאָכְלָה.
And I will abandon you to the wilderness, you and all the fish of your canals; on the face of the field you will fall; you will not be assembled or gathered; to the beasts of the land and to the birds of the heavens I have given you for food.note
Y te abandonaré en el desierto, a ti y a todos los peces de tus canales; sobre la faz del campo caerás; no serás recogido ni reunido; a las fieras de la tierra y a las aves de los cielos te he dado como comida.
6
וְיָדְעוּ כָּל-יֹשְׁבֵי מִצְרַיִם, כִּי אֲנִי יְהוָה, יַעַן הֱיוֹתָם מִשְׁעֶנֶת קָנֶה, לְבֵית יִשְׂרָאֵל.
And all the inhabitants of Egypt will know that I am Jehovah, because they have been a staff of reed to the house of Israel.note
Y sabrán todos los habitantes de Egipto que yo soy Jehová, por cuanto han sido un bastón de caña para la casa de Israel.
7
בְּתָפְשָׂם בְּךָ בכפך בַכַּף תֵּרוֹץ, וּבָקַעְתָּ לָהֶם כָּל-כָּתֵף; וּבְהִשָּׁעֲנָם עָלֶיךָ תִּשָּׁבֵר, וְהַעֲמַדְתָּ לָהֶם כָּל-מָתְנָיִם.  {ס}
When they took hold of you by the hand, you would be crushed, and split open all their shoulder; and when they leaned on you, you would break, and make all their loins stand.note
Cuando te tomaban con la mano, te astillabas y les abrías todo el hombro; y cuando se apoyaban en ti, te quebrabas, y les hacías quedar en pie todos los lomos.
8
לָכֵן, כֹּה אָמַר אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, הִנְנִי מֵבִיא עָלַיִךְ, חָרֶב; וְהִכְרַתִּי מִמֵּךְ, אָדָם וּבְהֵמָה.
Therefore thus says the Lord Jehovah: Look, I am bringing a sword upon you, and I will cut off from you man and beast.note
Por tanto, así dice el Señor Jehová: Mira, voy a traer sobre ti una espada, y cortaré de ti hombres y bestias.
9
וְהָיְתָה אֶרֶץ-מִצְרַיִם לִשְׁמָמָה וְחָרְבָּה, וְיָדְעוּ כִּי-אֲנִי יְהוָה:  יַעַן אָמַר יְאֹר לִי, וַאֲנִי עָשִׂיתִי.
And the land of Egypt will become a desolation and a ruin; and they will know that I am Jehovah; because he said, ‘The Nile is mine, and I made it,’note
Y la tierra de Egipto vendrá a ser una desolación y una ruina; y sabrán que yo soy Jehová; por cuanto él dijo: “El Nilo es mío, y yo lo hice”,
10
לָכֵן הִנְנִי אֵלֶיךָ, וְאֶל-יְאֹרֶיךָ; וְנָתַתִּי אֶת-אֶרֶץ מִצְרַיִם, לְחָרְבוֹת חֹרֶב שְׁמָמָה, מִמִּגְדֹּל סְוֵנֵה, וְעַד-גְּבוּל כּוּשׁ.
therefore, look, I am against you and against your canals; and I will make the land of Egypt ruins, drought, desolation, from Migdol to Syene and as far as the border of Cush.note
por tanto, mira, yo estoy contra ti y contra tus canales; y haré de la tierra de Egipto ruinas, sequía, desolación, desde Migdol hasta Sevene y hasta la frontera de Cus.
11
לֹא תַעֲבָר-בָּהּ רֶגֶל אָדָם, וְרֶגֶל בְּהֵמָה לֹא תַעֲבָר-בָּהּ; וְלֹא תֵשֵׁב, אַרְבָּעִים שָׁנָה.
No foot of man will pass through it, and no foot of beast will pass through it, and it will not be inhabited for forty years.note
No pasará por ella pie de hombre, ni pie de bestia pasará por ella, y no será habitada por cuarenta años.
12

And I will make the land of Egypt a desolation in the midst of desolate lands, and her cities in the midst of cities laid waste will be a desolation forty years; and I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations and disperse them through the lands.note
Y haré de la tierra de Egipto una desolación en medio de tierras desoladas, y sus ciudades en medio de ciudades arrasadas serán una desolación cuarenta años; y esparciré a los egipcios entre las naciones y los dispersaré por las tierras.

For thus says the Lord Jehovah: At the end of forty years I will gather the Egyptians from the peoples among whom they have been scattered;note
Porque así dice el Señor Jehová: Al cabo de cuarenta años reuniré a los egipcios de entre los pueblos en que han sido esparcidos;

and I will turn the captivity of Egypt, and bring them back to the land of Pathros, to the land of their origins; and there they will be a low kingdom.note
y haré volver el cautiverio de Egipto, y los haré regresar a la tierra de Patros, a la tierra de sus orígenes; y allí serán un reino bajo.
15
מִן-הַמַּמְלָכוֹת תִּהְיֶה שְׁפָלָה, וְלֹא-תִתְנַשֵּׂא עוֹד עַל-הַגּוֹיִם; וְהִמְעַטְתִּים--לְבִלְתִּי, רְדוֹת בַּגּוֹיִם.

De los reinos será el más bajo, y no se levantará más sobre las naciones; y los haré pocos, para que no dominen sobre las naciones.

And it happened in the twenty-seventh year, in the first month, on the first of the month, that the word of Jehovah came to me, saying:note
Y sucedió en el año veintisiete, en el mes primero, en el primero del mes, que vino a mí la palabra de Jehová, diciendo:

«Hijo de hombre, Nabucodonosor, rey de Babilonia, hizo que su ejército prestara un gran servicio contra Tiro; toda cabeza quedó calva, y todo hombro quedó pelado; pero no hubo salario de Tiro para él ni para su ejército por el servicio que prestó contra ella.

Por tanto, así dice el Señor Jehová: Mira, voy a dar a Nabucodonosor, rey de Babilonia, la tierra de Egipto; y él se llevará su multitud, y tomará su despojo y arrebatará su botín; y será el salario para su ejército.

Translator’s Notes

Verses 1–2 · Ezekiel 29:1–2 against Pharaoh and all Egypt

⭐ The date. The tenth year, tenth month, twelfth day. Chapter 26 was dated in the eleventh year, so the book does not run strictly by date. The Douay has “the eleventh day of the month”.

⭐ Against. ‘Set your face’ is said as of Sidon (28:21), here ‘against’ Pharaoh. The king is not named.

Verses 3–5 · Ezekiel 29:3–5 the sea-beast in the Nile

⚠ The beast. The word is spelled tannim, like ‘jackals’, and taken as tannin: the ‘great sea-beasts’ of Genesis 1:21, the serpent of Exodus 7:9. The KJV has “the great dragon”, the NIV “you great monster”.

⭐ Canals and Nile. Yeor is the Nile, and its plural is the ‘canals’ of Exodus 7:19. The KJV has “rivers”.

⚠ I have made myself. The verb carries the ending ‘me’: so the Douay, “I made myself”. The KJV reads “I have made it for myself”. In verse 9 the boast is shorter: the Hebrew has only ‘I made’, and ‘it’ is supplied.

⭐ Hooks. As the lion’s cub was led to Egypt with hooks (19:4). Here the hooks are in Pharaoh’s jaws, and he is drawn out with the fish clinging to him. ‘Hooks’ is spelled one way and read another (ketiv/qere).

⭐ Food. Food for birds and beasts, as the curse of Deuteronomy 28:26 says of Israel. ‘Beasts of the land’ as at 34:28.

Verses 6–7 · Ezekiel 29:6–7 a staff of reed

⭐ A staff of reed. The reed is the word printed ‘cane’ at 27:19; the staff is the one Elisha gives to Gehazi (2 Kings 4:29). An Assyrian officer calls Egypt a crushed reed, with the root of ‘be crushed’ in verse 7 (2 Kings 18:21; Isaiah 36:6). Judah’s king had sent to Egypt for horses and people (17:15).

⚠ You, they. In verses 3 to 5 and 7 the king is ‘you’; verse 6 says ‘they’, the people of Egypt.

⚠ Verse 7. ‘By the hand’ is written one way and read another. ‘Crush’ and ‘break’ are two verbs. ‘Make all their loins stand’ is what is written; the KJV keeps “madest all their loins to be at a stand”, while the NIV has “their backs were wrenched”.

Verses 8–12 · Ezekiel 29:8–12 forty years

⭐ A sword. ‘You’ is feminine in verse 8 and masculine in verse 10, which has ‘to you’ where verse 3 has ‘on you’; both are rendered ‘against’. ‘Man and beast’ as at 14:17.

⭐ From Migdol to Syene. The words stand in two verses of the Hebrew Bible: here and 30:6. The pair is usually taken to mark the land from end to end, and the verse goes on to the border of Cush. ‘Drought’ is the word printed so at Haggai 1:11; it follows ‘ruins’ and sounds like it. The KJV and Douay read “the tower of Syene”.

⭐ Forty years. The number of the wilderness (Numbers 14:33) and of the forty days, a day for a year, that Ezekiel lay for Judah (4:6). The chapter says no foot will pass; it does not say when.

⭐ Scatter and disperse. The pair said of Israel (20:23; 22:15) is now said of Egypt.

Verses 13–16 · Ezekiel 29:13–16 a low kingdom

⭐ Gathered again. Egypt too is given a return: ‘turn the captivity’, as promised even to Sodom (16:53), the idiom of shevut.

⭐ Pathros. A part of Egypt; the NIV says “Upper Egypt”. The name Patros stands in five verses of the Hebrew Bible. ‘Their origins’ as of Jerusalem at 16:3.

⭐ A low kingdom. The words stand in two verses of the Hebrew Bible: here and 17:14, where Judah was to be low and not lift itself up.

⭐ Remembrance. ‘Bringing iniquity to remembrance’ in these words stands in two verses: 21:28 and here. Egypt will no longer be a trust that reminds of it.

⚠ After them. ‘When they turn after them’ does not name either ‘they’ or ‘them’.

Verses 17–20 · Ezekiel 29:17–20 wages for Nebuchadrezzar

⭐ A later word. The twenty-seventh year, first month, first day: placed here beside the words on Egypt.

⭐ Service without wages. The army that was to come against Tyre (26:7) served hard and got nothing from it. ‘Rubbed bare’ is the word printed ‘polished’ of the sword at 21:14. The verse does not say what became of Tyre.

⭐ Multitude, spoil, plunder. ‘Spoil’ and ‘plunder’ have the roots of the two verbs at 26:12; the NIV reads ‘multitude’ as wealth. Wages for an army.

⭐ Recompense. Peullah, what is paid for work, printed ‘wages’ at Leviticus 19:13 and ‘recompense’ at Isaiah 40:10.

⚠ They worked for me. The verse turns from ‘he’ to ‘they’.

Verse 21 · Ezekiel 29:21 a horn for the house of Israel

⭐ A horn. Qeren. ‘I will make a horn sprout’ stands in two verses of the Hebrew Bible: here and Psalm 132:17, of David.

⭐ An opening of the mouth. The phrase stands in two verses of the Hebrew Bible: here, and 16:63, where Jerusalem is to open her mouth no more. ‘To you’ is not explained.

Patterns worth carrying forward

Where this translation keeps the exact / distinct words: “canals” at 3, 4, 5 and 10; “forty years” at 11, 12 and 13; “low” at 14 and 15; and “wages” at 18 and 19.

Echoes paid. The hooks of 19:4; the low kingdom of 17:14; the siege of Tyre of 26:7; the closed mouth of 16:63.

Echoes planted. Migdol to Syene again at 30:6; the sea-beast again at 32:2.
