The same letter, written from Macedonia. Paul and his co-workers urge the Corinthians not to receive God’s grace in vain, and quote Isaiah on the acceptable time. He lists the hardships by which the ministry is commended, and opens his heart to them. He sets righteousness against lawlessness and Christ against Belial, and quotes the promise that God will dwell among his people.
Undated by the text: the chapter names no year. It continues the letter of chapters 2 to 5 without a break.

2 Corinthians 6 — The Mister Translation

1
Συνεργοῦντες δὲ καὶ παρακαλοῦμεν μὴ εἰς κενὸν τὴν χάριν τοῦ θεοῦ δέξασθαι ὑμᾶς·
Working together with him, we also urge you not to accept the grace of God in vain.note
Como colaboradores de él, también les suplicamos que no acepten la bondad inmerecida de Dios y luego pierdan de vista su propósito.
2
λέγει γάρ· Καιρῷ δεκτῷ ἐπήκουσά σου καὶ ἐν ἡμέρᾳ σωτηρίας ἐβοήθησά σοι· ἰδοὺ νῦν καιρὸς εὐπρόσδεκτος, ἰδοὺ νῦν ἡμέρα σωτηρίας·
For he says: “In an acceptable time I heard you, and in a day of salvation I helped you.” Look! Now is the especially acceptable time; look! now is the day of salvation.note
Porque él dice: «Te oí en un tiempo favorable y te ayudé en un día de salvación». ¡Ahora es el tiempo especialmente favorable! ¡Ahora es el día de salvación!
3
μηδεμίαν ἐν μηδενὶ διδόντες προσκοπήν, ἵνα μὴ μωμηθῇ ἡ διακονία,
We give no cause for stumbling in anything, so that the ministry is not blamed.note
No damos motivo de tropiezo en nada, para que no se encuentre falta en nuestro ministerio.
4
ἀλλʼ ἐν παντὶ συνιστάνοντες ἑαυτοὺς ὡς θεοῦ διάκονοι· ἐν ὑπομονῇ πολλῇ, ἐν θλίψεσιν, ἐν ἀνάγκαις, ἐν στενοχωρίαις,
But in every way we recommend ourselves as God’s ministers: in much endurance, in tribulations, in needs, in difficulties,note
Más bien, nos recomendamos como ministros de Dios en todo lo que hacemos: aguantando muchas pruebas, sufrimientos, momentos de necesidad, dificultades,
5
ἐν πληγαῖς, ἐν φυλακαῖς, ἐν ἀκαταστασίαις, ἐν κόποις, ἐν ἀγρυπνίαις, ἐν νηστείαις,
in beatings, in imprisonments, in riots, in hard work, in sleepless nights, in times without food;note
golpes, prisiones, revueltas, trabajos pesados, noches sin dormir, ocasiones sin comer;
6
ἐν ἁγνότητι, ἐν γνώσει, ἐν μακροθυμίᾳ, ἐν χρηστότητι, ἐν πνεύματι ἁγίῳ, ἐν ἀγάπῃ ἀνυποκρίτῳ,
in purity, in knowledge, in patience, in kindness, in the holy spirit, in love free from hypocrisy,note
viviendo con pureza, comportándonos con conocimiento, siendo pacientes y bondadosos, dejándonos guiar por el espíritu santo, demostrando amor sin ser hipócritas,
7
ἐν λόγῳ ἀληθείας, ἐν δυνάμει θεοῦ· διὰ τῶν ὅπλων τῆς δικαιοσύνης τῶν δεξιῶν καὶ ἀριστερῶν,
in the word of truth, in God’s power; through the weapons of righteousness in the right hand and in the left,note
diciendo la verdad, confiando en el poder de Dios, sosteniendo las armas de la justicia con la derecha y con la izquierda;
8
διὰ δόξης καὶ ἀτιμίας, διὰ δυσφημίας καὶ εὐφημίας· ὡς πλάνοι καὶ ἀληθεῖς,
through glory and dishonor, through bad report and good report. We are regarded as deceivers, and yet we are truthful;note
cuando nos alaban o nos deshonran, cuando tenemos buena fama o mala fama. Nos ven como unos farsantes, aunque decimos la verdad;
9
ὡς ἀγνοούμενοι καὶ ἐπιγινωσκόμενοι, ὡς ἀποθνῄσκοντες καὶ ἰδοὺ ζῶμεν, ὡς παιδευόμενοι καὶ μὴ θανατούμενοι,
as unknown, and yet we are recognized; as dying, and yet look! we live; as punished, and yet not handed over to death;note
como unos desconocidos, aunque se nos conoce bien; como si estuviéramos muriendo, aunque estamos vivos; como si nos hubieran castigado, aunque no hemos sido entregados a la muerte;
10
ὡς λυπούμενοι ἀεὶ δὲ χαίροντες, ὡς πτωχοὶ πολλοὺς δὲ πλουτίζοντες, ὡς μηδὲν ἔχοντες καὶ πάντα κατέχοντες.
as sorrowing but always rejoicing, as poor but making many rich, as having nothing and yet possessing all things.note
como si estuviéramos tristes, aunque siempre estamos felices; como si fuéramos pobres, aunque hacemos ricos a muchos; como si no tuviéramos nada, aunque lo tenemos todo.
11
Τὸ στόμα ἡμῶν ἀνέῳγεν πρὸς ὑμᾶς, Κορίνθιοι, ἡ καρδία ἡμῶν πεπλάτυνται·
We have opened our mouth to you, Corinthians, and our heart has opened wide.note
Corintios, hemos abierto la boca para hablarles y les hemos abierto de par en par nuestro corazón.
12
οὐ στενοχωρεῖσθε ἐν ἡμῖν, στενοχωρεῖσθε δὲ ἐν τοῖς σπλάγχνοις ὑμῶν·
You are not restricted in us, but you are restricted in your own affections.note
Nosotros no le hemos puesto límites a nuestro cariño por ustedes, pero ustedes sí le han puesto límites a su tierno cariño por nosotros.
13
τὴν δὲ αὐτὴν ἀντιμισθίαν, ὡς τέκνοις λέγω, πλατύνθητε καὶ ὑμεῖς.
Now in return, I speak as to my children: open wide your hearts too.note
Así que, les hablo como si fueran mis hijos: correspondan a nuestro amor y también abran de par en par sus corazones.
14
Μὴ γίνεσθε ἑτεροζυγοῦντες ἀπίστοις· τίς γὰρ μετοχὴ δικαιοσύνῃ καὶ ἀνομίᾳ, ἢ τίς κοινωνία φωτὶ πρὸς σκότος;
Do not become unevenly yoked with unbelievers. For what fellowship do righteousness and lawlessness have? Or what sharing does light have with darkness?note
No se pongan bajo un yugo desigual con los no creyentes. Porque ¿qué relación tienen la justicia y la maldad? ¿O qué tienen en común la luz y la oscuridad?
15
τίς δὲ συμφώνησις Χριστοῦ πρὸς Βελιάρ, ἢ τίς μερὶς πιστῷ μετὰ ἀπίστου;
What harmony is there between Christ and Belial? Or what does a believer share with an unbeliever?note
Además, ¿qué armonía hay entre Cristo y Belial? ¿O qué tienen en común un creyente y un no creyente?
16
τίς δὲ συγκατάθεσις ναῷ θεοῦ μετὰ εἰδώλων; ἡμεῖς γὰρ ναὸς θεοῦ ἐσμεν ζῶντος· καθὼς εἶπεν ὁ θεὸς ὅτι Ἐνοικήσω ἐν αὐτοῖς καὶ ἐμπεριπατήσω, καὶ ἔσομαι αὐτῶν θεός, καὶ αὐτοὶ ἔσονταί μου λαός.
And what agreement does the temple of God have with idols? For we are a temple of the living God; just as God said: “I will dwell among them and walk among them, and I will be their God, and they will be my people.”note
¿Y qué acuerdo hay entre el templo de Dios y los ídolos? Porque nosotros somos el templo de un Dios vivo. Tal como dijo Dios: «Yo residiré entre ellos y andaré entre ellos, y yo seré su Dios, y ellos serán mi pueblo».
17
διὸ ἐξέλθατε ἐκ μέσου αὐτῶν, καὶ ἀφορίσθητε, λέγει κύριος, καὶ ἀκαθάρτου μὴ ἅπτεσθε· κἀγὼ εἰσδέξομαι ὑμᾶς·
“Therefore, come out from among them and separate yourselves,” says Jehovah, “and stop touching the unclean thing”; “and I will receive you.”note
«Por eso sálganse de entre ellos y sepárense —dice Jehová— y dejen de tocar la cosa impura»; «y yo los recibiré».

18
καὶ ἔσομαι ὑμῖν εἰς πατέρα, καὶ ὑμεῖς ἔσεσθέ μοι εἰς υἱοὺς καὶ θυγατέρας, λέγει κύριος παντοκράτωρ.
“And I will become a father to you, and you will become sons and daughters to me,” says Jehovah the Almighty.note
«Y yo seré un padre para ustedes, y ustedes serán hijos e hijas para mí», dice Jehová, el Todopoderoso.
Verses 1–2 · not in vain, and the acceptable time now
“Working together with him.” synergountes, working together, the participle that opens the sentence and makes the appeal a joint one with God; the verb that follows, parakaloumen, is the verb of appeal that runs through this letter. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “Working together with him, we also urge you not to accept the undeserved kindness of God and miss its purpose.” The KJV “We then, as workers together with him, beseech you also that ye receive not the grace of God in vain.”

“An acceptable time.” Paul quotes the word of Isaiah 49:8 in the Septuagint form, and puts it in the present: idou nyn kairos euprosdektos, look, now is the especially acceptable time. The compound with eu-, well received, is stronger than the plain word in the quotation. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “In an acceptable time I heard you, and in a day of salvation I helped you.” The ASV “At an acceptable time I hearkened unto thee.”

Verses 3–7 · no cause for stumbling, the list of hardships, and the weapons on the right and the left
“In every way, in much endurance.” en pantì ... en hypomonē pollē. From verse 4 on, the sentence is a list of nouns after en, each in the dative: endurance, tribulations, needs, difficulties, beatings, prisons, riots, hard work, sleepless nights, fasting, purity, knowledge, patience, kindness, the holy spirit, love unfeigned, the word of truth, God’s power. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “but in every way we recommend ourselves as God’s ministers, by the endurance of much, by tribulations, by times of need, by difficulties.” The KJV “But in all things approving ourselves as the ministers of God, in much patience, in afflictions, in necessities, in distresses.”

“Through the weapons of righteousness.” dia tōn hoplōn tēs dikaiosynēs, through the weapons of righteousness, and tōn dexiōn kai aristerōn, of the right and of the left, which is a way of saying both hands. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “through the weapons of righteousness in the right hand and in the left.” The ASV “by the armor of righteousness on the right hand and on the left.”

Verses 8–10 · glory and dishonor, the deceivers who are true, and dying yet living
“Through glory and dishonor.” dia doxēs kai atimias, through glory and dishonor. The pairs in verses 8 to 10 are set one against the other: each has a hard half and a true half, joined by all’, but, which turns the sentence. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “We are regarded as deceivers and yet we are truthful.”

“As dying, and yet we live.” hōs apothnēskontes kai idou zōmen, as dying, and look, we live. The participle is present, and it describes a state that goes on. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “as dying and yet look! we live.” The ASV “as dying, and behold, we live.”

Verses 11–13 · the open mouth and the enlarged heart, and the restricted affections
“Our mouth is open.” to stoma hēmōn aneōgen, our mouth has opened, and hē kardia hēmōn peplatynthai, our heart has been enlarged. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “We have opened our mouth to speak to you, Corinthians, and we have opened wide our heart.” The KJV “O ye Corinthians, our mouth is open unto you, our heart is enlarged.”

“Restricted in your own affections.” splanchnois, the inward parts, the seat of tender feeling, and stenochōreō, to be cramped or restricted. The sentence says the Corinthians have made the room narrow, not Paul. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “you are restricted in your own tender affections for us.” The KJV “ye are straitened in your own affections.”

Verses 14–16 · unevenly yoked, Belial, and the temple of the living God
“Unevenly yoked.” heterozygountes, yoked with what is of another kind; the picture is of two animals of unlike kind under one yoke. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “Do not become unevenly yoked with unbelievers.” The KJV “Be ye not unequally yoked together with unbelievers.”

“Belial.” Beliar, the name for a lawless power in later Jewish writing, set against Christ. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “what harmony is there between Christ and Beʹli·al?” The KJV “what concord hath Christ with Belial?”

“The temple of the living God.” hēmeis gar naos theou esmen zōntos, for we are a temple of the living God, and the sentence strings together words of God from the Scriptures about dwelling among his people. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “for we are a temple of a living God; just as God said.” The KJV “for ye are the temple of the living God; as God hath said, I will dwell in them.”

Verses 17–18 · come out and be separate, and a father to you
“Get out from among them.” exelthate ek mesou autōn kai aphoristhēte, come out from among them and be separated; the Greek says legei kyrios, says the Lord, and the NWT renders it “says Jehovah,” because the words are the God of Israel’s. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “Therefore, get out from among them, and separate yourselves,” says Jehovah. The KJV “Wherefore come out from among them, and be ye separate, saith the Lord.”

“A father to you.” kai estai hymin eis patera, and I will be to you a father, and the Greek ends with kyrios pantokratōr, the Lord almighty, which the NWT renders “Jehovah, the Almighty.” The shelf: the NWT 2013 “And I will become a father to you, and you will become sons and daughters to me.” The KJV “ye shall be my sons and daughters, saith the Lord Almighty.”

Patterns worth carrying forward

Where this translation keeps the exact words: “weapons” for hoplōn at 6:7; “temple” for naos at 6:16; “Belial” kept as the transliterated name at 6:15; “judgment seat” left to the previous chapter.

Echoes paid. The ministry of 6:3–4 returns from 3:6 and 4:1; the temple of 6:16 answers the building of 1 Corinthians 3:16; the mouth opened of 6:11 returns the heart of 3:2 and 3:3.

Echoes planted. The separation of 6:17 is the first of the commands the chapter closes on; the grace refused in vain (6:1) is the warning the next chapter takes up. Both carry on in the next chapter of this book.
