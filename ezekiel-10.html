Within the same vision: the man in linen is told to take coals of fire from among the cherubim to throw over the city; the cloud fills the house; the wheels and the four faces of chapter 1 are seen again, and the prophet knows the living creatures to be cherubim; the glory leaves the threshold and is over them at the east gate.
Continues the vision of chapter 8, dated there to the sixth year, sixth month, fifth day.

Ezekiel 10 — The Mister Translation

💬 Discuss this chapter
1
וָאֶרְאֶה, וְהִנֵּה אֶל-הָרָקִיעַ אֲשֶׁר עַל-רֹאשׁ הַכְּרֻבִים, כְּאֶבֶן סַפִּיר, כְּמַרְאֵה דְּמוּת כִּסֵּא--נִרְאָה, עֲלֵיהֶם.
And I looked, and here—upon the vault that was over the head of the cherubim there appeared above them something like a sapphire stone, like the appearance of the likeness of a throne.note
Y miré, y he aquí: sobre la bóveda que estaba sobre la cabeza de los querubines apareció sobre ellos algo como una piedra de zafiro, como la apariencia de la semejanza de un trono.
2
וַיֹּאמֶר אֶל-הָאִישׁ לְבֻשׁ הַבַּדִּים, וַיֹּאמֶר בֹּא אֶל-בֵּינוֹת לַגַּלְגַּל אֶל-תַּחַת לַכְּרוּב וּמַלֵּא חָפְנֶיךָ גַחֲלֵי-אֵשׁ מִבֵּינוֹת לַכְּרֻבִים, וּזְרֹק, עַל-הָעִיר; וַיָּבֹא, לְעֵינָי.
And he said to the man clothed in linen, and said, “Go in between the wheelwork, under the cherub, and fill your two hands with coals of fire from between the cherubim, and throw them over the city.” And he went in before my eyes.note
Y dijo al hombre vestido de lino, y dijo: «Entra entre el rodaje, debajo del querubín, y llena tus dos manos de carbones de fuego de entre los querubines, y arrójalos sobre la ciudad». Y él entró ante mis ojos.
3
וְהַכְּרֻבִים, עֹמְדִים מִימִין לַבַּיִת--בְּבֹאוֹ הָאִישׁ; וְהֶעָנָן מָלֵא, אֶת-הֶחָצֵר הַפְּנִימִית.
And the cherubim were standing on the right side of the house when the man went in, and the cloud filled the inner court.note
Y los querubines estaban de pie al lado derecho de la casa cuando entró el hombre, y la nube llenaba el atrio interior.
4
וַיָּרָם כְּבוֹד-יְהוָה מֵעַל הַכְּרוּב, עַל מִפְתַּן הַבָּיִת; וַיִּמָּלֵא הַבַּיִת, אֶת-הֶעָנָן, וְהֶחָצֵר מָלְאָה, אֶת-נֹגַהּ כְּבוֹד יְהוָה.
And the glory of Jehovah rose from the cherub to the threshold of the house; and the house was filled with the cloud, and the court was full of the brightness of the glory of Jehovah.note
Y la gloria de Jehová se levantó del querubín al umbral de la casa; y la casa se llenó de la nube, y el atrio estaba lleno del resplandor de la gloria de Jehová.
5
וְקוֹל, כַּנְפֵי הַכְּרוּבִים--נִשְׁמַע, עַד-הֶחָצֵר הַחִיצֹנָה:  כְּקוֹל אֵל-שַׁדַּי, בְּדַבְּרוֹ.
And the sound of the wings of the cherubim was heard as far as the outer court, like the voice of El Shaddai when he speaks.note
Y el sonido de las alas de los querubines se oía hasta el atrio exterior, como la voz de El Shadday cuando habla.
6
וַיְהִי, בְּצַוֺּתוֹ אֶת-הָאִישׁ לְבֻשׁ-הַבַּדִּים לֵאמֹר, קַח אֵשׁ מִבֵּינוֹת לַגַּלְגַּל, מִבֵּינוֹת לַכְּרוּבִים; וַיָּבֹא, וַיַּעֲמֹד, אֵצֶל, הָאוֹפָן.
And it happened, when he commanded the man clothed in linen, saying, “Take fire from between the wheelwork, from between the cherubim,” that he went in and stood beside the wheel.note
Y sucedió que, cuando mandó al hombre vestido de lino, diciendo: «Toma fuego de entre el rodaje, de entre los querubines», él entró y se puso junto a la rueda.
7
וַיִּשְׁלַח הַכְּרוּב אֶת-יָדוֹ מִבֵּינוֹת לַכְּרוּבִים, אֶל-הָאֵשׁ אֲשֶׁר בֵּינוֹת הַכְּרֻבִים, וַיִּשָּׂא וַיִּתֵּן, אֶל-חָפְנֵי לְבֻשׁ הַבַּדִּים; וַיִּקַּח, וַיֵּצֵא.
And the cherub put out his hand from between the cherubim to the fire that was between the cherubim, and lifted some and put it into the hands of the one clothed in linen, who took it and went out.note
Y el querubín extendió su mano de entre los querubines al fuego que estaba entre los querubines, y alzó de él y lo puso en las manos del vestido de lino, el cual lo tomó y salió.
8
וַיֵּרָא, לַכְּרֻבִים--תַּבְנִית, יַד-אָדָם, תַּחַת, כַּנְפֵיהֶם.
And there appeared on the cherubim the form of a human hand under their wings.note
Y apareció en los querubines la forma de una mano de ser humano debajo de sus alas.
9
וָאֶרְאֶה, וְהִנֵּה אַרְבָּעָה אוֹפַנִּים אֵצֶל הַכְּרוּבִים--אוֹפַן אֶחָד אֵצֶל הַכְּרוּב אֶחָד, וְאוֹפַן אֶחָד אֵצֶל הַכְּרוּב אֶחָד; וּמַרְאֵה, הָאוֹפַנִּים, כְּעֵין, אֶבֶן תַּרְשִׁישׁ.
And I looked, and here—four wheels beside the cherubim, one wheel beside one cherub and one wheel beside another cherub; and the appearance of the wheels was like the gleam of a beryl stone.note
Y miré, y he aquí: cuatro ruedas junto a los querubines, una rueda junto a un querubín y una rueda junto a otro querubín; y la apariencia de las ruedas era como el destello de una piedra de berilo.
10
וּמַרְאֵיהֶם--דְּמוּת אֶחָד, לְאַרְבַּעְתָּם:  כַּאֲשֶׁר יִהְיֶה הָאוֹפַן, בְּתוֹךְ הָאוֹפָן.
And as for their appearance, the four of them had one likeness, as though a wheel were within a wheel.note
Y en cuanto a su apariencia, las cuatro tenían una misma semejanza, como si una rueda estuviera dentro de otra rueda.
11
בְּלֶכְתָּם, אֶל-אַרְבַּעַת רִבְעֵיהֶם יֵלֵכוּ--לֹא יִסַּבּוּ, בְּלֶכְתָּם:  כִּי הַמָּקוֹם אֲשֶׁר-יִפְנֶה הָרֹאשׁ, אַחֲרָיו יֵלֵכוּ--לֹא יִסַּבּוּ, בְּלֶכְתָּם.
When they went, they went on their four sides; they did not turn as they went, for to the place where the head faced they went after it; they did not turn as they went.note
Cuando andaban, andaban sobre sus cuatro costados; no se volvían cuando andaban, porque al lugar adonde miraba la cabeza, iban tras ella; no se volvían cuando andaban.
12
וְכָל-בְּשָׂרָם, וְגַבֵּהֶם, וִידֵיהֶם, וְכַנְפֵיהֶם--וְהָאוֹפַנִּים, מְלֵאִים עֵינַיִם סָבִיב, לְאַרְבַּעְתָּם, אוֹפַנֵּיהֶם.
And all their flesh, and their backs, and their hands, and their wings, and the wheels, were full of eyes all around—the four of them had their wheels.note
Y toda su carne, y sus espaldas, y sus manos, y sus alas, y las ruedas, estaban llenas de ojos alrededor: los cuatro tenían sus ruedas.
13
לָאוֹפַנִּים--לָהֶם קוֹרָא הַגַּלְגַּל, בְּאָזְנָי.
As for the wheels, to them it was called in my ears, “The wheelwork.”note
En cuanto a las ruedas, a ellas se les llamó en mis oídos: «El rodaje».
14
וְאַרְבָּעָה פָנִים, לְאֶחָד:  פְּנֵי הָאֶחָד פְּנֵי הַכְּרוּב, וּפְנֵי הַשֵּׁנִי פְּנֵי אָדָם, וְהַשְּׁלִישִׁי פְּנֵי אַרְיֵה, וְהָרְבִיעִי פְּנֵי-נָשֶׁר.
And each had four faces: the face of the one was the face of the cherub, and the face of the second was the face of a human, and the third the face of a lion, and the fourth the face of an eagle.note
Y cada uno tenía cuatro rostros: el rostro del uno era el rostro del querubín, y el rostro del segundo era rostro de ser humano, y el tercero rostro de león, y el cuarto rostro de águila.
15
וַיֵּרֹמּוּ, הַכְּרוּבִים--הִיא הַחַיָּה, אֲשֶׁר רָאִיתִי בִּנְהַר-כְּבָר.
And the cherubim rose up; that is the living creature that I saw by the river Chebar.note
Y los querubines se levantaron; ese es el ser viviente que vi junto al río Quebar.
16
וּבְלֶכֶת, הַכְּרוּבִים, יֵלְכוּ הָאוֹפַנִּים, אֶצְלָם; וּבִשְׂאֵת הַכְּרוּבִים אֶת-כַּנְפֵיהֶם, לָרוּם מֵעַל הָאָרֶץ, לֹא-יִסַּבּוּ הָאוֹפַנִּים גַּם-הֵם, מֵאֶצְלָם.
And when the cherubim went, the wheels went beside them; and when the cherubim lifted their wings to rise from the earth, the wheels, they too, did not turn from beside them.note
Y cuando andaban los querubines, andaban las ruedas junto a ellos; y cuando los querubines alzaban sus alas para levantarse de la tierra, las ruedas, también ellas, no se volvían de su lado.
17
בְּעָמְדָם יַעֲמֹדוּ, וּבְרוֹמָם יֵרוֹמּוּ אוֹתָם:  כִּי רוּחַ הַחַיָּה, בָּהֶם.
When they stood, these stood; and when they rose, these rose with them, for the spirit of the living creature was in them.note
Cuando ellos se detenían, estas se detenían; y cuando ellos se levantaban, estas se levantaban con ellos, porque el espíritu del ser viviente estaba en ellas.
18
וַיֵּצֵא כְּבוֹד יְהוָה, מֵעַל מִפְתַּן הַבָּיִת; וַיַּעֲמֹד, עַל-הַכְּרוּבִים.
And the glory of Jehovah went out from over the threshold of the house and stood over the cherubim.note
Y la gloria de Jehová salió de sobre el umbral de la casa y se puso sobre los querubines.
19
וַיִּשְׂאוּ הַכְּרוּבִים אֶת-כַּנְפֵיהֶם וַיֵּרוֹמּוּ מִן-הָאָרֶץ לְעֵינַי, בְּצֵאתָם, וְהָאוֹפַנִּים, לְעֻמָּתָם; וַיַּעֲמֹד, פֶּתַח שַׁעַר בֵּית-יְהוָה הַקַּדְמוֹנִי, וּכְבוֹד אֱלֹהֵי-יִשְׂרָאֵל עֲלֵיהֶם, מִלְמָעְלָה.
And the cherubim lifted their wings and rose from the earth before my eyes when they went out, and the wheels alongside them; and it stood at the entrance of the east gate of the house of Jehovah, and the glory of the God of Israel was over them above.note
Y los querubines alzaron sus alas y se levantaron de la tierra ante mis ojos cuando salieron, y las ruedas a la par de ellos; y se puso a la entrada de la puerta oriental de la casa de Jehová, y la gloria del Dios de Israel estaba sobre ellos por encima.
20
הִיא הַחַיָּה, אֲשֶׁר רָאִיתִי תַּחַת אֱלֹהֵי-יִשְׂרָאֵל--בִּנְהַר-כְּבָר; וָאֵדַע, כִּי כְרוּבִים הֵמָּה.
That is the living creature that I saw under the God of Israel by the river Chebar; and I knew that they were cherubim.note
Ese es el ser viviente que vi debajo del Dios de Israel junto al río Quebar; y supe que eran querubines.
21
אַרְבָּעָה אַרְבָּעָה פָנִים לְאֶחָד, וְאַרְבַּע כְּנָפַיִם לְאֶחָד; וּדְמוּת יְדֵי אָדָם, תַּחַת כַּנְפֵיהֶם.
Each had four faces apiece, and each four wings, and the likeness of human hands was under their wings.note
Cada uno tenía cuatro rostros, y cada uno cuatro alas, y la semejanza de manos de ser humano había debajo de sus alas.
22
וּדְמוּת פְּנֵיהֶם--הֵמָּה הַפָּנִים אֲשֶׁר רָאִיתִי עַל-נְהַר-כְּבָר, מַרְאֵיהֶם וְאוֹתָם; אִישׁ אֶל-עֵבֶר פָּנָיו, יֵלֵכוּ.
And the likeness of their faces—they were the faces that I saw by the river Chebar, their appearance and themselves; each went straight ahead.note
Y la semejanza de sus rostros: eran los rostros que vi junto al río Quebar, su apariencia y ellos mismos; cada uno andaba de frente.

Translator’s Notes

Verses 1–2 · Ezekiel 10:1–2 the throne above the vault — coals of fire for the city

⭐ The vault and the throne again. The sight is that of 1:26: a vault, a sapphire stone, the likeness of a throne. “Sapphire stone” (even sappir) stands in two verses of the Hebrew Bible, there and here. Here no figure on the throne is described.

⚠ Who speaks. The verse reads ‘and he said to the man clothed in linen, and said’, twice, and does not name the speaker. The NIV and the TLB supply “the Lord”.

⭐ Coals of fire. Gachalei esh and the two handfuls are the words of the Day of Atonement, when a censer of coals of fire and two handfuls of incense are carried inside the veil (Leviticus 16:12, now on these pages, which prints ‘burning coals’ for the same Hebrew). Here the handfuls are of coals, and they are for the city. Chapter 1 had compared the living creatures to ‘coals of fire’ (1:13). In Isaiah 6:6 a seraph brings a glowing stone from the altar and touches the prophet’s lips with it (6:7), with a different noun.

Verses 3–5 · Ezekiel 10:3–5 the cloud fills the house — the voice of El Shaddai

⭐ The cloud. “And the cloud filled” (vehe’anan male) stands in two verses of the Hebrew Bible: here and 1 Kings 8:10 (now on these pages), at the dedication of Solomon’s temple, when the cloud filled the house and the glory filled it too. At the tent of meeting the cloud covered the tent and the glory filled it (Exodus 40:34). The counted phrase is verse 3’s, of the inner court; verse 4 has the house filled. Isaiah saw a house filled with smoke (6:4).

⚠ Right or south. The cherubim stand mimin, on the right of the house. The ASV has “on the right side of the house” and the NIV “on the south side of the temple”; to one facing east the right is south.

⭐ It rose. The glory rose from the cherub ‘to’ the threshold (al, ‘over’ or ‘upon’; verse 18 has it go out ‘from over the threshold’), vayyarom, a different verb from the ‘had gone up’ of 9:3. The wings sound ‘like the voice of El Shaddai’, the name God speaks to Abram (Genesis 17:1; Shaddai), and 1:24 had likened the sound of the wings to ‘the voice of Shaddai’, without ‘El’. The ASV has “as the voice of God Almighty when he speaketh”.

Verses 6–8 · Ezekiel 10:6–8 fire put into his hands — the form of a hand

⭐ He took it and went out. The man stands beside the wheel; a cherub gives him fire; he takes it and goes out. The chapter does not narrate the scattering over the city.

⚠ The cherub. Verse 7 says the cherub, singular, among the cherubim. The NIV has “one of the cherubim” and the KJV “one cherub”.

⭐ A hand. What appears under the wings is the tavnit, ‘form’, of a hand, the word of 8:3; “the form of a hand” stands in two verses of the Hebrew Bible, those two. Chapter 1 said simply that human hands were under their wings (1:8).

Verses 9–13 · Ezekiel 10:9–13 the four wheels — backs full of eyes — “the wheelwork”

⭐ Wheel within wheel. Beryl, four of one likeness, a wheel within a wheel, no turning: most of these words are chapter 1’s (1:16–17; ofan). “A wheel within a wheel” in this form stands in two verses, there and here. The wheels are ofannim; galgal, ‘wheelwork’, is a second word, kept apart.

⚠ The head. They follow ‘the head’ wherever it faces, and the verse does not say whose. The ASV has “the place whither the head looked”, the NIV “whatever direction the head faced” and the Geneva “whither the first went”.

⚠ Backs or rims. The noun of verse 12, gabbehem, is the one chapter 1 uses of the wheels’ ‘rims’ full of eyes (1:18). Here it stands in a list with flesh, hands and wings, and this translation prints ‘backs’, as the ASV does (“their whole body, and their backs”); the Geneva has “their rings” here.

⚠ A name or a cry. Verse 13 says that to the wheels ‘it was called’ haggalgal. Some take it as a name, some as a cry: the ASV has “they were called in my hearing the whirling wheels”, the KJV “it was cried unto them in my hearing, O wheel” and the Douay “these wheels he called voluble”.

Verses 14–17 · Ezekiel 10:14–17 four faces — a cherub’s face and no ox — one spirit

⚠ The face of the cherub. Here the chapter differs from chapter 1. There the four faces were human, lion, ox and eagle (1:10); here they are the cherub, a human, a lion and an eagle. The ox is not named, a cherub’s face is, and the order changes: the cherub’s face comes first, where the ox had been third. The text does not explain, and this translation does not. The TLB writes “the first was that of an ox”; the ASV keeps “the first face was the face of the cherub”. The four creatures of Revelation 4:7 (now on these pages) are like a lion, a calf, a man and an eagle.

⭐ The living creature. “That is the living creature” stands in two verses, 15 and 20, and the noun is singular for the whole. Verses 16–17 repeat the movement of chapter 1: wheels beside them, and “the spirit of the living creature”, which stands in three verses of the Hebrew Bible, two in chapter 1 and one here.

Verses 18–19 · Ezekiel 10:18–19 the glory leaves the threshold — the east gate

⭐ Out from the threshold. The glory goes out from over the threshold and stands over the cherubim. The cherubim lift their wings and rise, and at the entrance of the east gate of the house the glory of the God of Israel is over them. The chapter ends the movement there; the next step is told in 11:22–23 (now on these pages).

⚠ It stood. The verb of ‘stood’ in verse 19 is singular, though the cherubim are many; the Douay keeps it, “it stood in the entry of the east gate”, and the NIV has “They stopped at the entrance”.

Verses 20–22 · Ezekiel 10:20–22 “I knew that they were cherubim”

⭐ Recognition. Verse 15 had already named the sight as the living creature seen at the Chebar; here the prophet adds ‘I knew’ what the creatures of chapter 1 were: “they were cherubim” (keruvim hemmah) stands in this one verse (keruv). Verse 21 spells out four faces and four wings with human hands under them, and verse 22 says the faces were those seen at the Chebar.

⚠ Their appearance and themselves. The phrase of verse 22 is awkward in Hebrew. The ASV keeps “their appearances and themselves” and the NIV has “Their faces had the same appearance”. “Straight ahead”, el ever panav, stands in three verses, two in chapter 1 and this one.

Patterns worth carrying forward

Where this translation keeps the exact / distinct words: “wheelwork” for galgal at verses 2, 6 and 13, apart from “wheel” for ofan; “did not turn” at verses 11 and 16; “form” for tavnit at verse 8 and “likeness” for demut at verses 1, 10, 21 and 22; and “straight ahead” at verse 22, as in chapter 1.

Echoes paid. The vision of chapter 1 seen again, now named; the cloud of 1 Kings 8; and the coals of Leviticus 16.

Echoes planted. The glory leaving the city to the mountain on the east at 11:22–23 (now on these pages).

Ezekiel 11, now on these pages: spirit carries the prophet to the east gate, where twenty-five men stand, among them Jaazaniah son of Azzur and Pelatiah son of Benaiah.
