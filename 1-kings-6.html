In the fourth year of Solomon’s reign, in the month of Ziv, the house of Jehovah is begun, four hundred and eighty years after the going out of Egypt: sixty cubits long, with a porch, a side-structure of three levels, walls of cedar, a floor of cypress, an inner sanctuary of pure gold with two olive-wood cherubim, carved doors, and an inner court. In the middle of the building the word of Jehovah comes to Solomon: if he walks in the statutes, Jehovah will dwell in the midst of the children of Israel. The foundation is laid in the fourth year; in the eleventh year, in the month of Bul, it is finished, seven years.
Solomon’s fourth to eleventh years, the seven years of the building of the house of Jehovah.

1
וַיְהִי בִשְׁמוֹנִים שָׁנָה וְאַרְבַּע מֵאוֹת שָׁנָה לְצֵאת בְּנֵי-יִשְׂרָאֵל מֵאֶרֶץ-מִצְרַיִם בַּשָּׁנָה הָרְבִיעִית בְּחֹדֶשׁ זִו, הוּא הַחֹדֶשׁ הַשֵּׁנִי, לִמְלֹךְ שְׁלֹמֹה, עַל-יִשְׂרָאֵל; וַיִּבֶן הַבַּיִת, לַיהוָה.
And it was, in the four hundred and eightieth year after the children of Israel went out of the land of Egypt, in the fourth year, in the month of Ziv — it is the second month — of Solomon’s reigning over Israel, that he built the house for Jehovah.note
Y sucedió que en el año cuatrocientos ochenta después que los hijos de Israel salieron de la tierra de Egipto, en el cuarto año, en el mes de Ziv — es el mes segundo — del reinado de Salomón sobre Israel, él edificó la casa para Jehová.
2
וְהַבַּיִת, אֲשֶׁר בָּנָה הַמֶּלֶךְ שְׁלֹמֹה לַיהוָה--שִׁשִּׁים-אַמָּה אָרְכּוֹ, וְעֶשְׂרִים רָחְבּוֹ; וּשְׁלֹשִׁים אַמָּה, קוֹמָתוֹ.
And the house that King Solomon built for Jehovah: its length was sixty cubits, and its width twenty, and its height thirty cubits.note
Y la casa que el rey Salomón edificó para Jehová: su longitud era de sesenta codos, y su anchura de veinte, y su altura de treinta codos.
3
וְהָאוּלָם, עַל-פְּנֵי הֵיכַל הַבַּיִת--עֶשְׂרִים אַמָּה אָרְכּוֹ, עַל-פְּנֵי רֹחַב הַבָּיִת; עֶשֶׂר בָּאַמָּה רָחְבּוֹ, עַל-פְּנֵי הַבָּיִת.
And the porch in front of the temple of the house: its length was twenty cubits, across the width of the house; ten cubits was its width, in front of the house.note
Y el pórtico delante del templo de la casa: su longitud era de veinte codos, a lo ancho de la casa; diez codos era su anchura, delante de la casa.
4
וַיַּעַשׂ לַבָּיִת, חַלּוֹנֵי שְׁקֻפִים אֲטוּמִים.
And he made for the house windows with narrowed frames.note
E hizo para la casa ventanas de marcos estrechados.
5
וַיִּבֶן עַל-קִיר הַבַּיִת יצוע יָצִיעַ, סָבִיב, אֶת-קִירוֹת הַבַּיִת סָבִיב, לַהֵיכָל וְלַדְּבִיר; וַיַּעַשׂ צְלָעוֹת, סָבִיב.
And against the wall of the house he built a side-structure all around — against the walls of the house all around, around the temple and the inner sanctuary — and he made side rooms all around.note
Y edificó contra la pared de la casa una estructura lateral alrededor — contra las paredes de la casa alrededor, en torno al templo y al santuario interior — e hizo cámaras laterales alrededor.
6
היצוע הַיָּצִיעַ הַתַּחְתֹּנָה חָמֵשׁ בָּאַמָּה רָחְבָּהּ, וְהַתִּיכֹנָה שֵׁשׁ בָּאַמָּה רָחְבָּהּ, וְהַשְּׁלִישִׁית, שֶׁבַע בָּאַמָּה רָחְבָּהּ:  כִּי מִגְרָעוֹת נָתַן לַבַּיִת סָבִיב, חוּצָה, לְבִלְתִּי, אֲחֹז בְּקִירוֹת-הַבָּיִת.
The lowest level of the side-structure was five cubits wide, and the middle one six cubits wide, and the third seven cubits wide; for he made ledges for the house all around on the outside, so that nothing would take hold in the walls of the house.note
El nivel más bajo de la estructura lateral era de cinco codos de ancho, y el de en medio de seis codos de ancho, y el tercero de siete codos de ancho; porque hizo salientes para la casa alrededor, por fuera, para que nada se sujetara en las paredes de la casa.
7
וְהַבַּיִת, בְּהִבָּנֹתוֹ--אֶבֶן-שְׁלֵמָה מַסָּע, נִבְנָה; וּמַקָּבוֹת וְהַגַּרְזֶן כָּל-כְּלִי בַרְזֶל, לֹא-נִשְׁמַע בַּבַּיִת בְּהִבָּנֹתוֹ.
And the house, when it was being built, was built of whole stone from the quarry; and hammers and the axe — any tool of iron — were not heard in the house while it was being built.note
Y la casa, cuando se edificaba, fue edificada con piedra entera de cantera; y martillos y hacha — cualquier herramienta de hierro — no se oyeron en la casa mientras se edificaba.
8
פֶּתַח, הַצֵּלָע הַתִּיכֹנָה, אֶל-כֶּתֶף הַבַּיִת, הַיְמָנִית; וּבְלוּלִּים, יַעֲלוּ עַל-הַתִּיכֹנָה, וּמִן-הַתִּיכֹנָה, אֶל-הַשְּׁלִשִׁים.
The entrance of the middle side room was at the right shoulder of the house; and by winding stairs they went up to the middle one, and from the middle one to the third.note
La entrada de la cámara lateral de en medio estaba en el hombro derecho de la casa; y por escaleras de caracol subían a la de en medio, y de la de en medio a la tercera.
9
וַיִּבֶן אֶת-הַבַּיִת, וַיְכַלֵּהוּ; וַיִּסְפֹּן אֶת-הַבַּיִת גֵּבִים, וּשְׂדֵרֹת בָּאֲרָזִים.
So he built the house and finished it; and he covered the house with beams and rows of cedar.note
Así edificó la casa y la terminó; y cubrió la casa con vigas e hileras de cedro.
10
וַיִּבֶן אֶת-היצוע הַיָּצִיעַ עַל-כָּל-הַבַּיִת, חָמֵשׁ אַמּוֹת קוֹמָתוֹ; וַיֶּאֱחֹז אֶת-הַבַּיִת, בַּעֲצֵי אֲרָזִים.  {פ}
And he built the side-structure against all the house, five cubits its height; and it held to the house with timbers of cedar.note
Y edificó la estructura lateral contra toda la casa, de cinco codos su altura; y se sujetaba a la casa con maderas de cedro.
11
וַיְהִי, דְּבַר-יְהוָה, אֶל-שְׁלֹמֹה, לֵאמֹר.
And the word of Jehovah came to Solomon, saying:note
Y vino la palabra de Jehová a Salomón, diciendo:
12
הַבַּיִת הַזֶּה אֲשֶׁר-אַתָּה בֹנֶה, אִם-תֵּלֵךְ בְּחֻקֹּתַי וְאֶת-מִשְׁפָּטַי תַּעֲשֶׂה, וְשָׁמַרְתָּ אֶת-כָּל-מִצְוֺתַי, לָלֶכֶת בָּהֶם--וַהֲקִמֹתִי אֶת-דְּבָרִי אִתָּךְ, אֲשֶׁר דִּבַּרְתִּי אֶל-דָּוִד אָבִיךָ.
‘As for this house that you are building: if you walk in my statutes, and do my ordinances, and keep all my commandments, walking in them, then I will establish my word with you, which I spoke to David your father;note
«En cuanto a esta casa que estás edificando: si andas en mis estatutos, y practicas mis ordenanzas, y guardas todos mis mandamientos, andando en ellos, entonces yo estableceré mi palabra contigo, la que hablé a David tu padre;
13
וְשָׁכַנְתִּי, בְּתוֹךְ בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל; וְלֹא אֶעֱזֹב, אֶת-עַמִּי יִשְׂרָאֵל.  {פ}
and I will dwell in the midst of the children of Israel, and I will not forsake my people Israel.’note
y habitaré en medio de los hijos de Israel, y no abandonaré a mi pueblo Israel».
14
וַיִּבֶן שְׁלֹמֹה אֶת-הַבַּיִת, וַיְכַלֵּהוּ.
So Solomon built the house and finished it.note
Así edificó Salomón la casa y la terminó.
15
וַיִּבֶן אֶת-קִירוֹת הַבַּיִת מִבַּיְתָה, בְּצַלְעוֹת אֲרָזִים--מִקַּרְקַע הַבַּיִת עַד-קִירוֹת הַסִּפֻּן, צִפָּה עֵץ מִבָּיִת; וַיְצַף אֶת-קַרְקַע הַבַּיִת, בְּצַלְעוֹת בְּרוֹשִׁים.
And he built the walls of the house on the inside with boards of cedar — from the floor of the house to the walls of the ceiling he overlaid it with wood on the inside; and he overlaid the floor of the house with boards of cypress.note
Y edificó las paredes de la casa por dentro con tablas de cedro — desde el suelo de la casa hasta las paredes del techo la revistió de madera por dentro; y revistió el suelo de la casa con tablas de ciprés.
16
וַיִּבֶן אֶת-עֶשְׂרִים אַמָּה מירכותי מִיַּרְכְּתֵי הַבַּיִת, בְּצַלְעוֹת אֲרָזִים, מִן-הַקַּרְקַע, עַד-הַקִּירוֹת; וַיִּבֶן לוֹ מִבַּיִת לִדְבִיר, לְקֹדֶשׁ הַקֳּדָשִׁים.
And he built twenty cubits at the rear of the house with boards of cedar, from the floor to the walls; and he built for it on the inside an inner sanctuary, the most holy place.note
Y edificó veinte codos al fondo de la casa con tablas de cedro, desde el suelo hasta las paredes; y le edificó por dentro un santuario interior, el lugar santísimo.
17
וְאַרְבָּעִים בָּאַמָּה, הָיָה הַבָּיִת--הוּא, הַהֵיכָל לִפְנָי.
And forty cubits was the house — that is, the temple in front.note
Y cuarenta codos era la casa — es decir, el templo de enfrente.
18
וְאֶרֶז אֶל-הַבַּיִת, פְּנִימָה, מִקְלַעַת פְּקָעִים, וּפְטוּרֵי צִצִּים:  הַכֹּל אֶרֶז, אֵין אֶבֶן נִרְאָה.
And the cedar on the house inside was carved with gourds and open flowers; all of it was cedar; no stone was seen.note
Y el cedro de la casa por dentro estaba tallado con calabazas y flores abiertas; todo era cedro; no se veía piedra.
19
וּדְבִיר בְּתוֹךְ-הַבַּיִת מִפְּנִימָה, הֵכִין, לְתִתֵּן שָׁם, אֶת-אֲרוֹן בְּרִית יְהוָה.
And he prepared an inner sanctuary in the midst of the house, on the inside, to put there the ark of the covenant of Jehovah.note
Y preparó un santuario interior en medio de la casa, por dentro, para poner allí el arca del pacto de Jehová.
20
וְלִפְנֵי הַדְּבִיר עֶשְׂרִים אַמָּה אֹרֶךְ וְעֶשְׂרִים אַמָּה רֹחַב, וְעֶשְׂרִים אַמָּה קוֹמָתוֹ, וַיְצַפֵּהוּ, זָהָב סָגוּר; וַיְצַף מִזְבֵּחַ, אָרֶז.
And the front of the inner sanctuary: twenty cubits in length, and twenty cubits in width, and twenty cubits its height; and he overlaid it with pure gold; and he overlaid an altar of cedar.note
Y el frente del santuario interior: veinte codos de largo, y veinte codos de ancho, y veinte codos su altura; y lo revistió de oro puro; y revistió un altar de cedro.

21
וַיְצַף שְׁלֹמֹה אֶת-הַבַּיִת מִפְּנִימָה, זָהָב סָגוּר; וַיְעַבֵּר ברתיקות בְּרַתּוּקוֹת זָהָב, לִפְנֵי הַדְּבִיר, וַיְצַפֵּהוּ, זָהָב.
And Solomon overlaid the house on the inside with pure gold; and he drew chains of gold across in front of the inner sanctuary, and overlaid it with gold.note
Y Salomón revistió la casa por dentro de oro puro; e hizo pasar cadenas de oro delante del santuario interior, y lo revistió de oro.
22
וְאֶת-כָּל-הַבַּיִת צִפָּה זָהָב, עַד-תֹּם כָּל-הַבָּיִת; וְכָל-הַמִּזְבֵּחַ אֲשֶׁר-לַדְּבִיר, צִפָּה זָהָב.
And all the house he overlaid with gold, until all the house was finished; and all the altar that belonged to the inner sanctuary he overlaid with gold.note
Y toda la casa la revistió de oro, hasta que toda la casa quedó terminada; y todo el altar que pertenecía al santuario interior lo revistió de oro.
23
וַיַּעַשׂ בַּדְּבִיר, שְׁנֵי כְרוּבִים עֲצֵי-שָׁמֶן:  עֶשֶׂר אַמּוֹת, קוֹמָתוֹ.
And in the inner sanctuary he made two cherubim of olive wood, ten cubits their height.note
E hizo en el santuario interior dos querubines de madera de olivo, diez codos su altura.
24
וְחָמֵשׁ אַמּוֹת, כְּנַף הַכְּרוּב הָאֶחָת, וְחָמֵשׁ אַמּוֹת, כְּנַף הַכְּרוּב הַשֵּׁנִית; עֶשֶׂר אַמּוֹת, מִקְצוֹת כְּנָפָיו וְעַד-קְצוֹת כְּנָפָיו.
And five cubits was one wing of the cherub, and five cubits the other wing of the cherub; ten cubits from the tips of its wings to the tips of its wings.note
Y cinco codos era una ala del querubín, y cinco codos la otra ala del querubín; diez codos desde las puntas de sus alas hasta las puntas de sus alas.
25
וְעֶשֶׂר, בָּאַמָּה, הַכְּרוּב, הַשֵּׁנִי:  מִדָּה אַחַת וְקֶצֶב אֶחָד, לִשְׁנֵי הַכְּרֻבִים.
And ten cubits was the second cherub; one measure and one form for the two cherubim.note
Y diez codos era el segundo querubín; una medida y una forma para los dos querubines.
26
קוֹמַת הַכְּרוּב הָאֶחָד, עֶשֶׂר בָּאַמָּה; וְכֵן, הַכְּרוּב הַשֵּׁנִי.
The height of the one cherub was ten cubits, and so was the second cherub.note
La altura de un querubín era de diez codos, y lo mismo el segundo querubín.
27
וַיִּתֵּן אֶת-הַכְּרוּבִים בְּתוֹךְ הַבַּיִת הַפְּנִימִי, וַיִּפְרְשׂוּ אֶת-כַּנְפֵי הַכְּרֻבִים, וַתִּגַּע כְּנַף-הָאֶחָד בַּקִּיר, וּכְנַף הַכְּרוּב הַשֵּׁנִי נֹגַעַת בַּקִּיר הַשֵּׁנִי; וְכַנְפֵיהֶם אֶל-תּוֹךְ הַבַּיִת, נֹגְעֹת כָּנָף אֶל-כָּנָף.
And he set the cherubim in the midst of the inner house; and they spread out the wings of the cherubim, so that the wing of the one touched the wall, and the wing of the second cherub touched the second wall; and their wings touched in the midst of the house, wing to wing.note
Y puso los querubines en medio de la casa interior; y extendieron las alas de los querubines, de modo que el ala del uno tocaba la pared, y el ala del segundo querubín tocaba la segunda pared; y sus alas se tocaban en medio de la casa, ala con ala.
28
וַיְצַף אֶת-הַכְּרוּבִים, זָהָב.
And he overlaid the cherubim with gold.note
Y revistió de oro los querubines.
29
וְאֵת כָּל-קִירוֹת הַבַּיִת מֵסַב קָלַע, פִּתּוּחֵי מִקְלְעוֹת כְּרוּבִים וְתִמֹרֹת, וּפְטוּרֵי, צִצִּים--מִלִּפְנִים, וְלַחִיצוֹן.
And all the walls of the house all around he carved with carvings of cherubim and palm trees and open flowers, on the inside and on the outside.note
Y todas las paredes de la casa alrededor las talló con tallas de querubines y palmeras y flores abiertas, por dentro y por fuera.
30
וְאֶת-קַרְקַע הַבַּיִת, צִפָּה זָהָב--לִפְנִימָה, וְלַחִיצוֹן.
And the floor of the house he overlaid with gold, on the inside and on the outside.note
Y el suelo de la casa lo revistió de oro, por dentro y por fuera.
31
וְאֵת פֶּתַח הַדְּבִיר, עָשָׂה דַּלְתוֹת עֲצֵי-שָׁמֶן; הָאַיִל מְזוּזוֹת, חֲמִשִׁית.
And for the entrance of the inner sanctuary he made doors of olive wood; the lintel, the doorposts, a fifth.note
Y para la entrada del santuario interior hizo puertas de madera de olivo; el dintel, los postes, un quinto.
32

Translator's Notes — verse by verse

Verses 1–3 · 1 Kings 6 The four hundred and eightieth year — when the house was begun, Ziv, and its measures: sixty by twenty by thirty cubits

The date. “In the four hundred and eightieth year after the children of Israel went out of the land of Egypt, in the fourth year, in the month of Ziv — it is the second month — of Solomon’s reigning over Israel, that he built the house for Jehovah”: the going out is the one that came “at the end of four hundred and thirty years” (Exodus 12:41, now on these pages). The ASV has “in the four hundred and eightieth year after the children of Israel were come out of the land of Egypt”, the NIV “In the four hundred and eightieth year after the Israelites came out of Egypt”, and the NWT 1984 “in the four hundred and eightieth year after the sons of Israel came out from the land of Egypt”. The TLB turns the order round: “This was 480 years after the people of Israel left their slavery in Egypt.” The Hebrew number is 480. Rahlfs’ Septuagint has the four hundred and fortieth year, and this page keeps the Hebrew.

The month. The month is called chodesh here and yerach in verses 37–38. The ASV has “in the month Ziv, which is the second month”, the KJV “in the month Zif”, the Douay “in the month Zio”, and the NWT 1984 “in the month of Ziv, that is, the second month”. Rahlfs’ Greek says only “in the second month” here, and gives the name Nisō in its verse 1c.

The measures. “Its length was sixty cubits, and its width twenty, and its height thirty cubits”: the ASV has “the length thereof was threescore cubits, and the breadth thereof twenty cubits, and the height thereof thirty cubits”, the NIV “sixty cubits long, twenty wide and thirty high”, and the NWT 1984 “sixty cubits in its length, and twenty in its width, and thirty cubits in its height”. The TLB converts at a foot and a half to the cubit: “ninety feet long, thirty feet wide, and forty-five feet high”. Rahlfs’ Greek has forty cubits long, twenty wide and twenty-five high.

The porch, and the temple. “The porch in front of the temple of the house: its length was twenty cubits, across the width of the house; ten cubits was its width, in front of the house”: the word for “temple” is hekhal, the same as of Samuel lying down “in the temple of Jehovah, where the ark of God was” (1 Samuel 3:3, now on these pages). The ASV has “the porch before the temple of the house, twenty cubits was the length thereof, according to the breadth of the house”, the NIV “the portico at the front of the main hall of the temple”, the NWT 1984 “ten cubits it was in its depth”, and the TLB “a porch thirty feet long and fifteen feet deep”. In this chapter “the house” is the whole, and “the temple” is the large hall in it.

Verses 4–10 · 1 Kings 6 Windows with narrowed frames, a side-structure of three levels, ledges, whole stone from the quarry, and no iron tool heard

The windows. “And he made for the house windows with narrowed frames”: “narrowed frames”, shequfim atumim, stands only here, and the versions split. The ASV has “windows of fixed lattice-work”, the KJV “windows of narrow lights”, the NWT 1984 “windows of narrowing frames”, the NIV “narrow windows high up in the temple walls”, and the Douay “oblique windows”. The Geneva says which side is narrow: “broad without, and narrow within”. The Hebrew does not, and this page does not choose.

The side-structure, and the ledges. “And against the wall of the house he built a side-structure all around… and he made side rooms all around”: the side-structure, yatsia, is named in verses 5, 6 and 10, and the rooms are tsela’ot, “sides” (see side). The ASV has “he built stories round about” and “side-chambers”, the KJV “chambers round about”, the NIV “a structure around the building, in which there were side rooms”, and the NWT 1984 “a side structure all around”. “The lowest level… was five cubits wide, and the middle one six cubits wide, and the third seven cubits wide; for he made ledges for the house all around on the outside”: “ledges”, migra’ot, stands only here. The ASV has “he made offsets in the wall of the house round about”, the NWT 1984 “cutbacks”, the NIV “offset ledges around the outside”, and the KJV “narrowed rests”. The reason is given: “so that nothing would take hold in the walls of the house”, so that the beams rest on the ledges and are not set into the wall.

Whole stone, and no iron. “And the house, when it was being built, was built of whole stone from the quarry; and hammers and the axe — any tool of iron — were not heard in the house while it was being built”: the ASV has “stone made ready at the quarry”, the KJV “made ready before it was brought thither”, the NIV “only blocks dressed at the quarry were used”, and the NWT 1984 “quarry stone already completed”. The word is shelemah, “whole”, and “whole stones” stands in two verses of the altar, “Of whole stones you shall build the altar of Jehovah your God” (Deuteronomy 27:6) and “an altar of whole stones, on which no iron had been lifted up” (Joshua 8:31, both now on these pages); the law of the altar of stone forbids “cut stone”, “for if you have swung your tool over it, you have profaned it” (Exodus 20:21, now on these pages). The ASV has “neither hammer nor axe nor any tool of iron heard in the house”, the NIV “no hammer, chisel or any other iron tool was heard”, and the TLB “without the sound of hammer, ax, or any other tool”.

The right shoulder, the stairs, the cedar. “The entrance of the middle side room was at the right shoulder of the house; and by winding stairs they went up to the middle one, and from the middle one to the third”: the Hebrew says the “middle” room has the entrance, and then goes up to the middle one, so the versions mend it. The ASV has “the door for the middle side-chambers was in the right side of the house”, the NWT 1984 “the entrance of the lowest side chamber was on the right side of the house”, and the NIV “the entrance to the lowest floor was on the south side of the temple”. “Winding stairs”, lulim, stands only here. “He covered the house with beams and rows of cedar”: the ASV has “beams and planks of cedar”, the KJV “beams and boards of cedar”, the NIV “roofing it with beams and cedar planks”, and the NWT 1984 “beams and rows in cedarwood”.

Verses 11–14 · 1 Kings 6 The word of Jehovah to Solomon in the middle of the building — “if you walk in my statutes”, and “I will dwell in the midst of the children of Israel”

The word, in the middle. “And the word of Jehovah came to Solomon, saying”: the house is built and finished in verse 9, and the same sentence stands again in verse 14 with Solomon’s name added, and the word comes between. The ASV has “So Solomon built the house, and finished it” in verse 14, and the NIV “So Solomon built the temple and completed it”; verse 9 has the same sentence without his name. Rahlfs’ Greek has no verses numbered 11 to 14.

“If you walk.” “If you walk in my statutes, and do my ordinances, and keep all my commandments, walking in them, then I will establish my word with you, which I spoke to David your father”: it is David’s own charge to Solomon, “to walk in his ways, to keep his statutes, his commandments, his ordinances and his testimonies” (1 Kings 2:3), and the answer at Gibeon, “if you walk in my ways, to keep my statutes and my commandments, as David your father walked” (3:14, both now on these pages); the word is the one of Nathan, “I will establish the throne of his kingdom forever”, with its “when he does wrong, I will reprove him” (2 Samuel 7:13–14, now on these pages). The ASV has “then will I establish my word with thee”, the KJV “then will I perform my word with thee”, the NIV “I will fulfill through you the promise I gave to David your father”, and the NWT 1984 “I also shall certainly carry out my word with you”.

“I will dwell.” “And I will dwell in the midst of the children of Israel, and I will not forsake my people Israel”: “I will dwell in the midst of the children of Israel” stands in two verses, here and where the tent is promised, “And I will dwell among the children of Israel, and I will be their God” (Exodus 29:45, now on these pages); and “and I will not forsake my people Israel” stands only here. The ASV has “And I will dwell among the children of Israel, and will not forsake my people Israel”, the NIV “And I will live among the Israelites and will not abandon my people Israel”, and the NWT 1984 “I shall indeed reside in the middle of the sons of Israel, and I shall not leave my people Israel”.

Built and finished. “So Solomon built the house and finished it”: the verbs are vayiven and vaykhallehu, “and he built… and he finished it”, as in verse 9. The KJV has “So Solomon built the house, and finished it”, and the NWT 1984 “And Solomon continued building the house that he might finish it”. The verses that follow go back inside the house.

Verses 15–22 · 1 Kings 6 Cedar inside, a floor of cypress, and the inner sanctuary — the most holy place, twenty by twenty by twenty, in pure gold

Cedar and cypress. “He built the walls of the house on the inside with boards of cedar… and he overlaid the floor of the house with boards of cypress”: the cypress is berosh, the “timbers of cypress” of Hiram’s letter (1 Kings 5:22, now on these pages). The ASV has “boards of fir”, the KJV “planks of fir”, the NIV “planks of juniper”, the NWT 1984 “boards of juniper”, and the TLB “cypress boards”.

Four names for the parts. “And he built twenty cubits at the rear of the house… and he built for it on the inside an inner sanctuary, the most holy place”: the inner sanctuary is devir, and “the most holy place” is qodesh haqodashim, “the holy of holies”, the “most holy” beyond the veil (Exodus 26:33, now on these pages). “And forty cubits was the house — that is, the temple in front”: forty and twenty are the sixty of verse 2. The ASV has “an oracle, even for the most holy place”, the NIV “an inner sanctuary, the Most Holy Place”, the NWT 1984 “the innermost room, the Most Holy”, and the KJV “the oracle, even for the most holy place”.

Gourds, open flowers, and no stone seen. “The cedar on the house inside was carved with gourds and open flowers; all of it was cedar; no stone was seen”: “gourds”, peqa’im, stands in two verses, here and at the molten sea (1 Kings 7:24, now on these pages). The ASV has “carved with knops and open flowers”, the NIV “carved with gourds and open flowers”, the NWT 1984 “gourd-shaped ornaments and garlands of blossoms”, and the TLB “designs of rosebuds and open flowers”.

Gold, chains, and an altar of cedar. “Twenty cubits in length, and twenty cubits in width, and twenty cubits its height; and he overlaid it with pure gold; and he overlaid an altar of cedar”: “pure gold”, zahav sagur, stands in eight verses, two of them here and the rest in the vessels of the house and the king’s cups; sagur means “shut up”, and the five versions quoted here read it as pure gold. The ASV has “he covered the altar with cedar”, the KJV “so covered the altar which was of cedar”, the NIV “he also overlaid the altar of cedar”, and the NWT 1984 “to overlay the altar with cedarwood”. “He drew chains of gold across in front of the inner sanctuary”: the ASV has “chains of gold across before the oracle”, the KJV “a partition by the chains of gold”, the NIV “gold chains across the front of the inner sanctuary”, and the NWT 1984 “chainwork of gold”; the word for chains, written rattuqot in the margin, stands only here.

Verses 23–28 · 1 Kings 6 Two cherubim of olive wood, ten cubits high — wing to wing across the inner sanctuary

Olive wood. “And in the inner sanctuary he made two cherubim of olive wood, ten cubits their height”: the Hebrew is atsei shemen, “wood of oil”, and “wood of oil” stands in four verses, all in this chapter (verses 23, 31, 32, 33). The ASV has “two cherubim of olive-wood”, the KJV “olive tree”, the NIV “olive wood”, and the NWT 1984 “oil-tree wood”. The cherubim are the ones stationed “on the east side of the Garden of Eden” (Genesis 3:24, now on these pages), the two of gold on the cover of the ark (Exodus 25:18, now on these pages), and the ones above which Jehovah “sits” (2 Samuel 6:2, now on these pages).

Five and five. “And five cubits was one wing of the cherub, and five cubits the other wing of the cherub; ten cubits from the tips of its wings to the tips of its wings”: the ASV has “from the uttermost part of the one wing unto the uttermost part of the other were ten cubits”, the NIV “ten cubits from wing tip to wing tip”, and the NWT 1984 “ten cubits it was from the tip of his wing to the tip of his wing”. Four wings of five cubits make twenty, the width of the inner sanctuary (verse 20).

One measure and one form. “And ten cubits was the second cherub; one measure and one form for the two cherubim”: the ASV has “both the cherubim were of one measure and one form”, the KJV “one measure and one size”, the NIV “the two cherubim were identical in size and shape”, and the NWT 1984 “the same measure and the same shape”.

Wing to wing. “They spread out the wings of the cherubim, so that the wing of the one touched the wall, and the wing of the second cherub touched the second wall; and their wings touched in the midst of the house, wing to wing”: the ASV has “their wings touched one another in the midst of the house”, the NIV “their wings touched each other in the middle of the room”, and the NWT 1984 “reaching wing to wing”. The ark’s cherubim have their wings “upward” and their “faces one toward the other” (Exodus 25:20, now on these pages); the verses here say nothing of the faces.

Verses 29–35 · 1 Kings 6 Cherubim, palm trees and open flowers — a gold floor, and the doors: “a fifth”, “a fourth”, and leaves that turn

Carvings, and a floor of gold. “And all the walls of the house all around he carved with carvings of cherubim and palm trees and open flowers, on the inside and on the outside”: the ASV has “carved figures of cherubim and palm-trees and open flowers, within and without”, the NIV “cherubim, palm trees and open flowers”, and the NWT 1984 “engraved carvings of cherubs and palm-tree figures and engravings of blossoms”. “And the floor of the house he overlaid with gold, on the inside and on the outside”: the ASV has “within and without”.

“A fifth”, and “a fourth”. “And for the entrance of the inner sanctuary he made doors of olive wood; the lintel, the doorposts, a fifth”: the three words are uncertain, and the versions read them differently. The ASV has “the lintel and door-posts were a fifth part of the wall”, the NIV “that were one fifth of the width of the sanctuary”, the NWT 1984 “side pillars, doorposts [and] a fifth”, and the TLB “a five-sided opening”. For the temple: “doorposts of olive wood, a fourth”, and the ASV has “out of a fourth part of the wall”, the NWT 1984 “foursquare”, the NIV “one fourth of the width of the hall”, and the TLB “square doorposts”.

The gold brought down. “And two doors of olive wood; and he carved on them carvings of cherubim and palm trees and open flowers, and overlaid them with gold; and he brought the gold down upon the cherubim and upon the palm trees”: the verb is yarad, “go down”. The ASV has “he spread the gold upon the cherubim”, the NWT 1984 “he proceeded to beat the gold down upon the cherubs”, and the NIV “overlaid the cherubim and palm trees with hammered gold”.

Doors of cypress, and gold laid evenly. “And two doors of cypress wood: the two leaves of the one door turning, and the two leaves of the second door turning”: the cypress is berosh again, as in verse 15, and the “leaves” are tsela’im, “sides”. The ASV has “the two leaves of the one door were folding”, the NWT 1984 “turned on pivots”, the NIV “two leaves that turned in sockets”, and the TLB “each door was hinged to fold back upon itself”. “Laid evenly upon the engraved work”: the ASV has “gold fitted upon the graven work”, the NIV “gold hammered evenly over the carvings”, and the NWT 1984 “gold foil upon the representations”.

Verses 36–38 · 1 Kings 6 The inner court, the foundation in Ziv, the finishing in Bul — and seven years

The inner court. “And he built the inner court with three rows of hewn stone and a row of cedar beams”: “cedar beams” stands in two verses, here and at the great court (1 Kings 7:12, now on these pages). The ASV has “three courses of hewn stone, and a course of cedar beams”, the NIV “three courses of dressed stone and one course of trimmed cedar beams”, the NWT 1984 “three rows of hewn stone and a row of beams of cedarwood”, and the TLB “three layers of hewn stone and one layer of cedar beams”. “Hewn stone” is gazit, the stone of the king’s command (1 Kings 5:31, now on these pages), and the word that the law of the altar renders “cut stone” (Exodus 20:21); the page does not say how the two stand together.

The foundation. “In the fourth year the foundation of the house of Jehovah was laid, in the month of Ziv”: “in the month of Ziv”, yerach Ziv, stands only here. The ASV has “In the fourth year was the foundation of the house of Jehovah laid, in the month Ziv”, the NWT 1984 “In the fourth year the house of Jehovah had its foundation laid, in the lunar month of Ziv”, and the NIV “laid in the fourth year, in the month of Ziv”. The TLB gives a modern month: “the month of May”.

Bul, and seven years. “And in the eleventh year, in the month of Bul — it is the eighth month — the house was finished with all its parts and all its plan. So he built it in seven years”: “in the month of Bul”, yerach Bul, stands only here. The ASV has “finished throughout all the parts thereof, and according to all the fashion of it”, the NWT 1984 “finished as regards all its details and all its plan”, and the NIV “finished in all its details according to its specifications”. From the second month of the fourth year to the eighth month of the eleventh is seven years and six months by plain subtraction; the text gives the span as seven years, and the page does not say how the years are counted.

What the Greek does with the dates. Rahlfs’ Septuagint has no verses numbered 37 and 38, and carries the same dates in its verses 1c and 1d: the foundation “in the fourth year… in the month Nisō, the second month”, and the finishing “in the eleventh year in the month Baal, this is the eighth month”. It puts the king’s command about the great stones, which the Hebrew has at the end of chapter 5, in its verses 1a and 1b. This page keeps the Hebrew order.

Patterns worth carrying forward

Where this translation keeps the words exact and distinct: “the house” (the whole), “the temple” (hekhal, the large hall), “the inner sanctuary” (devir) and “the most holy place”; “side rooms” for tsela’ot, as in side; “whole stone” for shelemah, as in Deuteronomy 27:6; “hewn stone” for gazit, as in 1 Kings 5:31; “cypress” for berosh, as in 1 Kings 5:22; “pure gold”; “olive wood”; “gourds and open flowers”; “cherubim”; and “the children of Israel”; with the formulas that differ in the notes.

Echoes paid. The going out of Egypt (Exodus 12:41); Samuel in the temple where the ark was (1 Samuel 3:3); the altar of whole stones and the law against cut stone (Deuteronomy 27:6; Joshua 8:31; Exodus 20:21); David’s charge and the answer at Gibeon (1 Kings 2:3; 3:14); Nathan’s promise (2 Samuel 7:13–14); “I will dwell among the children of Israel” (Exodus 29:45); the cypress of Hiram’s letter and the hewn stones of the king’s command (1 Kings 5:22, 31); the most holy beyond the veil (Exodus 26:33); the cherubim of Eden, of the ark’s cover and of the ark’s throne (Genesis 3:24; Exodus 25:18, 20; 2 Samuel 6:2).

Echoes planted. The molten sea, with its gourds (1 Kings 7:24); the great court with its row of cedar beams (7:12); the pure gold of the vessels (7:49–50); and the inner sanctuary, where the ark is brought (8:6). The first three are in the next chapter, now on these pages; 1 Kings 8 is now on these pages too. The condition “if you walk in my statutes” is left standing, and the next chapters are the house’s first test.

Next in this book: 1 Kings 7 — Solomon builds his own house for thirteen years, the house of the forest of Lebanon and the porch of the throne; Hiram of Tyre, a worker in bronze, casts the two pillars Jachin and Boaz, the molten sea on twelve oxen, and the ten bases and lavers; and the vessels of the house are finished.
