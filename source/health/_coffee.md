  - The review in his screenshot: Lopes CR, Cunha RA. *Impact of coffee intake on human aging: Epidemiology and cellular mechanisms.* Ageing Research Reviews, Dec 2024. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.arr.2024.102581 (PubMed 39557300). Narrative review; ~17% lower all-cause mortality, "1.8 years" healthspan is that 17% restated; mechanisms strongest for stress adaptation and inflammation, unclear for stem cells.
  - Bigger evidence base: Poole R et al. *Coffee consumption and health: umbrella review of meta-analyses.* BMJ 2017;359:j5024. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.j5024 (PubMed 29167102). RR 0.83 all-cause mortality at 3–4 cups/day vs none; observational; harms in pregnancy and fracture risk in women.
