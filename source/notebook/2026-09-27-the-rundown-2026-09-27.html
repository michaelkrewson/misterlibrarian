World

Health & medicine

  • The FDA approves Atebrioz (zilurgisertib) on September 25 as the third treatment for fibrodysplasia ossificans progressiva, a rare bone disorder affecting roughly 300 people in the US, after a trial cohort showed a 3.2 cm³ average decrease in new bone growth versus a 24.6 cm³ increase on placebo. ↗
  • A meta-analysis of all eight randomized mammography trials, published September 14 in JNCI, estimates breast-cancer screening's true overdiagnosis rate at under 5% — far below the 30-50% figures long cited in the literature. ↗
