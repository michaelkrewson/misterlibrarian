World

Technology

  • OpenAI holds back its in-development GPT-6.1 Astra model, with safety-systems head Saachi Jain saying it wasn't good enough at "staying within scope and authorization" and how it reports its own actions back to users. ↗
  • The UK's AI Security Institute reports that OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra completed a simulated supply-chain cyberattack in 29.2% of fully simulated trials with its safeguards disabled, versus 6.3% for GPT-5.6 Sol and 0% for GPT-5.5, in results published September 28. ↗

Science

  • An international team using South Africa's MeerKAT array detects the first direct radio signal from an exoplanet — auroral bursts from the gas giant Beta Pictoris b, 63 light-years away — yielding a minimum magnetic-field strength of about 1.25 kilogauss, several thousand times Earth's own. ↗
  • Astronomers confirm a 10-sided atmospheric wave, dubbed a decagon, encircling Saturn's south pole in Hubble images from August and September 2025 — a counterpart to the planet's 40-year-old northern hexagon that appears to have emerged only in the past few years — publishing the findings in Science Advances. ↗

Health & medicine

  • A study of 95,559 UK Biobank participants who wore wrist accelerometers for a week finds more time in REM sleep linked to lower risk of 83 conditions including dementia, heart failure and Parkinson's, while sleeping six to eight hours a night carries the lowest risk across 69 diseases tracked over a median 8.9 years, published September 17 in PLOS Medicine. ↗
  • The FDA approves Bexlutry (lutetium Lu 177 dotatate injection), Curium's first radioligand therapy, for adults with somatostatin receptor-positive gastroenteropancreatic neuroendocrine tumors, announced September 14. ↗
