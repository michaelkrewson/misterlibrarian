Science & space

  • UBC researchers describe two sea spider species new to science from British Columbia's Salish Sea, the first found there in nearly a century, including the red-eyed, three-lipped Callipallene pilosuspedes, collected scuba diving to depths of 18 meters. ↗

Health & medicine

  • A study tracking more than 113,000 people for up to 20 years finds a transient ischemic attack, or "mini-stroke," carries a 34% higher long-term dementia risk than no stroke at all, with about one in five TIA patients diagnosed with dementia within roughly seven years. ↗
  • A Washington University-led phase 1/2 trial in 30 adults with AML or high-risk MDS finds that CRISPR-edited donor stem cells with the CD33 gene deleted engraft normally and let some patients tolerate CD33-targeted maintenance therapy afterward without prolonged, severe blood-cell suppression. ↗
