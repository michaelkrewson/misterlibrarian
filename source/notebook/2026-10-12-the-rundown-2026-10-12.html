Science

Health & medicine

  • The FDA approved atezolizumab (Tecentriq) plus chemotherapy on October 8 for stage III colon cancer with deficient DNA mismatch repair, based on the ATOMIC trial's finding of a 50% reduction in recurrence or death risk among 711 adult patients. ↗
  • A Vanderbilt-led study published October 8 in JAMA Otolaryngology found that genetic risk scores built from detailed hearing-test data on 16,000 BioVU biobank participants predicted hearing loss better than scores built from diagnostic billing codes alone. ↗
