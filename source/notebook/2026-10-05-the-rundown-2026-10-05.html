Science

  • The 2026 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine was awarded today to Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann and Georg Nagel for discovering light-gated ion channels, the basis of optogenetics, which lets researchers switch individual brain cells on and off with light. ↗
  • NASA astronaut Jessica Watkins commanded SpaceX's Crew-13 to the International Space Station on October 1 aboard Dragon Grace, becoming the first active NASA astronaut to fly twice on a Dragon spacecraft, for a planned six-month stay. ↗

Technology
