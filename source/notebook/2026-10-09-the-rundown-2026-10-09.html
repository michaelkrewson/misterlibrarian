The world

  • Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said October 1 that Ukraine used its domestically built FP-7 ballistic missile — made by the Ukrainian firm Fire Point, with a reported range near 200–250 km and a roughly 150 kg warhead — in combat for the first time, without disclosing the target or location. ↗
  • OPEC+'s seven core members agreed October 4 to hold November crude output at September's levels, about 31.01 million barrels a day combined, continuing a pause after four straight months of production hikes as Strait of Hormuz disruptions keep actual exports below target. ↗

Bitcoin & crypto

  • Bitcoin fell below $81,000 on October 8 as Middle East-driven market stress pushed crypto liquidations past $1.1 billion in 24 hours per CoinGlass, before partially recovering to around $82,500 by Friday morning. ↗

Science

  • The 2026 Nobel Prize in Chemistry went jointly to Henri B. Kagan of Université Paris-Sud and Kenso Soai, 95, of Tokyo University of Science, announced October 7 "for the discovery of non-linear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric organic synthesis." ↗

Space

  • NASA's Crew-13 launched October 1 aboard SpaceX's Dragon capsule Grace, carrying commander Jessica Watkins, pilot Luke Delaney, and mission specialists Joshua Kutryk (Canada) and Sergey Teteryatnikov (Russia) for a roughly six-month ISS stay; Watkins becomes the first NASA astronaut to fly twice on a Dragon. ↗

Arts & culture

Health & medicine
