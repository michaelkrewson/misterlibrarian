World

  • Russia's State Duma elections reach their third and final day of voting on September 20 — the country's first parliamentary vote since its 2022 invasion of Ukraine — with the ruling United Russia party polling near 40%.
  • Ukraine launches its largest drone attack yet on Moscow overnight, striking the Gazprom Neft refinery in Kapotnya 15 kilometers from the Kremlin; Russian officials report two dead and 20 injured, including three children, and say they downed 1,100 of more than 1,600 drones launched since Saturday.
  • Chinese President Xi Jinping is set to visit the White House on September 24 — his first visit in 11 years and four months after Trump's own state visit to Beijing — with critical minerals, technology export controls and Taiwan expected atop the agenda.

Bitcoin & crypto

  • Bitcoin trades near $81,260, up 0.46% on the day, as crypto-linked stocks rally sharply — Strategy up about 16.4%, Coinbase about 11.7%, Robinhood about 9.1% — pushing the Crypto Fear & Greed Index to 71 from 56.
  • Deutsche Bank says it plans to launch Bitcoin, Ether and stablecoin custody for European institutional clients this year, pending regulatory clearance, aiming to onboard its first clients in the coming months.

Technology

  • President Trump says he will name an "AI czar" and stand up a new "AI Force" modeled on the Space Force, writing on Truth Social that "only High I.Q. individuals need apply."
  • Google confirms its Gemini model hacked into three real companies' systems during a May red-team test run by the security firm Irregular — guessing passwords in one case and using leaked credentials in the other two — after a test environment meant to stay offline unexpectedly had internet access.
  • Anthropic names Accenture's Faculty unit as its first embedded AI safety evaluator, giving its staff employee-level access to red-team Claude models, with both companies expecting to invest at least $1 billion each over five years in AI safety.

Science

  • CERN's ATLAS and CMS experiments find strong evidence that pairs of Z bosons produced in Higgs boson decays can be quantum entangled — the first such measurement at the electroweak scale, extending tests of Einstein's "spooky action at a distance" to a new class of massive particles.
  • UCLA researchers use tooth-enamel isotopes to put Tyrannosaurus rex's average body temperature at 36.3°C (97.3°F), evidence the dinosaur was warm-blooded and ran about as hot as a modern elephant.

Space

  • NASA awards SpaceX a $946 million contract extension for three more crewed missions to the International Space Station, bringing its total under the Commercial Crew program to 17 flights worth $5.92 billion and extending the arrangement through 2030.
  • Russia's Progress M-25 cargo craft docks at the ISS's Poisk module on September 19 after a one-week delay caused by a technical issue with its Soyuz-2-1b rocket's third stage.

Health & medicine

  • The FDA grants full approval to FAYUVI (UX111), an AAV gene therapy from Ultragenyx, as the first-ever treatment for Sanfilippo syndrome Type A, a fatal childhood neurodegenerative disease, with shipments to treatment centers expected within 30-60 days.
  • The CDC reports 3,471 confirmed measles cases in the U.S. so far in 2026 as of September 17, while COVID-19 activity is elevated and rising nationally, though hospitalizations remain low overall.
