Bitcoin & crypto

Technology

  • Cisco Talos discloses CLOSEDQUORUM on September 22, a Windows implant it calls the first documented malware to hand tactical attack decisions to a panel of four commercial AI models — DeepSeek, Qwen, Mistral and Google Gemini — that vote on each move, while releasing an open-source detection toolkit called CAIRN and stating it has not confirmed the malware deployed against real victims. ↗
  • OpenAI launches GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna on September 22, roughly 90 minutes after Anthropic's own Claude Opus 5.5 price cut, pricing Sol at $2/$10 per million input/output tokens (down from $4/$20) and Luna at $0.10/$0.50 (down from $0.20/$1.20). ↗

Science & space

  • Paleontologists from Fundación Dinópolis identify 14 tail vertebrae found at El Castellar, Teruel, Spain as the first confirmed Diplodocus fossil found outside North America, suggesting the roughly 150-million-year-old sauropod crossed between continents during the Late Jurassic. ↗
  • A study led by Pablo Santos-Sanz of Spain's IAA-CSIC, using a James Webb Space Telescope stellar occultation from October 2022, finds the centaur Chariklo's ring system has visibly changed in just a few years — its inner ring growing denser and its outer ring fainter. ↗

Health & medicine

  • A Peking University-led study tracking more than 95,000 adults' wrist-monitor sleep data over nine years finds each 47.6-minute increase in REM sleep is tied to a lower risk of 83 diseases, including heart failure, dementia and Parkinson's, while more deep sleep is linked to lower risk of seven conditions including type 2 diabetes. ↗
  • A Mayo Clinic-led prospective study of 35,878 adults age 50 and older, published in Nature Medicine, finds a multicancer blood test detected signals across 17 cancer types with a positive predictive value of 60.3% and 99.64% specificity, catching 53% of cancers at stage 1 or 2. ↗
