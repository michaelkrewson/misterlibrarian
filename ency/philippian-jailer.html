The Philippian jailer

Luke calls him desmophylax (jailer) in verses 23, 27 and 36 and gives him no name. He is ordered to keep Paul and Silas “securely”; he puts them in the inner prison and secures their feet in the stocks (vv23–24). After the earthquake he wakes, sees the doors open, draws his sword and is about to kill himself, supposing the prisoners had escaped (v27); Paul cries out that they are all there. He asks for lights, leaps in, trembling, and says “Sirs, what must I do to be saved?” (v30), and is told “Believe on the Lord Jesus, and you will be saved, you and your household” (v31). They speak the word to him with all in his house; he washes them of the blows, and he and all his are baptized at once (vv32–33); he brings them up, sets a table, and “rejoiced with his whole household, having believed God” (v34). At v36 he tells Paul the magistrates have sent word to release them. Luke says nothing more of him, and does not say what became of him after v36.
