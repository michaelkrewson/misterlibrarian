🏺 Encyclopedia

Cornelius

A centurion of the ‘Italian cohort’ (speira) at Caesarea, ‘devout and fearing God with all his household,’ who gives alms to the Jewish people and prays continually (Acts 10:1–2). An angel tells him in a vision to send to Joppa for Simon who is called Peter; he sends two household servants and a devout soldier (10:7–8), gathers his relatives and close friends (10:24), and falls at Peter’s feet and is raised up (10:25–26). The Spirit falls on those who are listening while Peter is still speaking, and they are baptized (10:44–48). His name stands in eight verses of Acts 10 and in no other verse of the New Testament; Peter’s later retellings do not use it. ⚠ The Greek gives no date, no family name and no later history for him, and this library supplies none.
