🏺 Encyclopedia

Ananias of Damascus

A disciple in Damascus, to whom the Lord speaks in a vision and who answers ‘Look — here I am, Lord’ (Acts 9:10). He is sent to the house of Judas on the street called Straight to lay hands on Saul, objects that Saul has authority from the chief priests to bind all who call on the name (9:13–14), is told that Saul is ‘a chosen vessel’ (9:15), goes, says ‘Saul, brother,’ and Saul recovers sight and is baptized (9:17–18). Acts gives nothing else about him in this chapter; in Paul’s telling at 22:12 he is ‘devout according to the law, well spoken of by all the Jews who live there.’ ⚠ The name is the Greek form of Hebrew Ḥananyah, ‘Jehovah has been gracious,’ and is also the name of the man who lied at Acts 5:1; they are two men.
