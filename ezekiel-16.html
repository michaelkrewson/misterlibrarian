Ezekiel is told to make Jerusalem know her abominations: she is pictured as a child cast out at birth, taken in and married, who turns to others; judgment is pronounced, her sisters Samaria and Sodom are set beside her, and the covenant is to be remembered.
The chapter gives no date.

4
וּמוֹלְדוֹתַיִךְ, בְּיוֹם הוּלֶּדֶת אוֹתָךְ לֹא-כָרַּת שָׁרֵּךְ, וּבְמַיִם לֹא-רֻחַצְתְּ, לְמִשְׁעִי; וְהָמְלֵחַ לֹא הֻמְלַחַתְּ, וְהָחְתֵּל לֹא חֻתָּלְתְּ.

Y en cuanto a tu nacimiento: el día en que naciste no te cortaron el cordón del ombligo, ni fuiste lavada con agua para limpiarte; no fuiste salada en absoluto, ni envuelta en pañales en absoluto.
5
לֹא-חָסָה עָלַיִךְ עַיִן, לַעֲשׂוֹת לָךְ אַחַת מֵאֵלֶּה--לְחֻמְלָה עָלָיִךְ; וַתֻּשְׁלְכִי אֶל-פְּנֵי הַשָּׂדֶה, בְּגֹעַל נַפְשֵׁךְ, בְּיוֹם, הֻלֶּדֶת אֹתָךְ.

Ningún ojo te perdonó, para hacer por ti una de estas cosas, teniendo compasión de ti; sino que fuiste arrojada sobre la faz del campo, en el aborrecimiento de tu alma, el día en que naciste.
6

7

I made you a myriad, like the growth of the field; and you grew and became great, and came to the ornament of ornaments; breasts were formed and your hair sprouted, yet you were naked and bare.note
Te hice una miríada, como lo que brota en el campo; y creciste y te hiciste grande, y llegaste al adorno de los adornos; se formaron los pechos y brotó tu cabello, pero estabas desnuda y descubierta.
8
וָאֶעֱבֹר עָלַיִךְ וָאֶרְאֵךְ, וְהִנֵּה עִתֵּךְ עֵת דֹּדִים, וָאֶפְרֹשׂ כְּנָפִי עָלַיִךְ, וָאֲכַסֶּה עֶרְוָתֵךְ; וָאֶשָּׁבַע לָךְ וָאָבוֹא בִבְרִית אֹתָךְ, נְאֻם אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה--וַתִּהְיִי-לִי.
And I passed by you and saw you, and look, your time was the time of love; and I spread my wing over you and covered your nakedness; and I swore to you and entered into a covenant with you, declares the Lord Jehovah, and you became mine.note
Y pasé junto a ti y te vi, y mira, tu tiempo era el tiempo del amor; y extendí mi ala sobre ti y cubrí tu desnudez; y te juré y entré en pacto contigo, declara el Señor Jehová, y fuiste mía.
9
וָאֶרְחָצֵךְ בַּמַּיִם, וָאֶשְׁטֹף דָּמַיִךְ מֵעָלָיִךְ; וָאֲסֻכֵךְ, בַּשָּׁמֶן.
And I washed you in water, and rinsed your blood from off you, and anointed you with oil.note
Y te lavé con agua, y enjuagué de ti tu sangre, y te ungí con aceite.
10
וָאַלְבִּישֵׁךְ רִקְמָה, וָאֶנְעֲלֵךְ תָּחַשׁ; וָאֶחְבְּשֵׁךְ בַּשֵּׁשׁ, וַאֲכַסֵּךְ מֶשִׁי.
And I clothed you with embroidered work, and shod you with tachash, and wound you in fine linen, and covered you with silk.note
Y te vestí de bordado, y te calcé de tajash, y te ceñí de lino fino, y te cubrí de seda.
11
וָאֶעְדֵּךְ, עֶדִי; וָאֶתְּנָה צְמִידִים עַל-יָדַיִךְ, וְרָבִיד עַל-גְּרוֹנֵךְ.
And I adorned you with ornaments, and put bracelets on your hands and a collar on your throat.note
Y te adorné con adornos, y puse brazaletes en tus manos y un collar en tu garganta.
12
וָאֶתֵּן נֶזֶם, עַל-אַפֵּךְ, וַעֲגִילִים, עַל-אָזְנָיִךְ; וַעֲטֶרֶת תִּפְאֶרֶת, בְּרֹאשֵׁךְ.

Y puse un aro en tu nariz, y zarcillos en tus orejas, y una corona de gloria en tu cabeza.
13

So you were adorned with gold and silver, and your clothing was fine linen and silk and embroidered work; fine flour and honey and oil you ate. And you became very, very beautiful, and you became fit for royalty.note
Así fuiste adornada con oro y plata, y tu vestido era lino fino y seda y bordado; comiste flor de harina y miel y aceite. Y te hiciste muy, muy hermosa, y llegaste a ser apta para la realeza.
14

Y salió tu nombre entre las naciones a causa de tu hermosura, porque era completa por mi esplendor que yo había puesto sobre ti, declara el Señor Jehová.
15

Pero confiaste en tu hermosura, y te prostituiste a causa de tu nombre, y derramaste tus prostituciones sobre todo el que pasaba: suya fue.
16

And you took some of your garments and made yourself high places of many patches, and played the harlot on them—such things do not come, and it shall not be.note
Y tomaste algunos de tus vestidos y te hiciste lugares altos de muchos remiendos, y te prostituiste sobre ellos: tales cosas no llegan, ni será.
17
וַתִּקְחִי כְּלֵי תִפְאַרְתֵּךְ, מִזְּהָבִי וּמִכַּסְפִּי אֲשֶׁר נָתַתִּי לָךְ, וַתַּעֲשִׂי-לָךְ, צַלְמֵי זָכָר; וַתִּזְנִי-בָם.
And you took your articles of glory, of my gold and of my silver that I had given you, and made yourself images of a male, and played the harlot with them.note
Y tomaste tus objetos de gloria, de mi oro y de mi plata que yo te había dado, y te hiciste imágenes de varón, y te prostituiste con ellas.
18
וַתִּקְחִי אֶת-בִּגְדֵי רִקְמָתֵךְ, וַתְּכַסִּים; וְשַׁמְנִי, וּקְטָרְתִּי, נתתי נָתַתְּ, לִפְנֵיהֶם.
And you took your embroidered garments and covered them, and my oil and my incense you set before them.note
Y tomaste tus vestidos bordados y las cubriste, y mi aceite y mi incienso pusiste delante de ellas.
19
וְלַחְמִי אֲשֶׁר-נָתַתִּי לָךְ סֹלֶת וָשֶׁמֶן וּדְבַשׁ, הֶאֱכַלְתִּיךְ, וּנְתַתִּיהוּ לִפְנֵיהֶם לְרֵיחַ נִיחֹחַ, וַיֶּהִי:  נְאֻם, אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה.
And my bread that I gave you—the fine flour and oil and honey I fed you—you set it before them as a soothing aroma; and so it was, declares the Lord Jehovah.note
Y mi pan que yo te di —la flor de harina y el aceite y la miel con que te alimenté—, lo pusiste delante de ellas como aroma apacible; y así fue, declara el Señor Jehová.
20
וַתִּקְחִי אֶת-בָּנַיִךְ וְאֶת-בְּנוֹתַיִךְ, אֲשֶׁר יָלַדְתְּ לִי, וַתִּזְבָּחִים לָהֶם, לֶאֱכוֹל--הַמְעַט, מתזנותך מִתַּזְנוּתָיִךְ.
And you took your sons and your daughters, whom you had borne to me, and sacrificed these to them to be eaten. Were your harlotries a small thing,note
Y tomaste a tus hijos y a tus hijas, que me habías dado a luz, y se los sacrificaste a ellas para ser comidos. ¿Eran poca cosa tus prostituciones,
21
וַתִּשְׁחֲטִי, אֶת-בָּנָי; וַתִּתְּנִים, בְּהַעֲבִיר אוֹתָם לָהֶם.
that you slaughtered my sons and gave them up, by making them pass over to them?note
para que degollaras a mis hijos y los entregaras, haciéndolos pasar a ellas?
22
וְאֵת כָּל-תּוֹעֲבֹתַיִךְ וְתַזְנֻתַיִךְ, לֹא זכרתי זָכַרְתְּ אֶת-יְמֵי נְעוּרָיִךְ--בִּהְיוֹתֵךְ עֵירֹם וְעֶרְיָה, מִתְבּוֹסֶסֶת בְּדָמֵךְ הָיִית.
And in all your abominations and your harlotries you did not remember the days of your youth, when you were naked and bare, when you were thrashing about in your blood.note
Y en todas tus abominaciones y tus prostituciones no te acordaste de los días de tu juventud, cuando estabas desnuda y descubierta, cuando estabas revolviéndote en tu sangre.
23
וַיְהִי, אַחֲרֵי כָּל-רָעָתֵךְ:  אוֹי אוֹי לָךְ, נְאֻם אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה.
And it came about, after all your wickedness—woe, woe to you! declares the Lord Jehovah—note
Y sucedió, después de toda tu maldad —¡ay, ay de ti!, declara el Señor Jehová—,
24
וַתִּבְנִי-לָךְ, גָּב; וַתַּעֲשִׂי-לָךְ רָמָה, בְּכָל-רְחוֹב.
that you built yourself a mound, and made yourself a height in every square.note
que te edificaste un montículo, y te hiciste una altura en toda plaza.
25
אֶל-כָּל-רֹאשׁ דֶּרֶךְ, בָּנִית רָמָתֵךְ, וַתְּתַעֲבִי אֶת-יָפְיֵךְ, וַתְּפַשְּׂקִי אֶת-רַגְלַיִךְ לְכָל-עוֹבֵר; וַתַּרְבִּי, אֶת-תזנותך תַּזְנוּתָיִךְ.
At every head of the way you built your height, and made your beauty an abomination, and spread your feet to every passer-by, and multiplied your harlotries.note
En toda cabecera de camino edificaste tu altura, e hiciste de tu hermosura una abominación, y abriste tus pies a todo el que pasaba, y multiplicaste tus prostituciones.
26
וַתִּזְנִי אֶל-בְּנֵי-מִצְרַיִם שְׁכֵנַיִךְ, גִּדְלֵי בָשָׂר; וַתַּרְבִּי אֶת-תַּזְנֻתֵךְ, לְהַכְעִיסֵנִי.
And you played the harlot with the sons of Egypt, your neighbors, great of flesh, and multiplied your harlotry, to provoke me to anger.note
Y te prostituiste con los hijos de Egipto, tus vecinos, grandes de carne, y multiplicaste tu prostitución, para provocarme a ira.
27
וְהִנֵּה נָטִיתִי יָדִי עָלַיִךְ, וָאֶגְרַע חֻקֵּךְ; וָאֶתְּנֵךְ בְּנֶפֶשׁ שֹׂנְאוֹתַיִךְ, בְּנוֹת פְּלִשְׁתִּים--הַנִּכְלָמוֹת, מִדַּרְכֵּךְ זִמָּה.
And look, I stretched out my hand against you and diminished your allotted portion, and gave you over to the appetite of those who hate you, the daughters of the Philistines, who felt disgraced by your way of depravity.note
Y mira, extendí mi mano contra ti y disminuí tu porción señalada, y te entregué al apetito de las que te aborrecen, las hijas de los filisteos, que se sentían afrentadas por tu camino de perversidad.
28
וַתִּזְנִי אֶל-בְּנֵי אַשּׁוּר, מִבִּלְתִּי שָׂבְעָתֵךְ; וַתִּזְנִים, וְגַם לֹא שָׂבָעַתְּ.
And you played the harlot with the sons of Assyria, because you were not satisfied; you played the harlot with them, and still you were not satisfied.note
Y te prostituiste con los hijos de Asiria, porque no te saciabas; te prostituiste con ellos, y aun así no te saciaste.
29
וַתַּרְבִּי אֶת-תַּזְנוּתֵךְ אֶל-אֶרֶץ כְּנַעַן, כַּשְׂדִּימָה; וְגַם-בְּזֹאת, לֹא שָׂבָעַתְּ.
And you multiplied your harlotry to the land of Canaan, to Chaldea; and even with this you were not satisfied.note
Y multiplicaste tu prostitución hacia la tierra de Canaán, a Caldea; y ni aun con esto te saciaste.
30
מָה אֲמֻלָה לִבָּתֵךְ, נְאֻם אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה--בַּעֲשׂוֹתֵךְ, אֶת-כָּל-אֵלֶּה, מַעֲשֵׂה אִשָּׁה-זוֹנָה, שַׁלָּטֶת.
How feeble is your heart, declares the Lord Jehovah, when you do all these things, the work of a woman, a domineering harlot,note
¡Qué débil está tu corazón, declara el Señor Jehová, cuando haces todas estas cosas, la obra de una mujer, una prostituta dominante,
31
בִּבְנוֹתַיִךְ גַּבֵּךְ בְּרֹאשׁ כָּל-דֶּרֶךְ, וְרָמָתֵךְ עשיתי עָשִׂית בְּכָל-רְחוֹב; וְלֹא-הייתי הָיִית כַּזּוֹנָה, לְקַלֵּס אֶתְנָן.
when you built your mound at the head of every way, and made your height in every square! Yet you were not like a harlot, in that you scorned a wage.note
cuando edificabas tu montículo en la cabecera de todo camino, y hacías tu altura en toda plaza! Sin embargo, no fuiste como una prostituta, pues despreciabas la paga.
32
הָאִשָּׁה, הַמְּנָאָפֶת--תַּחַת אִישָׁהּ, תִּקַּח אֶת-זָרִים.
The wife who commits adultery! Instead of her husband she takes strangers.note
¡La esposa que comete adulterio! En lugar de su marido toma extraños.
33
לְכָל-זֹנוֹת, יִתְּנוּ-נֵדֶה; וְאַתְּ נָתַתְּ אֶת-נְדָנַיִךְ, לְכָל-מְאַהֲבַיִךְ, וַתִּשְׁחֳדִי אוֹתָם לָבוֹא אֵלַיִךְ מִסָּבִיב, בְּתַזְנוּתָיִךְ.
To all harlots they give a present, but you gave your presents to all your lovers, and bribed them to come to you from all around for your harlotries.note
A todas las prostitutas les dan un regalo, pero tú diste tus regalos a todos tus amantes, y los sobornaste para que vinieran a ti de todo alrededor por tus prostituciones.
34
וַיְהִי-בָךְ הֵפֶךְ מִן-הַנָּשִׁים בְּתַזְנוּתַיִךְ, וְאַחֲרַיִךְ לֹא זוּנָּה; וּבְתִתֵּךְ אֶתְנָן, וְאֶתְנַן לֹא נִתַּן-לָךְ--וַתְּהִי לְהֶפֶךְ.
So in you it was the opposite of other women in your harlotries: no one went after you to play the harlot; and in that you gave a wage and no wage was given to you, you were the opposite.note
Así en ti sucedía lo contrario de otras mujeres en tus prostituciones: nadie iba tras de ti para prostituirse; y en que tú dabas paga y no se te daba paga, eras lo contrario.
35
לָכֵן זוֹנָה, שִׁמְעִי דְּבַר-יְהוָה.  {פ}
Therefore, harlot, hear the word of Jehovah.note
Por tanto, prostituta, oye la palabra de Jehová.
36
כֹּה-אָמַר אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, יַעַן הִשָּׁפֵךְ נְחֻשְׁתֵּךְ וַתִּגָּלֶה עֶרְוָתֵךְ, בְּתַזְנוּתַיִךְ, עַל-מְאַהֲבָיִךְ; וְעַל, כָּל-גִּלּוּלֵי תוֹעֲבוֹתַיִךְ, וְכִדְמֵי בָנַיִךְ, אֲשֶׁר נָתַתְּ לָהֶם.
Thus says the Lord Jehovah: Because your lust was poured out and your nakedness uncovered in your harlotries with your lovers, and because of all the idols of your abominations, and for the blood of your sons that you gave to them,note
Así dice el Señor Jehová: Porque tu lujuria fue derramada y tu desnudez descubierta en tus prostituciones con tus amantes, y a causa de todos los ídolos de tus abominaciones, y por la sangre de tus hijos que les diste,
37
לָכֵן הִנְנִי מְקַבֵּץ אֶת-כָּל-מְאַהֲבַיִךְ, אֲשֶׁר עָרַבְתְּ עֲלֵיהֶם, וְאֵת כָּל-אֲשֶׁר אָהַבְתְּ, עַל כָּל-אֲשֶׁר שָׂנֵאת; וְקִבַּצְתִּי אֹתָם עָלַיִךְ מִסָּבִיב, וְגִלֵּיתִי עֶרְוָתֵךְ אֲלֵהֶם, וְרָאוּ, אֶת-כָּל-עֶרְוָתֵךְ.
therefore look, I am gathering all your lovers to whom you were pleasing, and all whom you loved along with all whom you hated; I will gather them against you from all around, and uncover your nakedness to them, and they will see all your nakedness.note
por tanto, mira, voy a reunir a todos tus amantes a quienes fuiste agradable, y a todos los que amaste junto con todos los que aborreciste; los reuniré contra ti de todo alrededor, y descubriré tu desnudez ante ellos, y verán toda tu desnudez.
38
וּשְׁפַטְתִּיךְ מִשְׁפְּטֵי נֹאֲפוֹת, וְשֹׁפְכֹת דָּם; וּנְתַתִּיךְ, דַּם חֵמָה וְקִנְאָה.
And I will judge you with the judgments of adulteresses and of women who shed blood, and I will give you the blood of fury and jealousy.note
Y te juzgaré con los juicios de las adúlteras y de las que derraman sangre, y te daré la sangre de furia y de celos.
39
וְנָתַתִּי אֹתָךְ בְּיָדָם, וְהָרְסוּ גַבֵּךְ וְנִתְּצוּ רָמֹתַיִךְ, וְהִפְשִׁיטוּ אוֹתָךְ בְּגָדַיִךְ, וְלָקְחוּ כְּלֵי תִפְאַרְתֵּךְ; וְהִנִּיחוּךְ, עֵירֹם וְעֶרְיָה.
And I will give you into their hand, and they will throw down your mound and break down your heights, and strip you of your garments, and take your articles of glory, and leave you naked and bare.note
Y te entregaré en su mano, y derribarán tu montículo y demolerán tus alturas, y te despojarán de tus vestidos, y tomarán tus objetos de gloria, y te dejarán desnuda y descubierta.
40
וְהֶעֱלוּ עָלַיִךְ קָהָל, וְרָגְמוּ אוֹתָךְ בָּאָבֶן; וּבִתְּקוּךְ, בְּחַרְבוֹתָם.
And they will bring up an assembly against you, and stone you with stones, and cut you to pieces with their swords.note
Y harán subir contra ti una asamblea, y te apedrearán con piedras, y te harán pedazos con sus espadas.
41
וְשָׂרְפוּ בָתַּיִךְ, בָּאֵשׁ, וְעָשׂוּ-בָךְ שְׁפָטִים, לְעֵינֵי נָשִׁים רַבּוֹת; וְהִשְׁבַּתִּיךְ, מִזּוֹנָה, וְגַם-אֶתְנַן, לֹא תִתְּנִי-עוֹד.
And they will burn your houses with fire and execute judgments on you before the eyes of many women; and I will make you cease from being a harlot, and you will also give no more wage.note
Y quemarán tus casas con fuego y ejecutarán juicios en ti ante los ojos de muchas mujeres; y haré que dejes de ser prostituta, y tampoco darás más paga.
42
וַהֲנִחֹתִי חֲמָתִי בָּךְ, וְסָרָה קִנְאָתִי מִמֵּךְ; וְשָׁקַטְתִּי, וְלֹא אֶכְעַס עוֹד.
And I will rest my fury on you, and my jealousy will turn away from you; and I will be quiet, and will no longer be angry.note
Y haré reposar mi furia sobre ti, y mis celos se apartarán de ti; y estaré tranquilo, y no me enojaré más.
43
יַעַן, אֲשֶׁר לֹא-זכרתי זָכַרְתְּ אֶת-יְמֵי נְעוּרַיִךְ, וַתִּרְגְּזִי-לִי, בְּכָל-אֵלֶּה; וְגַם-אֲנִי הֵא דַּרְכֵּךְ בְּרֹאשׁ נָתַתִּי, נְאֻם אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, וְלֹא עשיתי עָשִׂית אֶת-הַזִּמָּה, עַל כָּל-תּוֹעֲבֹתָיִךְ.
Because you did not remember the days of your youth, but enraged me with all these things, therefore I too, look, I have brought your way upon your head, declares the Lord Jehovah; and you will not commit this depravity on top of all your abominations.note
Por cuanto no te acordaste de los días de tu juventud, sino que me enfureciste con todas estas cosas, por eso yo también, mira, he hecho recaer tu camino sobre tu cabeza, declara el Señor Jehová; y no cometerás esta perversidad encima de todas tus abominaciones.
44
הִנֵּה, כָּל-הַמֹּשֵׁל, עָלַיִךְ יִמְשֹׁל, לֵאמֹר:  כְּאִמָּה, בִּתָּהּ.
Look, everyone who uses proverbs will use this proverb about you: ‘Like mother, her daughter.’note
Mira, todo el que usa proverbios usará este proverbio contra ti: “Como la madre, su hija”.
45
בַּת-אִמֵּךְ אַתְּ, גֹּעֶלֶת אִישָׁהּ וּבָנֶיהָ; וַאֲחוֹת אֲחוֹתֵךְ אַתְּ, אֲשֶׁר גָּעֲלוּ אַנְשֵׁיהֶן וּבְנֵיהֶן--אִמְּכֶן חִתִּית, וַאֲבִיכֶן אֱמֹרִי.
You are your mother’s daughter, who loathed her husband and her children; and you are your sisters’ sister, who loathed their husbands and their children. Your mother was a Hittite, and your father an Amorite.note
Tú eres hija de tu madre, que aborreció a su marido y a sus hijos; y eres hermana de tus hermanas, que aborrecieron a sus maridos y a sus hijos. La madre de ustedes fue una hitita, y su padre un amorreo.
46
וַאֲחוֹתֵךְ הַגְּדוֹלָה שֹׁמְרוֹן הִיא וּבְנוֹתֶיהָ, הַיּוֹשֶׁבֶת עַל-שְׂמֹאולֵךְ; וַאֲחוֹתֵךְ הַקְּטַנָּה מִמֵּךְ, הַיּוֹשֶׁבֶת מִימִינֵךְ--סְדֹם, וּבְנוֹתֶיהָ.
And your elder sister is Samaria, she and her daughters, who lives on your left; and your younger sister, who lives on your right, is Sodom and her daughters.note
Y tu hermana mayor es Samaria, ella y sus hijas, que habita a tu izquierda; y tu hermana menor, que habita a tu derecha, es Sodoma y sus hijas.
47
וְלֹא בְדַרְכֵיהֶן הָלַכְתְּ, וּכְתוֹעֲבוֹתֵיהֶן עשיתי עָשִׂית:  כִּמְעַט קָט, וַתַּשְׁחִתִי מֵהֵן בְּכָל-דְּרָכָיִךְ.
Yet you did not walk in their ways or do according to their abominations; as if that were only a very little, you became more corrupt than they in all your ways.note
Y no anduviste en sus caminos ni hiciste según sus abominaciones; como si eso fuera muy poca cosa, te corrompiste más que ellas en todos tus caminos.
48
חַי-אָנִי, נְאֻם אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, אִם-עָשְׂתָה סְדֹם אֲחוֹתֵךְ, הִיא וּבְנוֹתֶיהָ--כַּאֲשֶׁר עָשִׂית, אַתְּ וּבְנוֹתָיִךְ.
As I live, declares the Lord Jehovah, your sister Sodom, she and her daughters, has not done as you have done, you and your daughters.note
Vivo yo, declara el Señor Jehová, que tu hermana Sodoma, ella y sus hijas, no ha hecho como has hecho tú, tú y tus hijas.
49
הִנֵּה-זֶה הָיָה, עֲוֺן סְדֹם אֲחוֹתֵךְ:  גָּאוֹן שִׂבְעַת-לֶחֶם וְשַׁלְוַת הַשְׁקֵט, הָיָה לָהּ וְלִבְנוֹתֶיהָ, וְיַד-עָנִי וְאֶבְיוֹן, לֹא הֶחֱזִיקָה.
Look, this was the iniquity of your sister Sodom: pride, fullness of bread and quiet ease were hers and her daughters’, and she did not strengthen the hand of the poor and needy.note
Mira, esta fue la iniquidad de tu hermana Sodoma: orgullo, hartura de pan y tranquilo sosiego tuvieron ella y sus hijas, y no fortaleció la mano del pobre y del necesitado.
50
וַתִּגְבְּהֶינָה, וַתַּעֲשֶׂינָה תוֹעֵבָה לְפָנָי; וָאָסִיר אֶתְהֶן, כַּאֲשֶׁר רָאִיתִי.  {ס}
And they were haughty and committed abomination before me; and I removed them when I saw it.note
Y se ensoberbecieron e hicieron abominación delante de mí; y las quité cuando lo vi.
51
וְשֹׁמְרוֹן, כַּחֲצִי חַטֹּאתַיִךְ לֹא חָטָאָה; וַתַּרְבִּי אֶת-תּוֹעֲבוֹתַיִךְ, מֵהֵנָּה, וַתְּצַדְּקִי אֶת-אחותך אֲחוֹתַיִךְ, בְּכָל-תּוֹעֲבֹתַיִךְ אֲשֶׁר עשיתי עָשִׂית.
And Samaria did not sin even half your sins; you multiplied your abominations more than they, and justified your sisters by all your abominations that you did.note
Y Samaria no pecó ni la mitad de tus pecados; multiplicaste tus abominaciones más que ellas, e hiciste justas a tus hermanas con todas las abominaciones que hiciste.
52
גַּם-אַתְּ שְׂאִי כְלִמָּתֵךְ, אֲשֶׁר פִּלַּלְתְּ לַאֲחוֹתֵךְ, בְּחַטֹּאתַיִךְ אֲשֶׁר-הִתְעַבְתְּ מֵהֵן, תִּצְדַּקְנָה מִמֵּךְ; וְגַם-אַתְּ בּוֹשִׁי וּשְׂאִי כְלִמָּתֵךְ, בְּצַדֶּקְתֵּךְ אַחְיוֹתֵךְ.
You too, bear your disgrace, you who have pleaded for your sisters. Through your sins, in which you acted more abominably than they, they are more righteous than you. So you too, be ashamed and bear your disgrace, in that you have justified your sisters.note
Tú también, carga con tu afrenta, tú que has abogado por tus hermanas. Por tus pecados, en los que actuaste de modo más abominable que ellas, ellas son más justas que tú. Así que tú también, avergüénzate y carga con tu afrenta, pues has hecho justas a tus hermanas.
53
וְשַׁבְתִּי, אֶת-שְׁבִיתְהֶן--אֶת-שבית שְׁבוּת סְדֹם וּבְנוֹתֶיהָ, וְאֶת-שבית שְׁבוּת שֹׁמְרוֹן וּבְנוֹתֶיהָ; ושבית וּשְׁבוּת שְׁבִיתַיִךְ, בְּתוֹכָהְנָה.
And I will turn their captivity—the captivity of Sodom and her daughters, and the captivity of Samaria and her daughters—and the captivity of your captivity in their midst,note
Y haré volver su cautiverio —el cautiverio de Sodoma y de sus hijas, y el cautiverio de Samaria y de sus hijas—, y el cautiverio de tu cautiverio en medio de ellas,
54
לְמַעַן, תִּשְׂאִי כְלִמָּתֵךְ, וְנִכְלַמְתְּ, מִכֹּל אֲשֶׁר עָשִׂית--בְּנַחֲמֵךְ, אֹתָן.
so that you may bear your disgrace and be disgraced for all that you have done, in your being a comfort to them.note
para que cargues con tu afrenta y quedes afrentada por todo lo que has hecho, al ser tú un consuelo para ellas.
55
וַאֲחוֹתַיִךְ, סְדֹם וּבְנוֹתֶיהָ תָּשֹׁבְןָ לְקַדְמָתָן, וְשֹׁמְרוֹן וּבְנוֹתֶיהָ, תָּשֹׁבְןָ לְקַדְמָתָן; וְאַתְּ, וּבְנוֹתַיִךְ, תְּשֻׁבֶינָה, לְקַדְמַתְכֶן.
And your sisters, Sodom and her daughters, will return to their former state, and Samaria and her daughters will return to their former state, and you and your daughters will return to your former state.note
Y tus hermanas, Sodoma y sus hijas, volverán a su estado anterior, y Samaria y sus hijas volverán a su estado anterior, y tú y tus hijas volverán a su estado anterior.
56
וְלוֹא הָיְתָה סְדֹם אֲחוֹתֵךְ, לִשְׁמוּעָה בְּפִיךְ, בְּיוֹם, גְּאוֹנָיִךְ.
And your sister Sodom was not something heard from your mouth in the day of your pride,note
Y tu hermana Sodoma no fue cosa que se oyera de tu boca en el día de tu orgullo,
57
בְּטֶרֶם, תִּגָּלֶה רָעָתֵךְ, כְּמוֹ עֵת חֶרְפַּת בְּנוֹת-אֲרָם, וְכָל-סְבִיבוֹתֶיהָ בְּנוֹת פְּלִשְׁתִּים; הַשָּׁאטוֹת אוֹתָךְ, מִסָּבִיב.
before your wickedness was uncovered, as at the time of the reproach of the daughters of Aram and of all around her, the daughters of the Philistines, who despise you on every side.note
antes que se descubriera tu maldad, como en el tiempo del oprobio de las hijas de Aram y de todas las de su alrededor, las hijas de los filisteos, que te desprecian por todos lados.
58
אֶת-זִמָּתֵךְ וְאֶת-תּוֹעֲבוֹתַיִךְ, אַתְּ נְשָׂאתִים--נְאֻם, יְהוָה.  {ס}
Your depravity and your abominations, you have borne them, declares Jehovah.note
Tu perversidad y tus abominaciones, tú las has cargado, declara Jehová.
59
כִּי כֹה אָמַר אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, ועשית וְעָשִׂיתִי אוֹתָךְ כַּאֲשֶׁר עָשִׂית--אֲשֶׁר-בָּזִית אָלָה, לְהָפֵר בְּרִית.
For thus says the Lord Jehovah: I will do with you as you have done, you who despised the oath by breaking the covenant.note
Porque así dice el Señor Jehová: Haré contigo como tú has hecho, tú que despreciaste el juramento al quebrantar el pacto.
60
וְזָכַרְתִּי אֲנִי אֶת-בְּרִיתִי אוֹתָךְ, בִּימֵי נְעוּרָיִךְ; וַהֲקִימוֹתִי לָךְ, בְּרִית עוֹלָם.
But I myself will remember my covenant with you in the days of your youth, and I will establish for you an everlasting covenant.note
Pero yo mismo me acordaré de mi pacto contigo en los días de tu juventud, y estableceré para ti un pacto eterno.
61
וְזָכַרְתְּ אֶת-דְּרָכַיִךְ, וְנִכְלַמְתְּ, בְּקַחְתֵּךְ אֶת-אֲחוֹתַיִךְ הַגְּדֹלוֹת מִמֵּךְ, אֶל-הַקְּטַנּוֹת מִמֵּךְ; וְנָתַתִּי אֶתְהֶן לָךְ לְבָנוֹת, וְלֹא מִבְּרִיתֵךְ.
And you will remember your ways and be disgraced, when you receive your sisters, those older than you together with those younger than you; and I will give them to you as daughters, but not from your covenant.note

Translator’s Notes

Verses 1–5 · Ezekiel 16:1–5 the child thrown into the field

⭐ A city addressed as a woman. Verse 3 names who is spoken to: Jerusalem. The whole speech is one picture, and ‘you’ is feminine and singular nearly throughout. Its subject is what verse 2 calls her abominations.

⭐ Parents. The Amorite and the Hittite are peoples of Canaan (Genesis 10:15–16). Verse 45 returns to them in the reverse order.

⚠ Cleansing. Lemish’i, printed ‘for cleansing’, is uncertain; this form stands in one verse of the Hebrew Bible, this one. The KJV has “to supple thee”, the Douay “for thy health”.

⭐ No eye spared you. The eye that spares and the pity are the pair of 5:11 and 9:10, there said of Jehovah’s eye.

⚠ Loathing of your soul. ‘Your soul’ is the child herself or her life. The verb behind ‘loathing’ returns in verse 45 of a woman who loathed husband and children.

Verses 6–14 · Ezekiel 16:6–14 “live” — the wing — the covenant — the clothing

⭐ Twice. ‘In your blood, live’ is said twice in verse 6, and both are printed. ‘In your blood’ may go with ‘I said’ or with ‘live’. The KJV joins it to the saying: “when thou wast in thy blood, Live”.

⭐ Thrashing about. This form stands in two verses of the Hebrew Bible: verses 6 and 22. The KJV has “polluted”, the NIV “kicking about in your blood”.

⚠ Ornament of ornaments. The Hebrew ‘adi ‘adayim is printed as it stands. The NIV reads it of growing up, “entered puberty”; the KJV has “excellent ornaments”.

⭐ My wing. ‘I spread my wing over you’ uses the words Ruth uses to Boaz, ‘Spread your wing over your handmaid’ (Ruth 3:9). Kanaf is a wing, and also the edge of a garment; the KJV has “skirt”. What follows names the act: an oath and a covenant (brit).

⭐ Tachash. The word of verse 10 is the one for the tabernacle’s covering skins (Exodus 25:5; tachash; Numbers 4:6). What animal or colour it names is not settled. The KJV has “badgers’ skin”, the ASV “sealskin”, the NIV “fine leather”.

⭐ Ring and collar. A ring for the nose is put on Rebekah (Genesis 24:47), and the word for ‘collar’ (ravid) is that of the gold collar put on Joseph (Genesis 41:42).

⭐ Fit. ‘You became fit’ is the verb tsalach, which asked at 15:4 whether charred wood is fit for any work. The KJV has “thou didst prosper into a kingdom”, the NIV “rose to be a queen”.

⚠ Written one way, read another. In the Hebrew lines of this chapter some words appear twice: first the written form without vowels, then the form read aloud. Many of the written forms end like a verb for ‘I’, where ‘you’ (feminine) is read, as in ‘you ate’ in verse 13.

Verses 15–22 · Ezekiel 16:15–22 the gifts turned elsewhere

⭐ One root. ‘Played the harlot’, ‘harlot’ and ‘harlotries’ all come from zanah, and one English family is kept for them here. Earlier pages print the verb also as ‘go whoring’ (6:9) and ‘played the prostitute’ (Genesis 38:24). The spelling taznut stands in 17 verses of the Hebrew Bible, all of them in Ezekiel.

⭐ My gifts. The things used are named as God’s own: ‘my gold’, ‘my silver’, ‘my oil’, ‘my incense’, ‘my bread’, and then ‘my sons’. The foods of verse 19 are those of verse 13. ‘Soothing aroma’ is the phrase for an offering to Jehovah (Leviticus 1:9), said here of what is set before images.

⚠ Hard endings. ‘His it was’ (verse 15) and ‘such things do not come, and it shall not be’ (verse 16) are short and obscure in Hebrew, and are left so. ‘High places of many patches’ are patched or particoloured ones; the KJV has “deckedst thy high places with divers colours”.

⭐ Making them pass. Verse 21 says the children were made to ‘pass over’ to the images, the verb of Leviticus 18:21. This verse does not say ‘through the fire’; Deuteronomy 18:10 does. The KJV supplies “through the fire” here.

Verses 23–34 · Ezekiel 16:23–34 mounds and heights — Egypt, Assyria, Chaldea — the wage

⭐ Three words for the places. ‘Mound’ (gav), ‘height’ (ramah) and, in verse 16, ‘high places’ (bamot) are three different nouns and are kept apart.

⚠ The Hebrew’s own words. Verse 25 says ‘feet’ and verse 26 ‘flesh’, and both are kept as they stand. ‘Made your beauty an abomination’ is the verb of the noun ‘abomination’.

⭐ Depravity. Zimmah (verses 27, 43 and 58) is the word of Leviticus 19:29.

⚠ Canaan or traders? Verse 29 has ‘the land of Canaan, to Chaldea’. ‘Canaan’ can also mean a trader: the ASV has “unto the land of traffic”, the NIV “a land of merchants”. Ezekiel 17:4 sets ‘a land of Canaan’ beside ‘a city of merchants’.

⚠ Feeble heart. The first words of verse 30 are difficult. They are read here, with the KJV’s “How weak is thine heart”, of a weak or languishing heart. Others read anger: the NIV has “I am filled with fury against you”. This form of the word printed ‘feeble’ stands in one verse of the Hebrew Bible.

⭐ A wage. Etnan is a harlot’s pay, the ‘wage of a prostitute’ of Deuteronomy 23:19. The point of verses 31–34 is that here it goes the wrong way. ‘You scorned a wage’ is also read otherwise: the Douay has “by disdain enhanceth her price”. ‘Your presents’ in verse 33 is a form found in one verse of the Hebrew Bible.

Verses 35–43 · Ezekiel 16:35–43 “hear the word” — the sentence

⚠ What was poured out? The word in verse 36 is nechushtekh, which looks like the word for bronze or copper with ‘your’; this form stands in one verse of the Hebrew Bible. The Douay takes it as “thy money”, the KJV as “thy filthiness”, the NIV as “your lust”. The reading printed here follows the last and is not certain.

⭐ Uncovered. ‘Your nakedness’ answers verse 8: what was covered there is uncovered in verses 36 and 37.

⭐ The judgments. The two charges of verse 38 are adultery and shed blood. Stoning (verse 40) is the penalty of several laws, among them Deuteronomy 17:5. Verses 39 and 41 turn from the woman to mounds and houses, so the city shows through the picture.

⚠ Jealousy. Qin’ah is printed ‘zeal’ at 5:13; in a speech about a husband and wife it is printed ‘jealousy’. ‘I will rest my fury’ is the same wording as there.

⚠ The end of verse 43. The Hebrew reads ‘and you will not do the depravity’ or the like. The KJV has “thou shalt not commit this lewdness”; the NIV makes it a question, “Did you not add lewdness to all your other detestable practices?”

⭐ Way on the head. As at 9:10 and 11:21.

Verses 44–50 · Ezekiel 16:44–50 “like mother” — two sisters — Sodom

⭐ Left and right. Facing east, the left hand is north and the right hand south. ‘Daughters’ of a city can mean its dependent towns. ‘Elder’ and ‘younger’ are literally ‘big’ and ‘small’.

⚠ A very little. The phrase kim’at qat stands in one verse of the Hebrew Bible. The KJV has “as if that were a very little thing”; the NIV reads time, “soon”.

⭐ What verse 49 says of Sodom. It lists pride, plenty of bread, quiet ease, and not strengthening the hand of the poor and needy; verse 50 adds haughtiness and ‘abomination’. Genesis 13:13 and Genesis 19:5 tell of Sodom too. Matthew 11:24 says it will be more bearable for the land of Sodom than for Capernaum.

⚠ When I saw it. The words of verse 50 are ‘as I saw’. The KJV has “as I saw good”, the NIV “as you have seen”.

Verses 51–58 · Ezekiel 16:51–58 the sisters “justified” — captivity turned

⭐ Justified. To justify is to make appear in the right: beside her, the sisters look righteous. The word does not say that they were.

⭐ Turning captivity. The phrase of verse 53 is that of Deuteronomy 30:3, and many versions read it of restored fortunes; the NIV has “restore the fortunes”. Sodom is named among those to be restored.

⚠ Aram. The Hebrew printed here has Aram in verse 57, as the KJV’s “Syria”. The NIV has “daughters of Edom”; the two names differ in Hebrew by one similar letter.

Verses 59–63 · Ezekiel 16:59–63 “I will remember my covenant”

⭐ Remembering. Verses 22 and 43 said she did not remember the days of her youth. Here God remembers the covenant of those days (verse 60), and then she remembers (verses 61 and 63). ‘I will remember my covenant’ recalls Leviticus 26:42.

⭐ Everlasting covenant. Brit olam is the phrase of Genesis 9:16 and Genesis 17:7, and ‘establish’ is the verb used with it there.

⚠ Not from your covenant. Verse 61 says the sisters are given as daughters ‘not from your covenant’ without saying more. The KJV has “but not by thy covenant”.

⭐ Atonement. The last verb, from kipper, is the verb of ‘atonement’ (Leviticus 16:30). The KJV has “when I am pacified toward thee”, the ASV “when I have forgiven thee”. The phrase for an ‘opening of the mouth’ stands in two verses of the Hebrew Bible: here and Ezekiel 29:21.

Patterns worth carrying forward

Where this translation keeps the exact / distinct words: “harlot” and its family throughout; “mound”, “height” and “high places” for three nouns; “depravity” at 27, 43 and 58; “naked and bare” at 7, 22 and 39; “passed by you” at 6 and 8.

Echoes paid. The wing of Ruth 3:9; ‘fit’ from 15:4; the eye that does not spare, of 5:11; and fury brought to rest, of 5:13.

Echoes planted. Two sisters again at Ezekiel 23:4. Despising the oath and breaking the covenant again at Ezekiel 17:18.
