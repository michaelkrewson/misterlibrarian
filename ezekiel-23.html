Ezekiel tells of two sisters, standing for Samaria and Jerusalem, their alliances with Assyria, Babylon and Egypt, and the sentence on them.
The chapter gives no date.

וַיְהִי דְבַר-יְהוָה, אֵלַי לֵאמֹר.
And the word of Jehovah came to me, saying:note
Y vino a mí la palabra de Jehová, diciendo:
2
בֶּן-אָדָם--שְׁתַּיִם נָשִׁים, בְּנוֹת אֵם-אַחַת הָיוּ.
“Son of man, there were two women, daughters of one mother.note
«Hijo de hombre, hubo dos mujeres, hijas de una misma madre.
3
וַתִּזְנֶינָה בְמִצְרַיִם, בִּנְעוּרֵיהֶן זָנוּ; שָׁמָּה, מֹעֲכוּ שְׁדֵיהֶן, וְשָׁם עִשּׂוּ, דַּדֵּי בְּתוּלֵיהֶן.
And they played the harlot in Egypt; in their youth they played the harlot. There their breasts were pressed, and there the bosoms of their virginity were handled.note
Y se prostituyeron en Egipto; en su juventud se prostituyeron. Allí fueron apretados sus pechos, y allí fueron manoseados los senos de su virginidad.

And their names were Oholah the elder and Oholibah her sister; and they became mine, and bore sons and daughters. And as for their names, Samaria is Oholah, and Jerusalem is Oholibah.note
Y sus nombres eran Oholá la mayor y Oholibá su hermana; y llegaron a ser mías, y dieron a luz hijos e hijas. Y en cuanto a sus nombres, Samaria es Oholá, y Jerusalén es Oholibá.

And Oholah played the harlot while she was mine; and she lusted after her lovers, after the Assyrians who were near,note
Y Oholá se prostituyó siendo mía; y se apasionó por sus amantes, por los asirios que estaban cerca,

clothed in blue, governors and prefects, desirable young men all of them, horsemen riding on horses.note
vestidos de azul, gobernadores y prefectos, jóvenes codiciables todos ellos, jinetes que montan caballos.
7

Y sus prostituciones desde Egipto no las dejó; porque se habían acostado con ella en su juventud, y ellos habían manoseado los senos de su virginidad, y habían derramado su prostitución sobre ella.

Therefore I gave her into the hand of her lovers, into the hand of the sons of Assyria, after whom she lusted.note
Por eso la entregué en mano de sus amantes, en mano de los hijos de Asiria, por quienes se apasionó.
10
הֵמָּה, גִּלּוּ עֶרְוָתָהּ--בָּנֶיהָ וּבְנוֹתֶיהָ לָקָחוּ, וְאוֹתָהּ בַּחֶרֶב הָרָגוּ; וַתְּהִי-שֵׁם, לַנָּשִׁים, וּשְׁפוּטִים, עָשׂוּ בָהּ.  {ס}
They uncovered her nakedness; they took her sons and her daughters, and her they killed with the sword; and she became a name among women, and they executed judgments on her.note
Ellos descubrieron su desnudez; tomaron a sus hijos y a sus hijas, y a ella la mataron a espada; y vino a tener nombre entre las mujeres, y ejecutaron juicios en ella.
11
וַתֵּרֶא אֲחוֹתָהּ אָהֳלִיבָה, וַתַּשְׁחֵת עַגְבָתָהּ מִמֶּנָּה; וְאֶת-תַּזְנוּתֶיהָ--מִזְּנוּנֵי, אֲחוֹתָהּ.
And her sister Oholibah saw it, and she was more corrupt in her lust than she, and in her harlotries more than her sister’s whoredoms.note
Y lo vio su hermana Oholibá, y se corrompió en su pasión más que ella, y en sus prostituciones más que las fornicaciones de su hermana.
12
אֶל-בְּנֵי אַשּׁוּר עָגָבָה פַּחוֹת וּסְגָנִים קְרֹבִים, לְבֻשֵׁי מִכְלוֹל, פָּרָשִׁים, רֹכְבֵי סוּסִים--בַּחוּרֵי חֶמֶד, כֻּלָּם.
She lusted after the sons of Assyria, governors and prefects who were near, clothed in full splendor, horsemen riding on horses, desirable young men all of them.note
Se apasionó por los hijos de Asiria, gobernadores y prefectos que estaban cerca, vestidos con todo esplendor, jinetes que montan caballos, jóvenes codiciables todos ellos.
13
וָאֵרֶא, כִּי נִטְמָאָה:  דֶּרֶךְ אֶחָד, לִשְׁתֵּיהֶן.
And I saw that she had defiled herself; the two of them had one way.note
Y vi que se había contaminado; las dos tenían un mismo camino.
14
וַתּוֹסֶף, אֶל-תַּזְנוּתֶיהָ; וַתֵּרֶא, אַנְשֵׁי מְחֻקֶּה עַל-הַקִּיר, צַלְמֵי כשדיים כַשְׂדִּים, חֲקֻקִים בַּשָּׁשַׁר.
And she added to her harlotries: she saw men carved on the wall, images of Chaldeans engraved in vermilion,note
Y aumentó sus prostituciones: vio hombres grabados en la pared, imágenes de caldeos talladas en bermellón,
15
חֲגוֹרֵי אֵזוֹר בְּמָתְנֵיהֶם, סְרוּחֵי טְבוּלִים בְּרָאשֵׁיהֶם--מַרְאֵה שָׁלִשִׁים, כֻּלָּם:  דְּמוּת בְּנֵי-בָבֶל כַּשְׂדִּים, אֶרֶץ מוֹלַדְתָּם.
girded with belts on their loins, with flowing turbans on their heads, all of them with the look of officers, a likeness of the sons of Babylon, whose land of birth is Chaldea.note
ceñidos con cintos en sus lomos, con turbantes colgantes en sus cabezas, todos ellos con aspecto de oficiales, semejanza de los hijos de Babilonia, cuya tierra natal es Caldea.

Y se apasionó por ellos al verlos sus ojos, y les envió mensajeros a Caldea.

And she uncovered her harlotries and uncovered her nakedness; and my soul was wrenched away from her, as my soul had been wrenched away from her sister.note
Y ella descubrió sus prostituciones y descubrió su desnudez; y mi alma se arrancó de ella, como mi alma se había arrancado de su hermana.
19
וַתַּרְבֶּה, אֶת-תַּזְנוּתֶיהָ, לִזְכֹּר אֶת-יְמֵי נְעוּרֶיהָ, אֲשֶׁר זָנְתָה בְּאֶרֶץ מִצְרָיִם.
And she multiplied her harlotries, remembering the days of her youth, when she played the harlot in the land of Egypt.note
Y multiplicó sus prostituciones, acordándose de los días de su juventud, cuando se prostituyó en la tierra de Egipto.
20
וַתַּעְגְּבָה, עַל פִּלַגְשֵׁיהֶם, אֲשֶׁר בְּשַׂר-חֲמוֹרִים בְּשָׂרָם, וְזִרְמַת סוּסִים זִרְמָתָם.
And she lusted after their paramours, whose flesh is the flesh of donkeys and whose flow is the flow of horses.note
Y se apasionó por sus queridos, cuya carne es carne de asnos y cuyo flujo es flujo de caballos.
21

Así dice el Señor Jehová: La copa de tu hermana beberás, la honda y la ancha; serás cosa de risa y de burla; es mucho lo que cabe.
33
שִׁכָּרוֹן וְיָגוֹן, תִּמָּלֵאִי; כּוֹס שַׁמָּה וּשְׁמָמָה, כּוֹס אֲחוֹתֵךְ שֹׁמְרוֹן.
With drunkenness and sorrow you will be filled, a cup of horror and desolation, the cup of your sister Samaria.note
De embriaguez y de dolor serás llenada, copa de espanto y desolación, la copa de tu hermana Samaria.
34
וְשָׁתִית אוֹתָהּ וּמָצִית, וְאֶת-חֲרָשֶׂיהָ תְּגָרֵמִי--וְשָׁדַיִךְ תְּנַתֵּקִי:  כִּי אֲנִי דִבַּרְתִּי, נְאֻם אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה.  {ס}
And you will drink it and drain it, and its shards you will gnaw, and your breasts you will tear; for I have spoken, declares the Lord Jehovah.note
Y la beberás y la apurarás, y roerás sus tiestos, y te desgarrarás los pechos; porque yo he hablado, declara el Señor Jehová.
35
לָכֵן, כֹּה אָמַר אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, יַעַן שָׁכַחַתְּ אוֹתִי, וַתַּשְׁלִיכִי אוֹתִי אַחֲרֵי גַוֵּךְ; וְגַם-אַתְּ שְׂאִי זִמָּתֵךְ, וְאֶת-תַּזְנוּתָיִךְ.  {ס}
Therefore thus says the Lord Jehovah: Because you have forgotten me and cast me behind your back, you too bear your depravity and your harlotries.”note
Por tanto, así dice el Señor Jehová: Por cuanto me has olvidado y me has echado detrás de tu espalda, carga tú también tu perversidad y tus prostituciones».
36
וַיֹּאמֶר יְהוָה, אֵלַי, בֶּן-אָדָם, הֲתִשְׁפּוֹט אֶת-אָהֳלָה וְאֶת-אָהֳלִיבָה; וְהַגֵּד לָהֶן, אֵת תוֹעֲבוֹתֵיהֶן.
And Jehovah said to me: “Son of man, will you judge Oholah and Oholibah? Then tell them their abominations.note
Y Jehová me dijo: «Hijo de hombre, ¿juzgarás a Oholá y a Oholibá? Diles, pues, sus abominaciones.
37
כִּי נִאֵפוּ, וְדָם בִּידֵיהֶן, וְאֶת-גִּלּוּלֵיהֶן, נִאֵפוּ; וְגַם אֶת-בְּנֵיהֶן אֲשֶׁר יָלְדוּ-לִי, הֶעֱבִירוּ לָהֶם לְאָכְלָה.
For they have committed adultery, and blood is on their hands, and with their idols they have committed adultery; and even their sons, whom they bore to me, they have made pass over to them for food.note
Porque han cometido adulterio, y hay sangre en sus manos, y con sus ídolos han cometido adulterio; y aun a sus hijos, que me dieron a luz, los han hecho pasar a ellos como comida.
38
עוֹד זֹאת, עָשׂוּ לִי:  טִמְּאוּ אֶת-מִקְדָּשִׁי בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא, וְאֶת-שַׁבְּתוֹתַי חִלֵּלוּ.
This too they have done to me: they have defiled my sanctuary on that day, and my Sabbaths they have profaned.note
Esto también me han hecho: han contaminado mi santuario en aquel día, y mis sábados han profanado.
39
וּבְשַׁחֲטָם אֶת-בְּנֵיהֶם לְגִלּוּלֵיהֶם, וַיָּבֹאוּ אֶל-מִקְדָּשִׁי בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא לְחַלְּלוֹ; וְהִנֵּה-כֹה עָשׂוּ, בְּתוֹךְ בֵּיתִי.
And when they had slaughtered their sons to their idols, they came into my sanctuary on that day to profane it; and look, thus they have done in the midst of my house.note
Y cuando habían degollado a sus hijos para sus ídolos, entraban en mi santuario aquel mismo día para profanarlo; y mira, así han hecho en medio de mi casa.
40
וְאַף, כִּי תִשְׁלַחְנָה לַאֲנָשִׁים, בָּאִים, מִמֶּרְחָק--אֲשֶׁר מַלְאָךְ שָׁלוּחַ אֲלֵיהֶם, וְהִנֵּה-בָאוּ, לַאֲשֶׁר רָחַצְתְּ כָּחַלְתְּ עֵינַיִךְ, וְעָדִית עֶדִי.
And what is more, they sent for men coming from far away, to whom a messenger was sent, and look, they came—those for whom you washed yourself, painted your eyes, and decked yourself with ornaments;note
Y más aún: enviaron por hombres que vinieran de lejos, a quienes se envió un mensajero, y mira, vinieron, aquellos para quienes te lavaste, te pintaste los ojos y te engalanaste con adornos;
41
וְיָשַׁבְתְּ עַל-מִטָּה כְבוּדָּה, וְשֻׁלְחָן עָרוּךְ לְפָנֶיהָ; וּקְטָרְתִּי וְשַׁמְנִי, שַׂמְתְּ עָלֶיהָ.
and you sat on a splendid couch, with a table set before it, and my incense and my oil you put on it.note
y te sentaste en un lecho espléndido, con una mesa dispuesta delante de él, y pusiste sobre ella mi incienso y mi aceite.
42
וְקוֹל הָמוֹן, שָׁלֵו בָהּ, וְאֶל-אֲנָשִׁים מֵרֹב אָדָם, מוּבָאִים סובאים סָבָאִים מִמִּדְבָּר; וַיִּתְּנוּ צְמִידִים אֶל-יְדֵיהֶן, וַעֲטֶרֶת תִּפְאֶרֶת עַל-רָאשֵׁיהֶן.
And the sound of a carefree crowd was in her; and with men of the common mass Sabeans were brought from the wilderness, and they put bracelets on the women’s hands and crowns of glory on their heads.note
Y había en ella voz de una muchedumbre despreocupada; y con los hombres del común de la gente fueron traídos sabeos del desierto, y pusieron brazaletes en las manos de las mujeres y coronas de gloria en sus cabezas.
43
וָאֹמַר, לַבָּלָה נִאוּפִים; עת עַתָּה יזנה יִזְנוּ תַזְנוּתֶהָ, וָהִיא.
And I said of her who was worn out with adulteries: Now they will commit her harlotries with her, even her!note
Y dije de la gastada por los adulterios: ¡Ahora cometerán con ella sus prostituciones, y aun ella!
44
וַיָּבוֹא אֵלֶיהָ, כְּבוֹא אֶל-אִשָּׁה זוֹנָה; כֵּן בָּאוּ, אֶל-אָהֳלָה וְאֶל-אָהֳלִיבָה--אִשֹּׁת, הַזִּמָּה.
And they went in to her, as one goes in to a woman who is a prostitute; so they went in to Oholah and to Oholibah, women of depravity.note
Y se llegaron a ella, como quien se llega a una mujer prostituta; así se llegaron a Oholá y a Oholibá, mujeres de perversidad.
45
וַאֲנָשִׁים צַדִּיקִם, הֵמָּה יִשְׁפְּטוּ אוֹתְהֶם--מִשְׁפַּט נֹאֲפוֹת, וּמִשְׁפַּט שֹׁפְכוֹת דָּם:  כִּי נֹאֲפֹת הֵנָּה, וְדָם בִּידֵיהֶן.  {ס}
But righteous men, they will judge them with the judgment of adulteresses and the judgment of women who shed blood; for adulteresses they are, and blood is on their hands.note
Pero hombres justos, ellos las juzgarán con el juicio de las adúlteras y el juicio de las que derraman sangre; porque adúlteras son, y hay sangre en sus manos.
46
כִּי כֹּה אָמַר, אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה:  הַעֲלֵה עֲלֵיהֶם קָהָל, וְנָתֹן אֶתְהֶן לְזַעֲוָה וְלָבַז.
For thus says the Lord Jehovah: Bring up an assembly against them, and give them over to horror and to plunder.note
Porque así dice el Señor Jehová: Hagan subir contra ellas una asamblea, y entréguenlas al espanto y al saqueo.
47
וְרָגְמוּ עֲלֵיהֶן אֶבֶן קָהָל, וּבָרֵא אוֹתְהֶן בְּחַרְבוֹתָם; בְּנֵיהֶם וּבְנוֹתֵיהֶם יַהֲרֹגוּ, וּבָתֵּיהֶן בָּאֵשׁ יִשְׂרֹפוּ.
And the assembly will stone them with stones, and cut them down with their swords; their sons and their daughters they will kill, and their houses they will burn with fire.note
Y la asamblea las apedreará con piedras, y las tajará con sus espadas; matarán a sus hijos y a sus hijas, y quemarán con fuego sus casas.
48
וְהִשְׁבַּתִּי זִמָּה, מִן-הָאָרֶץ; וְנִוַּסְּרוּ כָּל-הַנָּשִׁים, וְלֹא תַעֲשֶׂינָה כְּזִמַּתְכֶנָה.
And I will make depravity cease from the land, and all women will take warning and not do according to your depravity.note
Y haré que cese de la tierra la perversidad, y todas las mujeres quedarán advertidas y no harán conforme a la perversidad de ustedes.
49
וְנָתְנוּ זִמַּתְכֶנָה עֲלֵיכֶן, וַחֲטָאֵי גִלּוּלֵיכֶן תִּשֶּׂאינָה; וִידַעְתֶּם, כִּי אֲנִי אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה.  {פ}
And your depravity will be laid upon you, and the sins of your idols you will bear; and you will know that I am the Lord Jehovah.”note
Y se pondrá sobre ustedes su perversidad, y cargarán los pecados de sus ídolos; y sabrán que yo soy el Señor Jehová».

Translator’s Notes

Verses 1–4 · Ezekiel 23:1–4 two sisters, Oholah and Oholibah

⚠ The language of this chapter. The chapter speaks of two cities’ alliances and idols as a woman’s unfaithfulness, in sexual terms that are blunt in the Hebrew. This translation keeps the Hebrew’s own words and adds no detail. Versions differ in how far they soften or spell out some lines.

⭐ Two sisters. Chapter 16 called Samaria Jerusalem’s elder sister (16:46). Here the two are told side by side. ‘Played the harlot’ is that chapter’s verb.

⚠ The names. The names are usually explained from ohel, ‘tent’: Oholah, ‘her tent’, and Oholibah, ‘my tent is in her’. The chapter itself explains only which city each one is.

Verses 5–10 · Ezekiel 23:5–10 Oholah and Assyria

⭐ Lusted. Agav, a rare verb of burning desire that runs through verses 5 to 20. ‘Lovers’ are those she sought.

⚠ Near. The Assyrians are qerovim, ‘near ones’. The KJV has “the Assyrians her neighbours”, the NIV “warriors”.

⭐ Splendid young men. Blue clothing, governors, horsemen: ‘desirable young men all of them’ is said three times (verses 6, 12, 23), the third time of an army coming against her.

⭐ Idols. The political tie and the worship go together: ‘with all their idols she defiled herself’.

⭐ A name. She became ‘a name’ among women, something spoken of. The NWT 1984 has “infamy to women”.

Verses 11–21 · Ezekiel 23:11–21 Oholibah, Assyria, Babylon and Egypt

⭐ Three nouns. ‘Harlotries’ is taznut; ‘whoredoms’ (verses 11, 29) is zenunim; ‘prostitution’ (verse 27) is zenut.

⭐ On the wall. She saw figures ‘carved on the wall’, the words of the room in 8:10. The word for vermilion, shashar, stands in two verses of the Hebrew Bible, here and Jeremiah 22:14.

⭐ Officers. Shalishim, as over Pharaoh’s chariots at Exodus 14:7.

⭐ Wrenched away. The verb of verses 17 and 18 (and, in a related form, 22 and 28) is akin to the one said of Jacob’s thigh, put out of joint (Genesis 32:26).

⚠ Verses 20 and 21. The Hebrew is coarse on purpose. ‘Paramours’ is the word elsewhere used for concubines, here of men. ‘Flow’ is a noun of the root used for a sweeping flood at Psalm 90:5. ‘Depravity’ is the word of chapters 16 and 22. Verse 21 turns suddenly from ‘she’ to ‘you’.

Verses 22–27 · Ezekiel 23:22–27 the lovers turned against her

⭐ Pekod, Shoa, Koa. Named with Babylon and the Chaldeans; usually taken as peoples to the east of Babylonia.

⚠ Officers and men of renown. ‘Men of renown’ is literally ‘called ones’. The NWT 1984 has “warriors and summoned ones”, the KJV “great lords and renowned”.

⚠ With weapons. The first word of verse 24’s list, hotsen, stands in one verse of the Hebrew Bible, and its meaning is uncertain. The ASV has “with weapons, chariots, and wagons”, the KJV “with chariots, wagons, and wheels”.

⭐ Jealousy and fury. Jealousy as at 16:38 and 16:42. Verse 26 repeats the stripping of 16:39. The mutilation of verse 25 is stated and not described further.

⭐ Egypt remembered no more. The story began in Egypt (verse 3) and the sentence ends it there.

Verses 28–31 · Ezekiel 23:28–31 into the hand of those she hates

⭐ Naked and bare. The pair of 16:39, and of the days of youth at 16:22.

⭐ Went whoring after. The words of 20:30; verse 30 names what the figure has meant all along, ‘after the nations’ and ‘their idols’.

⭐ Her cup. The sister’s way leads to the sister’s cup.

Verses 32–35 · Ezekiel 23:32–35 the cup of Samaria

⭐ A cup. Kos, the cup of judgment: Jeremiah 25:15 has the cup of the wine of fury, and Lamentations 4:21 says the cup will pass to Edom. Verses 32 to 34 read as a short song.

⚠ Its shards. ‘Gnaw’ is a rare word. The NIV has “chew on its pieces”.

⭐ Behind your back. ‘Behind your back’ stands in three verses of the Hebrew Bible: 1 Kings 14:9, Isaiah 38:17 and here. In Hezekiah’s song it is God who casts sins behind his back; here it is God who has been cast there.

Verses 36–39 · Ezekiel 23:36–39 ‘will you judge Oholah and Oholibah?’

⭐ Will you judge? The question of 20:4 and 22:2, here with ‘tell them’ where those verses have ‘make known’.

⭐ Adultery and blood. The two charges of 16:38. Adultery is said ‘with their idols’.

⭐ Pass over. As at 16:21; the Hebrew of verse 37 does not say ‘fire’. The law forbade giving a child to Molech in these terms (Leviticus 18:21). The NWT 1984 has “they made pass through [the fire] to them as food”.

⭐ The same day. The offence is joined: the sanctuary entered on the very day of the slaughter, and the Sabbaths of chapters 20 and 22.

Verses 40–44 · Ezekiel 23:40–44 messengers, a couch and a table

⚠ Who is spoken to? The Hebrew moves from ‘they’ to a feminine singular ‘you’ in verse 40 and back to ‘her’ and ‘their’. The translation follows it.

⭐ Painted eyes. The verb stands in one verse of the Hebrew Bible. Jezebel painted her eyes with another word (2 Kings 9:30).

⭐ My incense and my oil. As at 16:18, what belongs to God is set before others. The crown of glory is that of 16:12.

⚠ Sabeans or drunkards. Verse 42 has one form written and another read (ketiv/qere), and both are printed above; this translation follows the reading tradition. The KJV has “Sabeans from the wilderness”, the ASV “drunkards from the wilderness”.

⚠ Verse 43. It is very hard, and has written and read forms too. The KJV makes it a question, “Will they now commit whoredoms with her”.

Verses 45–49 · Ezekiel 23:45–49 the judgment of the assembly

⚠ Righteous men. The verses do not say who they are.

⭐ The sentence. An assembly, stoning and swords, as at 16:40. ‘Horror’ and ‘plunder’ are the words of curses on the nation. ‘Cut them down’ has the root of ‘cut out’ at 21:24.

⭐ All women will take warning. The figure ends as it began, with women; verse 49 turns to ‘you’ and names the thing meant, ‘the sins of your idols’.

Patterns worth carrying forward

Where this translation keeps the exact / distinct words: “harlotries”, “whoredoms” and “prostitution” for three nouns; “wrenched away” at 17, 18, 22 and 28; “desirable young men” at 6, 12 and 23; “depravity” through verses 21–49; and “cup” at 31–33.

Echoes paid. The sisters and the sentence of chapter 16; the carved wall of 8:10; the question of 22:2; and 1 Kings 14:9’s ‘behind your back’.

Echoes planted. The day the king of Babylon laid siege to Jerusalem, at Ezekiel 24:2.
