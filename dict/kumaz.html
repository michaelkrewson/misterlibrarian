📖 Dictionary

kumaz כּוּמָז kumaz

An obscure gold ornament, occurring in only two verses of the whole Hebrew Bible — Exodus 35:22 (already on these pages) and Numbers 31:50 (now on these pages) — and this translation renders the identical word two different ways in its only two appearances, an honest admission rather than a resolved decision. At Exodus 35:22, with no competing 'bracelet' word in that verse, this translation read kumaz as 'bracelets'; here it stands in a list that already has a genuine bracelet (tzamid) two words earlier, so this translation renders it 'pendants' instead, purely to avoid printing one English word for two different Hebrew ones in the same sentence. The whole shelf is scattered on it: KJV 'tablets', ASV 'armlets', NIV 'necklaces', NWT 1984 'female ornaments', Geneva 'ornaments of the legs' — five versions, five different guesses, and no consensus on what the object actually looked like. → first discussed at Num 31:50
