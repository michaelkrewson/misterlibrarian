Ezekiel 31 — The Mister Translation

💬 Discuss this chapter
1
וַיְהִי, בְּאַחַת עֶשְׂרֵה שָׁנָה, בַּשְּׁלִישִׁי, בְּאֶחָד לַחֹדֶשׁ; הָיָה דְבַר-יְהוָה, אֵלַי לֵאמֹר.
And it happened in the eleventh year, in the third month, on the first of the month, that the word of Jehovah came to me, saying:note
Y sucedió en el año undécimo, en el mes tercero, en el primero del mes, que vino a mí la palabra de Jehová, diciendo:
2
בֶּן-אָדָם, אֱמֹר אֶל-פַּרְעֹה מֶלֶךְ-מִצְרַיִם וְאֶל-הֲמוֹנוֹ:  אֶל-מִי, דָּמִיתָ בְגָדְלֶךָ.
“Son of man, say to Pharaoh king of Egypt and to his multitude: Whom are you like in your greatness?note
«Hijo de hombre, di al faraón, rey de Egipto, y a su multitud: ¿A quién te pareces en tu grandeza?
3
הִנֵּה אַשּׁוּר אֶרֶז בַּלְּבָנוֹן, יְפֵה עָנָף וְחֹרֶשׁ מֵצַל--וּגְבַהּ קוֹמָה; וּבֵין עֲבֹתִים, הָיְתָה צַמַּרְתּוֹ.
Look, Assyria was a cedar in Lebanon, beautiful of boughs, a shading thicket, and high in height; and its treetop was among the thick foliage.note
Mira, Asiria era un cedro en el Líbano, de hermoso ramaje, espesura que daba sombra, y alto de estatura; y su copa estaba entre el follaje espeso.
4
מַיִם גִּדְּלוּהוּ, תְּהוֹם רֹמְמָתְהוּ; אֶת-נַהֲרֹתֶיהָ, הֹלֵךְ סְבִיבוֹת מַטָּעָהּ, וְאֶת-תְּעָלֹתֶיהָ שִׁלְחָה, אֶל כָּל-עֲצֵי הַשָּׂדֶה.
Waters made it great, the deep made it grow high; with her rivers she went round about its planting, and she sent out her conduits to all the trees of the field.note
Las aguas lo hicieron grande, el abismo lo hizo crecer en alto; con sus ríos iba alrededor de su plantío, y enviaba sus acequias a todos los árboles del campo.
5
עַל-כֵּן גָּבְהָא קֹמָתוֹ, מִכֹּל עֲצֵי הַשָּׂדֶה; וַתִּרְבֶּינָה סַרְעַפֹּתָיו וַתֶּאֱרַכְנָה פֹארֹתָו, מִמַּיִם רַבִּים--בְּשַׁלְּחוֹ.
Therefore its height was higher than all the trees of the field; and its forks multiplied, and its sprigs grew long from many waters, when it shot them out.note
Por eso su estatura fue más alta que la de todos los árboles del campo; y se multiplicaron sus horquillas, y se alargaron sus retoños por las muchas aguas, cuando él los echaba.
6
בִּסְעַפֹּתָיו קִנְנוּ, כָּל-עוֹף הַשָּׁמַיִם, וְתַחַת פֹּארֹתָיו יָלְדוּ, כֹּל חַיַּת הַשָּׂדֶה; וּבְצִלּוֹ, יֵשְׁבוּ, כֹּל, גּוֹיִם רַבִּים.
In its forks all the birds of the heavens made nests, and under its sprigs all the beasts of the field gave birth, and in its shade sat all the many nations.note
En sus horquillas anidaron todas las aves de los cielos, y debajo de sus retoños parían todas las bestias del campo, y a su sombra se sentaban todas las muchas naciones.
7
וַיְּיִף בְּגָדְלוֹ, בְּאֹרֶךְ דָּלִיּוֹתָיו:  כִּי-הָיָה שָׁרְשׁוֹ, אֶל-מַיִם רַבִּים.
And it was beautiful in its greatness, in the length of its branches, for its root was by many waters.note
Y era hermoso en su grandeza, en la largura de sus ramas, porque su raíz estaba junto a muchas aguas.
8
אֲרָזִים לֹא-עֲמָמֻהוּ, בְּגַן-אֱלֹהִים--בְּרוֹשִׁים לֹא דָמוּ אֶל-סְעַפֹּתָיו, וְעַרְמֹנִים לֹא-הָיוּ כְּפֹרֹאתָיו:  כָּל-עֵץ, בְּגַן-אֱלֹהִים--לֹא-דָמָה אֵלָיו, בְּיָפְיוֹ.
Cedars in the garden of God could not hide it; cypresses were not like its forks, and plane trees were not like its sprigs; no tree in the garden of God was like it in its beauty.note
Los cedros en el jardín de Dios no lo podían ocultar; los cipreses no eran como sus horquillas, ni los plátanos como sus retoños; ningún árbol en el jardín de Dios se le parecía en su hermosura.
9
יָפֶה עֲשִׂיתִיו, בְּרֹב דָּלִיּוֹתָיו; וַיְקַנְאֻהוּ, כָּל-עֲצֵי-עֵדֶן, אֲשֶׁר, בְּגַן הָאֱלֹהִים.  {פ}
I made it beautiful in the abundance of its branches, and all the trees of Eden that were in the garden of God envied it.note
Lo hice hermoso en la abundancia de sus ramas, y lo envidiaban todos los árboles de Edén que había en el jardín de Dios.
10
לָכֵן, כֹּה אָמַר אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, יַעַן, אֲשֶׁר גָּבַהְתָּ בְּקוֹמָה; וַיִּתֵּן צַמַּרְתּוֹ אֶל-בֵּין עֲבוֹתִים, וְרָם לְבָבוֹ בְּגָבְהוֹ.
Therefore thus says the Lord Jehovah: Because you were high in height, and it set its treetop among the thick foliage, and its heart was lifted up in its tallness,note
Por tanto, así dice el Señor Jehová: Por cuanto fuiste alto de estatura, y él puso su copa entre el follaje espeso, y su corazón se elevó en su altura,
11
וְאֶתְּנֵהוּ--בְּיַד, אֵיל גּוֹיִם; עָשׂוֹ יַעֲשֶׂה לוֹ, כְּרִשְׁעוֹ גֵּרַשְׁתִּהוּ.
I will give it into the hand of the mighty one of the nations; he will surely deal with it; according to its wickedness I have driven it out.note
lo daré en mano del poderoso de las naciones; de cierto lo tratará; conforme a su maldad lo he expulsado.
12
וַיִּכְרְתֻהוּ זָרִים עָרִיצֵי גוֹיִם, וַיִּטְּשֻׁהוּ; אֶל-הֶהָרִים וּבְכָל-גֵּאָיוֹת נָפְלוּ דָלִיּוֹתָיו, וַתִּשָּׁבַרְנָה פֹרֹאתָיו בְּכֹל אֲפִיקֵי הָאָרֶץ, וַיֵּרְדוּ מִצִּלּוֹ כָּל-עַמֵּי הָאָרֶץ, וַיִּטְּשֻׁהוּ.
And strangers, the most ruthless of the nations, cut it down and abandoned it; on the mountains and in all the valleys its branches fell, and its sprigs were broken in all the streambeds of the land; and all the peoples of the earth went down from its shade and abandoned it.note
Y extraños, los más despiadados de las naciones, lo cortaron y lo abandonaron; sobre los montes y en todos los valles cayeron sus ramas, y sus retoños fueron quebrados en todos los cauces de la tierra; y todos los pueblos de la tierra bajaron de su sombra y lo abandonaron.
13
עַל-מַפַּלְתּוֹ יִשְׁכְּנוּ, כָּל-עוֹף הַשָּׁמָיִם; וְאֶל-פֹּרֹאתָיו הָיוּ, כֹּל חַיַּת הַשָּׂדֶה.
On its fall all the birds of the heavens dwell, and by its sprigs are all the beasts of the field,note
Sobre su caída habitan todas las aves de los cielos, y junto a sus retoños están todas las bestias del campo,
14
לְמַעַן אֲשֶׁר לֹא-יִגְבְּהוּ בְקוֹמָתָם כָּל-עֲצֵי-מַיִם, וְלֹא-יִתְּנוּ אֶת-צַמַּרְתָּם אֶל-בֵּין עֲבֹתִים, וְלֹא-יַעַמְדוּ אֵלֵיהֶם בְּגָבְהָם, כָּל-שֹׁתֵי מָיִם:  כִּי-כֻלָּם נִתְּנוּ לַמָּוֶת אֶל-אֶרֶץ תַּחְתִּית, בְּתוֹךְ בְּנֵי אָדָם--אֶל-יוֹרְדֵי בוֹר.  {פ}
so that no trees by the water may grow high in their height, nor set their treetop among the thick foliage, nor their mighty ones stand in their tallness, all that drink water; for all of them are given to death, to the land below, in the midst of the sons of man, to those who go down to the pit.note
para que ningún árbol junto a las aguas crezca alto en su estatura, ni ponga su copa entre el follaje espeso, ni se mantengan en su altura sus poderosos, todos los que beben aguas; porque todos ellos son entregados a la muerte, a la tierra de abajo, en medio de los hijos de hombre, a los que descienden a la fosa.
15
כֹּה-אָמַר אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, בְּיוֹם רִדְתּוֹ שְׁאוֹלָה הֶאֱבַלְתִּי כִּסֵּתִי עָלָיו אֶת-תְּהוֹם, וָאֶמְנַע נַהֲרוֹתֶיהָ, וַיִּכָּלְאוּ מַיִם רַבִּים; וָאַקְדִּר עָלָיו לְבָנוֹן, וְכָל-עֲצֵי הַשָּׂדֶה עָלָיו עֻלְפֶּה.
Thus says the Lord Jehovah: On the day it went down to Sheol I caused mourning; I covered the deep over it, and I withheld her rivers, and many waters were held back; and I darkened Lebanon over it, and all the trees of the field fainted over it.note
Así dice el Señor Jehová: El día en que descendió al Seol, hice hacer duelo; cubrí sobre él el abismo, y retuve sus ríos, y las muchas aguas fueron detenidas; y oscurecí sobre él el Líbano, y todos los árboles del campo desfallecieron por él.
16
מִקּוֹל מַפַּלְתּוֹ הִרְעַשְׁתִּי גוֹיִם, בְּהוֹרִדִי אֹתוֹ שְׁאוֹלָה אֶת-יוֹרְדֵי בוֹר; וַיִּנָּחֲמוּ בְּאֶרֶץ תַּחְתִּית, כָּל-עֲצֵי-עֵדֶן--מִבְחַר וְטוֹב-לְבָנוֹן, כָּל-שֹׁתֵי מָיִם.
At the sound of its fall I made the nations quake, when I brought it down to Sheol with those who go down to the pit; and in the land below all the trees of Eden were comforted, the choice and best of Lebanon, all that drink water.note
Al sonido de su caída hice temblar a las naciones, cuando lo hice descender al Seol con los que descienden a la fosa; y en la tierra de abajo fueron consolados todos los árboles de Edén, lo escogido y lo mejor del Líbano, todos los que beben aguas.
17
גַּם-הֵם, אִתּוֹ יָרְדוּ שְׁאוֹלָה--אֶל-חַלְלֵי-חָרֶב; וּזְרֹעוֹ יָשְׁבוּ בְצִלּוֹ, בְּתוֹךְ גּוֹיִם.
They too went down with it to Sheol, to those slain by the sword; and its arm, that sat in its shade in the midst of the nations.note
También ellos descendieron con él al Seol, a los muertos a espada; y su brazo, que se sentaba a su sombra en medio de las naciones.
18
אֶל-מִי דָמִיתָ כָּכָה בְּכָבוֹד וּבְגֹדֶל, בַּעֲצֵי-עֵדֶן; וְהוּרַדְתָּ אֶת-עֲצֵי-עֵדֶן אֶל-אֶרֶץ תַּחְתִּית, בְּתוֹךְ עֲרֵלִים תִּשְׁכַּב אֶת-חַלְלֵי-חֶרֶב--הוּא פַרְעֹה וְכָל-הֲמוֹנֹה, נְאֻם אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה.  {פ}
Whom are you like in this way, in glory and in greatness, among the trees of Eden? Yet you will be brought down with the trees of Eden to the land below; in the midst of the uncircumcised you will lie, with those slain by the sword. This is Pharaoh and all his multitude, declares the Lord Jehovah.”note
¿A quién te pareces así, en gloria y en grandeza, entre los árboles de Edén? Pues serás derribado con los árboles de Edén a la tierra de abajo; en medio de los incircuncisos yacerás, con los muertos a espada. Este es el faraón y toda su multitud, declara el Señor Jehová».

Translator’s Notes

Verses 1–2 · Ezekiel 31:1–2 whom are you like?

⭐ The date. The eleventh year, third month, first day.

⭐ To Pharaoh and his multitude. The chapter is addressed to them, tells of a great tree, and in verse 18 comes back to say who is meant. The question of verse 2 returns there.

Verses 3–9 · Ezekiel 31:3–9 the cedar

⚠ Assyria. The word is Ashur, printed ‘Assyria’ at 23:12. Assyria is held up to Pharaoh as a cedar in Lebanon. The KJV has “the Assyrian was a cedar”.

⚠ Four words for branches. This translation keeps one word for each: ‘boughs’ (anaf, verse 3), ‘branches’ (daliyot) and ‘sprigs’ (porot), as at 17:6 and 17:23, and ‘forks’ (se’appot, once spelled with an extra letter). The versions use ‘boughs’ and ‘branches’ more freely.

⚠ A shading thicket. The words are not certain; the KJV has “with a shadowing shroud”. ‘Thick foliage’ is the phrase of 19:11; the NWT 1984 reads “clouds”.

⭐ The deep. Tehom waters the tree; in verses 4 and 15 the ‘her’ of ‘her rivers’ is the deep.

⭐ Birds and beasts. Nests in it, young born under it, nations in its shade: compare the cedar of 17:23 and the tree of Daniel 4:9 (4:12 in English Bibles).

⭐ The garden of God. As at 28:13. ‘Hide’ is the verb of 28:3, where no secret is hidden. The trees of Eden envy it.

Verses 10–14 · Ezekiel 31:10–14 cut down

⚠ You, it. Verse 10 begins ‘you were high’ and goes on ‘it set’. The heart ‘lifted up’ has the words of Deuteronomy 8:14.

⚠ The mighty one. El goyim; el here can be ‘mighty one’ or is spelled like ‘ram’, and no one is named. The verbs of verses 11 to 13 mix their forms, and the versions differ between past and future.

⭐ Strangers. ‘Strangers, the most ruthless of the nations’, as at 28:7.

⭐ Why. So that no tree by the water should grow so high. ‘All that drink water’ widens the lesson beyond one tree.

⚠ Their mighty ones. The word in verse 14 can also be read ‘to them’.

Verses 15–17 · Ezekiel 31:15–17 down to Sheol

⭐ Mourning. The deep is covered, rivers withheld, Lebanon darkened, the trees faint. ‘Withheld’ is the verb of Genesis 30:2, and ‘held back’ of the rain at Genesis 8:2.

⭐ Below. Sheol, the pit and ‘the land below’. The words erets tachtit stand in three verses of the Hebrew Bible, verses 14, 16 and 18; 26:20 has a plural form, ‘the lower parts’.

⭐ Comforted. The trees of Eden are comforted in the land below; the verse does not say why.

⚠ Its arm. Verse 17 has ‘its arm’ as pointed; the KJV keeps “they that were his arm”, the NWT 1984 has “his seed”.

Verse 18 · Ezekiel 31:18 this is Pharaoh

⭐ The question again. ‘Whom are you like’ in these words stands in two verses of the Hebrew Bible, verses 2 and 18.

⭐ Uncircumcised. Among the uncircumcised, as said to Tyre’s leader (28:10). ‘This is Pharaoh’ names the one the picture was for.

Patterns worth carrying forward

Where this translation keeps the exact / distinct words: “forks”, “sprigs” and “branches” are kept apart; “treetop” and “thick foliage” at 3, 10 and 14; “land below” at 14, 16 and 18; and “Sheol” at 15, 16 and 17.

Echoes paid. The cedar of 17:23; the thick foliage of 19:11; the ruthless strangers of 28:7; the garden of God of 28:13.

Echoes planted. Pharaoh again at 32:2; ‘Pharaoh and all his multitude’ at 32:32.
