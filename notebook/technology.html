TECHNOLOGY · SEPTEMBER 21, 2026 The Calling Card in a Cat's Kidneys Cat kidneys are stuffed with fat droplets, and nobody has known why for about 110 years. Then the obvious follow-up, which has a real and complicated literature: do people have one too, and could you identify someone from it? This is how the mechanism works and where it actually stands. TECHNOLOGY · SEPTEMBER 12, 2026 Galileo's Experiment, Done With One Atom in Two Places A team including Roger Penrose has dropped a rubidium atom that was, in the quantum sense, in two places at once — one half falling freely, the other held still — and read off how gravity changed it. Einstein's equivalence principle survived. What makes it worth an entry is not the result but the ladder it starts: the same instrument, with heavier and heavier objects, could eventually test Penrose's own idea that gravity is what stops big things from being in two places at once. He helped build the tool that could prove him wrong.
