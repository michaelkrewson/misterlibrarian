The same letter, written from Macedonia. Paul says the ministry he has was given by mercy, and that he has renounced hidden shame. He speaks of the veiled gospel, of the god of this system who has blinded unbelievers, and of the light of God shining in hearts. He calls the apostles clay jars holding a treasure, and sets the wasting outer person against the renewed inner one.
Undated by the text: the chapter names no year. It continues the letter of chapters 2 and 3 without a break.

2 Corinthians 4 — The Mister Translation

💬 Discuss this chapter
1
Διὰ τοῦτο, ἔχοντες τὴν διακονίαν ταύτην καθὼς ἠλεήθημεν, οὐκ ἐγκακοῦμεν
Therefore, since we have this ministry as we received mercy, we do not lose heart.note
Por eso, teniendo este ministerio por la misericordia que recibimos, no nos rendimos.
2
ἀλλὰ ἀπειπάμεθα τὰ κρυπτὰ τῆς αἰσχύνης, μὴ περιπατοῦντες ἐν πανουργίᾳ μηδὲ δολοῦντες τὸν λόγον τοῦ θεοῦ, ἀλλὰ τῇ φανερώσει τῆς ἀληθείας συνιστάνοντες ἑαυτοὺς πρὸς πᾶσαν συνείδησιν ἀνθρώπων ἐνώπιον τοῦ θεοῦ.
We have renounced the hidden things of shame, not walking in cunning or handling the word of God deceitfully, but by the open showing of the truth we commend ourselves to every human conscience before God.note
Hemos renunciado a las cosas vergonzosas que se hacen a escondidas; no andamos con astucia ni manipulamos la palabra de Dios, sino que, con la manifestación de la verdad, nos recomendamos ante toda conciencia humana, delante de Dios.
3
εἰ δὲ καὶ ἔστιν κεκαλυμμένον τὸ εὐαγγέλιον ἡμῶν, ἐν τοῖς ἀπολλυμένοις ἐστὶν κεκαλυμμένον,
And if our gospel is veiled, it is veiled to those who are perishing.note
Y si nuestras buenas noticias están veladas, están veladas para los que se pierden.
4
ἐν οἷς ὁ θεὸς τοῦ αἰῶνος τούτου ἐτύφλωσεν τὰ νοήματα τῶν ἀπίστων εἰς τὸ μὴ αὐγάσαι τὸν φωτισμὸν τοῦ εὐαγγελίου τῆς δόξης τοῦ Χριστοῦ, ὅς ἐστιν εἰκὼν τοῦ θεοῦ.
The god of this age has blinded the minds of the unbelievers, so that the light of the gospel of the glory of Christ, who is the image of God, does not shine on them.note
El dios de este mundo ha cegado las mentes de los incrédulos, para que no les brille la luz de las buenas noticias de la gloria de Cristo, que es la imagen de Dios.
5
οὐ γὰρ ἑαυτοὺς κηρύσσομεν ἀλλὰ Χριστὸν Ἰησοῦν κύριον, ἑαυτοὺς δὲ δούλους ὑμῶν διὰ Ἰησοῦν.
For we do not preach ourselves, but Christ Jesus as Lord, and ourselves as your slaves for Jesus’ sake.note
Porque no predicamos acerca de nosotros mismos, sino que predicamos que Jesucristo es Señor, y que nosotros somos sus esclavos por causa de Jesús.
6
ὅτι ὁ θεὸς ὁ εἰπών· Ἐκ σκότους φῶς λάμψει, ὃς ἔλαμψεν ἐν ταῖς καρδίαις ἡμῶν πρὸς φωτισμὸν τῆς γνώσεως τῆς δόξης τοῦ θεοῦ ἐν προσώπῳ Χριστοῦ.
For God, who said, “Light shall shine out of darkness,” has shone in our hearts to give the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Christ.note
Porque Dios, que dijo: «De la oscuridad brille la luz», ha brillado en nuestros corazones para iluminarnos con el conocimiento de la gloria de Dios en el rostro de Cristo.
7
Ἔχομεν δὲ τὸν θησαυρὸν τοῦτον ἐν ὀστρακίνοις σκεύεσιν, ἵνα ἡ ὑπερβολὴ τῆς δυνάμεως ᾖ τοῦ θεοῦ καὶ μὴ ἐξ ἡμῶν·
But we have this treasure in clay jars, so that the surpassing power may be from God and not from us.note
Pero tenemos este tesoro en vasijas de barro, para que el poder que supera lo normal venga de Dios y no de nosotros.
8
ἐν παντὶ θλιβόμενοι ἀλλʼ οὐ στενοχωρούμενοι, ἀπορούμενοι ἀλλʼ οὐκ ἐξαπορούμενοι,
We are pressed on every side but not crushed; we are perplexed but not in despair;note
Nos oprimen de toda manera, pero no nos aprietan hasta no poder movernos; estamos indecisos, pero no sin salida;
9
διωκόμενοι ἀλλʼ οὐκ ἐγκαταλειπόμενοι, καταβαλλόμενοι ἀλλʼ οὐκ ἀπολλύμενοι,
persecuted but not abandoned; struck down but not destroyed;note
nos persiguen, pero no estamos abandonados; nos derriban, pero no nos destruyen;
10
πάντοτε τὴν νέκρωσιν τοῦ Ἰησοῦ ἐν τῷ σώματι περιφέροντες, ἵνα καὶ ἡ ζωὴ τοῦ Ἰησοῦ ἐν τῷ σώματι ἡμῶν φανερωθῇ·
always carrying about in the body the dying of Jesus, so that the life of Jesus may also be shown in our body.note
Siempre llevamos en el cuerpo la muerte de Jesús, para que la vida de Jesús también se muestre en nuestro cuerpo.
11
ἀεὶ γὰρ ἡμεῖς οἱ ζῶντες εἰς θάνατον παραδιδόμεθα διὰ Ἰησοῦν, ἵνα καὶ ἡ ζωὴ τοῦ Ἰησοῦ φανερωθῇ ἐν τῇ θνητῇ σαρκὶ ἡμῶν.
For we who live are always being handed over to death for Jesus’ sake, so that the life of Jesus may also be shown in our mortal flesh.note
Porque nosotros, que vivimos, somos entregados siempre a la muerte por causa de Jesús, para que la vida de Jesús también se muestre en nuestra carne mortal.
12
ὥστε ὁ θάνατος ἐν ἡμῖν ἐνεργεῖται, ἡ δὲ ζωὴ ἐν ὑμῖν.
So death is at work in us, but life in you.note
Así que la muerte actúa en nosotros, pero la vida actúa en ustedes.
13
Ἔχοντες δὲ τὸ αὐτὸ πνεῦμα τῆς πίστεως, κατὰ τὸ γεγραμμένον· Ἐπίστευσα, διὸ ἐλάλησα, καὶ ἡμεῖς πιστεύομεν, διὸ καὶ λαλοῦμεν,
Having the same spirit of faith, as it is written, “I believed, and so I spoke,” we also believe, and so we speak,note
Teniendo el mismo espíritu de fe, según está escrito: «Creí, por eso hablé»; también nosotros creemos, y por eso hablamos,
14
εἰδότες ὅτι ὁ ἐγείρας τὸν Ἰησοῦν καὶ ἡμᾶς σὺν Ἰησοῦ ἐγερεῖ καὶ παραστήσει σὺν ὑμῖν.
knowing that the One who raised Jesus will raise us also with Jesus and will present us together with you.note
sabiendo que el que resucitó a Jesús también nos resucitará con Jesús y nos presentará junto con ustedes.
15
τὰ γὰρ πάντα διʼ ὑμᾶς, ἵνα ἡ χάρις πλεονάσασα διὰ τῶν πλειόνων τὴν εὐχαριστίαν περισσεύσῃ εἰς τὴν δόξαν τοῦ θεοῦ.
For all things are for your sake, so that grace, multiplied through more people, may increase thanksgiving to the glory of God.note
Porque todo esto es por ustedes, para que la bondad inmerecida, extendiéndose a más personas, haga abundar la acción de gracias para la gloria de Dios.
16
Διὸ οὐκ ἐγκακοῦμεν ἀλλʼ εἰ καὶ ὁ ἔξω ἡμῶν ἄνθρωπος διαφθείρεται, ἀλλʼ ὁ ἔσω ἡμῶν ἀνακαινοῦται ἡμέρᾳ καὶ ἡμέρᾳ.
So we do not lose heart; even though our outer self is wasting away, our inner self is being renewed day by day.note
Por eso no nos rendimos. Aunque nuestro hombre exterior se va desgastando, el interior se renueva día tras día.
17
τὸ γὰρ παραυτίκα ἐλαφρὸν τῆς θλίψεως ἡμῶν καθʼ ὑπερβολὴν εἰς ὑπερβολὴν αἰώνιον βάρος δόξης κατεργάζεται ἡμῖν,
For our light and momentary affliction produces for us an eternal weight of glory beyond all comparison,note
Porque nuestra aflicción momentánea y liviana produce para nosotros una gloria eterna, de un peso incomparablemente mayor,
18
μὴ σκοπούντων ἡμῶν τὰ βλεπόμενα ἀλλὰ τὰ μὴ βλεπόμενα, τὰ γὰρ βλεπόμενα πρόσκαιρα, τὰ δὲ μὴ βλεπόμενα αἰώνια.
as we look not at what is seen but at what is not seen; for what is seen is temporary, but what is not seen is eternal.note
pues fijamos la vista no en las cosas que se ven, sino en las que no se ven; porque las que se ven son temporales, pero las que no se ven son eternas.
Verses 1–2 · the ministry received by mercy, the renounced shame, and the commending to conscience

“Since we have this ministry.” echontes tēn diakonian tautēn, having this ministry. The noun is the word of 3:6–9, and the sentence says the ministry came from mercy, not from merit. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “Therefore, since we have this ministry through the mercy that was shown us, we do not give up.” The KJV “Therefore seeing we have this ministry, as we have received mercy, we faint not.”

“We have renounced the hidden things of shame.” apeipametha ta krypta tēs aischynēs, we have renounced the hidden things of shame. The phrase names what is done in secret, and the sentence sets it against the open showing of the truth. The verb apeipomai is to disown or renounce. The shelf: the TNM 2019 “hemos renunciado a las cosas vergonzosas que se hacen a escondidas y no actuamos con astucia ni manipulamos la palabra de Dios.”

Verses 3–4 · a veiled gospel, the god of this age, and the image of God

“Veiled to those who are perishing.” en tois apollymenois estin kekalymmenon. The gospel is veiled, and the sentence says the veil is among those who are perishing. The verb apollymi is to destroy, and the word names the people who are lost. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “If, in fact, the good news we declare is veiled, it is veiled among those who are perishing.” The KJV “But if our gospel be hid, it is hid to them that are lost.”

“The god of this age.” ho theos tou aiōnos toutou, the god of this age. The phrase is aiōn, age, which the NWT renders as “system of things” and the KJV as “world.” The text does not name the figure here, and the note records that commentators identify him in different ways; the translation keeps the Greek noun. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “among whom the god of this system of things has blinded the minds of the unbelievers.” The KJV “In whom the god of this world hath blinded the minds of them which believe not.”

“The image of God.” hos estin eikōn tou theou, who is the image of God. The sentence calls Christ the image of God, and the light of the gospel is his glory. The shelf: the TNM 2019 “acerca del Cristo, que es la imagen de Dios.”

Verses 5–6 · not preaching ourselves, Christ Jesus as Lord, and the light out of darkness

“Christ Jesus as Lord.” Christon Iēsoun kyrion, Christ Jesus as Lord. The word kyrios is the title of Jesus here, and the translation keeps “Lord.” The shelf: the NWT 2013 “For we are preaching, not about ourselves, but about Jesus Christ as Lord.” The KJV “For we preach not ourselves, but Christ Jesus the Lord.”

“Light out of darkness.” ek skotous phōs lampsei, light shall shine out of darkness. The quotation echoes Genesis 1:3 in the Greek; the sentence applies it to the light that shone in the Corinthians’ hearts. The shelf: the TNM 2019 “Que brille la luz en medio de la oscuridad”.

Verses 7–12 · the treasure in clay jars, the afflictions, and death at work in us and life in you

“This treasure in clay jars.” ostrakinois skeuesin, earthen vessels. The image is a clay jar holding a treasure, and the sentence says the surpassing power is God’s and not theirs. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “However, we have this treasure in earthen vessels, so that the power beyond what is normal may be God’s and not from us.” The KJV “But we have this treasure in earthen vessels, that the excellency of the power may be of God, and not of us.”

“Pressed but not crushed.” The four pairs in verses 8 and 9 each set a hardship against a refusal to be overcome: pressed and not crushed, perplexed and not in despair, persecuted and not abandoned, struck down and not destroyed. The shelf: the KJV “We are troubled on every side, yet not distressed; we are perplexed, but not in despair.”

“The dying of Jesus.” tēn nekrōsin tou Iēsou, the dying of Jesus. The noun is the word for death as a condition, and the sentence says the dying is carried in the body so the life of Jesus may be shown. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “Always we endure in our body the death-dealing treatment that Jesus suffered, that the life of Jesus may also be made manifest in our body.” The TNM 2019 “Constantemente aguantamos en nuestro cuerpo el trato mortal que sufrió Jesús”.

“Death at work in us.” ho thanatos en hēmin energeitai, death is at work in us, but life in you. The sentence closes the section with the contrast between the apostles and the congregation. The shelf: the KJV “So then death worketh in us, but life in you.”

Verses 13–15 · the same spirit of faith, the raising with Jesus, and grace multiplied to thanksgiving

“I believed, and so I spoke.” Episteusa, dio elalēsa, I believed, therefore I spoke. The quotation is from the Greek of the Psalms, Psalm 116:10 in the Hebrew numbering. Paul says the Corinthians and he share the same spirit of faith and so they speak. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “Now because we have the same spirit of faith as that of which it is written: “I exercised faith, therefore I spoke”.” The KJV “I believed, and therefore have I spoken; we also believe, and therefore speak.”

“The One who raised Jesus.” ho egeiras ton Iēsoun, the one who raised Jesus. The sentence says God will raise Paul and the Corinthians with Jesus. The shelf: the TNM 2019 “pues sabemos que el que resucitó a Jesús también nos resucitará a nosotros para estar con Jesús y nos presentará junto con ustedes.”

Verses 16–18 · the outer and inner self, the light affliction, and the things seen and unseen

“The outer self and the inner.” ho exō anthrōpos and ho esō anthrōpos, the outer and the inner person. The outer self is wasting away, and the inner is renewed day by day. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “even if the man we are outside is wasting away, certainly the man we are inside is being renewed from day to day.”

“The things seen and unseen.” ta blepomena and ta mē blepomena, the things seen and not seen. The seen are temporary, and the unseen are eternal. The shelf: the KJV “While we look not at the things which are seen, but at the things which are not seen: for the things which are seen are temporal; but the things which are not seen are eternal.”

Patterns worth carrying forward

Where this translation keeps the exact words: “treasure” for thēsauros at 4:7; “light” for phōs and phōtismos at 4:4–6; “dying” for nekrōsis at 4:10; “age” for aiōn at 4:4, which the NWT and KJV render as “system of things” and “world”.

Echoes paid. The ministry of 3:6–9 returns as the ministry received by mercy (4:1); the veil of 3:13–16 returns as the veiled gospel (4:3); the image of 3:18 returns as the image of God that is Christ (4:4); the glory of 3:7–11 returns as the glory of the gospel (4:4) and an eternal weight of glory (4:17); and the face of 3:7 returns in the face of Christ (4:6).

Echoes planted. The resurrection (4:14) is the hope the next chapters take up; the eternal (4:18) returns in chapter 5, where Paul speaks of an eternal house not made with hands (5:1).
