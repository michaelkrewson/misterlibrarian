In the seventh year, fifth month, tenth day, elders of Israel come to inquire of Jehovah, and Ezekiel answers with the history of their fathers from Egypt onward.
The seventh year of King Jehoiachin's exile as the book counts, usually reckoned as 591 BCE.

1
וַיְהִי בַּשָּׁנָה הַשְּׁבִיעִית, בַּחֲמִשִׁי בֶּעָשׂוֹר לַחֹדֶשׁ, בָּאוּ אֲנָשִׁים מִזִּקְנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל, לִדְרֹשׁ אֶת-יְהוָה; וַיֵּשְׁבוּ, לְפָנָי.  {ס}
And it happened in the seventh year, in the fifth month, on the tenth of the month, that men of the elders of Israel came to inquire of Jehovah, and they sat before me.note
Y sucedió en el año séptimo, en el mes quinto, a los diez del mes, que vinieron hombres de los ancianos de Israel para consultar a Jehová, y se sentaron delante de mí.
2
וַיְהִי דְבַר-יְהוָה, אֵלַי לֵאמֹר.
And the word of Jehovah came to me, saying:note
Y vino a mí la palabra de Jehová, diciendo:
3
בֶּן-אָדָם, דַּבֵּר אֶת-זִקְנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל וְאָמַרְתָּ אֲלֵהֶם כֹּה אָמַר אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, הֲלִדְרֹשׁ אֹתִי, אַתֶּם בָּאִים:  חַי-אָנִי אִם-אִדָּרֵשׁ לָכֶם, נְאֻם אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה.
“Son of man, speak with the elders of Israel and say to them, Thus says the Lord Jehovah: Is it to inquire of me that you are coming? As I live, I will not let myself be inquired of by you, declares the Lord Jehovah.note
«Hijo de hombre, habla con los ancianos de Israel y diles: Así dice el Señor Jehová: ¿Es para consultarme para lo que vienen? Vivo yo, que no me dejaré consultar por ustedes, declara el Señor Jehová.
4
הֲתִשְׁפֹּט אֹתָם, הֲתִשְׁפּוֹט בֶּן-אָדָם:  אֶת-תּוֹעֲבֹת אֲבוֹתָם, הוֹדִיעֵם.
Will you judge them? Will you judge, son of man? Make known to them the abominations of their fathers.note
¿Los juzgarás? ¿Juzgarás, hijo de hombre? Hazles saber las abominaciones de sus padres.
5
וְאָמַרְתָּ אֲלֵיהֶם, כֹּה-אָמַר אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, בְּיוֹם בָּחֳרִי בְיִשְׂרָאֵל, וָאֶשָּׂא יָדִי לְזֶרַע בֵּית יַעֲקֹב וָאִוָּדַע לָהֶם בְּאֶרֶץ מִצְרָיִם; וָאֶשָּׂא יָדִי לָהֶם לֵאמֹר, אֲנִי יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵיכֶם.
And say to them, Thus says the Lord Jehovah: On the day when I chose Israel, and lifted up my hand to the offspring of the house of Jacob, and made myself known to them in the land of Egypt, and lifted up my hand to them, saying, ‘I am Jehovah your God’—note
Y diles: Así dice el Señor Jehová: El día en que escogí a Israel, y alcé mi mano a la descendencia de la casa de Jacob, y me di a conocer a ellos en la tierra de Egipto, y alcé mi mano a ellos, diciendo: “Yo soy Jehová su Dios”;
6
בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא, נָשָׂאתִי יָדִי לָהֶם, לְהוֹצִיאָם, מֵאֶרֶץ מִצְרָיִם:  אֶל-אֶרֶץ אֲשֶׁר-תַּרְתִּי לָהֶם, זָבַת חָלָב וּדְבַשׁ--צְבִי הִיא, לְכָל-הָאֲרָצוֹת.
on that day I lifted up my hand to them to bring them out from the land of Egypt into a land that I had scouted out for them, flowing with milk and honey; it is the glory of all lands.note
aquel día alcé mi mano a ellos para sacarlos de la tierra de Egipto a una tierra que yo había reconocido para ellos, que mana leche y miel; es la gloria de todas las tierras.
7
וָאֹמַר אֲלֵהֶם, אִישׁ שִׁקּוּצֵי עֵינָיו הַשְׁלִיכוּ, וּבְגִלּוּלֵי מִצְרַיִם, אַל-תִּטַּמָּאוּ:  אֲנִי, יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵיכֶם.
And I said to them, ‘Throw away, each one, the detestable things of his eyes, and do not defile yourselves with the idols of Egypt; I am Jehovah your God.’note
Y les dije: “Arrojen, cada uno, las cosas detestables de sus ojos, y no se contaminen con los ídolos de Egipto; yo soy Jehová su Dios”.
8
וַיַּמְרוּ-בִי, וְלֹא אָבוּ לִשְׁמֹעַ אֵלַי--אִישׁ אֶת-שִׁקּוּצֵי עֵינֵיהֶם לֹא הִשְׁלִיכוּ, וְאֶת-גִּלּוּלֵי מִצְרַיִם לֹא עָזָבוּ; וָאֹמַר לִשְׁפֹּךְ חֲמָתִי עֲלֵיהֶם, לְכַלּוֹת אַפִּי בָּהֶם, בְּתוֹךְ, אֶרֶץ מִצְרָיִם.
But they rebelled against me and were not willing to listen to me; they did not each throw away the detestable things of their eyes, and the idols of Egypt they did not forsake. And I said I would pour out my fury upon them, to spend my anger against them in the midst of the land of Egypt.note
Pero se rebelaron contra mí y no quisieron escucharme; no arrojó cada uno las cosas detestables de sus ojos, ni abandonaron los ídolos de Egipto. Y dije que derramaría mi furia sobre ellos, para acabar mi ira contra ellos en medio de la tierra de Egipto.
9
וָאַעַשׂ לְמַעַן שְׁמִי, לְבִלְתִּי הֵחֵל לְעֵינֵי הַגּוֹיִם אֲשֶׁר-הֵמָּה בְתוֹכָם--אֲשֶׁר נוֹדַעְתִּי אֲלֵיהֶם לְעֵינֵיהֶם, לְהוֹצִיאָם מֵאֶרֶץ מִצְרָיִם.
But I acted for the sake of my name, so that it would not be profaned before the eyes of the nations among whom they were, before whose eyes I had made myself known to them, to bring them out from the land of Egypt.note
Pero actué por causa de mi nombre, para que no fuera profanado ante los ojos de las naciones entre las cuales estaban, ante cuyos ojos me había dado a conocer a ellos, para sacarlos de la tierra de Egipto.
10
וָאוֹצִיאֵם, מֵאֶרֶץ מִצְרָיִם; וָאֲבִאֵם, אֶל-הַמִּדְבָּר.
So I brought them out from the land of Egypt and brought them into the wilderness.note
Así que los saqué de la tierra de Egipto y los llevé al desierto.
11
וָאֶתֵּן לָהֶם אֶת-חֻקּוֹתַי, וְאֶת-מִשְׁפָּטַי הוֹדַעְתִּי אוֹתָם:  אֲשֶׁר יַעֲשֶׂה אוֹתָם הָאָדָם, וָחַי בָּהֶם.
And I gave them my statutes, and my judgments I made known to them—the one who does them will live by them.note
Y les di mis estatutos, y mis juicios les hice conocer: el que los cumpla vivirá por ellos.
12
וְגַם אֶת-שַׁבְּתוֹתַי, נָתַתִּי לָהֶם, לִהְיוֹת לְאוֹת, בֵּינִי וּבֵינֵיהֶם--לָדַעַת, כִּי אֲנִי יְהוָה מְקַדְּשָׁם.
And also my Sabbaths I gave them, to be a sign between me and them, that they might know that I am Jehovah who sanctifies them.note
Y también mis sábados les di, para que fueran una señal entre yo y ellos, para que supieran que yo soy Jehová que los santifica.
13
וַיַּמְרוּ-בִי בֵית-יִשְׂרָאֵל בַּמִּדְבָּר, בְּחֻקּוֹתַי לֹא-הָלָכוּ וְאֶת-מִשְׁפָּטַי מָאָסוּ אֲשֶׁר יַעֲשֶׂה אֹתָם הָאָדָם וָחַי בָּהֶם, וְאֶת-שַׁבְּתֹתַי, חִלְּלוּ מְאֹד; וָאֹמַר לִשְׁפֹּךְ חֲמָתִי עֲלֵיהֶם, בַּמִּדְבָּר--לְכַלּוֹתָם.
But the house of Israel rebelled against me in the wilderness; in my statutes they did not walk, and my judgments they rejected—the one who does them will live by them—and my Sabbaths they greatly profaned. And I said I would pour out my fury upon them in the wilderness, to make an end of them.note
Pero la casa de Israel se rebeló contra mí en el desierto; en mis estatutos no anduvieron, y mis juicios rechazaron —el que los cumpla vivirá por ellos—, y mis sábados profanaron en gran manera. Y dije que derramaría mi furia sobre ellos en el desierto, para acabar con ellos.
14
וָאֶעֱשֶׂה, לְמַעַן שְׁמִי, לְבִלְתִּי הֵחֵל לְעֵינֵי הַגּוֹיִם, אֲשֶׁר הוֹצֵאתִים לְעֵינֵיהֶם.
But I acted for the sake of my name, so that it would not be profaned before the eyes of the nations, before whose eyes I had brought them out.note
Pero actué por causa de mi nombre, para que no fuera profanado ante los ojos de las naciones, ante cuyos ojos los había sacado.
15
וְגַם-אֲנִי, נָשָׂאתִי יָדִי לָהֶם--בַּמִּדְבָּר:  לְבִלְתִּי הָבִיא אוֹתָם אֶל-הָאָרֶץ אֲשֶׁר-נָתַתִּי, זָבַת חָלָב וּדְבַשׁ--צְבִי הִיא, לְכָל-הָאֲרָצוֹת.
And I also lifted up my hand to them in the wilderness, not to bring them into the land that I had given, flowing with milk and honey; it is the glory of all lands—note
Y también yo alcé mi mano a ellos en el desierto, para no llevarlos a la tierra que yo había dado, que mana leche y miel; es la gloria de todas las tierras;
16
יַעַן בְּמִשְׁפָּטַי מָאָסוּ, וְאֶת-חֻקּוֹתַי לֹא-הָלְכוּ בָהֶם, וְאֶת-שַׁבְּתוֹתַי, חִלֵּלוּ:  כִּי אַחֲרֵי גִלּוּלֵיהֶם, לִבָּם הֹלֵךְ.
because my judgments they rejected, and in my statutes they did not walk, and my Sabbaths they profaned; for their heart was going after their idols.note
porque mis juicios rechazaron, y en mis estatutos no anduvieron, y mis sábados profanaron; pues tras sus ídolos iba su corazón.
17
וַתָּחָס עֵינִי עֲלֵיהֶם, מִשַּׁחֲתָם; וְלֹא-עָשִׂיתִי אוֹתָם כָּלָה, בַּמִּדְבָּר.
Yet my eye spared them from destroying them, and I did not make a full end of them in the wilderness.note
Con todo, mi ojo los perdonó para no destruirlos, y no acabé del todo con ellos en el desierto.
18
וָאֹמַר אֶל-בְּנֵיהֶם, בַּמִּדְבָּר, בְּחוּקֵּי אֲבוֹתֵיכֶם אַל-תֵּלֵכוּ, וְאֶת-מִשְׁפְּטֵיהֶם אַל-תִּשְׁמֹרוּ; וּבְגִלּוּלֵיהֶם, אַל-תִּטַּמָּאוּ.
And I said to their sons in the wilderness, ‘In the statutes of your fathers do not walk, and their judgments do not keep, and with their idols do not defile yourselves.note
Y dije a sus hijos en el desierto: “En los estatutos de sus padres no anden, y sus juicios no guarden, y con sus ídolos no se contaminen.
19
אֲנִי יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵיכֶם, בְּחֻקּוֹתַי לֵכוּ; וְאֶת-מִשְׁפָּטַי שִׁמְרוּ, וַעֲשׂוּ אוֹתָם.
I am Jehovah your God; in my statutes walk, and my judgments keep, and do them;note
Yo soy Jehová su Dios; en mis estatutos anden, y mis juicios guarden, y cúmplanlos;
20
וְאֶת-שַׁבְּתוֹתַי, קַדֵּשׁוּ; וְהָיוּ לְאוֹת, בֵּינִי וּבֵינֵיכֶם--לָדַעַת, כִּי אֲנִי יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵיכֶם.
and my Sabbaths sanctify, and they will be a sign between me and you, that you may know that I am Jehovah your God.’note
y mis sábados santifiquen, y serán una señal entre yo y ustedes, para que sepan que yo soy Jehová su Dios”.
21
וַיַּמְרוּ-בִי הַבָּנִים, בְּחֻקּוֹתַי לֹא-הָלָכוּ וְאֶת-מִשְׁפָּטַי לֹא-שָׁמְרוּ לַעֲשׂוֹת אוֹתָם אֲשֶׁר יַעֲשֶׂה אוֹתָם הָאָדָם וָחַי בָּהֶם--אֶת-שַׁבְּתוֹתַי, חִלֵּלוּ; וָאֹמַר לִשְׁפֹּךְ חֲמָתִי עֲלֵיהֶם, לְכַלּוֹת אַפִּי בָּם--בַּמִּדְבָּר.
But the sons rebelled against me; in my statutes they did not walk, and my judgments they did not keep, to do them—the one who does them will live by them—my Sabbaths they profaned. And I said I would pour out my fury upon them, to spend my anger against them in the wilderness.note
Pero los hijos se rebelaron contra mí; en mis estatutos no anduvieron, y mis juicios no guardaron para cumplirlos —el que los cumpla vivirá por ellos—; mis sábados profanaron. Y dije que derramaría mi furia sobre ellos, para acabar mi ira contra ellos en el desierto.
22
וַהֲשִׁבֹתִי, אֶת-יָדִי, וָאַעַשׂ, לְמַעַן שְׁמִי--לְבִלְתִּי הֵחֵל לְעֵינֵי הַגּוֹיִם, אֲשֶׁר-הוֹצֵאתִי אוֹתָם לְעֵינֵיהֶם.
But I drew back my hand, and acted for the sake of my name, so that it would not be profaned before the eyes of the nations, before whose eyes I had brought them out.note
Pero retiré mi mano, y actué por causa de mi nombre, para que no fuera profanado ante los ojos de las naciones, ante cuyos ojos los había sacado.
23
גַּם-אֲנִי, נָשָׂאתִי אֶת-יָדִי לָהֶם--בַּמִּדְבָּר:  לְהָפִיץ אֹתָם בַּגּוֹיִם, וּלְזָרוֹת אוֹתָם בָּאֲרָצוֹת.
I also lifted up my hand to them in the wilderness, to scatter them among the nations and disperse them through the lands,note
También yo alcé mi mano a ellos en el desierto, para esparcirlos entre las naciones y dispersarlos por las tierras,
24
יַעַן מִשְׁפָּטַי לֹא-עָשׂוּ, וְחֻקּוֹתַי מָאָסוּ, וְאֶת-שַׁבְּתוֹתַי, חִלֵּלוּ; וְאַחֲרֵי גִּלּוּלֵי אֲבוֹתָם, הָיוּ עֵינֵיהֶם.
because my judgments they had not done, and my statutes they had rejected, and my Sabbaths they had profaned, and their eyes were after the idols of their fathers.note
porque mis juicios no habían cumplido, y mis estatutos habían rechazado, y mis sábados habían profanado, y tras los ídolos de sus padres estaban sus ojos.
25
וְגַם-אֲנִי נָתַתִּי לָהֶם, חֻקִּים לֹא טוֹבִים; וּמִשְׁפָּטִים--לֹא יִחְיוּ, בָּהֶם.
And I also gave them statutes that were not good, and judgments by which they would not live;note
Y también yo les di estatutos que no eran buenos, y juicios por los cuales no vivirían;
26
וָאֲטַמֵּא אוֹתָם בְּמַתְּנוֹתָם, בְּהַעֲבִיר כָּל-פֶּטֶר רָחַם:  לְמַעַן אֲשִׁמֵּם--לְמַעַן אֲשֶׁר יֵדְעוּ, אֲשֶׁר אֲנִי יְהוָה.  {ס}
and I defiled them by their gifts, when they made all that opens the womb pass over, so that I might make them desolate, so that they might know that I am Jehovah.note
y los contaminé con sus dádivas, cuando hacían pasar todo lo que abre la matriz, para asolarlos, para que supieran que yo soy Jehová.
27
לָכֵן דַּבֵּר אֶל-בֵּית יִשְׂרָאֵל, בֶּן-אָדָם, וְאָמַרְתָּ אֲלֵיהֶם, כֹּה אָמַר אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה:  עוֹד, זֹאת גִּדְּפוּ אוֹתִי אֲבוֹתֵיכֶם, בְּמַעֲלָם בִּי, מָעַל.
Therefore speak to the house of Israel, son of man, and say to them, Thus says the Lord Jehovah: In this too your fathers blasphemed me, when they committed a trespass against me.note
Por tanto, habla a la casa de Israel, hijo de hombre, y diles: Así dice el Señor Jehová: También en esto me blasfemaron sus padres, cuando cometieron transgresión contra mí.
28
וָאֲבִיאֵם, אֶל-הָאָרֶץ, אֲשֶׁר נָשָׂאתִי אֶת-יָדִי, לָתֵת אוֹתָהּ לָהֶם; וַיִּרְאוּ כָל-גִּבְעָה רָמָה וְכָל-עֵץ עָבֹת, וַיִּזְבְּחוּ-שָׁם אֶת-זִבְחֵיהֶם וַיִּתְּנוּ-שָׁם כַּעַס קָרְבָּנָם, וַיָּשִׂימוּ שָׁם רֵיחַ נִיחוֹחֵיהֶם, וַיַּסִּיכוּ שָׁם אֶת-נִסְכֵּיהֶם.
When I had brought them into the land that I had lifted up my hand to give to them, they saw every high hill and every thick tree, and there they sacrificed their sacrifices, and there they gave the provocation of their offering, and there they set their soothing aroma, and there they poured out their drink offerings.note
Cuando los hube llevado a la tierra que yo había alzado mi mano para darles, vieron toda colina alta y todo árbol espeso, y allí sacrificaron sus sacrificios, y allí dieron la provocación de su ofrenda, y allí pusieron su aroma apacible, y allí derramaron sus libaciones.
29
וָאֹמַר אֲלֵהֶם--מָה הַבָּמָה, אֲשֶׁר-אַתֶּם הַבָּאִים שָׁם; וַיִּקָּרֵא שְׁמָהּ בָּמָה, עַד הַיּוֹם הַזֶּה.  {ס}
And I said to them, ‘What is the high place to which you are going?’ And its name has been called Bamah to this day.note
Y les dije: “¿Qué es el lugar alto adonde ustedes van?”. Y su nombre se ha llamado Bamá hasta el día de hoy.
30
לָכֵן אֱמֹר אֶל-בֵּית יִשְׂרָאֵל, כֹּה אָמַר אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, הַבְּדֶרֶךְ אֲבוֹתֵיכֶם, אַתֶּם נִטְמְאִים; וְאַחֲרֵי שִׁקּוּצֵיהֶם, אַתֶּם זֹנִים.
Therefore say to the house of Israel, Thus says the Lord Jehovah: Are you defiling yourselves in the way of your fathers, and going whoring after their detestable things?note
Por tanto, di a la casa de Israel: Así dice el Señor Jehová: ¿Se están contaminando ustedes en el camino de sus padres, y se prostituyen tras sus cosas detestables?
31
וּבִשְׂאֵת מַתְּנֹתֵיכֶם בְּהַעֲבִיר בְּנֵיכֶם בָּאֵשׁ אַתֶּם נִטְמְאִים לְכָל-גִּלּוּלֵיכֶם, עַד-הַיּוֹם,  {ס}  וַאֲנִי אִדָּרֵשׁ לָכֶם, בֵּית יִשְׂרָאֵל:  חַי-אָנִי, נְאֻם אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, אִם-אִדָּרֵשׁ, לָכֶם.
And in lifting up your gifts, in making your sons pass through the fire, you are defiling yourselves with all your idols to this day. And shall I let myself be inquired of by you, house of Israel? As I live, declares the Lord Jehovah, I will not let myself be inquired of by you.note
Y al alzar sus dádivas, al hacer pasar a sus hijos por el fuego, ustedes se contaminan con todos sus ídolos hasta el día de hoy. ¿Y he de dejarme consultar por ustedes, casa de Israel? Vivo yo, declara el Señor Jehová, que no me dejaré consultar por ustedes.
32
וְהָעֹלָה, עַל-רוּחֲכֶם--הָיוֹ, לֹא תִהְיֶה:  אֲשֶׁר אַתֶּם אֹמְרִים, נִהְיֶה כַגּוֹיִם כְּמִשְׁפְּחוֹת הָאֲרָצוֹת--לְשָׁרֵת, עֵץ וָאָבֶן.
And what comes up in your spirit shall by no means be—what you are saying, ‘We will be like the nations, like the families of the lands, ministering to wood and stone.’note
Y lo que sube en su espíritu de ningún modo será, eso que ustedes dicen: “Seremos como las naciones, como las familias de las tierras, sirviendo a la madera y a la piedra”.
33
חַי-אָנִי, נְאֻם אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה:  אִם-לֹא בְּיָד חֲזָקָה וּבִזְרוֹעַ נְטוּיָה, וּבְחֵמָה שְׁפוּכָה--אֶמְלוֹךְ עֲלֵיכֶם.
As I live, declares the Lord Jehovah, surely with a strong hand and with an outstretched arm and with poured-out fury I will be king over you.note
Vivo yo, declara el Señor Jehová, que con mano fuerte y con brazo extendido y con furia derramada reinaré sobre ustedes.
34
וְהוֹצֵאתִי אֶתְכֶם, מִן-הָעַמִּים, וְקִבַּצְתִּי אֶתְכֶם, מִן-הָאֲרָצוֹת אֲשֶׁר נְפוֹצֹתֶם בָּם--בְּיָד חֲזָקָה וּבִזְרוֹעַ נְטוּיָה, וּבְחֵמָה שְׁפוּכָה.
And I will bring you out from the peoples, and gather you from the lands in which you have been scattered, with a strong hand and with an outstretched arm and with poured-out fury.note
Y los sacaré de entre los pueblos, y los reuniré de las tierras en que han sido esparcidos, con mano fuerte y con brazo extendido y con furia derramada.
35
וְהֵבֵאתִי אֶתְכֶם, אֶל-מִדְבַּר הָעַמִּים; וְנִשְׁפַּטְתִּי אִתְּכֶם שָׁם, פָּנִים אֶל-פָּנִים.
And I will bring you into the wilderness of the peoples, and there I will enter into judgment with you face to face.note
Y los llevaré al desierto de los pueblos, y allí entraré en juicio con ustedes cara a cara.
36
כַּאֲשֶׁר נִשְׁפַּטְתִּי אֶת-אֲבוֹתֵיכֶם, בְּמִדְבַּר אֶרֶץ מִצְרָיִם--כֵּן אִשָּׁפֵט אִתְּכֶם, נְאֻם אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה.
As I entered into judgment with your fathers in the wilderness of the land of Egypt, so I will enter into judgment with you, declares the Lord Jehovah.note
Como entré en juicio con sus padres en el desierto de la tierra de Egipto, así entraré en juicio con ustedes, declara el Señor Jehová.
37
וְהַעֲבַרְתִּי אֶתְכֶם, תַּחַת הַשָּׁבֶט; וְהֵבֵאתִי אֶתְכֶם, בְּמָסֹרֶת הַבְּרִית.
And I will make you pass under the rod, and I will bring you into the bond of the covenant.note
Y los haré pasar bajo la vara, y los haré entrar en el vínculo del pacto.
38
וּבָרוֹתִי מִכֶּם, הַמֹּרְדִים וְהַפּוֹשְׁעִים בִּי--מֵאֶרֶץ מְגוּרֵיהֶם אוֹצִיא אוֹתָם, וְאֶל-אַדְמַת יִשְׂרָאֵל לֹא יָבוֹא; וִידַעְתֶּם, כִּי-אֲנִי יְהוָה.
And I will purge out from among you the rebels and those who transgress against me; from the land of their sojournings I will bring them out, but into the ground of Israel he shall not come; and you will know that I am Jehovah.note
Y limpiaré de entre ustedes a los rebeldes y a los que transgreden contra mí; de la tierra donde peregrinan los sacaré, pero al suelo de Israel no entrará; y sabrán que yo soy Jehová.
39
וְאַתֶּם בֵּית-יִשְׂרָאֵל כֹּה-אָמַר אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, אִישׁ גִּלּוּלָיו לְכוּ עֲבֹדוּ, וְאַחַר, אִם-אֵינְכֶם שֹׁמְעִים אֵלָי; וְאֶת-שֵׁם קָדְשִׁי לֹא תְחַלְּלוּ-עוֹד, בְּמַתְּנוֹתֵיכֶם וּבְגִלּוּלֵיכֶם.
And you, house of Israel, thus says the Lord Jehovah: Go, serve each one his idols, and afterwards, if you are not listening to me …! But my holy name you shall not profane again with your gifts and with your idols.note
Y ustedes, casa de Israel, así dice el Señor Jehová: Vayan, sirva cada uno a sus ídolos, y después, ¡si no me están escuchando…! Pero mi santo nombre no lo profanarán más con sus dádivas y con sus ídolos.
40
כִּי בְהַר-קָדְשִׁי בְּהַר מְרוֹם יִשְׂרָאֵל, נְאֻם אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה--שָׁם יַעַבְדֻנִי כָּל-בֵּית יִשְׂרָאֵל כֻּלֹּה, בָּאָרֶץ:  שָׁם אֶרְצֵם--וְשָׁם אֶדְרוֹשׁ אֶת-תְּרוּמֹתֵיכֶם וְאֶת-רֵאשִׁית מַשְׂאוֹתֵיכֶם, בְּכָל-קָדְשֵׁיכֶם.
For on my holy mountain, on the mountain height of Israel, declares the Lord Jehovah, there all the house of Israel, all of it, will serve me in the land; there I will accept them, and there I will require your contributions and the first of your portions, with all your holy things.note
Porque en mi santo monte, en el monte de la altura de Israel, declara el Señor Jehová, allí me servirá en el país toda la casa de Israel, toda ella; allí los aceptaré, y allí requeriré sus contribuciones y las primicias de sus porciones, con todas sus cosas santas.
41
בְּרֵיחַ נִיחֹחַ, אֶרְצֶה אֶתְכֶם, בְּהוֹצִיאִי אֶתְכֶם מִן-הָעַמִּים, וְקִבַּצְתִּי אֶתְכֶם מִן-הָאֲרָצוֹת אֲשֶׁר נְפֹצֹתֶם בָּם; וְנִקְדַּשְׁתִּי בָכֶם, לְעֵינֵי הַגּוֹיִם.
As a soothing aroma I will accept you, when I bring you out from the peoples and gather you from the lands in which you have been scattered; and I will be sanctified in you before the eyes of the nations.note
Como aroma apacible los aceptaré, cuando los saque de entre los pueblos y los reúna de las tierras en que han sido esparcidos; y seré santificado en ustedes ante los ojos de las naciones.
42
וִידַעְתֶּם כִּי-אֲנִי יְהוָה, בַּהֲבִיאִי אֶתְכֶם אֶל-אַדְמַת יִשְׂרָאֵל--אֶל-הָאָרֶץ, אֲשֶׁר נָשָׂאתִי אֶת-יָדִי, לָתֵת אוֹתָהּ, לַאֲבוֹתֵיכֶם.
And you will know that I am Jehovah, when I bring you into the ground of Israel, into the land that I lifted up my hand to give to your fathers.note
Y sabrán que yo soy Jehová, cuando los traiga al suelo de Israel, a la tierra que alcé mi mano para dar a sus padres.
43
וּזְכַרְתֶּם-שָׁם, אֶת-דַּרְכֵיכֶם וְאֵת כָּל-עֲלִילוֹתֵיכֶם, אֲשֶׁר נִטְמֵאתֶם, בָּם; וּנְקֹטֹתֶם, בִּפְנֵיכֶם, בְּכָל-רָעוֹתֵיכֶם, אֲשֶׁר עֲשִׂיתֶם.
And there you will remember your ways and all your deeds by which you have defiled yourselves, and you will loathe yourselves in your own sight for all your evils that you have done.note
Y allí se acordarán de sus caminos y de todas sus obras con que se han contaminado, y se aborrecerán a sí mismos en su propia presencia por todas las maldades que han cometido.
44
וִידַעְתֶּם כִּי-אֲנִי יְהוָה, בַּעֲשׂוֹתִי אִתְּכֶם לְמַעַן שְׁמִי:  לֹא כְדַרְכֵיכֶם הָרָעִים וְכַעֲלִילוֹתֵיכֶם הַנִּשְׁחָתוֹת, בֵּית יִשְׂרָאֵל--נְאֻם, אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה.  {פ}
And you will know that I am Jehovah, when I act with you for the sake of my name, not according to your evil ways nor according to your corrupt deeds, house of Israel, declares the Lord Jehovah.”note
Y sabrán que yo soy Jehová, cuando actúe con ustedes por causa de mi nombre, no según sus malos caminos ni según sus obras corruptas, casa de Israel, declara el Señor Jehová».

Translator’s Notes

Verses 1–4 · Ezekiel 20:1–4 the elders come to inquire — ‘I will not let myself be inquired of’

⭐ A date. The seventh year, fifth month, tenth day. The book counts from King Jehoiachin’s exile (1:2), and this is usually reckoned as 591 BCE, some eleven months after the date of 8:1. The TLB turns it into “Late in July, six years after King Jeconiah was captured”.

⭐ Elders again. Elders sat before the prophet at 8:1 and 14:1.

⭐ Inquire. Darash, to seek out a god or a prophet with a question. It stands in verse 1, twice in verse 3 and twice in verse 31, and returns in verse 40 as ‘require’. 14:3 asked whether such men should be answered at all.

⚠ As I live. An oath: ‘I will not let myself be inquired of by you’ is put in the form ‘if I let myself be inquired of’. The Douay has “I will not answer you”. The TLB writes “How dare you come to ask my help?”

⭐ Will you judge? The question is asked twice, and the second time with no object. The doubled ‘will you judge’ returns at 22:2, and the question again at 23:36. What follows is not an answer to the elders’ question but a history of their fathers’ abominations.

Verses 5–9 · Ezekiel 20:5–9 Egypt — the lifted hand — for the sake of my name

⭐ Lifted up my hand. The gesture of an oath; the ASV and NIV have words for swearing in places. The words stand in verse 5 twice and in verses 6, 15, 23, 28 and 42. With the object marker, ‘I lifted up my hand’ (nasati et yadi) stands in seven verses of the Hebrew Bible, among them Exodus 6:8, Numbers 14:30 and 36:7, and here at verses 23, 28 and 42.

⭐ Made myself known. In verses 5 and 9, the verb of Exodus 6:3, ‘by my name Jehovah I did not make myself known to them’.

⭐ Scouted out. Tur, the verb of the spies of Numbers 13:2. Here God has done the scouting.

⭐ The glory of all lands. Tsevi, beauty or glory. ‘It is the glory’ (tsevi hi) stands in two verses of the Hebrew Bible, here and verse 15. The ASV has “which is the glory of all lands”, the NIV “the most beautiful of all lands”, the NWT 1984 “the decoration of all the lands”.

⭐ Idols of Egypt. Exodus does not tell of such a command in Egypt. Joshua 24:14 speaks of gods the fathers served there. ‘Idols’ is Ezekiel’s scornful word, gillulim; ‘detestable things’ is another, shiqqutsim.

⭐ Spend my anger. The words of 7:8; they return in verse 21. ‘Rebelled’ (verses 8, 13, 21) is marah.

⭐ For the sake of my name. The first of the turns in the story: verses 9, 14 and 22, and again at 44. The reason given is that the name not be profaned before the nations who watched. Moses pleaded in a similar way (Exodus 32:12, Numbers 14:15–16); compare 36:22. The TLB adds a reason of its own, “lest the Egyptians laugh at Israel’s God who couldn’t keep them from harm”.

Verses 10–17 · Ezekiel 20:10–17 the first generation in the wilderness — statutes and Sabbaths

⭐ He will live by them. The words of Leviticus 18:5, printed as there. They come three times (verses 11, 13, 21). ‘Which the man does them’, in either spelling, stands in four verses of the Hebrew Bible: Leviticus 18:5 and these three. Romans 10:5 and Galatians 3:12 take the sentence up. The NIV has “by which the person who obeys them will live”.

⭐ Sabbaths. The Sabbath as a sign is the language of Exodus 31:13. ‘My Sabbaths’ comes in verses 12, 13, 16, 20, 21 and 24. Jehovah ‘sanctifies them’, the people. The NVI has «he consagrado los sábados para mí», that the Sabbaths were consecrated.

⭐ Greatly. Only verse 13 says the Sabbaths were profaned ‘greatly’.

⭐ The oath in the wilderness. Numbers 14:28–35 tells of the generation that would die in the wilderness and not enter. The chapter does not name the spies.

⭐ My eye spared. Chus. Earlier the book said ‘my eye will not spare’ (5:11, 7:4). ‘Full end’ is the word of Ezekiel’s own cry when Pelatiah died (11:13). ‘To make an end of them’ in verse 13 is the related verb, the one printed ‘spend’ in verses 8 and 21.

Verses 18–26 · Ezekiel 20:18–26 the sons — ‘statutes that were not good’

⭐ The same story again. The sons are warned not to walk in their fathers’ statutes, they rebel, and the hand is drawn back for the name’s sake.

⭐ Two forms of ‘statute’. ‘My statutes’ in this chapter is the feminine chuqqotai (verses 11, 13, 16, 19, 21, 24). The fathers’ statutes (verse 18) and the statutes that were not good (verse 25) are masculine, chuqqim.

⭐ Scatter. The oath to scatter is already sworn in the wilderness. The warning stands at Leviticus 26:33 and Deuteronomy 28:64; Psalm 106:26–27 also joins the lifted hand to a fall in the wilderness and a scattering of the offspring.

⚠ I gave them statutes not good. The verb is the same ‘I gave’ as in verses 11 and 12. Readers differ on what is meant. Some take it as permission or abandonment, God leaving the people to bad laws: the NWT 1984 has “let them have regulations that were not good”, the TLB “I let them adopt customs and laws which were worthless”. Some connect it with the law of the firstborn (Exodus 13:2, 22:29) taken as a demand for sacrifice, though Exodus 13:13 commands that a son be redeemed. Others read a punishment by which sin was given its own course as law. Jeremiah says of the burning of children ‘which I did not command’ (19:5). The verse itself does not say which laws are meant. The NIV adds a word, “other statutes”.

⚠ All that opens the womb. The phrase of Exodus 13:12, where the same verb is printed ‘set apart’, literally ‘make pass over’. Verse 26 does not mention fire; verse 31 does. The KJV has “to pass through the fire all that openeth the womb”, the NIV “the sacrifice of every firstborn”. ‘Make them desolate’ can also be heard as ‘fill them with horror’.

Verses 27–31 · Ezekiel 20:27–31 in the land — the high place — back to the elders

⭐ Blasphemed. The form giddefu stands in three verses of the Hebrew Bible: here, and 2 Kings 19:6 and Isaiah 37:6. ‘Trespass’ is the word of 17:20.

⭐ High hill, thick tree. The places of 6:13. ‘Thick tree’ stands in three verses of the Hebrew Bible: Leviticus 23:40, Nehemiah 8:15 and here. The soothing aroma and the drink offerings are words of the altar, spent here on the hills.

⚠ What is the high place? A play on sounds: mah, ‘what’, and ba, ‘come, go in’, are heard in bamah, ‘high place’: mah ha-bamah asher attem ha-baim. The point of the play is not explained. The NIV sets the naming apart as a remark, “(It is called Bamah to this day.)”; the TLB translates the name, “The Place of Sacrifice”.

⭐ Now you. Verse 30 turns from the fathers to those listening, with questions. ‘Going whoring’ is the verb of chapter 16.

⭐ Through the fire. Forbidden at Deuteronomy 18:10; compare Deuteronomy 12:31 and Ezekiel 16:21.

⭐ A break inside the verse. The Hebrew text printed here marks a paragraph break in the middle of verse 31, just before ‘shall I let myself be inquired of’. The question and oath of verse 3 return: the chapter has come back to the elders.

Verses 32–38 · Ezekiel 20:32–38 ‘like the nations’ — a second wilderness — under the rod

⭐ What comes up in your spirit. As at 11:5.

⭐ Like the nations. Israel asked for a king to be ‘like all the nations’ (1 Samuel 8:20), and Jehovah said the people had rejected him ‘from being king over them’ (8:7). Here the answer to being like the nations is ‘I will be king over you’. The chapter does not cite Samuel. ‘Wood and stone’ stands in seven verses of the Hebrew Bible, among them Deuteronomy 4:28 and 28:36.

⭐ Ministering. In verse 32 the verb is that of priestly service (Deuteronomy 10:8), not the ‘serve’ of verses 39 and 40.

⭐ Strong hand, outstretched arm. The words of the exodus (Deuteronomy 4:34), with something added. ‘Poured-out fury’ in these words stands in two verses of the Hebrew Bible, verses 33 and 34. The power once turned on Egypt is now to be used on Israel.

⭐ Wilderness of the peoples. The phrase stands in one verse of the Hebrew Bible. ‘Face to face’ (panim el panim) stands in five verses: Genesis 32:30, Exodus 33:11, Deuteronomy 34:10, Judges 6:22 and here. ‘Enter into judgment’ is as at 17:20. The NIV has “execute judgment upon you”.

⚠ Under the rod. As a shepherd counts the flock (Leviticus 27:32, where shevet is printed ‘shepherd’s staff’; it was ‘sceptre’ at 19:11). ‘Make you pass’ is the verb of verses 26 and 31. The NIV has “I will take note of you as you pass under my rod”; the Douay “I will make you subject to my sceptre”; the TLB “I will count you carefully and let only a small quota return”.

⚠ The bond of the covenant. The word bemasoret stands in one verse of the Hebrew Bible. It is usually traced to asar, ‘to bind’; another explanation connects it with handing over.

⭐ Purge. The ‘rebels’ and ‘those who transgress’ have the two roots of 2:3, marad and pasha, not the marah of verses 8, 13 and 21. They are brought out of the land of their sojournings but do not reach the ground of Israel, as the first generation left Egypt and did not enter. ‘He shall not come’ is singular in the Hebrew.

Verses 39–44 · Ezekiel 20:39–44 the holy mountain — remembering — not according to your ways

⚠ Go, serve your idols. Verse 39 is difficult. After ‘go, serve’ comes ‘and afterwards, if you are not listening to me’, and the sentence breaks off or runs into ‘but’. The NIV reads a promise, “afterward you will surely listen to me”; the KJV has “and hereafter also, if ye will not hearken unto me”.

⭐ The mountain height of Israel. The words merom Yisrael stand in three verses of the Hebrew Bible: 17:23, here and 34:14. ‘All of it’ stresses the whole house. The TLB names the place, “For at Jerusalem in my holy mountain”; the Hebrew does not.

⭐ I will require. Darash again: God will not let himself be inquired of, but will seek their contributions. ‘Portions’ is from nasa, to lift, the verb of the lifted hand.

⭐ Sanctified. In verse 12 Jehovah sanctifies the people; here he is sanctified in them, as Leviticus 22:32 says he will be, and as at 36:23.

⭐ Loathe yourselves. ‘And you will loathe yourselves’ stands in two verses of the Hebrew Bible: here and 36:31. Compare 6:9. Memory comes after the return, not before it.

⭐ Not according to your ways. The last ‘for the sake of my name’. Compare 36:32. The Douay reads “when I shall have done well by you”.

⚠ Where the chapter ends. English Bibles go on to 20:45–49, the fire in the southern forest. In the Hebrew those verses are 21:1–5, and this translation follows the Hebrew, so its verse numbers in chapter 21 will differ from the English ones.

Patterns worth carrying forward

Where this translation keeps the exact / distinct words: “inquire” at 1, 3 and 31; “lifted up my hand” at 5, 6, 15, 23, 28 and 42; “for the sake of my name” at 9, 14, 22 and 44; “the one who does them will live by them” at 11, 13 and 21; “rebelled” at 8, 13 and 21, apart from the “rebels” of 38; and “soothing aroma” at 28 and 41.

Echoes paid. Leviticus 18:5; the Sabbath sign of Exodus 31:13; the self-loathing foretold beside 36:31; the lifted hand of 36:7; and the mountain height of 17:23.

Echoes planted. ‘Will you judge’ at Ezekiel 22:2; the lifted hand at Ezekiel 47:14.
