After the house of Jehovah, Solomon builds his own house for thirteen years: the House of the Forest of Lebanon, the porch of the pillars and the porch of the throne, his dwelling, and a house for Pharaoh’s daughter, all of costly stones. Hiram of Tyre, a bronze-worker, casts for the house of Jehovah two pillars (Jachin and Boaz), the sea on twelve oxen, and ten stands with ten lavers, in the plain of the Jordan between Succoth and Zarethan; Solomon makes the golden furnishings and brings in the holy things of David.
After the seven years of the house of Jehovah (1 Kings 6:38): the thirteen years of Solomon’s own house, and the furnishing of the temple.

1
וְאֶת-בֵּיתוֹ בָּנָה שְׁלֹמֹה, שְׁלֹשׁ עֶשְׂרֵה שָׁנָה; וַיְכַל, אֶת-כָּל-בֵּיתוֹ.
And his own house Solomon built in thirteen years; and he finished all his house.note
Y su propia casa edificó Salomón en trece años; y terminó toda su casa.
2
וַיִּבֶן אֶת-בֵּית יַעַר הַלְּבָנוֹן, מֵאָה אַמָּה אָרְכּוֹ וַחֲמִשִּׁים אַמָּה רָחְבּוֹ, וּשְׁלֹשִׁים אַמָּה, קוֹמָתוֹ; עַל, אַרְבָּעָה טוּרֵי עַמּוּדֵי אֲרָזִים, וּכְרֻתוֹת אֲרָזִים, עַל-הָעַמּוּדִים.
And he built the House of the Forest of Lebanon, a hundred cubits its length and fifty cubits its width and thirty cubits its height, upon four rows of pillars of cedar, with beams of cedar on the pillars.note
Y edificó la Casa del Bosque del Líbano, cien codos su longitud y cincuenta codos su anchura y treinta codos su altura, sobre cuatro filas de columnas de cedro, con vigas de cedro sobre las columnas.
3
וְסָפֻן בָּאֶרֶז, מִמַּעַל עַל-הַצְּלָעוֹת אֲשֶׁר עַל-הָעַמּוּדִים, אַרְבָּעִים, וַחֲמִשָּׁה--חֲמִשָּׁה עָשָׂר, הַטּוּר.
And it was paneled with cedar above, upon the sides that were on the pillars, forty-five, fifteen to the row.note
Y estaba revestida de cedro arriba, sobre los costados que estaban sobre las columnas, cuarenta y cinco, quince por fila.
4
וּשְׁקֻפִים, שְׁלֹשָׁה טוּרִים; וּמֶחֱזָה אֶל-מֶחֱזָה, שָׁלֹשׁ פְּעָמִים.
And there were frames in three rows, and view facing view, three times.note
Y había marcos en tres filas, y vista frente a vista, tres veces.
5
וְכָל-הַפְּתָחִים וְהַמְּזוּזוֹת, רְבֻעִים שָׁקֶף; וּמוּל מֶחֱזָה אֶל-מֶחֱזָה, שָׁלֹשׁ פְּעָמִים.
And all the doorways and the doorposts were squared in frame; and opposite, view facing view, three times.note
Y todas las entradas y los postes eran de marco cuadrado; y enfrente, vista frente a vista, tres veces.
6
וְאֵת אוּלָם הָעַמּוּדִים, עָשָׂה, חֲמִשִּׁים אַמָּה אָרְכּוֹ, וּשְׁלֹשִׁים אַמָּה רָחְבּוֹ; וְאוּלָם, עַל-פְּנֵיהֶם, וְעַמֻּדִים וְעָב, עַל-פְּנֵיהֶם.
And he made the porch of the pillars, fifty cubits its length and thirty cubits its width; and a porch in front of them, and pillars and a thick beam in front of them.note
E hizo el pórtico de las columnas, cincuenta codos su longitud y treinta codos su anchura; y un pórtico delante de ellas, y columnas y una viga gruesa delante de ellas.
7
וְאוּלָם הַכִּסֵּא אֲשֶׁר יִשְׁפָּט-שָׁם, אֻלָם הַמִּשְׁפָּט עָשָׂה; וְסָפוּן בָּאֶרֶז, מֵהַקַּרְקַע עַד-הַקַּרְקָע.
And the porch of the throne, where he would judge, the porch of judgment, he made; and it was paneled with cedar from floor to floor.note
Y el pórtico del trono, donde había de juzgar, el pórtico del juicio, lo hizo; y estaba revestido de cedro de suelo a suelo.
8
וּבֵיתוֹ אֲשֶׁר-יֵשֶׁב שָׁם חָצֵר הָאַחֶרֶת, מִבֵּית לָאוּלָם, כַּמַּעֲשֶׂה הַזֶּה, הָיָה; וּבַיִת יַעֲשֶׂה לְבַת-פַּרְעֹה, אֲשֶׁר לָקַח שְׁלֹמֹה, כָּאוּלָם, הַזֶּה.
And his house where he would dwell, the other court, within the porch, was of the same work as this. And a house he made for the daughter of Pharaoh, whom Solomon had taken, like this porch.note
Y su casa donde había de morar, el otro atrio, dentro del pórtico, era de la misma obra que esta. E hizo una casa para la hija de Faraón, a la que Salomón había tomado, como este pórtico.
9
כָּל-אֵלֶּה אֲבָנִים יְקָרֹת, כְּמִדּוֹת גָּזִית, מְגֹרָרוֹת בַּמְּגֵרָה, מִבַּיִת וּמִחוּץ; וּמִמַּסָּד, עַד-הַטְּפָחוֹת, וּמִחוּץ, עַד-הֶחָצֵר הַגְּדוֹלָה.
All these were of costly stones, by the measures of hewn stone, sawn with the saw, on the inside and on the outside; and from the foundation to the copings, and on the outside to the great court.note
Todas estas eran de piedras costosas, según las medidas de la piedra labrada, aserradas con la sierra, por dentro y por fuera; y desde el cimiento hasta los remates, y por fuera hasta el gran atrio.
10
וּמְיֻסָּד, אֲבָנִים יְקָרוֹת אֲבָנִים גְּדֹלוֹת--אַבְנֵי עֶשֶׂר אַמּוֹת, וְאַבְנֵי שְׁמֹנֶה אַמּוֹת.
And the foundation was of costly stones, great stones, stones of ten cubits and stones of eight cubits.note
Y el cimiento era de piedras costosas, piedras grandes, piedras de diez codos y piedras de ocho codos.
11
וּמִלְמַעְלָה, אֲבָנִים יְקָרוֹת כְּמִדּוֹת גָּזִית--וָאָרֶז.
And above, costly stones, by the measures of hewn stone — and cedar.note
Y arriba, piedras costosas, según las medidas de la piedra labrada — y cedro.
12
וְחָצֵר הַגְּדוֹלָה סָבִיב, שְׁלֹשָׁה טוּרִים גָּזִית, וְטוּר, כְּרֻתֹת אֲרָזִים; וְלַחֲצַר בֵּית-יְהוָה הַפְּנִימִית, וּלְאֻלָם הַבָּיִת.  {פ}
And the great court all around: three rows of hewn stone and a row of cedar beams; and so for the inner court of the house of Jehovah, and for the porch of the house.note
Y el gran atrio alrededor: tres hileras de piedra labrada y una hilera de vigas de cedro; y lo mismo para el atrio interior de la casa de Jehová y para el pórtico de la casa.
13
וַיִּשְׁלַח הַמֶּלֶךְ שְׁלֹמֹה, וַיִּקַּח אֶת-חִירָם מִצֹּר.
And King Solomon sent and took Hiram from Tyre.note
Y el rey Salomón envió y tomó a Hiram de Tiro.
14
בֶּן-אִשָּׁה אַלְמָנָה הוּא מִמַּטֵּה נַפְתָּלִי, וְאָבִיו אִישׁ-צֹרִי חֹרֵשׁ נְחֹשֶׁת, וַיִּמָּלֵא אֶת-הַחָכְמָה וְאֶת-הַתְּבוּנָה וְאֶת-הַדַּעַת, לַעֲשׂוֹת כָּל-מְלָאכָה בַּנְּחֹשֶׁת; וַיָּבוֹא אֶל-הַמֶּלֶךְ שְׁלֹמֹה, וַיַּעַשׂ אֶת-כָּל-מְלַאכְתּוֹ.
He was the son of a widow woman, of the tribe of Naphtali, and his father was a man of Tyre, a worker in bronze; and he was filled with the wisdom and the understanding and the knowledge to do every work in bronze; and he came to King Solomon and did all his work.note
Era hijo de una mujer viuda, de la tribu de Neftalí, y su padre era un hombre de Tiro, obrero en bronce; y estaba lleno de la sabiduría y del entendimiento y del conocimiento para hacer toda obra en bronce; y vino al rey Salomón e hizo toda su obra.
15
וַיָּצַר אֶת-שְׁנֵי הָעַמּוּדִים, נְחֹשֶׁת:  שְׁמֹנֶה עֶשְׂרֵה אַמָּה, קוֹמַת הָעַמּוּד הָאֶחָד, וְחוּט שְׁתֵּים-עֶשְׂרֵה אַמָּה, יָסֹב אֶת-הָעַמּוּד הַשֵּׁנִי.
And he formed the two pillars, of bronze: eighteen cubits the height of the one pillar, and a thread of twelve cubits went around the second pillar.note
Y formó las dos columnas, de bronce: dieciocho codos la altura de una columna, y un hilo de doce codos rodeaba la segunda columna.
16
וּשְׁתֵּי כֹתָרֹת עָשָׂה, לָתֵת עַל-רָאשֵׁי הָעַמּוּדִים--מֻצַק נְחֹשֶׁת:  חָמֵשׁ אַמּוֹת, קוֹמַת הַכֹּתֶרֶת הָאֶחָת, וְחָמֵשׁ אַמּוֹת, קוֹמַת הַכֹּתֶרֶת הַשֵּׁנִית.
And two capitals he made, to put on the tops of the pillars, cast in bronze: five cubits the height of the one capital, and five cubits the height of the second capital.note
Y dos capiteles hizo, para ponerlos sobre las cabezas de las columnas, de bronce fundido: cinco codos la altura de un capitel, y cinco codos la altura del segundo capitel.
17
שְׂבָכִים מַעֲשֵׂה שְׂבָכָה, גְּדִלִים מַעֲשֵׂה שַׁרְשְׁרוֹת, לַכֹּתָרֹת, אֲשֶׁר עַל-רֹאשׁ הָעַמּוּדִים--שִׁבְעָה לַכֹּתֶרֶת הָאֶחָת, וְשִׁבְעָה לַכֹּתֶרֶת הַשֵּׁנִית.
Nets of net-work, twisted cords of chain-work, for the capitals that were on the top of the pillars: seven for the one capital, and seven for the second capital.note
Redes de obra de red, cordones trenzados de obra de cadena, para los capiteles que estaban sobre la cabeza de las columnas: siete para un capitel, y siete para el segundo capitel.
18
וַיַּעַשׂ, אֶת-הָעַמּוּדִים; וּשְׁנֵי טוּרִים סָבִיב עַל-הַשְּׂבָכָה הָאֶחָת, לְכַסּוֹת אֶת-הַכֹּתָרֹת אֲשֶׁר עַל-רֹאשׁ הָרִמֹּנִים, וְכֵן עָשָׂה, לַכֹּתֶרֶת הַשֵּׁנִית.
And he made the pillars; and two rows all around upon the one net, to cover the capitals that were on the top of the pomegranates; and so he did for the second capital.note
E hizo las columnas; y dos hileras alrededor sobre la una red, para cubrir los capiteles que estaban sobre la cabeza de las granadas; y así hizo con el segundo capitel.
19
וְכֹתָרֹת, אֲשֶׁר עַל-רֹאשׁ הָעַמּוּדִים--מַעֲשֵׂה, שׁוּשַׁן בָּאוּלָם:  אַרְבַּע, אַמּוֹת.
And the capitals that were on the top of the pillars were of lily-work, in the porch: four cubits.note
Y los capiteles que estaban sobre la cabeza de las columnas eran de obra de lirios, en el pórtico: cuatro codos.
20
וְכֹתָרֹת, עַל-שְׁנֵי הָעַמּוּדִים, גַּם-מִמַּעַל מִלְּעֻמַּת הַבֶּטֶן, אֲשֶׁר לְעֵבֶר שבכה הַשְּׂבָכָה; וְהָרִמּוֹנִים מָאתַיִם טֻרִים סָבִיב, עַל הַכֹּתֶרֶת הַשֵּׁנִית.
And capitals on the two pillars, also above, next to the belly that was beyond the net; and the pomegranates were two hundred, in rows all around, upon the second capital.note
Y capiteles sobre las dos columnas, también arriba, junto al vientre que estaba del lado de la red; y las granadas eran doscientas, en hileras alrededor, sobre el segundo capitel.
21
וַיָּקֶם, אֶת-הָעַמֻּדִים, לְאֻלָם, הַהֵיכָל; וַיָּקֶם אֶת-הָעַמּוּד הַיְמָנִי, וַיִּקְרָא אֶת-שְׁמוֹ יָכִין, וַיָּקֶם אֶת-הָעַמּוּד הַשְּׂמָאלִי, וַיִּקְרָא אֶת-שְׁמוֹ בֹּעַז.
And he raised up the pillars at the porch of the temple; and he raised up the right pillar and called its name Jachin, and he raised up the left pillar and called its name Boaz.note
Y levantó las columnas junto al pórtico del templo; y levantó la columna derecha y llamó su nombre Jaquín, y levantó la columna izquierda y llamó su nombre Booz.
22
וְעַל רֹאשׁ הָעַמּוּדִים, מַעֲשֵׂה שׁוֹשָׁן; וַתִּתֹּם, מְלֶאכֶת הָעַמּוּדִים.
And on the top of the pillars was lily-work; and the work of the pillars was completed.note
Y sobre la cabeza de las columnas había obra de lirios; y se terminó la obra de las columnas.
23
וַיַּעַשׂ אֶת-הַיָּם, מוּצָק:  עֶשֶׂר בָּאַמָּה מִשְּׂפָתוֹ עַד-שְׂפָתוֹ עָגֹל סָבִיב, וְחָמֵשׁ בָּאַמָּה קוֹמָתוֹ, וקוה וְקָו שְׁלֹשִׁים בָּאַמָּה, יָסֹב אֹתוֹ סָבִיב.
And he made the sea, cast: ten cubits from its brim to its brim, round all around, and five cubits its height, and a line of thirty cubits went around it all around.note
E hizo el mar, fundido: diez codos de borde a borde, redondo alrededor, y cinco codos su altura, y un cordel de treinta codos lo rodeaba alrededor.
24
וּפְקָעִים מִתַּחַת לִשְׂפָתוֹ סָבִיב, סֹבְבִים אֹתוֹ--עֶשֶׂר בָּאַמָּה, מַקִּפִים אֶת-הַיָּם סָבִיב; שְׁנֵי טוּרִים הַפְּקָעִים, יְצֻקִים בִּיצֻקָתוֹ.
And gourds under its brim all around, encircling it — ten to the cubit, surrounding the sea all around; two rows of gourds, cast in its casting.note
Y calabazas bajo su borde alrededor, rodeándolo — diez por codo, cercando el mar alrededor; dos hileras de calabazas, fundidas en su fundición.
25
עֹמֵד עַל-שְׁנֵי עָשָׂר בָּקָר, שְׁלֹשָׁה פֹנִים צָפוֹנָה וּשְׁלֹשָׁה פֹנִים יָמָּה וּשְׁלֹשָׁה פֹּנִים נֶגְבָּה וּשְׁלֹשָׁה פֹּנִים מִזְרָחָה, וְהַיָּם עֲלֵיהֶם, מִלְמָעְלָה; וְכָל-אֲחֹרֵיהֶם, בָּיְתָה.
It stood upon twelve oxen: three facing north and three facing west and three facing south and three facing east, and the sea upon them, above; and all their hindquarters were inward.note
Estaba sobre doce bueyes: tres mirando al norte y tres mirando al oeste y tres mirando al sur y tres mirando al este, y el mar sobre ellos, encima; y todas sus partes traseras hacia adentro.
26
וְעָבְיוֹ טֶפַח, וּשְׂפָתוֹ כְּמַעֲשֵׂה שְׂפַת-כּוֹס פֶּרַח שׁוֹשָׁן; אַלְפַּיִם בַּת, יָכִיל.  {פ}
And its thickness was a handbreadth, and its brim like the work of the brim of a cup, a lily blossom; it held two thousand baths.note
Y su grosor era de un palmo, y su borde como la obra del borde de una copa, una flor de lirio; contenía dos mil batos.
27
וַיַּעַשׂ אֶת-הַמְּכֹנוֹת עֶשֶׂר, נְחֹשֶׁת:  אַרְבַּע בָּאַמָּה, אֹרֶךְ הַמְּכוֹנָה הָאֶחָת, וְאַרְבַּע בָּאַמָּה רָחְבָּהּ, וְשָׁלֹשׁ בָּאַמָּה קוֹמָתָהּ.
And he made the ten stands, of bronze: four cubits the length of the one stand and four cubits its width and three cubits its height.note
E hizo las diez basas, de bronce: cuatro codos la longitud de una basa y cuatro codos su anchura y tres codos su altura.
28
וְזֶה מַעֲשֵׂה הַמְּכוֹנָה, מִסְגְּרֹת לָהֶם; וּמִסְגְּרֹת, בֵּין הַשְׁלַבִּים.
And this was the work of the stand: they had panels; and the panels were between the crossbars.note
Y esta era la obra de la basa: tenían paneles; y los paneles estaban entre los travesaños.

Translator's Notes — verse by verse

Verses 1–6 · 1 Kings 7 The House of the Forest of Lebanon — Solomon’s own house in thirteen years, a hundred cubits by fifty by thirty, and the porch of the pillars

Thirteen years. “And his own house Solomon built in thirteen years; and he finished all his house”: the Hebrew puts “his own house” first, and the house of Jehovah took seven years (1 Kings 6:38, now on these pages). When Solomon married Pharaoh’s daughter he brought her to the city of David “until he had finished building his house and the house of Jehovah” (1 Kings 3:1, now on these pages). The ASV has “And Solomon was building his own house thirteen years, and he finished all his house”, the NWT 1984 “so that he finished all his own house”, and the NIV “It took Solomon thirteen years, however, to complete the construction of his palace”.

The House of the Forest of Lebanon. “A hundred cubits its length and fifty cubits its width and thirty cubits its height, upon four rows of pillars of cedar”: “the House of the Forest of Lebanon” stands in five verses, here, in the king’s shields and drinking vessels (1 Kings 10:17, 21) and in their parallels (2 Chronicles 9:16, 20), none of them translated here yet. Against the house of Jehovah (1 Kings 6:2) it is longer by forty cubits and wider by thirty, and the same thirty cubits high. The ASV has “the house of the forest of Lebanon”, the NIV “the Palace of the Forest of Lebanon”, the NWT 1984 “the House of the Forest of Lebanon”, and the Douay “the house of the forest of Libanus”.

Four rows, and forty-five. “And it was paneled with cedar above, upon the sides that were on the pillars, forty-five, fifteen to the row”: forty-five at fifteen to the row is three rows, and verse 2 has four rows of pillars; the Hebrew does not say what the forty-five are, and the versions choose. The ASV has “the forty and five beams, that were upon the pillars; fifteen in a row”, the KJV “beams, that lay on forty five pillars, fifteen in a row”, the NIV “forty-five beams, fifteen to a row”, and the NWT 1984 “the forty-five pillars. There were fifteen to a row”.

Frames, view facing view, and the porch. “And there were frames in three rows, and view facing view, three times”: the word is mechezeh, “a seeing”. The ASV has “window was over against window in three ranks”, the KJV “light was against light in three ranks”, the NWT 1984 “an illumination opening opposite an illumination opening”, and the NIV “windows were placed high in sets of three, facing each other”. “And he made the porch of the pillars, fifty cubits its length and thirty cubits its width; and a porch in front of them, and pillars and a thick beam in front of them”: the last word, av, “thick”, is uncertain. The ASV has “a threshold”, the KJV “the thick beam”, the NWT 1984 “a canopy”, and the NIV “an overhanging roof”.

Verses 7–12 · 1 Kings 7 The porch of the throne, a house for Pharaoh’s daughter, costly stones sawn with the saw, and the great court

The porch of judgment. “And the porch of the throne, where he would judge, the porch of judgment, he made; and it was paneled with cedar from floor to floor”: it is the place for what Israel saw in him, “the wisdom of God was within him, to do justice” (1 Kings 3:28, now on these pages). The ASV has “the porch of the throne where he was to judge, even the porch of judgment”, the NIV “the throne hall, the Hall of Justice”, and the NWT 1984 “the Porch of the Throne where he would do judging”. For “from floor to floor” the ASV has “from floor to floor”, the KJV “from one side of the floor to the other”, the NIV “from floor to ceiling”, and the NWT 1984 “from the floor to the rafters”.

His own house, and Pharaoh’s daughter’s. “And his house where he would dwell, the other court, within the porch, was of the same work as this. And a house he made for the daughter of Pharaoh, whom Solomon had taken, like this porch”: the daughter is the one of the marriage alliance (1 Kings 3:1). The ASV has “his house where he was to dwell, the other court within the porch, was of the like work”, the NIV “the palace in which he was to live, set farther back, was similar in design”, and the NWT 1984 “at the other courtyard”.

Costly stones, and the saw. “All these were of costly stones, by the measures of hewn stone, sawn with the saw, on the inside and on the outside”: “hewn stone” is gazit (see hewn stone), and “with the saw” stands in three verses, here, where David puts the people of Ammon “to the saw” (2 Samuel 12:31, now on these pages) and in the parallel of 1 Chronicles 20:3. The ASV has “sawed with saws, within and without”, the NWT 1984 “sawed with stone-saws”, the NIV “cut to size and smoothed on their inner and outer faces”, and the Douay “sawed by a certain rule and measure”. “And the foundation was of costly stones, great stones, stones of ten cubits and stones of eight cubits”: the ASV has “stones of ten cubits, and stones of eight cubits”, and the NIV “some measuring ten cubits and some eight”.

The great court. “And the great court all around: three rows of hewn stone and a row of cedar beams; and so for the inner court of the house of Jehovah, and for the porch of the house”: the inner court of the house is the one of 1 Kings 6:36 (now on these pages), and “cedar beams” are the two verses that name them. The ASV has “like as the inner court of the house of Jehovah, and the porch of the house”, the NIV “as was the inner courtyard of the temple of the Lord with its portico”, the NWT 1984 “three rows of hewn stone and a row of beams of cedarwood”, and the Douay “three rows of hewed stones, and one row of planks of cedar”.

Verses 13–22 · 1 Kings 7 Hiram, a worker in bronze — wisdom, understanding and knowledge; two pillars, nets and pomegranates; Jachin and Boaz

Hiram of Tyre. “And King Solomon sent and took Hiram from Tyre. He was the son of a widow woman, of the tribe of Naphtali, and his father was a man of Tyre, a worker in bronze”: the name is the king’s name (1 Kings 5:15, now on these pages), and the verses do not say the two are kin. The ASV has “a worker in brass”, the NWT 1984 “a worker in copper”, the NIV “a skilled craftsman in bronze”, and the Douay “an artificer in brass”; the NIV writes the name “Huram”.

“Filled.” “And he was filled with the wisdom and the understanding and the knowledge to do every work in bronze”: “worker in bronze”, choresh nechoshet, stands in two verses, here and of Tubal-cain, who “forged every tool of bronze and iron” (Genesis 4:22, now on these pages); and the three words are the three of Bezalel, “filled with the spirit of God, in wisdom, and in understanding, and in knowledge” (Exodus 31:3, now on these pages). The ASV has “filled with wisdom and understanding and skill”, the KJV “wisdom, and understanding, and cunning”, the NIV “filled with wisdom, with understanding and with knowledge”, and the NWT 1984 “full of the wisdom and the understanding and the knowledge”.

The pillars. “Eighteen cubits the height of the one pillar, and a thread of twelve cubits went around the second pillar… five cubits the height of the one capital”: the ASV has “a line of twelve cubits compassed either of them about”, the NIV “twelve cubits in circumference”, and the NWT 1984 “a string of twelve cubits would measure around each”. “Nets of net-work, twisted cords of chain-work… seven for the one capital”, and two hundred pomegranates in rows upon each capital (verse 20), which are the four hundred of verse 42. The capitals are five cubits high in verse 16 and “lily-work… four cubits” in verse 19, and “lily-work” stands in two verses, 19 and 22, both here; the text does not say how the five and the four fit. In verse 18 the capitals are “on the top of the pomegranates”, where the ASV has “upon the top of the pillars”.

Jachin and Boaz. “And he raised up the pillars at the porch of the temple; and he raised up the right pillar and called its name Jachin, and he raised up the left pillar and called its name Boaz”: Jachin is also a son of Simeon (Genesis 46:10; Exodus 6:15) and Boaz is the man of Ruth (Ruth 2:1, all now on these pages); the verse does not say the pillars are named for them, or who “called”. The ASV has “he set up the right pillar, and called the name thereof Jachin; and he set up the left pillar, and called the name thereof Boaz”, the NIV “The pillar to the south he named Jakin and the one to the north Boaz”, the NWT 1984 “the right-hand pillar and called its name Jaʹchin”, and the Douay “Booz”.

Verses 23–26 · 1 Kings 7 The sea — ten cubits across, gourds under the brim, twelve oxen, and two thousand baths

The measures. “Ten cubits from its brim to its brim, round all around, and five cubits its height, and a line of thirty cubits went around it all around”: the line is written qaveh and read qav, and the page prints the reading. The ASV has “a line of thirty cubits compassed it round about”, the NIV “It took a line of thirty cubits to measure around it”, and the NWT 1984 “it took a line of thirty cubits to circle all around it”. A circle ten cubits across is about thirty-one and a half around; the text gives thirty, and the page does not say more.

The gourds. “And gourds under its brim all around, encircling it — ten to the cubit… two rows of gourds, cast in its casting”: they are the peqa’im of the carved cedar (1 Kings 6:18, now on these pages), here in bronze. If the line is thirty, ten to the cubit is three hundred in a row and six hundred in two. The ASV has “knops”, the KJV “knops compassing it, ten in a cubit”, the NIV “gourds encircled it — ten to a cubit”, and the NWT 1984 “gourd-shaped ornaments… ten in a cubit”.

Twelve oxen. “It stood upon twelve oxen: three facing north and three facing west and three facing south and three facing east, and the sea upon them, above; and all their hindquarters were inward”: the ASV has “twelve oxen, three looking toward the north, and three looking toward the west”, the NIV “twelve bulls”, and the NWT 1984 “twelve bulls, three facing north”. The verses do not say what the sea was for; the laver of the tent is “for washing” (Exodus 30:18, now on these pages), and the parallel account in Chronicles says that the sea was for the priests to wash in (2 Chronicles 4:6).

A handbreadth, and the lily. “And its thickness was a handbreadth, and its brim like the work of the brim of a cup, a lily blossom; it held two thousand baths”: “the brim of a cup” stands in two verses, here and 2 Chronicles 4:5, where the number is three thousand baths. The ASV has “it held two thousand baths”, the KJV “it contained two thousand baths”, the NIV “It held two thousand baths”, and the NWT 1984 “Two thousand bath measures were what it would contain”.

Verses 27–39 · 1 Kings 7 The ten stands and the ten lavers — panels and crossbars, lions, oxen and cherubim, chariot wheels, and the right and left shoulders of the house

The ten stands. “Four cubits the length of the one stand and four cubits its width and three cubits its height… this was the work of the stand: they had panels; and the panels were between the crossbars”: “crossbars” stands in two verses, 28 and 29, both here. The ASV has “bases”, “panels” and “ledges”, the KJV “borders” and “ledges”, the NIV “movable stands” and “side panels attached to uprights”, and the NWT 1984 “carriages”, “sidewalls” and “crossbars”.

Lions, oxen and cherubim. “And upon the panels that were between the crossbars were lions, oxen and cherubim… and beneath the lions and the oxen were wreaths, work hanging down”: the cherubim are the ones of the inner sanctuary (see cherub), and the word for wreaths, loyot, is named in verses 29, 30 and 36. The ASV has “wreaths of hanging work”, the KJV “certain additions made of thin work”, the NIV “wreaths of hammered work”, and the NWT 1984 “wreaths in hanging work”. “One casting, one measure, one form for all of them” answers “one measure and one form” of the two cherubim (1 Kings 6:25).

Wheels like a chariot’s. “And four wheels of bronze for the one stand, and axles of bronze”: the word for axles has the consonants of the Philistine “lords”, seranim (1 Samuel 29:2, now on these pages). “And the work of the wheels was like the work of a chariot wheel: their axles and their rims and their spokes and their hubs — all of it cast”: the ASV has “their axletrees, and their felloes, and their spokes, and their naves, were all molten”, the NIV “the axles, rims, spokes and hubs were all of cast metal”, and the NWT 1984 “they were all cast”. The mouth, the “crown” and the shoulders of verses 30 to 35 are obscure, and the versions differ; the NWT 1984 has “and upward was [?] cubits”, a question mark where the number of cubits of the mouth should be.

The lavers, and the shoulders of the house. “Ten lavers of bronze: forty baths the one laver held, four cubits the one laver”: ten of forty baths are four hundred, and the sea held two thousand. The laver is kiyyor, the “laver of bronze” of the tent (Exodus 30:18). The ASV has “ten lavers of brass: one laver contained forty baths”. The NIV has “ten bronze basins, each holding forty baths”. The NWT 1984 has “ten basins of copper”. “Five at the right shoulder of the house and five at the left shoulder of the house; and the sea he set at the right shoulder of the house, eastward, toward the south”: “the right shoulder” is the one of 1 Kings 6:8. The ASV has “five on the right side of the house, and five on the left side of the house”, the NIV “five of the stands on the south side of the temple and five on the north”, and the NWT 1984 “five carriages on the right side of the house”.

Verses 40–47 · 1 Kings 7 Hiram finishes the work — the list, burnished bronze, cast between Succoth and Zarethan, and a weight that was not searched out

The list. “And Hiram made the lavers and the shovels and the bowls. And Hiram finished doing all the work that he did for King Solomon, the house of Jehovah”: the first “Hiram” of the verse is spelled with a vav, Hirom, and the rest of the chapter has Hiram. The list is two pillars, two bowls, two nets and four hundred pomegranates; ten stands and ten lavers; the one sea and twelve oxen; and the pots, shovels and bowls. The ASV has “the lavers, and the shovels, and the basins”, the NIV “the pots and shovels and sprinkling bowls”, and the NWT 1984 “the basins and the shovels and the bowls”; the Hebrew has the word for lavers in verse 40 and the word for pots in verse 45.

Burnished bronze. “All these vessels that Hiram made for King Solomon, the house of Jehovah, of burnished bronze”: the ASV has “burnished brass”, the KJV “bright brass”, the NIV “burnished bronze”, and the NWT 1984 “polished copper”.

Between Succoth and Zarethan. “In the plain of the Jordan the king cast them, in the thickness of the ground, between Succoth and Zarethan”: “between Succoth and Zarethan” stands only here. Succoth is where Jacob “built himself a house, and made booths for his livestock” (Genesis 33:17), and Zarethan is the city beside which the waters of the Jordan stood in a heap (Joshua 3:16) and the district of Baana (1 Kings 4:12, all now on these pages). The ASV has “in the clay ground”, the NIV “in clay molds”, and the NWT 1984 “in the clay mold”. The king cast them, though Hiram made them.

Not searched out. “And Solomon left all the vessels, because of the exceeding great number — the weight of the bronze was not searched out”: the ASV has “Solomon left all the vessels unweighed, because they were exceeding many”, the KJV “neither was the weight of the brass found out”, the NIV “the weight of the bronze was not determined”, and the NWT 1984 “The weight of the copper was not ascertained”. After the stalls and the men that are counted (1 Kings 5:6, 5:29), this is a quantity the text declares uncounted.

Verses 48–51 · 1 Kings 7 The vessels of gold — the altar, the table, ten lampstands before the inner sanctuary, and “the holy things of David”

The altar and the table. “And Solomon made all the vessels that belonged to the house of Jehovah: the altar of gold, and the table of gold on which was the bread of the presence”: the bread is “bread of the presence, before me always” (Exodus 25:30, now on these pages). The ASV has “the table whereupon the showbread was, of gold”, the NIV “the golden table on which was the bread of the Presence”, and the NWT 1984 “the table on which was the showbread, of gold”.

Ten lampstands. “And the lampstands, five on the right and five on the left, before the inner sanctuary — pure gold”: the tent had one, “a lampstand of pure gold; of hammered work” (Exodus 25:31), and the pure gold, zahav sagur, is the gold of the inner sanctuary (1 Kings 6:20). The ASV has “the candlesticks, five on the right side, and five on the left, before the oracle, of pure gold”, the NIV “the lampstands of pure gold (five on the right and five on the left, in front of the inner sanctuary)”, and the NWT 1984 “five to the right and five to the left before the innermost room, of pure gold”. “The flower and the lamps and the tongs, of gold”: “tongs” stands in three verses, here, the parallel of 2 Chronicles 4:21 and the coal that touches Isaiah’s mouth (Isaiah 6:6), neither of them translated here yet. The ASV has “the flowers, and the lamps, and the tongs, of gold”.

Cups, fire-pans, and sockets. “And the cups and the snuffers and the bowls and the spoons and the fire-pans — pure gold; and the sockets for the doors of the inner house, for the most holy place”: the vessel names are hard to match, and each version has its own. The ASV has “the cups, and the snuffers, and the basins, and the spoons, and the firepans, of pure gold”, the KJV “the bowls, and the snuffers, and the basons, and the spoons, and the censers of pure gold”, the NIV “gold sockets for the doors of the innermost room, the Most Holy Place”, and the NWT 1984 “the sockets for the doors of the inner house, that is, the Most Holy”. The ASV has “the hinges, both for the doors of the inner house, the most holy place”.

The holy things of David. “And all the work that King Solomon did for the house of Jehovah was completed; and Solomon brought in the holy things of David his father — the silver and the gold and the vessels”: “the holy things of David” stands only here, and David had dedicated to Jehovah “the silver and the gold that he had dedicated from all the nations that he had subdued” (2 Samuel 8:11, now on these pages). The ASV has “the things which David his father had dedicated, even the silver, and the gold, and the vessels”, the NIV “the things his father David had dedicated”, and the NWT 1984 “the things made holy by David his father”.

Patterns worth carrying forward

Where this translation keeps the words exact and distinct: “bronze” for nechoshet, as in 1 Kings 4:13; “hewn stone” for gazit, as in 1 Kings 6:36; “cedar beams”; “gourds” for peqa’im, as in 1 Kings 6:18; “cherubim”; “pure gold”, as in 1 Kings 6:20; “the inner sanctuary” and “the most holy place”; “the right shoulder of the house”, as in 1 Kings 6:8; “the laver” for kiyyor, as in Exodus 30:18; “the stand”, “the sea” and “one casting, one measure, one form”; and “bread of the presence”, as in Exodus 25:30; with the formulas that differ in the notes.

Echoes paid. Seven years and the finished house (1 Kings 6:38; 3:1); the wisdom that does justice (3:28); Pharaoh’s daughter (3:1); the inner court with its cedar beams and the gourds of the cedar (6:36; 6:18); the hewn stone and the saw (2 Samuel 12:31); the king’s Hiram (5:15); the forger of bronze and the filled craftsman (Genesis 4:22; Exodus 31:3); Jachin, Boaz and Zarethan (Genesis 46:10; Exodus 6:15; Ruth 2:1; Joshua 3:16; 1 Kings 4:12); Succoth (Genesis 33:17); the cherubim and “one measure and one form” (6:25); the right shoulder (6:8); the laver of bronze, the bread of the presence and the lampstand (Exodus 30:18; 25:30, 31); the pure gold of the inner sanctuary (6:20); the Philistine lords and the axles (1 Samuel 29:2); and what David dedicated (2 Samuel 8:11).

Echoes planted. The pure gold and the cups of the king’s table in 1 Kings 10, and the Chronicler’s numbers for the sea, three thousand baths, and the tongs (2 Chronicles 4:5, 21); the “holy things of David” put in the treasuries of the house; and the inner sanctuary, where the ark is brought, which the next chapter reaches.
