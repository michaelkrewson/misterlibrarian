In the first year of Belshazzar, Daniel dreams of the four winds on the great sea and four great beasts, each different: a lion with eagle’s wings and a man’s heart, a bear raised on one side, a leopard with four wings and four heads, and a fourth beast with iron teeth and ten horns, among which comes a little horn with eyes and a mouth; thrones are placed, the Ancient of Days sits with books opened and a river of fire, the beast is killed, and one like a son of man comes with the clouds to the Ancient of Days and is given dominion; an attendant tells him the four beasts are four kings, the fourth a fourth kingdom, the horn a king who wars on the holy ones for a time, times and half a time, until the court sits and the kingdom is given to the people of the holy ones of the Most High; Daniel keeps it in his heart.
Dated by its own first verse to the first year of Belshazzar (before the feast of Daniel 5 and the vision of Daniel 8, which is dated to his third year). The last of the book’s Aramaic chapters. ⚠ The chapter does not name the four kingdoms or the ten kings; identifications are later readings.

1
א  בִּשְׁנַת חֲדָה, לְבֵלְאשַׁצַּר מֶלֶךְ בָּבֶל, דָּנִיֵּאל חֵלֶם חֲזָה, וְחֶזְוֵי רֵאשֵׁהּ עַל-מִשְׁכְּבֵהּ; בֵּאדַיִן חֶלְמָא כְתַב, רֵאשׁ מִלִּין אֲמַר.
In the first year of Belshazzar king of Babylon, Daniel saw a dream, and visions of his head on his bed; then he wrote the dream, and told the sum of the matters.note
En el primer año de Belsasar rey de Babilonia, Daniel vio un sueño, y visiones de su cabeza en su cama; luego escribió el sueño, y refirió la suma de los asuntos.
2
ב  עָנֵה דָנִיֵּאל וְאָמַר, חָזֵה הֲוֵית בְּחֶזְוִי עִם-לֵילְיָא; וַאֲרוּ, אַרְבַּע רוּחֵי שְׁמַיָּא, מְגִיחָן, לְיַמָּא רַבָּא.
Daniel answered and said: I was watching in my vision by night, and behold, the four winds of heaven were bursting out upon the great sea.note
Daniel respondió y dijo: Yo miraba en mi visión de noche, y he aquí que los cuatro vientos del cielo irrumpían sobre el gran mar.
3
ג  וְאַרְבַּע חֵיוָן רַבְרְבָן, סָלְקָן מִן-יַמָּא, שָׁנְיָן, דָּא מִן-דָּא.
And four great beasts came up from the sea, each different from the other.note
Y cuatro bestias grandes subían del mar, diferentes la una de la otra.
4
ד  קַדְמָיְתָא כְאַרְיֵה, וְגַפִּין דִּי-נְשַׁר לַהּ; חָזֵה הֲוֵית עַד דִּי-מְּרִיטוּ גפיה גַפַּהּ וּנְטִילַת מִן-אַרְעָא, וְעַל-רַגְלַיִן כֶּאֱנָשׁ הֳקִימַת, וּלְבַב אֱנָשׁ, יְהִיב לַהּ.
The first was like a lion, and it had the wings of an eagle; I was watching until its wings were plucked, and it was lifted from the earth, and made to stand on two feet like a man, and the heart of a man was given to it.note
La primera era como un león, y tenía alas de águila; yo miraba hasta que sus alas fueron arrancadas, y fue levantada de la tierra, y se la hizo ponerse sobre dos pies como un hombre, y le fue dado un corazón de hombre.

5
ה  וַאֲרוּ חֵיוָה אָחֳרִי תִנְיָנָה דָּמְיָה לְדֹב, וְלִשְׂטַר-חַד הֳקִמַת, וּתְלָת עִלְעִין בְּפֻמַּהּ, בֵּין שניה שִׁנַּהּ; וְכֵן אָמְרִין לַהּ, קוּמִי אֲכֻלִי בְּשַׂר שַׂגִּיא.
And behold another beast, a second, like a bear; and it was raised up on one side, and three ribs were in its mouth between its teeth; and thus they said to it: Arise, eat much flesh.note
Y he aquí otra bestia, una segunda, semejante a un oso; y estaba levantada de un lado, y tenía en su boca tres costillas entre sus dientes; y así le decían: Levántate, come mucha carne.
6
ו  בָּאתַר דְּנָה חָזֵה הֲוֵית, וַאֲרוּ אָחֳרִי כִּנְמַר, וְלַהּ גַּפִּין אַרְבַּע דִּי-עוֹף, עַל-גביה גַּבַּהּ; וְאַרְבְּעָה רֵאשִׁין לְחֵיוְתָא, וְשָׁלְטָן יְהִיב לַהּ.
After this I was watching, and behold another, like a leopard, and it had four wings of a bird on its back; and the beast had four heads, and dominion was given to it.note
Después de esto yo miraba, y he aquí otra, semejante a un leopardo, y tenía cuatro alas de ave en su espalda; y la bestia tenía cuatro cabezas, y le fue dado dominio.
7
ז  בָּאתַר דְּנָה חָזֵה הֲוֵית בְּחֶזְוֵי לֵילְיָא, וַאֲרוּ חֵיוָה רביעיה רְבִיעָאָה דְּחִילָה וְאֵימְתָנִי וְתַקִּיפָא יַתִּירָה וְשִׁנַּיִן דִּי-פַרְזֶל לַהּ רַבְרְבָן, אָכְלָה וּמַדֱּקָה, וּשְׁאָרָא ברגליה בְּרַגְלַהּ רָפְסָה; וְהִיא מְשַׁנְּיָה, מִן-כָּל-חֵיוָתָא דִּי קדמיה קָדָמַהּ, וְקַרְנַיִן עֲשַׂר, לַהּ.
After this I was watching in the visions of the night, and behold a fourth beast, dreadful and terrifying and exceedingly strong; and it had great teeth of iron; it devoured and crushed, and what was left it trampled with its feet; and it was different from all the beasts before it, and it had ten horns.note
Después de esto yo miraba en las visiones de la noche, y he aquí una cuarta bestia, espantosa y terrible y extraordinariamente fuerte; y tenía grandes dientes de hierro; devoraba y desmenuzaba, y lo que sobraba lo hollaba con sus pies; y era diferente de todas las bestias anteriores, y tenía diez cuernos.

8
ח  מִשְׂתַּכַּל הֲוֵית בְּקַרְנַיָּא, וַאֲלוּ קֶרֶן אָחֳרִי זְעֵירָה סִלְקָת ביניהון בֵּינֵיהֵן, וּתְלָת מִן-קַרְנַיָּא קַדְמָיָתָא, אתעקרו אֶתְעֲקַרָה מִן-קדמיה קֳדָמַהּ; וַאֲלוּ עַיְנִין כְּעַיְנֵי אֲנָשָׁא, בְּקַרְנָא-דָא, וּפֻם, מְמַלִּל רַבְרְבָן.
I was considering the horns, and behold, another horn, a little one, came up among them, and three of the first horns were plucked up from before it; and behold, in this horn were eyes like the eyes of a man, and a mouth speaking great things.note
Yo consideraba los cuernos, y he aquí que otro cuerno, uno pequeño, subía entre ellos, y tres de los primeros cuernos fueron arrancados de delante de él; y he aquí que en este cuerno había ojos como ojos de hombre, y una boca que hablaba grandes cosas.
9
ט  חָזֵה הֲוֵית, עַד דִּי כָרְסָוָן רְמִיו, וְעַתִּיק יוֹמִין, יְתִב; לְבוּשֵׁהּ כִּתְלַג חִוָּר, וּשְׂעַר רֵאשֵׁהּ כַּעֲמַר נְקֵא, כָּרְסְיֵהּ שְׁבִבִין דִּי-נוּר, גַּלְגִּלּוֹהִי נוּר דָּלִק.
I was watching until thrones were placed, and the Ancient of Days sat; his clothing was white as snow, and the hair of his head like pure wool; his throne was flames of fire, its wheels a burning fire.note
Yo miraba hasta que fueron colocados tronos, y se sentó un Anciano de días; su vestido era blanco como la nieve, y el pelo de su cabeza como lana limpia; su trono, llamas de fuego, sus ruedas, fuego ardiente.

A river of fire was flowing and coming out from before him; a thousand thousands served him, and ten thousand times ten thousand stood before him; the court sat, and books were opened.note
Un río de fuego fluía y salía de delante de él; mil millares le servían, y diez mil veces diez mil estaban de pie delante de él; el tribunal se sentó, y los libros fueron abiertos.

I was watching then because of the sound of the great words that the horn was speaking; I was watching until the beast was killed, and its body destroyed, and given to the burning of the fire.note
Yo miraba entonces a causa del sonido de las grandes palabras que hablaba el cuerno; miraba hasta que la bestia fue muerta, y su cuerpo destruido, y entregado al fuego ardiente.

12
יב  וּשְׁאָר, חֵיוָתָא, הֶעְדִּיו, שָׁלְטָנְהוֹן; וְאַרְכָה בְחַיִּין יְהִיבַת לְהוֹן, עַד-זְמַן וְעִדָּן.
And as for the rest of the beasts, their dominion was taken away; yet a lengthening of life was given them, until a season and a time.note
Y en cuanto a las demás bestias, su dominio les fue quitado; pero les fue dado un alargamiento de vida hasta una sazón y un tiempo.
13
יג  חָזֵה הֲוֵית, בְּחֶזְוֵי לֵילְיָא, וַאֲרוּ עִם-עֲנָנֵי שְׁמַיָּא, כְּבַר אֱנָשׁ אָתֵה הֲוָא; וְעַד-עַתִּיק יוֹמַיָּא מְטָה, וּקְדָמוֹהִי הַקְרְבוּהִי.
I was watching in the visions of the night, and behold, with the clouds of heaven one like a son of man was coming; and he reached the Ancient of Days, and they brought him near before him.note
Yo miraba en las visiones de la noche, y he aquí que con las nubes del cielo venía uno como un hijo de hombre; y llegó hasta el Anciano de días, y lo hicieron acercarse delante de él.

14
יד  וְלֵהּ יְהִב שָׁלְטָן, וִיקָר וּמַלְכוּ, וְכֹל עַמְמַיָּא אֻמַּיָּא וְלִשָּׁנַיָּא, לֵהּ יִפְלְחוּן; שָׁלְטָנֵהּ שָׁלְטָן עָלַם, דִּי-לָא יֶעְדֵּה, וּמַלְכוּתֵהּ, דִּי-לָא תִתְחַבַּל.  {פ}
And to him was given dominion, and glory, and kingdom, that all peoples, nations and languages should serve him; his dominion is an everlasting dominion, which will not pass away, and his kingdom one that will not be destroyed.note
Y le fue dado dominio, y gloria, y reino, para que todos los pueblos, naciones y lenguas le sirvieran; su dominio es un dominio eterno, que no pasará, y su reino uno que no será destruido.

טו  אֶתְכְּרִיַּת רוּחִי אֲנָה דָנִיֵּאל, בְּגוֹ נִדְנֶה; וְחֶזְוֵי רֵאשִׁי, יְבַהֲלֻנַּנִי.
My spirit was grieved, I Daniel, in the midst of the sheath, and the visions of my head alarmed me.note
Mi espíritu se afligió, yo Daniel, en medio de la vaina, y las visiones de mi cabeza me alarmaron.
16
טז  קִרְבֵת, עַל-חַד מִן-קָאֲמַיָּא, וְיַצִּיבָא אֶבְעֵא-מִנֵּהּ, עַל-כָּל-דְּנָה; וַאֲמַר-לִי, וּפְשַׁר מִלַּיָּא יְהוֹדְעִנַּנִי.
I came near to one of those standing there, and asked him the certainty of all this; and he said to me, and made known to me the interpretation of the matters:note
Me acerqué a uno de los que estaban allí de pie, y le pregunté la certeza de todo esto; y él me habló, y me dio a conocer la interpretación de los asuntos:
17
יז  אִלֵּין חֵיוָתָא רַבְרְבָתָא, דִּי אִנִּין אַרְבַּע--אַרְבְּעָה מַלְכִין, יְקוּמוּן מִן-אַרְעָא.
These great beasts, which are four, are four kings, who will arise from the earth.note
Estas grandes bestias, que son cuatro, son cuatro reyes que se levantarán de la tierra.
18
יח  וִיקַבְּלוּן, מַלְכוּתָא, קַדִּישֵׁי, עֶלְיוֹנִין; וְיַחְסְנוּן מַלְכוּתָא עַד-עָלְמָא, וְעַד עָלַם עָלְמַיָּא.
But the holy ones of the Most High will receive the kingdom, and possess the kingdom forever, forever and ever.note
Pero los santos del Altísimo recibirán el reino, y poseerán el reino para siempre, por siempre jamás.
19
יט  אֱדַיִן, צְבִית לְיַצָּבָא עַל-חֵיוְתָא רְבִיעָיְתָא, דִּי-הֲוָת שָׁנְיָה, מִן-כלהון כָּלְּהֵן:  דְּחִילָה יַתִּירָה, שניה שִׁנַּהּ דִּי-פַרְזֶל וטפריה וְטִפְרַהּ דִּי-נְחָשׁ, אָכְלָה מַדְּקָה, וּשְׁאָרָא ברגליה בְּרַגְלַהּ רָפְסָה.
Then I desired to be certain about the fourth beast, which was different from all of them, exceedingly dreadful, its teeth of iron and its claws of bronze, which devoured, crushed, and what was left trampled with its feet;note
Entonces quise tener certeza sobre la cuarta bestia, que era diferente de todas ellas, extremadamente espantosa, con dientes de hierro y uñas de bronce, que devoraba, desmenuzaba, y lo que sobraba hollaba con sus pies;
20
כ  וְעַל-קַרְנַיָּא עֲשַׂר, דִּי בְרֵאשַׁהּ, וְאָחֳרִי דִּי סִלְקַת, ונפלו וּנְפַלָה מִן-קדמיה קֳדָמַהּ תְּלָת; וְקַרְנָא דִכֵּן וְעַיְנִין לַהּ, וּפֻם מְמַלִּל רַבְרְבָן, וְחֶזְוַהּ, רַב מִן-חַבְרָתַהּ.
and about the ten horns that were on its head, and the other that came up, and before which three fell; and that horn that had eyes, and a mouth speaking great things, and whose appearance was greater than its companions.note
y sobre los diez cuernos que tenía en su cabeza, y el otro que subió, y delante del cual cayeron tres; y aquel cuerno que tenía ojos, y una boca que hablaba grandes cosas, y cuyo aspecto era mayor que el de sus compañeros.
21
כא  חָזֵה הֲוֵית--וְקַרְנָא דִכֵּן, עָבְדָא קְרָב עִם-קַדִּישִׁין; וְיָכְלָה, לְהֹן.
I was watching, and that horn made war with the holy ones, and prevailed over them,note
Yo miraba, y aquel cuerno hacía guerra contra los santos, y prevalecía sobre ellos,
22
כב  עַד דִּי-אֲתָה, עַתִּיק יוֹמַיָּא, וְדִינָא יְהִב, לְקַדִּישֵׁי עֶלְיוֹנִין; וְזִמְנָא מְטָה, וּמַלְכוּתָא הֶחֱסִנוּ קַדִּישִׁין.
until the Ancient of Days came, and judgment was given to the holy ones of the Most High; and the time came, and the holy ones possessed the kingdom.note
hasta que vino el Anciano de días, y se dio juicio a favor de los santos del Altísimo; y llegó el tiempo, y los santos poseyeron el reino.

כג  כֵּן, אֲמַר, חֵיוְתָא רְבִיעָיְתָא, מַלְכוּ רביעיה רְבִיעָאָה תֶּהֱוֵא בְאַרְעָא דִּי תִשְׁנֵא מִן-כָּל-מַלְכְוָתָא; וְתֵאכֻל, כָּל-אַרְעָא, וּתְדוּשִׁנַּהּ, וְתַדְּקִנַּהּ.
Thus he said: The fourth beast will be a fourth kingdom on the earth, which will be different from all the kingdoms; and it will devour all the earth, and trample it, and crush it.note

Translator's Notes — verse by verse

Verses 1–3 · Daniel 7:1–3 The first year of Belshazzar — four winds on the great sea — four beasts, each different

⚠ This chapter is in Aramaic, and it is the last Aramaic chapter of the book: the language changed at 2:4 and goes back to Hebrew at 8:1. It is also earlier than the chapter before it. The date is “the first year of Belshazzar”, so the dream is dated before the feast of Daniel 5 (now on these pages) and before the vision of chapter 8, which begins “in the third year”. The book is in the order of its telling, not of its dates. Daniel “wrote the dream, and told the sum of the matters”: the KJV has “dream and visions of his head upon his bed” and “the sum of the matters”, the NIV “visions passed through his mind as he was lying in bed” and “He wrote down the substance of his dream”, and the NWT 1984 “The complete account of the matters he told”.

⭐ Four winds, one sea. “the four winds of heaven” stands in this one verse of the Aramaic. The verb megichan is a bursting-out, and the shelf splits on it: the KJV has “the four winds of the heaven strove upon the great sea”, the ASV “the four winds of heaven brake forth upon the great sea”, the NIV “the four winds of heaven churning up the great sea” and the NWT 1984 “the four winds of the heavens were stirring up the vast sea”. The beasts come up from the sea (verse 3) and the interpreter will say they are kings who rise from the earth (verse 17).

⭐ Different, five times. The beasts are shanyan, da min da, “different, this from that”, and the root shna, to differ, is the chapter’s refrain: the fourth beast “was different from all the beasts before it” (verse 7) and “different from all of them” (verse 19), its kingdom “will be different from all the kingdoms” (verse 23), and the little horn’s king “will be different from the former ones” (verse 24). The KJV prints one word for all of it, “diverse one from another”, and the NIV “each different from the others”.

Verses 4–8 · Daniel 7:4–8 A lion, a bear, a leopard — a fourth beast with no name — a little horn with eyes and a mouth

⭐ The first beast, and a heart. The first is like a lion with an eagle’s wings, though the Douay has “The first was like a lioness”. Its wings are plucked, it is lifted from the earth and made to stand on two feet like a man, and the heart of a man is given it: the KJV has “a man’s heart was given to it”, the NIV “the mind of a human was given to it” and the NWT 1984 “there was given to it the heart of a man”. Nebuchadnezzar’s sentence was the other way: “let a beast’s heart be given to him” (Daniel 4:16, now on these pages). The word for “given” is yehiv, and it will be the chapter’s other refrain (see verse 27).

⭐ The bear, the leopard. The second is like a bear, raised up on one side, with three ribs in its mouth, and a voice says to it: the KJV has “Arise, devour much flesh”, the NIV “Get up and eat your fill of flesh” and the NWT 1984 “Get up, eat much flesh”; the one who speaks is not named. The third is like a leopard, with four wings of a bird and four heads, and the KJV has “dominion was given to it”. The chapter does not say what these first three stand for. ⚠ Readers have put names to them, most often Babylon, Media and Persia, but the Aramaic gives only an animal each, and the book names kingdoms in another place (the ram and goat of Daniel 8:20–21).

⭐ The fourth, with no animal. Of the fourth beast the verse never says “like”: it is dechilah ve’emtani vetaqqifa yattira, and the KJV has “dreadful and terrible, and strong exceedingly” and the NIV “terrifying and frightening and very powerful”. It has great teeth of iron, devours, crushes and tramples what is left, and has ten horns. Revelation takes the pieces of all four animals for its own beast (Revelation 13:2, now on these pages) and the ten horns for ten kings (Revelation 17:12, now on these pages).

⭐ A little horn, with eyes and a mouth. Among the ten comes up another horn, small, and three of the first are plucked up from before it; it has eyes like the eyes of a man and “a mouth speaking great things” stands in two verses, here and at verse 20. The KJV has “a mouth speaking great things”, the NIV “a mouth that spoke boastfully” and the NWT 1984 “a mouth speaking grandiose things”. It is a mouth, and in verse 11 it is the sound of its words that holds the seer’s eyes.

Verses 9–12 · Daniel 7:9–12 Thrones placed — the Ancient of Days — a river of fire, books opened — the beast killed

⚠ Placed, or cast down? Verse 9 begins with thrones. The KJV has “the thrones were cast down”; every other version on the shelf has them set up: the ASV “thrones were placed”, the NIV “thrones were set in place”, the NWT 1984 “thrones placed”, the Geneva “the thrones were set up”, the Douay “thrones were placed” and the TLB “thrones were put in place”. The verb is remiyu, “were thrown”, and the sense of throwing a seat into place is the common one; this translation prints “placed” and says that the older English reads it the other way.

⭐ The Ancient of Days. “the Ancient of Days”, attiq yomin, stands in this one verse, 9, and the form attiq yomaya stands in two verses, 13 and 22. The KJV has “the Ancient of days did sit, whose garment was white as snow, and the hair of his head like the pure wool”, and the TLB adds a gloss: “the Ancient of Days—the Almighty God—sat down to judge”. The figure is not named beyond that, and Revelation gives the same white hair and snow to the one among the lampstands (Revelation 1:14, now on these pages).

⭐ The court. “the books were opened” stands in this one verse, and “the court sat” stands in two verses, 10 and 26. The KJV has “the judgment was set, and the books were opened”, the NIV “The court was seated, and the books were opened” and the NWT 1984 “The Court took its seat, and there were books that were opened”. A thousand thousands serve him, ten thousand times ten thousand stand: the NWT 1984 has “a thousand thousands that kept ministering to him, and ten thousand times ten thousand”. The numbers are in Revelation 5:11 and the books in Revelation 20:12, both now on these pages. The servants here serve with shamash; the peoples of verse 14 serve with another verb.

⭐ The beast killed; the others live on. Verse 11 ends the fourth beast: the KJV has “I beheld even till the beast was slain, and his body destroyed, and given to the burning flame” and the NIV “the beast was slain and its body destroyed and thrown into the blazing fire”. The rest have their dominion taken away but live on: the KJV has “their dominion taken away: yet their lives were prolonged for a season and time” and the NWT 1984 “there was a lengthening in life given to them for a time and a season”.

Verses 13–14 · Daniel 7:13–14 One like a son of man, with the clouds of heaven — to him was given dominion

⭐ With the clouds. “like a son of man”, kevar enash, stands in this one verse, and “with the clouds of heaven” stands in this one verse. Ke is “like”, and bar enash is the Aramaic idiom for a human being: not an animal, after four animals. He is not said to be a man or the Man; he is like one. ⚠ The shelf prints it three ways. The KJV has “one like the Son of man came with the clouds of heaven”, with a capital letter and “the”; the Geneva has “one like the son of man” and the Douay “one like the son of man”; the ASV has “one like unto a son of man”, the NIV “one like a son of man, coming with the clouds of heaven” and the NWT 1984 “someone like a son of man happened to be coming”; and the TLB has “a Man—or so he seemed to be”. The Aramaic has no “the”; the capital and the article are the translators’, reading ahead to the Gospels.

⭐ Where he goes. He does not come down; he reaches the Ancient of Days and is brought near before him, so the picture is an arrival at the throne, as Acts has the cloud take Jesus up (Acts 1:9, now on these pages). The Gospels give the figure the same two words, clouds and glory, in the last days: Matthew 24:30, Mark 13:26 and Luke 21:27, and Jesus’s answer to the high priest, joined with Psalm 110:1 (Matthew 26:64, Mark 14:62); and Revelation has it twice (Revelation 1:7, Revelation 14:14). All of these are now on these pages; the Aramaic of this verse, im ananei shemaya, is the only place the phrase is found.

⭐ Given, and served. Verse 14 gives him shaltan, viqar umalku, dominion, glory and kingdom: the KJV has “there was given him dominion, and glory, and a kingdom, that all people, nations, and languages, should serve him”, the NWT 1984 “that the peoples, national groups and languages should all serve even him”, the TLB “so that all people of every language must obey him” and the NIV “all nations and peoples of every language worshiped him”. The verb is plch, the one used in Daniel 3:12 of not serving Nebuchadnezzar’s gods. “one that will not be destroyed” stands in two verses, this and Daniel 6:26 (the Hebrew Bible’s 6:27; now on these pages), where Darius says it of the God of Daniel.

Verses 15–18 · Daniel 7:15–18 Daniel is troubled — one of those standing — four kings — the holy ones of the Most High

⭐ He asks. Daniel is alarmed: the KJV has “I Daniel was grieved in my spirit in the midst of my body”, the ASV “my spirit was grieved in the midst of my body” and the NWT 1984 “my spirit was distressed within on account of it”; the word the shelf prints as “body” is nidneh, a sheath. He comes up to one of those standing, the attendants of verse 10, and asks yatsiva, “the certainty”, and the answer is an interpretation, pshar, the word of Daniel 5:26.

⭐ Four kings, and a kingdom. The answer is short: the KJV has “These great beasts, which are four, are four kings, which shall arise out of the earth” and the NIV “The four great beasts are four kings that will rise from the earth”. And then: “the holy ones of the Most High” stands in four verses, 18, 22, 25 and 27, always with the plural form elyonin, while verse 25 also has the singular illaah, “the Most High”, for the one the little horn speaks against. The shelf prints the KJV “the saints of the most High”, the Douay “the saints of the most High God”, the NIV “the holy people of the Most High”, the NWT 1984 “the holy ones of the Supreme One” and the TLB “the people of the Most High God”.

⚠ Who are the holy ones? The chapter says that they receive the kingdom (verse 18), are warred on (21) and are given it (22, 27); it does not say who they are. Readers take them as the people of Israel, or as heavenly beings, or as both; and the figure of verse 13 as the same people, or as a person who stands for them. This page prints what the verses say and marks that the reading is open.

Verses 19–25 · Daniel 7:19–25 The fourth beast explained — the horn makes war — a time, times and half a time

⭐ The fourth, again. Daniel asks about the fourth beast and the ten horns and the horn with the eyes. New in verse 19 are the claws: the KJV has “whose teeth were of iron, and his nails of brass”, the NIV “iron teeth and bronze claws” and the NWT 1984 “the claws of which were of copper”. The interpreter calls it a fourth kingdom (verse 23), and its ten horns ten kings (verse 24), with another after them “different from the former ones”. ⚠ The chapter names none of the four kingdoms, nor the ten, nor the other; any identification is a reading from outside the verses.

⭐ Three kings brought low. Yehashpil, “he will bring low”, is the root of mashpil and hashpelt in Daniel 5:19 and 5:22: God brings low whom he wishes, Belshazzar did not bring his heart low, and here the little king brings low three kings. The KJV has “he shall subdue three kings”, the ASV “he shall put down three kings”, the NIV “he will subdue three kings” and the NWT 1984 “three kings he will humiliate”.

⭐ War, and until. Verse 21 is the horn making war with the holy ones and prevailing; verse 22 is the turn, until: the KJV has “Until the Ancient of days came, and judgment was given to the saints of the most High” and the NWT 1984 “judgment itself was given in favor of the holy ones of the Supreme One”. In verse 25 the king speaks against the Most High, wears out the holy ones and intends to change times and law.

⚠ A time, times and half a time. “a time and times and half a time” stands in this one verse. The KJV has “a time and times and the dividing of time”, the ASV “a time and times and half a time”, the NIV “a time, times and half a time” and the Douay “a time, and times, and half a time”; and the TLB has “three and a half years”, a count the Aramaic does not give: iddan is “a time”, the word of “let seven times pass over him” (Daniel 4:16, where the chapter did not say how long a time was either). The same span, in Hebrew words, is in Daniel 12:7 and, in Revelation, Revelation 12:14; both are now on these pages.

Verses 26–28 · Daniel 7:26–28 The court sits again — the kingdom was given to the people of the holy ones — Daniel keeps it in his heart

⭐ The court, and the end. The court sits a second time (verse 26, as at verse 10): the KJV has “But the judgment shall sit, and they shall take away his dominion, to consume and to destroy it unto the end”, the NIV “But the court will sit, and his power will be taken away and completely destroyed forever” and the NWT 1984 “his own rulership they finally took away, in order to annihilate [him] and to destroy [him] totally”. The ones who take it away are they, not named.

⭐ Given. The kingdom is given, and the word is the chapter’s thread: the heart of a man was given to the lion (verse 4), dominion was given to the leopard (6), the burned body was given to the fire (11), life was given to the rest of the beasts (12), dominion was given to the one like a son of man (14), judgment was given to the holy ones (22), the holy ones are given into the horn’s hand (25) and the kingdom was given to the people of the holy ones (27). Whoever gives is never named. The tense is the Aramaic perfect, yehivat, “was given”, as the NWT 1984 has it: “the grandeur of the kingdoms under all the heavens were given to the people who are the holy ones of the Supreme One”; the KJV has “shall be given to the people of the saints of the most High” and the NIV “will be handed over to the holy people of the Most High”. “the people of the holy ones of the Most High” stands in this one verse, and it is the only place the verses say “the people of”.

⚠ His kingdom, whose? The last words of verse 27 are malkuteh malkut alam, “his kingdom is an everlasting kingdom”, and leh yiplechun, “will serve him”; the pronoun is singular. “kingdom is an everlasting kingdom”, in the form of this verse, stands in two verses, here and Daniel 4:3 (the Hebrew Bible’s 3:33; now on these pages, on the Daniel 3 page), where Nebuchadnezzar says it of the Most High. The shelf prints the KJV “whose kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and all dominions shall serve and obey him” and the NIV “His kingdom will be an everlasting kingdom, and all rulers will worship and obey him”, with one reader; and the NWT 1984 “Their kingdom is an indefinitely lasting kingdom, and all the rulerships will serve and obey even them” and the TLB “they shall rule all things forever, and all rulers shall serve and obey them”, with a people. The Aramaic does not settle whether it is the Most High’s kingdom or the holy ones’.

⭐ Daniel, afterwards. “my thoughts alarmed me” stands in this one verse, and “my brightness was changed” stands in this one verse: the roots of what happened to Belshazzar at the writing on the wall, his thoughts alarmed him and his brightness was changed (Daniel 5:6, 5:9, 5:10, now on these pages), now said of the one who read it. The KJV has “my cogitations much troubled me, and my countenance changed in me”, the NIV “I, Daniel, was deeply troubled by my thoughts, and my face turned pale” and the NWT 1984 “my own thoughts kept frightening me a great deal, so that my very complexion changed in me”. And he says nothing: the KJV has “I kept the matter in my heart” and the NIV “I kept the matter to myself”. “Up to here is the end of the matter” closes the Aramaic of the book.

Patterns worth carrying forward

Where this translation keeps the words exact and distinct: “different” for the root shna at verses 3, 7, 19, 23 and 24; “given” for yehiv at verses 4, 6, 11, 12, 14, 22, 25 and 27; “watching” for chazeh havet at verses 2, 4, 6, 7, 9, 11, 13 and 21; “alarmed” for bhl at verses 15 and 28; “brought low” at verse 24; “holy ones” for qaddishin at verses 18, 21, 22, 25 and 27; “one like a son of man” with no capital letters. The Name is not written anywhere in the chapter; God is attiq yomin, “the Ancient of Days”, and illaah and elyonin, “the Most High”.

Echoes paid. Daniel 3:12; Daniel 4:3 and 4:16; Daniel 5:6, 5:9, 5:10, 5:19, 5:22 and 5:26; Daniel 6:26; Daniel 12:7; Revelation 1:7, 1:14, 5:11, 12:14, 13:2, 14:14, 17:12 and 20:12; Matthew 24:30 and 26:64; Mark 13:26 and 14:62; Luke 21:27; Acts 1:9.

Echoes planted. Daniel 8:20–21; Psalm 110:1. Neither is translated here yet.

⚠ A note on order: Daniel stands on these pages at chapters 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 11 and 12, chosen by how often a passage is read rather than in sequence. The sequential thread is unchanged: next in sequence is Daniel 8.
