About

Notes from the road and from the table — what was worth the trip, what was worth the plate, and what wasn't. This is a personal notebook — where we went, what we ate, what it cost in time and shoe leather, and whether it was worth it. No sponsored posts, no press trips. Once in a while there's a disclosed affiliate link, when it's directly relevant to what I'm actually writing about — always flagged plainly right where it appears, never folded in silently. If something was disappointing, it still says so.

Photographs are my own unless credited otherwise. Places and prices were true on the day I wrote them down and probably aren't any more — check before you go.

Librarian's Stars

Entries that review somewhere carry a rating out of five. Notes and odds and ends don't — not everything is a verdict.

A scale is worth nothing unless it can say no, and the failure mode is obvious: if everywhere I enjoyed gets five, the number stops carrying information at all. That is how the big review sites ended up averaging four-and-a-half out of five and telling you nothing. So the levels below are a contract. Three stars is a good meal. Five is meant to stay rare.

5Worth crossing a city for
4I'll be going back on purpose
3Glad I went
2Fine. Something was off, or it wasn't for me
1Wouldn't return

Half stars exist for the places that sit between two of these.

Bookmarked

This page is a page turned down to come back to — places spotted, recommended, or walked past with a window worth a second look, filed there until there's time to actually sit down and eat. No stars yet, because there's no meal to score. Once one of them gets eaten, it graduates into a real entry and comes off the shelf.

Following along

There's an RSS feed if you'd like new entries to come to you. There's no newsletter, no tracking beyond an anonymous, cookie-less page counter, and nothing here is for sale.

A note on timing

Entries generally go up after I'm home again, not while I'm away.
