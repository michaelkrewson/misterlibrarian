The same letter, written from Macedonia while Paul sends the brothers ahead to Corinth. He writes about the ministry for the holy ones, tells the Corinthians what the Macedonians were told of their readiness, and sows the picture of sowing sparingly or bountifully. He ends with thanks to God for the gift.
Undated by the text: the chapter names no year. It continues the letter of chapters 2 to 8 without a break; its time-marker is the year since Achaia began the gift (9:2).

2 Corinthians 9 — The Mister Translation

1
Περὶ μὲν γὰρ τῆς διακονίας τῆς εἰς τοὺς ἁγίους περισσόν μοί ἐστιν τὸ γράφειν ὑμῖν,
Now concerning the ministry for the holy ones, it is not really necessary for me to write to you,note
En cuanto a este servicio a favor de los santos, no es necesario que les escriba,
2
οἶδα γὰρ τὴν προθυμίαν ὑμῶν ἣν ὑπὲρ ὑμῶν καυχῶμαι Μακεδόσιν ὅτι Ἀχαΐα παρεσκεύασται ἀπὸ πέρυσι, καὶ τὸ ὑμῶν ζῆλος ἠρέθισε τοὺς πλείονας.
for I know your willingness, about which I boast to the Macedonians, that Achaia has been ready for a year; and your zeal has stirred up the majority of them.note
porque conozco su buena disposición y presumo de ella ante los macedonios diciendo que Acaya está lista para ayudar desde hace un año. Es más, el entusiasmo de ustedes ha motivado a la mayoría de ellos.
3
ἔπεμψα δὲ τοὺς ἀδελφούς, ἵνα μὴ τὸ καύχημα ἡμῶν τὸ ὑπὲρ ὑμῶν κενωθῇ ἐν τῷ μέρει τούτῳ, ἵνα καθὼς ἔλεγον παρεσκευασμένοι ἦτε,
But I am sending the brothers, so that our boasting about you might not prove empty in this respect, and that you may really be ready, just as I said you would be.note
Pero envío a los hermanos para que ustedes de verdad estén listos, como dije que estarían, y así no hayamos presumido de su buena disposición en vano.
4
μή πως ἐὰν ἔλθωσιν σὺν ἐμοὶ Μακεδόνες καὶ εὕρωσιν ὑμᾶς ἀπαρασκευάστους καταισχυνθῶμεν ἡμεῖς, ἵνα μὴ λέγωμεν ὑμεῖς, ἐν τῇ ὑποστάσει ταύτῃ
Otherwise, if Macedonians come with me and find you not ready, we, to say nothing of you, would be put to shame by our confidence in you.note
Porque, si los macedonios fueran conmigo y ustedes no estuvieran listos, nosotros —y no digamos ustedes— quedaríamos avergonzados por haber confiado en ustedes.
5
ἀναγκαῖον οὖν ἡγησάμην παρακαλέσαι τοὺς ἀδελφοὺς ἵνα προέλθωσιν εἰς ὑμᾶς καὶ προκαταρτίσωσι τὴν προεπηγγελμένην εὐλογίαν ὑμῶν, ταύτην ἑτοίμην εἶναι οὕτως ὡς εὐλογίαν καὶ μὴ ὡς πλεονεξίαν.
So I thought it necessary to encourage the brothers to go to you ahead of time and to get your promised generous gift ready in advance, so that it may be ready as a generous gift and not as something extorted.note
Por eso creí necesario animar a los hermanos a ir con tiempo adonde están ustedes y preparar con antelación la generosa donación que prometieron. Así estará lista una donación generosa, y no una donación hecha a la fuerza.
6
Τοῦτο δέ, ὁ σπείρων φειδομένως φειδομένως καὶ θερίσει, καὶ ὁ σπείρων ἐπʼ εὐλογίαις ἐπʼ εὐλογίαις καὶ θερίσει.
But this is the point: whoever sows sparingly will also reap sparingly, and whoever sows bountifully will also reap bountifully.note
Relacionado con esto, el que siembra poco cosechará poco y el que siembra mucho cosechará mucho.
7
ἕκαστος καθὼς προῄρηται τῇ καρδίᾳ, μὴ ἐκ λύπης ἢ ἐξ ἀνάγκης· ἱλαρὸν γὰρ δότην ἀγαπᾷ ὁ θεός.
Let each one give as he has decided in his heart, not grudgingly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver.note
Que cada uno haga lo que ha decidido en su corazón, y no de mala gana ni a la fuerza, porque Dios ama al que da con alegría.
8
δυνατεῖ δὲ ὁ θεὸς πᾶσαν χάριν περισσεῦσαι εἰς ὑμᾶς, ἵνα ἐν παντὶ πάντοτε πᾶσαν αὐτάρκειαν ἔχοντες περισσεύητε εἰς πᾶν ἔργον ἀγαθόν·
And God is able to cause all his undeserved kindness to abound toward you, so that you always have complete sufficiency in everything and abound in every good work.note
Es más, Dios es capaz de mostrarles gran bondad inmerecida a ustedes para que nunca les falte lo necesario y además tengan bastante para realizar todo tipo de buenas obras.
9
(καθὼς γέγραπται· Ἐσκόρπισεν, ἔδωκεν τοῖς πένησιν, ἡ δικαιοσύνη αὐτοῦ μένει εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα·
(As it is written: “He has scattered widely; he has given to the poor. His righteousness remains forever.”note
(Así como está escrito: «Él ha repartido generosamente; les ha dado a los pobres. Su justicia permanece para siempre».
10
ὁ δὲ ἐπιχορηγῶν σπόρον τῷ σπείροντι καὶ ἄρτον εἰς βρῶσιν χορηγήσει καὶ πληθυνεῖ τὸν σπόρον ὑμῶν καὶ αὐξήσει τὰ γενήματα τῆς δικαιοσύνης ὑμῶν·)
Now the one who supplies seed to the sower and bread for eating will supply and multiply your seed and increase the harvest of your righteousness.)note
El que suministra abundante semilla al sembrador y pan para comer suministrará y multiplicará la semilla para que ustedes siembren y aumentará la cosecha de la justicia de ustedes).
11
ἐν παντὶ πλουτιζόμενοι εἰς πᾶσαν ἁπλότητα, ἥτις κατεργάζεται διʼ ἡμῶν εὐχαριστίαν τῷ θεῷ—
You are being enriched in everything for all generosity, which through us produces thanksgiving to God.note
A ustedes se les está enriqueciendo en todo para que sean generosos de todas las maneras posibles, y mediante nosotros esa generosidad provoca muestras de agradecimiento a Dios.
12
ὅτι ἡ διακονία τῆς λειτουργίας ταύτης οὐ μόνον ἐστὶν προσαναπληροῦσα τὰ ὑστερήματα τῶν ἁγίων, ἀλλὰ καὶ περισσεύουσα διὰ πολλῶν εὐχαριστιῶν τῷ θεῷ—
For the ministry of this public service is not only supplying what the holy ones lack, but it also overflows with many expressions of thanks to God.note
Porque este servicio público que se está realizando no consiste solo en satisfacer ampliamente las necesidades de los santos, sino también en ser ricos en muchas muestras de agradecimiento a Dios.
13
διὰ τῆς δοκιμῆς τῆς διακονίας ταύτης δοξάζοντες τὸν θεὸν ἐπὶ τῇ ὑποταγῇ τῆς ὁμολογίας ὑμῶν εἰς τὸ εὐαγγέλιον τοῦ Χριστοῦ καὶ ἁπλότητι τῆς κοινωνίας εἰς αὐτοὺς καὶ εἰς πάντας,
Through the proof this ministry gives, they glorify God for your submission to the good news of the Christ, as you have declared publicly, and for the generosity of your contribution to them and to all.note
Así, ellos le darán gloria a Dios porque estas labores de socorro prueban que ustedes son sumisos a las buenas noticias acerca del Cristo, como ustedes declararon públicamente, y que son generosos en sus contribuciones para ellos y para todos.
14
καὶ αὐτῶν δεήσει ὑπὲρ ὑμῶν ἐπιποθούντων ὑμᾶς διὰ τὴν ὑπερβάλλουσαν χάριν τοῦ θεοῦ ἐφʼ ὑμῖν.
And with their prayers for you, they long for you because of the surpassing kindness of God toward you.note
Y, cuando ellos rueguen por ustedes, expresarán el cariño que les tienen debido a la extraordinaria bondad inmerecida de Dios que se les ha mostrado a ustedes.
15
χάρις τῷ θεῷ ἐπὶ τῇ ἀνεκδιηγήτῳ αὐτοῦ δωρεᾷ.
Thanks be to God for his indescribable gift.note
Le damos gracias a Dios por su indescriptible regalo.
Verses 1–5 · the ministry for the holy ones, the boasting that must not prove empty, and not as something extorted
“Not necessary to write to you.” peri men gar tēs diakonias tēs eis tous hagious perisson moi estin to graphein hymin, it is superfluous for me to write to you about the ministry for the holy ones. The verb is perisson, superfluous, and the sentence then turns to the Macedonians and to the boast Paul makes about the Corinthians. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “it is not really necessary for me to write you.” The ASV “it is superfluous for me to write to you.”

“Not as something extorted.” kai mē hōs pleonexian, not as a matter of greed, and the word pleonexia is the word the NWT and the ASV render as extortion. The gift is to be given freely, and not forced out of them. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “so that this might be ready as a generous gift, and not as something extorted.” The KJV “as a matter of bounty, and not as of covetousness.”

Verses 6–7 · sowing sparingly and bountifully, and God who loves a cheerful giver
“Whoever sows sparingly.” ho speiron pheidomenōs pheidomenōs kai therisei, whoever sows sparingly will also reap sparingly. The sentence is an image from farming set against giving, and the same verb is used for both sowing and reaping. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “whoever sows sparingly will also reap sparingly, and whoever sows bountifully will also reap bountifully.” The ASV “He that soweth sparingly shall reap also sparingly.”

“God loves a cheerful giver.” hilaron gar doten agapa ho theos, for God loves a cheerful giver, and the adjective hilaros is cheerful or glad. The verse says the giving is not to be done grudgingly or under compulsion. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “not grudgingly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver.” The ASV “not grudgingly, or of necessity: for God loveth a cheerful giver.”

Verses 8–10 · the abounding grace, the scattering to the poor, and the seed that multiplies
“Complete sufficiency in everything.” pasan autarkeian echontes, having all sufficiency, and perisseuēte eis pan ergon agathon, that you may abound in every good work. The sentence joins the grace of God to the Corinthians’ sufficiency and their work. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “you are always completely self-sufficient in everything, as well as having plenty for every good work.” The ASV “having always all sufficiency in everything, may abound unto every good work.”

“He has scattered widely; he has given to the poor.” The words of verse 9 are a quotation of the psalm about the righteous man, in the Septuagint numbering Psalm 111:9, and the sentence ends on the righteousness that remains forever. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “He has distributed widely; he has given to the poor. His righteousness continues forever.” The ASV “He hath scattered abroad, he hath given to the poor; His righteousness abideth for ever.”

Verses 11–15 · enriched for all generosity, the ministry that overflows in thanks, and the indescribable gift
“Enriched in everything for all generosity.” en panti ploutizomenoi eis pasan haplotēta, enriched in everything for every kind of generosity. The word haplotēs means singleness or generosity of heart, the NWT renders it as generosity and the ASV as liberality. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “you are being enriched for every sort of generosity, which produces through us an expression of thanks to God.” The ASV “ye being enriched in everything unto all liberality, which worketh through us thanksgiving to God.”

“The ministry overflows.” hē diakonia tēs leitourgias tautēs, the ministry of this public service, and perisseuousa, overflowing with many thanksgivings to God. The sentence says the ministry supplies what the holy ones lack and also overflows in thanks. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “to provide well for the needs of the holy ones but also to be rich in many expressions of thanks to God.” The ASV “aboundeth also through many thanksgivings unto God.”

“His indescribable gift.” tē anekdiēgētō autou dōreā, for his indescribable free gift. The adjective means one that cannot be told in full. The shelf: the NWT 2013 “Thanks be to God for his indescribable free gift.” The KJV “Thanks be unto God for his unspeakable gift.”

Patterns worth carrying forward

Where this translation keeps the exact words: “ministry” for diakonia at 9:1, 9:12 and 9:13; “generosity” for haplotēs at 9:11 and 9:13; “thanksgiving” for eucharistia at 9:11 and 9:12; “indescribable” for anekdiēgētos at 9:15.

Echoes paid. The ministry of 9:1 is the collection of chapter 8 (8:4); the boast of 9:2 returns the boast over the Corinthians of 7:14 and 8:24; the seed of 9:6 is the sowing of the gift, and the righteousness of 9:9–10 is the righteousness of 5:21.

Echoes planted. The chapter ends on the gift as thanksgiving to God (9:12–15), and the last four chapters turn to Paul’s own authority and the sharp letter.
